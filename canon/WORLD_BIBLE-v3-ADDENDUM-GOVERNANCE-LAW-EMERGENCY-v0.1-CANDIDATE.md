# 1. Baseline civic architecture

## Shipwide / civilization-wide elected legislature
Working inherited term:
**선내의회 / Civic Assembly**.

Role:
- law,
- budget,
- public policy,
- emergency-law framework,
- oversight,
- executive selection/replacement.

Approximate seat count remains author-side ~120–180 class; exact number unnecessary unless vote count matters.

## Collective civic executive
~5–7 members candidate.

No president/captain sovereign figure.

Role:
- coordinate civil agencies,
- execute law/budget,
- cross-agency emergency policy,
- public accountability.

## Local councils
Own ordinary district/local implementation:
- neighborhood services,
- schools/community facilities,
- local housing administration,
- public realm,
- local grants/complaints.

They do not own strategic life-support or independent armed force.

## Judiciary
Multi-level courts handle:
- civil/property/contract,
- criminal,
- family/guardianship,
- administrative disputes,
- rights/charter review.

# 2. Technical authority vs political authority

Critical v3 rule:
**technical feasibility does not equal civic sovereignty.**

Examples:
- structural specialist may declare a component unsafe under professional safety rules,
- medical team may determine clinical capacity,
- logistics may state actual transport constraints,
- emergency command may close immediate danger zones,
- elected/civic institutions decide distributive/political choices inside those real constraints.

This prevents protagonist competence from becoming magical political authority.

---

# 3. Protagonist authority boundary

As ship-damage analyst, protagonist may:
- inspect damage,
- issue/record professional safety findings,
- recommend temporary access restriction,
- refuse to certify unsafe reopening,
- advise incident command about structural consequence.

He may NOT by profession alone:
- order citywide evacuation,
- arrest suspects,
- seize resources,
- access sealed personal/archival data,
- override medical triage,
- command armed response,
- declare emergency government.

Important plot benefit:
he often knows `this cannot be opened safely` while someone else owns the decision of what to do about trapped people.

---

# 4. Emergency response authority

Use layered incident authority.

## Immediate local life-safety action
Emergency-response command may:
- establish hazard perimeter,
- close route/sector temporarily,
- direct evacuation,
- deploy rescue teams,
- coordinate police/medical/technical units,
where delay creates imminent danger.

Selene-type commander can own these decisions in Act2.

## Extended restrictions
Longer closures/resource controls require:
- documented necessity,
- civil/agency oversight,
- periodic review,
- rights challenge where practicable.

## Strategic emergency powers
Sustained civilization-wide rationing, work reassignment, broad curfew/access or election effects require stronger legal authority and review.

Act6 should stress these safeguards rather than pretend they do not exist.

# 5. Security / criminal investigation

Sora-type investigators may:
- secure scenes,
- interview witnesses,
- access lawful operational records,
- seek warrants/orders for protected personal data,
- investigate sabotage/fraud/violence.

Emergency exceptions:
limited when immediate life danger exists, logged and reviewable.

Hard:
closed city != total warrantless surveillance.

Infrastructure telemetry can be dense while personal life remains legally protected.

---

# 6. Memory anomaly and evidence law

A claimed cross-history memory is NOT automatically legal proof.

Baseline legal treatment before ontology reveal:
- testimony may be reported,
- corroboration required,
- impossible specificity can justify investigation but not guilt,
- medical/privacy safeguards apply,
- fraud based on claimed foreknowledge remains ordinary fraud if elements are met.

After lineage truth:
other-history evidence can establish historical/ontological facts but does NOT automatically create local criminal liability.

Hard:
**A current person is not guilty of a counterpart's crime.**

Likewise:
- being heroic elsewhere grants no office here,
- remembered marriage grants no spouse status here,
- remembered debt/property does not transfer automatically.

---

# 7. Memory privacy

No routine compulsory `memory extraction` power.

If advanced clinical/technical methods can inspect memory-related data, they require:
- consent or narrow judicial/medical authority,
- purpose limitation,
- minimum necessary access,
- audit trail.

Act8 old system practices may violate this norm historically, which creates conflict.

Hard:
Do not invent a perfect mind-reading police tool.

---

# 8. Family / kinship law under lineage conflict

Current-history legal relations control current rights unless a valid current legal process changes them.

Therefore another-history memory does not automatically grant:
- custody,
- parenthood,
- marriage,
- inheritance,
- guardianship.

Emotional claims can be real without being enforceable legal claims.

Branch-born child:
independent person with full rights in its own lived society.
Cannot be treated as property of source-lineage parents/system.

---

# 9. D3 personhood crisis — late-series legal transition

Before Act7/8, H-B institutions do not understand the full cross-lineage field.

When D3 truth becomes undeniable, existing H-B law is insufficient because:
- other lived societies exist,
- some are quiescent,
- some contain unique persons/children,
- H-B cannot simply claim sovereignty over them because reader follows H-B.

Selected transition:
**Emergency Transitional Compact**, not instant permanent constitution.

Principles:
- active D3 persons are not disposable experimental property,
- quiescent claims are not forfeited by silence,
- no vote manufacturing via arbitrary new D3 forks,
- HUMAN:1 gets no special seat/veto,
- shared governance controls cross-lineage/external commons only,
- internal societies retain autonomy subject to minimum personhood rights.

Permanent federal/dual representation can remain post-ending work.

---

# 10. Quiescent D3 representation

Open societies can speak for themselves.
Quiescent society cannot meaningfully cast ordinary live votes.

Working safeguard candidate:
- continuity trustees / limited guardianship representation for preservation interests,
- narrow mandate: prevent destruction, protect wake/review rights, maintain provenance,
- no power to invent political preferences for sleeping populations.

This is a rights-preservation mechanism, not proxy sovereign rule.

---

# 11. Election and HUMAN:1

Retain hard v2 rule:
**Natural-Origin status never changes vote weight.**

When HUMAN:1 eventually becomes a social/legal person after birth/development:
- ordinary citizenship/personhood path,
- no founder veto,
- no ownership of ship/planet,
- no hereditary sovereignty.

Before capacity for civic participation, ordinary child/guardian law applies.

---

# 12. Archive / information governance

Hana-type archive system protects:
- provenance,
- correction history,
- access class,
- legal preservation holds.

A record can be:
- wrong,
- incomplete,
- politically edited,
- inherited from another lived history,
without making `the archive` omniscient.

Act5 needs lawful friction around access rather than protagonist simply hacking everything.

---

# 13. Logistics/resource authority

Mira-type allocation staff control operations only inside policy/delegation.

They can:
- reroute capacity,
- prioritize under standing emergency rules,
- expose impossible constraints.

They cannot:
- permanently redefine citizen rights,
- seize all private assets,
- declare who morally deserves survival.

Act6 conflict comes when technical allocation creates de facto political power under scarcity.

---

# 14. Medical authority

Leila/Rafi distinction:
- field rescue determines immediate stabilization/transport decisions,
- hospital triage determines capacity/treatment priority under clinical rules,
- civic authority sets lawful public-health/emergency frameworks,
- no one doctor becomes city ruler because medicine is scarce.

Patient autonomy remains default until incapacity/emergency exceptions.

---

# 15. Local district politics

Normal political conflict should include boring issues before existential ones:
- transit frequency,
- housing redevelopment,
- school allocation,
- noise/industrial zoning,
- market/co-op regulation,
- maintenance budgets,
- privacy/sensor policy,
- park/green access.

This makes later emergency-authority fights feel like stress on real institutions rather than sudden dystopia switch.
