# 1. Core Premise — CANON

- The interstellar generation ship is **Meridian / 메리디언**.
- Citizens usually say `본선 / 선내 / 배`; the ship proper name appears mainly in historical/legal/mission contexts.
- Current population is **roughly 300,000**, dynamically changing within a safe operating band.
- Citizens are genuinely born, grow, form families, learn, work, become ill, age and can die.
- Most citizens have living human-form biological bodies and brains.
- The decisive old technical distinction is **cognitive developmental origin**, not body material.
- At the major story Reveal, the actual current active population contains exactly **one Natural/Bio-Origin Cognition citizen**.
- The overwhelming remainder are **Seeded/Synthetic-Origin Cognition** citizens.
- Modern civic society ordinarily recognizes both as people/humans.

Interpretive rule:
> `우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다` is the shocking consequence of a narrow historical classification, not the narrator's ultimate moral definition of personhood.

FORBIDDEN:
- narrator declaring the other citizens objectively non-persons
- human-good / AI-evil binary
- chosen-one authority based on Natural-Origin status

# 2. Biological Body / Cognitive Origin — CANON

Most citizens possess:
- human-form DNA/cells/organs
- living biological brains
- real metabolism
- sleep, hunger, illness, injury and aging

Their consciousness is not centrally rendered by a ship computer.

The old origin categories concern **how the early high-order cognitive network began to self-organize**.

### Natural-Origin Cognition
Early high-order cognitive development proceeds without successful Connectomic Seed completion.

### Seeded/Synthetic-Origin Cognition
A verified artificial developmental prior influences the early high-order cognitive-network formation trajectory during the prenatal critical window.

---

# 3. Developmental Connectomic Seed — CANON

Common term: **시드**
Technical term: **발달 커넥톰 시드**

The Seed is a computationally generated developmental prior/attractor applied during an early prenatal critical window.

It influences the initial conditions of high-order self-model/cognitive-network organization.

It does NOT:
- upload finished memories
- specify exact adult personality
- assign loyalty or profession
- insert hidden commands
- remote-control the person
- create administrator keys
- create prophecy/special powers

After Seed completion, the person develops through ordinary pregnancy, birth, childhood, family, education, relationships and lived experience.

---

# 4. Seed Success Verification — SV-H1 CANON

A citizen is classified as confirmed Seeded-Origin only after **verified successful prenatal Seed completion**, not merely a treatment order or attempt.

During the critical window the medical process uses closed-loop developmental monitoring to verify that the generated prior has actually affected the intended early-network formation trajectory.

Verification observes treatment response, not `consciousness itself`.

Possible outcomes:
1. successful Seed completion
2. incomplete first attempt → repeat/adjustment within the window
3. incomplete/unverified course → recorded as incomplete/ambiguous, not automatically Seeded
4. deliberate full-window refusal → no Seed completion

The treatment-response signature is **transient**. Decades of plasticity, learning, aging and injury erase any simple unique adult marker.

Therefore:
- prenatal provenance can establish origin
- ordinary adult medical imaging cannot reliably determine origin

Historical/deceased ambiguous records may exist, but the current active audit reviews active ambiguity and the factual present count remains exactly one Natural-Origin citizen.

# 5. Reproduction — CANON

- Natural conception is possible.
- Assisted conception is possible.
- Prenatal Seed is standard current medical care.
- Natural-Origin development requires the full relevant Seed window to pass without verified Seed completion.
- Full-window refusal is legal but extraordinarily uncommon.

FORBIDDEN:
- universal forced infertility
- central baby factory as default society
- Seed-free fetus automatically nonviable
- unseeded birth automatically criminal

# 6. Historical Transition — H8R-2 CANON

Departure-era passengers were Natural-Origin humans.

They were not exterminated, secretly replaced, or mass-uploaded into machines.

Historical chain:
1. Early-voyage neurodevelopment, space medicine and Continuity research produce Seed technology.
2. Parents increasingly choose Seed because of probabilistic medical benefits, treatment predictability and compatibility with then-primitive recovery medicine.
3. Seeded-born children are biologically conceived/born and grow through normal lived development.
4. Rights disputes lead to legal normalization of Seeded citizens as full civic persons.
5. Provider expertise, longitudinal data and legal/medical network effects make Seed the overwhelmingly normal prenatal path.
6. Natural-Origin generations age and die under finite lifespan.
7. Rare unseeded exceptions continue historically.
8. At the present story moment, one active Natural-Origin citizen remains.

FORBIDDEN:
- exact-one Witness/Origin Continuity Protocol
- hereditary last-human bloodline
- mass adult artificial-substrate transition
- one catastrophic event replacing the original human population

# 10. Reconstruction / Death / Aging — CANON

Common social umbrella: **복원**
Medical act: **신경재건**

Normal Reconstruction repairs injury relative to the **current** neural anchor.
Old maps do not routinely roll a person back decades younger.

Advanced medicine extends healthspan through organ/tissue/vascular/cancer/metabolic treatment, but routine full-brain chronological reset does not exist.

ELASTIC lifespan range:
- ordinary center ~100–110 years
- broad ordinary ~90–120
- exceptional ~120–130+

True death remains possible through catastrophic anchor destruction, unrecoverable decline, or chosen finality.

# 17. Civil Power / Heat / Ecology — CANON / ELASTIC NUMBERS

# 28. Count Audit / Publication Privacy — CP-H1 CANON

The exact Count is not an automatic public census field.

Sequence:
1. restricted internal crosswalk of legacy provenance + prenatal Seed records + current alive registry
2. independent archive/medical/legal verification
3. privacy review because small-cell `1` creates re-identification risk
4. public-interest/legal-necessity review because the old category is referenced by future arrival-transition schemas
5. a legally required schema-integrity report releases the exact aggregate `1` while keeping identity/provenance sealed
6. the unique aggregate then creates real-world re-identification pressure

The exact count is released because the scope of a legally consequential legacy category—zero, one, or many—is materially relevant to public-law review. The release is lawful but controversial, not automatic or obviously wise.

This public schema audit occurs years before the Final Approach certification becomes binding, allowing society to live with the discovery before late legal transition.
