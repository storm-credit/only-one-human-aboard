# 1. Series Length / Macro Rule

## LOCK CANDIDATE
Target main-series length:
`~200~230 episodes` current architecture.

The work may expand modestly if episode blueprint requires, but must not extend by withholding the one-human answer.

## Frozen Structural Principle
**The major factual Reveal occurs in the first third. Reveal aftermath is longer than Reveal buildup.**

## FORBIDDEN
- 150~200 episodes of `who is the one human?` delay
- one final ontology twist replacing social consequences

# 2. Reader Promise — LOCK CANDIDATE

At every scale the work should deliver:
1. ordinary life in an unusual closed civilization
2. personal continuity/family cases that expose world rules
3. relationships not automatically canceled by memory/body/origin changes
4. decisions that create new problems instead of resetting
5. personal choices scaling into civic/resource consequences
6. mystery rewards that open action, not just another secret

20 / 100 / 200 episode regression passed at design level.

## ACT 5 — `미래를 위해 현재를 뜯는 법`

- Old Quarter/place conflict and ship redundancy costs become concrete
- legacy arrival categories collide with modern civic law

Act baseline change:

## ACT 6 — `두 고향`
Current range: **157~194**

Primary functions:
- multi-year consequences and time expansion
- Noah and C8 children become adults with independent paths
- Ella's identity no longer centered on restoration
- fixed factions split across ship/orbit/surface/new-habitat choices
- families no longer share one future automatically

## ACT 7 — `도착은 판정이 아니다`
Current range: **195~230**

Primary functions:
- legacy transition categories become operationally dangerous but do not erase ship citizenship
- multiple institutions independently handle rights/legal/asset problems
