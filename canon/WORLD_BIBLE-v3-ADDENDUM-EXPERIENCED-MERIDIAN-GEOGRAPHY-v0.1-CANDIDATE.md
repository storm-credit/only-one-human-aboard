# WORLD BIBLE v3 ADDENDUM — EXPERIENCED MERIDIAN GEOGRAPHY v0.1

Status: `DEEP-DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》
Scope: reader-facing human-scale Meridian geography inside a D3 lived civilization.

Important:
- This is **Experienced Meridian**, not a literal map of the microscopic outer substrate lattice.
- Citizens live in a coherent human-scale physical-experience environment with stable local engineering rules.
- Damage, injury, distance, access, fire, pressure, heat, transport and death are real within that lived civilization.
- Outer-Ark resource constraints may translate into capacity limits through C3, but there is no simple `this street = one outer circuit board` one-to-one map.
- Final proper district names remain subject to G10 language/naming pass. Functional names below are current working civic labels.

# 0. Geography design goals

The city must support:
1. ordinary commuting and neighborhood identity,
2. a 300k-scale mature society,
3. protagonist damage-analysis work,
4. Mira logistics network,
5. emergency-response geography,
6. hospital/archive/government separation,
7. Act2 closed-sector survival,
8. Act5 legacy/forbidden-space exploration,
9. recurring places worth losing,
10. clear reader orientation without map-lore overload.

Hard:
Use **6~8 major zones**, not 30 named districts.
Minor neighborhoods can exist but only recurrent ones get names.

# 1. Experienced-city topology

Working topology:
**three broad inhabited/service bands linked by a central transfer spine and ring/local transit.**

Reader does not need engineering diagrams.
Useful mental model:

`Civic/Core`
↕
`Residential/Commercial ring-bands`
↔ `Transit/Logistics hubs`
↔ `Industrial/Utility/Old Works`

with schools, hospitals, parks, markets and community spaces distributed rather than all centralized.

The city has:
- old areas,
- renovated areas,
- expensive convenient areas,
- industrial edges,
- quiet family areas,
- awkward legacy corridors,
- sealed service volumes.

This history texture matters more than perfect symmetry.

# 9. Legacy Layer — restricted spaces, not a normal district

Working label: **Legacy Service Layer**.

This is NOT one giant secret city underneath everything.
It is a discontinuous set of:
- old service corridors,
- sealed structural volumes,
- retired utility routes,
- archived maintenance interfaces,
- physically awkward retrofits,
- sectors removed from normal public circulation.

Most citizens know old/closed service spaces exist.
What they do NOT know is that some contain evidence inconsistent with local civic history.

Uses:
- Arun old incident location,
- Act5 forbidden-history investigation,
- lineage namespace/maintenance identifier evidence.

Hard:
No endless maze.
Each access needs a real historical/maintenance reason.

# 12. Transit grammar

Use three reader-comprehensible levels:

1. **Spine Express**
Fast cross-zone trunk.

2. **Ring / Belt Tram**
Main ordinary commuting loops.

3. **Local corridors/shuttles/walkways**
Neighborhood movement.

Freight uses partly separated routes but shares transfer choke points.

Working subjective travel bands:
- same neighborhood: 5~15 min,
- adjacent major zones: 10~25 min,
- cross-city routine: 25~45 min,
- service/legacy access: variable and often slower.

Hard:
Distances matter in disasters.
Characters cannot teleport because plot needs them.

# 13. Act2 catastrophe geography — leading

Working crisis location:
**Old Works ↔ Midring/Transfer boundary infrastructure sector**.

Why:
- cooling/power distribution can plausibly affect industry + residents + transport,
- protagonist is legitimately present through work,
- Mira/Selene/Rafi/Juno can own different consequences in different zones,
- no single room contains entire crisis.

Initiating physical chain:
1. old cooling/thermal distribution segment failure in Old Works boundary,
2. heat/coolant damages adjacent electrical distribution,
3. automated isolation closes transfer/service routes,
4. power/ventilation instability hits nearby Midring residential sectors and Transfer Belt nodes,
5. sabotage suspicion triggers security restrictions,
6. human decisions produce secondary failures,
7. outer C3 thermal-margin loss reduces safety margin,
8. final C4 continuity failover misbinding occurs only at climax.

Reader geography requirement:
Always know at minimum:
- protagonist sector,
- trapped residential sector,
- active evacuation route,
- medical destination,
- blocked transfer point.

Do not explain underlying substrate truth.

# 14. Act5 forbidden-history geography

Do NOT introduce a wholly unknown giant city.

Leading:
a **retired legacy service complex / partially redeveloped old sector** whose official history is mundane enough to exist publicly.

Discovery comes from contradiction:
- material age does not match recorded renovation,
- one sealed route implies an older traffic pattern,
- memorial/service records refer to casualty logistics with no matching disaster,
- obsolete identifiers map to lineage/maintenance namespaces that should not coexist.

This preserves mystery without `secret city under city` cliché.

---

# 15. Class/status geography

Do not create simple rich-center / poor-rim map.

Status comes from combinations:
- commute convenience,
- unit size,
- quiet,
- school access,
- maintenance age,
- green/public-space access,
- job schedule,
- social network,
- prestige of occupation.

Examples:
- some Old Works families have stable respected union/co-op status despite older housing,
- some Central residents pay heavily for convenience but have smaller units,
- some Rim families enjoy newer larger units but worse transit resilience.

This gives politics without caste map.

# 17. Geography harness

PASS if:
- reader can infer where major characters normally are,
- protagonist cannot reach every crisis instantly,
- Mira/Selene/Rafi/Juno own distinct spatial problems during Act2,
- ordinary family/community life has places unrelated to mystery,
- Act5 legacy evidence grows from existing city history,
- no secret-dungeon dependence,
- geography supports at least 3 major secondary↔secondary edges,
- districts have distinct social/work texture without caste caricature.

FAIL if:
- every important facility is next door,
- all mysteries occur in Old Works,
- every district exists only for one plot arc,
- city feels like seven themed game levels,
- outer-substrate truth leaks through ordinary geography too early.
