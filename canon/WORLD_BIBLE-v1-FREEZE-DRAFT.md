# 1. Core Premise — LOCK

- The story takes place aboard the interstellar generation ship **Meridian / 메리디언**.
- Citizens usually say `본선 / 선내 / 배`; the proper ship name is used more in historical/legal contexts.
- Current population is **roughly 300,000**, dynamically changing within a safe capacity band.
- Citizens are born, grow, form families, work, become ill, age, and can die.
- Most citizens have living human-form biological bodies and brains.
- The decisive old technical distinction is **cognitive developmental origin**, not body material.
- At story start, one active citizen matches the old `Natural/Bio-Origin Cognition` category.
- The rest overwhelmingly match `Seeded/Synthetic-Origin Cognition`.
- Modern society ordinarily recognizes both as people/humans.

Interpretive rule:
`우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다` is the shocking consequence of an old narrow classification, not the narrator's ultimate moral definition of personhood.

# 2. Developmental Connectomic Seed — LOCK

Common term: **시드**
Technical term: **발달 커넥톰 시드**

The Seed is a computationally generated developmental prior/attractor applied during an early prenatal critical window to influence initial high-order cognitive-network self-organization.

It does NOT:
- upload finished memories
- dictate exact personality
- insert loyalty/commands
- remote-control
- create admin keys
- create prophecy/special powers

After Seeding, a person develops through ordinary pregnancy, birth, childhood, learning, family and lived experience.

# 3. Reproduction — LOCK

- Natural conception is possible.
- Assisted conception is possible.
- Prenatal Seed is standard current medical care.
- A Natural-Origin cognitive development requires full-window Seed refusal/omission.
- Full-window refusal is legal but extremely uncommon.

FORBIDDEN:
- universal forced infertility
- central baby factory as default society
- Seed-free fetus automatically nonviable
- unseeded birth automatically criminal

# 4. History — H8R-2 LOCK

Departure-era passengers were Natural-Origin humans.

They were not exterminated, secretly replaced, or mass-uploaded into machines.

Historical chain:
1. early-voyage neurodevelopment/space-medicine/continuity research produces Seed technology
2. parents increasingly choose it for risk reduction, medical predictability, and compatibility with then-primitive recovery medicine
3. Seeded-born children grow normally as biological children
4. rights disputes lead to legal normalization of Seeded citizens as full civic persons
5. medical/provider/data network effects make Seed the overwhelmingly normal prenatal path
6. older Natural-Origin generations age and die under finite lifespan
7. rare unseeded exceptions continue historically
8. current active count happens to be one

FORBIDDEN:
- Witness Protocol
- exact-one preservation law
- hereditary last-human lineage
- mass adult artificial-substrate transition

# 8. Reconstruction / Death / Aging — LOCK

Common social term: **복원**
Medical act: **신경재건**

Normal Reconstruction repairs damage relative to the **current** neural anchor.
Old scans cannot routinely roll a citizen back decades younger.

Advanced medicine can extend healthspan through organ/tissue/vascular/cancer/metabolic treatment, but routine full-brain chronological reset does not exist.

ELASTIC lifespan range:
- center ~100~110 years
- broad ordinary ~90~120
- exceptional ~120~130+

True death remains possible through catastrophic anchor destruction, unrecoverable systemic decline, or chosen finality.
