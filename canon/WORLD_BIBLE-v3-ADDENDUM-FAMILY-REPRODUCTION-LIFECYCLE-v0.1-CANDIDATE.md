# WORLD BIBLE v3 ADDENDUM — FAMILY / REPRODUCTION / LIFECYCLE v0.1

Status: `OFFICIAL ACTIVE WORLD CANON — v3 PROMOTED 2026-08-21 (CHG-055)`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Make family, children, aging and death ordinary enough to sustain a 300k-person civilization while remaining compatible with ultradense D3 personhood and closed-population constraints.

---

# 0. Core principle

Meridian citizens are full persons with ordinary family lives.

They are not:
- immortal software,
- mass-produced adult NPCs,
- legally owned copies of parent lineages,
- disposable branch outputs.

A child born inside a D3 lived civilization is a **new person**.
Its rights do not depend on whether its parents' lineage existed elsewhere.

---

# 1. Family forms

Normal recognized households include:
- married couples,
- unmarried long-term partners,
- single-parent households,
- adoptive/foster families,
- multigenerational households,
- child-free partnerships,
- communal/shared-care households,
- queer families.

Legal parenthood is based on declared/recognized caregiving responsibility, not only genetic contribution.

Working candidate:
- 1~3 legal parents/primary guardians may be recognized where family formation requires it,
- wider kin/communal caregivers can hold delegated care authority.

Do not make unconventional households exotic in prose.

---

# 2. Reproduction — selected hybrid

Selected direction:
**developmental biological-equivalent reproduction + assisted reproduction + optional ectogenesis.**

Citizens possess embodied reproductive biology within Experienced Meridian.
New children emerge through real developmental continuity rather than instant adult instantiation.

Possible routes:
1. body-borne pregnancy,
2. artificial gestation/ectogenesis,
3. assisted conception using parental/donor germline material,
4. adoption/foster care.

Why hybrid:
- preserves ordinary family/pregnancy experience,
- avoids making every birth a laboratory event,
- permits medical/population planning,
- supports diverse bodies/families,
- keeps birth as gradual creation of a new continuity.

Hard:
No `download a child` process.
No adult clone child appearing fully formed.

---

# 3. How a new person exists under D3 ontology

Author-side model:

During development:
- a new biological/experiential body forms,
- a new continuity substrate allocation/anchor gradually stabilizes,
- identity emerges through development and experience,
- it is not a forked parent's continuing self.

Therefore:
parental lineage data may contribute heritable traits, but the child's continuity is its own.

This is one of the strongest later proofs that old Continuity Doctrine is ethically obsolete.

---

# 4. Population planning

A closed city cannot ignore birth/death balance.

Baseline model:
**forecast + voluntary planning + capacity incentives, not routine coercive birth permits.**

Normal tools:
- population forecasts,
- counseling,
- housing/childcare capacity planning,
- healthcare scheduling,
- temporary incentives for underrepresented age cohorts,
- support/waiting incentives when particular cohorts are already large.

Hard:
- no baseline one-child rule,
- no state eugenics scoring,
- no compulsory pairing,
- no reproductive privilege based on occupation/class.

In severe crisis histories, coercive reproductive policy can emerge as a political failure/choice, but that is not baseline Meridian.

---

# 5. Conception / genetic diversity

Citizens can combine inherited biological-equivalent traits through ordinary partnered or assisted reproduction.

Medical screening can reduce severe preventable disease but cannot become `perfect baby optimization` by default.

Possible safeguards:
- consent for donor material,
- limits on non-medical enhancement,
- privacy of hereditary data,
- anti-discrimination law,
- genetic diversity monitoring at population level without ranking individual worth.

The Natural-Origin genetic archive associated with HUMAN:1 is separate from ordinary citizen family planning.

---

# 6. Pregnancy and gestation culture

Pregnancy is socially ordinary but medically well-supported.

Body-borne gestation:
- chosen when desired/medically suitable.

Ectogenesis:
- common enough not to be stigmatized,
- used for medical risk, family structure, personal preference, work/health reasons.

Hard:
Do not imply `natural pregnancy = authentic family` or `artificial gestation = lesser`.

Workplace/family law includes:
- gestational leave,
- caregiving leave,
- partner/guardian leave,
- medical accommodations.

---

# 7. Birth / registration

Birth creates:
- independent civic identity,
- healthcare record,
- guardianship record,
- housing/education service claims,
- continuity/personhood recognition.

Important later:
A branch-born child's registration may expose conflicts in obsolete lineage law.

Old doctrine might classify the surrounding D3 society as derivative while the child has no source counterpart at all.
That contradiction becomes legally and emotionally explosive in Act8.

---

# 8. Childhood

Children are visible throughout Meridian.

They have:
- daycare/community care,
- primary/secondary school,
- clubs,
- sports,
- games,
- friendship conflicts,
- exams/projects,
- household chores,
- internet/media lives,
- ordinary rebellion and boredom.

Closed-habitat literacy includes:
- fire/pressure emergency behavior,
- evacuation basics,
- resource awareness,
- basic first aid/safety.

But children are not raised as permanent emergency cadets.

---

# 9. Adolescence / adulthood

Working civil-majority candidate:
**18 years**, subject to v2 legal regression.

Before full majority:
- staged medical/privacy autonomy,
- education/career choices expand progressively,
- apprenticeship may begin before 18,
- hazardous work access remains restricted.

Ages in prose should feel culturally normal, not governed by complicated sci-fi age tiers unless plot requires it.

---

# 10. Education-to-work transition

Common paths:
- academic higher education,
- technical apprenticeship,
- vocational credential,
- civic-service training,
- mixed work/study,
- later adult reskilling.

No single prestige ladder.

Niko fits technical apprenticeship/qualification path but must have peers and social life beyond protagonist mentorship.

---

# 11. Aging

Medicine significantly improves healthy lifespan but does not abolish aging.

Working range:
- visible functional aging remains normal,
- many citizens remain active well into later life,
- average lifespan can plausibly be around a century or somewhat longer,
- exact actuarial number is not frozen because the story does not need a demographic textbook.

Why no immortality:
- continuity still depends on active physical process,
- biological/embodied systems accumulate damage,
- radical indefinite renewal would weaken generational/civic turnover and death stakes,
- treatment can extend life but not guarantee endless continuation.

Gideon at ~58 should read as experienced senior professional, not frail elder.

---

# 12. Chronic illness / disability

Advanced medicine does NOT erase disability.

Present:
- chronic pain,
- sensory differences,
- mobility impairment,
- neurological conditions,
- psychiatric illness,
- congenital conditions,
- long-term rehabilitation.

Assistive technology is strong.

Hard:
Do not use `future medicine cured everything` as default.
Do not use disability only as tragedy clue.

---

# 13. Death

Death means end of the active subjective continuity when it cannot be preserved before causal break.

Hard:
- stored records are not resurrection,
- another lineage counterpart is not resurrection,
- an old snapshot successor is not automatically legally/emotionally the same dead person.

Families can still preserve:
- messages,
- photos/video,
- journals,
- non-interactive memory archives,
- recorded voice.

Strong guardrail:
Full generative `dead loved one chatbot/avatar` should be tightly restricted, culturally controversial or uncommon because it would blur the exact death/personhood stakes the novel needs.

---

# 14. Funeral / memorial practice

Practical body disposition:
- material-return/cremation-equivalent processes,
- medical/research donation where consented,
- limited ceremonial physical remains where culturally appropriate.

Social practices:
- family/religious rites,
- memorial walls/gardens,
- private remembrance gatherings,
- disaster memorials,
- archived messages.

Important setting rule:
A memorial asserts that **this person died in this civic history**.

Therefore later contradictions involving:
- memorial for a living H-B person,
- incompatible death dates,
- remembered funeral that has no local record,
are emotionally stronger than database numbers alone.

---

# 15. Inheritance / household continuity

Ordinary property/lease/business rights pass through legal succession.

Counterpart rule:
A same-lineage counterpart from another history does NOT automatically inherit:
- spouse status,
- parental rights,
- property,
- debt,
- criminal responsibility,
- office,
from another history's person.

This becomes crucial once other-history evidence becomes public.

---

# 16. Cross-lineage kinship conflicts

Possible later cases:
- someone remembers a spouse who never married them here,
- a parent remembers a child not born in H-B,
- a child/family exists in one D3 lineage with no counterpart in another,
- current living person appears in authentic death records elsewhere.

Hard rule:
**memory creates an emotional claim, not automatic legal custody/intimacy.**

Current person retains autonomy.

This protects H-B as the real current life.

---

# 17. Protagonist family — current candidate

Opening:
- both parents alive,
- one adult sibling household,
- niece/nephew candidate,
- no spouse/child for protagonist,
- former serious relationship with H-A Mira.

Use family for:
- ordinary meals/messages/obligations,
- school/childcare texture,
- generational disagreement,
- non-work identity,
- occasional crisis consequence.

Do NOT use family as recurring hostage mechanism.

At least half of protagonist-family scenes series-wide should be non-crisis.

---

# 18. H-A / H-B family-delta rule

Family should be an early stabilizer after H-B transition.

Recommended:
- parents broadly same and alive,
- sibling broadly same identity,
- only one subtle family detail initially differs,
- larger kinship contradiction delayed until reader has accepted H-B as its own current world.

Reason:
If romance + Arun + parents + sibling + child status all differ immediately, Act3 stops feeling like possible regression too quickly.

---

# 19. Crisis policy variations

Act6 can pressure family/reproduction through:
- housing displacement,
- childcare shutdown,
- family separation during evacuation,
- medical fertility/gestation capacity shortages,
- debate over future population targets,
- emergency guardianship.

Avoid using forced pregnancy/sterilization casually; such material would dominate the moral tone and require dedicated treatment.

---

# 20. Act8 rights-case candidate

Strong candidate case:
A fully lived D3 lineage contains a child born after divergence with no source counterpart.

Old system logic struggles because:
- parent lineages may be derivative by legacy doctrine,
- child is not a copied prior person,
- ending/erasing the lineage ends a unique child's life.

This turns personhood ethics into a concrete family problem before abstract debate.

Hard:
Do not make this child HUMAN:1.
Do not make the child magically special.

---

# 21. Scene-use examples

Ordinary:
- sibling asks protagonist to pick up niece/nephew after school,
- ectogenesis appointment conflicts with work schedule,
- family debates moving to newer Rim housing,
- elderly parent refuses a convenience treatment,
- memorial anniversary forgotten by one relative,
- teenager hides apprenticeship application.

Mystery-use:
- person remembers a child's allergy that current child does not have,
- memorial lists a living person,
- a family photo composition differs from someone's certainty,
- old guardianship record has impossible provenance.

Most family scenes should remain ordinary.
