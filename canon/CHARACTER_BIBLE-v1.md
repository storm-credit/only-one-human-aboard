# 2. Maren Vale / 마렌 베일 — CANON

- female
- age 41 at story start; ~55 near ending
- ordinary Seeded-Origin citizen
- older sister of Ella Vale
- aunt and long-term temporary guardian of Noah Vale during Ella's recovery
- **연속성 심사관** at **연속성심사국**

## Authority
Maren may make temporary/reviewable Continuity-right decisions within ordinary professional jurisdiction.

She cannot:
- issue final criminal judgments
- permanently revoke citizenship
- force Reconstruction
- rewrite records at will
- decide settlement policy
- define human status for the civilization

## Mandatory Family-Case Recusal — COI-H1 CANON
Maren is automatically recused from any formal adjudication directly involving:
- Ella's Continuity status
- Ella's parental reinstatement
- Noah's guardianship/residence
- Maren's own guardianship actions
- legal effect of Maren's hidden Ella record

Other adjudicators/panels and ordinary civic appeal/family-rights processes make those formal decisions.

In her family dispute Maren is a:
- relative
- current guardian
- witness
- record holder
- possible ethics-review subject

She is **not the judge of her own family**.

## Family Emergency Role
Long before the accident, Ella's emergency-planning bundle named Maren as:
- first-choice guardian for Noah if Ella became incapacitated
- primary family emergency contact with limited designated health/planning-record access

This access exists because of family planning, not Maren's office.
Clinical/ethics bodies—not Maren—control the Reconstruction decision.

## Core Goal
Keep Ella's return, Noah's life and her own professional standing from collapsing into one another.

## False Belief
If facts are classified correctly and the correct decision is made, emotional ambiguity can also be contained.

## Strengths
- identifies factual contradictions
- makes decisions under pressure
- reliable logistics/follow-through
- practical care

## Flaws
- protects by deciding for others
- applies principles selectively inside family
- handles practical damage before apology
- resists admitting uncertainty

## Voice
`clarify → narrow → decide`

Precise, short-to-medium; more formal when emotional.
Dry, low-frequency humor.
When angry, quieter and more exact rather than louder.

## Secret — PR-H1 CANON
About 1–2 years before Ella's accident, Ella drafted but did not formally register a conditional refusal of aggressive Reconstruction when severe autobiographical/relational loss was expected. A private message/context makes the fear serious.

After the accident, Maren lawfully accesses Ella's designated emergency planning folder and sees the draft/context, but does not surface it to the clinicians/ethics process.

The draft was not a binding directive. Disclosure could have triggered stricter substituted-judgment/ethics review or delay, not an automatic ban.

Maren's rationalizations include:
- it was unregistered
- Ella remained undecided
- clinicians believed meaningful recovery possible
- Noah was nine with no other legal parent
- Maren wanted her sister back

Ella later discovers the old draft and Maren's emergency-access metadata independently; Maren does not control the reveal timing.

## Arc
control/protect
→ professional decisions create costs
→ family hypocrisy exposed
→ learns family cannot be solved by ruling
→ accepts recusal/others' agency
→ contributes one narrow final Continuity judgment without becoming civilization's sovereign
→ remains family without owning others' choices

FORBIDDEN:
- hidden Natural-Origin twist
- anchorless-copy special origin
- genius detective who sees every answer first
- final constitutional savior

# 3. Ella Vale / 엘라 베일 — CANON

- female
- age 39 start; ~53 ending
- Maren's younger sister
- Noah's planned sole legal parent
- ordinary Seeded-Origin citizen

## Parenthood — FCH-C
Ella chose planned sole parenthood in her late 20s using ordinary assisted reproductive medicine and donor genetic material.

The donor is not a legal/social parent.
Ella carried and raised Noah.
Maren was named first-choice emergency guardian.

## Accident / Reconstruction — FC-T2
- catastrophic accident around Ella age 36 / Noah age 9
- surviving neural anchor preserved
- staged Reconstruction + long rehabilitation + functional/legal recovery over roughly three years
- limited Ella/Noah contact can occur during recovery
- story begins when Ella is capable of seriously reclaiming ordinary parental life

Ella is not a total stranger to Noah.
She is his familiar mother who remembers an earlier version of him and missed three years of ordinary shared life.

## Goal
Recover ordinary parenthood, work, relationships and agency.

## Need
Stop treating restoration of the old life as the only proof that she remains Ella.

## Strengths
- sociable
- initiates relationships/work recovery
- rebuilds networks without Maren

## Flaws
- pushes old intimacy/routines too quickly
- sees remembered Noah rather than current Noah
- rushes lost-time recovery

## Voice
`reconnect → normalize → push forward`

Longer and more associative than Maren; memory/name/anecdote rich.

## Career
Food/hospitality cooperative operations/management.
Not a famous chef. Work includes venue/staff/service/cooperative realities.

## Arc
returned parent
→ attempts old role restoration
→ independently rebuilds work/life
→ uses outside legal support
→ discovers Maren's hidden preference knowledge
→ stops defining self as `restored Ella`
→ forms a new adult relationship with Noah rather than recreating childhood exactly

FORBIDDEN:
fake-parent villain, passive restoration symbol.

# 9. Amara Okoro / 아마라 오코로 — CANON

- female
- age 45 start; ~59 ending
- sole active Natural/Bio-Origin citizen at the major Reveal
- biological body/brain like other citizens
- no special cognition/power/authority

## Origin
Amara's parents are ordinary Seeded-Origin adults who deliberately declined prenatal Seed throughout the relevant critical window during her pregnancy.

Amara knows the family fact that her parents refused Seed.
She does not know at story start that she is the only active Natural-Origin citizen or that the old schema maps this category to a narrow `Human` label.

## Job
2환 water/reclamation maintenance mid-level worker or shift lead.

Hard guardrail:
NOT chief engineer, last water expert, arrival-system specialist, or irreplaceable colony technician.

## Family
Spouse: **Leo Ortega / 레오 오르테가**, male, age 44 start, school operations/administration in 1환.

First child: **Kai Okoro / 케이 오코로**, male, age 18, ordinary Seeded-Origin, Spine logistics/traffic apprenticeship.

Second child: **Mina Okoro / 미나 오코로**, female, age 8, ordinary Seeded-Origin, school/friend life important; adult path is not preordained.

## Pre-Reveal Goal
Decide how/where the family will live before career, school and arrival-era opportunities pull them in different directions.

## Flaw
Confuses `protect family` with `keep family in one configuration / control the family boundary`.

## Voice
`reduce scale → practical consequence → protect family boundary`

## Pre-Reveal Appearance Rule
At least three meaningful appearances before identity confirmation:
1. worker/operational participant
2. commute/housing/family issue
3. arrival-resource/utility reserve issue

No ominous origin clue is attached to her beforehand.

## Self-Suspicion Guardrail — CANON
Once the public Meaning explanation makes clear that full Seed non-completion corresponds to the old Natural-Origin category, Amara can reasonably—and likely strongly—suspect that she may be the one because she knows her own family history.

The later identity phase is therefore about:
- protected official confirmation
- family decision-making under uncertainty
- public re-identification pressure
not a contrived detective mystery in which Amara fails to understand her own birth history.

## Public Identity — ID-H1R / HT-H1
After Count=1, public inference combines:
- anonymized prenatal ethics review
- one parent's modest public Seed-refusal/consent-rights trace
- ordinary age/residence/family metadata

Several false candidates exist.
Official provenance remains protected.
Amara ultimately chooses limited factual public confirmation when continued speculation harms family/others.

Her disclosure may conflict with Leo/Kai's preferred privacy strategy.

## Parents
Both parents are expected to be alive in their 70s and are ordinary Seeded-Origin supporting characters.
One remains more convinced their refusal was a legitimate good decision; the other increasingly regrets imposing a rare irreversible choice on a child who could not consent.
Neither is a prophet or secret historian.

## Arc
ordinary worker/parent
→ legacy category publicly imposed
→ family becomes political object
→ temptation to use status for family protection
→ family members choose separate paths
→ remains historically known but never becomes office/title/legitimacy key

FORBIDDEN:
last-human queen, breeding duty, genetic/archive key, special power, unique engineer, sole settler authority.
