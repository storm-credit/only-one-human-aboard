# WORLD BIBLE v3 ADDENDUM — LANGUAGE / NAMING / CULTURE v0.1

Status: `DEEP-DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Give Meridian a culture that feels centuries old and lived-in without requiring a conlang or burying the novel under lore.

---

# 0. Core cultural rule

Meridian culture is:
- descended from multiple Earth cultures,
- transformed by centuries of closed-city life,
- internally diverse,
- not one sterile `spaceship culture`,
- not a museum preserving 21st-century Earth unchanged.

Reader-facing rule:
Use a **small recurring cultural vocabulary** and imply much more beyond it.

---

# 1. Language architecture

Selected direction:
**one mature civic lingua franca + preserved heritage languages + strong translation support.**

## Civic lingua franca
Used for:
- government,
- education baseline,
- cross-district work,
- emergency communication,
- mass media.

Author does NOT need to construct a full conlang.
Korean prose represents translated speech.

## Heritage languages
Remain through:
- family,
- religion,
- music,
- food,
- names,
- private/community education,
- inherited sayings.

## Translation support
Advanced but not magical mind-reading.
Useful for:
- speech/text translation,
- archived Earth materials,
- cross-community communication.

Hard:
Translation cannot erase:
- accent,
- register,
- private slang,
- cultural reference,
- deliberate ambiguity.

# 2. Naming grammar

Centuries of intermarriage and cultural mixing mean name alone does not cleanly reveal ethnicity.

Normal patterns can include:
- Earth-derived given + Earth-derived family name from different linguistic traditions,
- inherited family name with changed pronunciation,
- shortened/lengthened given names,
- two family names in some traditions,
- chosen adult name in limited cultural communities,
- conventional single family name in most civic records.

Hard:
Do NOT force every major character into `2 Korean syllables + 2 Korean syllables` rhythm.

For Korean readability, preferred protagonist-name rhythm:
- given name 3~4 Korean syllables,
- family name 2~4 Korean syllables,
- asymmetric sound pattern,
- easy to distinguish from Mira/Selene/Rafi/Sora.

Current protagonist final name remains TBD.

Existing major names remain C1 candidates, not ethnicity locks.

---

# 3. Protagonist naming filter

Reject if:
- sounds like a game codename,
- too short/choppy in Korean,
- overly aristocratic/fantasy-coded,
- strongly duplicates another core name rhythm,
- surname was inherited only from old placeholder without story reason.

Prefer:
- ordinary adult name,
- memorable when repeated hundreds of times,
- natural in Korean narration,
- plausible in mixed Meridian culture.

Final naming should happen after reading names aloud in:
- command dialogue,
- family dialogue,
- romantic conflict,
- injury/emergency call,
- formal report.

---

# 4. Register / speech culture

Meridian Common has ordinary social registers:
- formal civic/professional,
- workplace casual,
- family intimate,
- youth slang,
- service/customer politeness,
- emergency command language.

Do not make every profession speak in technical jargon.

Emergency communication is standardized and concise because closed-city safety depends on it.

---

# 5. Worker / city slang — limited set

Use only if natural; do not build glossary-heavy prose.

Working examples, final phrasing can be localized in Korean prose:

- `dead line` / `죽은 라인` — a service route with no live capacity; technical origin, sometimes used metaphorically.
- `double-door` / `두 문` — safety-minded phrase for needing a second barrier/backup before trusting something.
- `shift ghost` — someone who is always present on night/odd shifts but barely seen socially; workplace slang.
- `old skin` — patched exterior/old surface layer on equipment/buildings; not ontology term.

Hard:
No slang may accidentally reveal substrate/lineage truth.

# 6. Food culture — recurring anchors

Keep 4~7 repeatable anchors, not a food encyclopedia.

## F1 Late-shift broth/noodle shops
A broad category of cheap hot food around Transfer Belt/Old Works.
Different stalls inherit Earth noodle/soup traditions.

Use:
protagonist work routine, Rafi/Selene post-shift scenes, ordinary recurring place.

## F2 Fermented staple spreads/sides
Fermentation remains important because it is efficient, flavorful and culturally diverse.
Different households maintain family variants.

Use:
family specificity and cross-lineage memory clues.

## F3 Cultured-protein grill/street food
Everyday affordable protein in familiar grilled/fried forms.
Nobody treats `cultured` as futuristic novelty.

## F4 Garden-grown fresh premium produce
Fresh seasonal herbs/fruit/vegetables from controlled agriculture have more cultural/status value than calories alone imply.

## F5 Festival heritage meals
Small batches use preserved/rare heritage ingredients or reconstructed recipes.
Expensive/special without becoming luxury aristocracy.

## F6 Juno community meal
Not a single universal dish.
Juno's place is known for a rotating communal menu using surplus/seasonal inputs.
This supports social-network scenes without magical `food solves conflict` trope.

# 7. Leisure / sport anchors

Use 3~4 recurring forms.

## S1 Rebound — inherited v2-compatible ship-born sport candidate
Team sport using a controlled variable-gravity/low-g court and wall/rebound geometry.

Purpose:
- unmistakably Meridian-born culture,
- school/amateur/pro league variations possible,
- visually distinct but not plot-critical.

Keep rules simple in prose.

## S2 Ordinary court/endurance sports
Running, cycling-equivalent, racket/ball sports, strength training remain recognizable descendants of Earth forms.

## S3 Tabletop / strategy / simulation clubs
Useful cross-class leisure and teen/adult social space.

## S4 Maker / repair / craft clubs
Not every member is technical professional.
Includes art fabrication, old-object restoration, miniature models, clothing, community projects.

Possible protagonist hobby candidate may come from here, but do not choose until personality/name pass.

# 9. Civic observances — keep small

## O1 Departure/Launch remembrance
Historical/cultural, not constant patriotic ritual.
Meaning varies by family and politics.

## O2 Charter/Civic Day
Marks a major civic/legal milestone rather than `we are all one perfect people` propaganda.

## O3 Major disaster memorial
One or a small number of known historical disasters with public remembrance.
Important because later absent-history memorials can conflict with what everyone thinks belongs on the calendar.

## O4 Approach observance — late series only
As destination becomes physically relevant, new public practices emerge organically rather than existing unchanged for centuries.

Hard:
Do not create ten named holidays.

# 10. Religion / philosophy

Earth-descended religions continue in changed forms alongside secular/nonreligious life.

Possible institutions:
- congregations,
- meditation/philosophy groups,
- family rites,
- memorial practices,
- interfaith civic services.

No religion is authorially `the wrong explanation` by default.

Closed-city theological/philosophical questions may include:
- continuity,
- death,
- generations born in transit,
- destination meaning,
without any faith having hidden technical truth automatically.

# 11. Folk belief / divination

Retained at low density:
- tarot-like practices,
- astrology/saju descendants,
- luck rituals,
- physiognomy folklore,
- work/launch superstitions.

Most are:
- culture,
- entertainment,
- counseling,
- identity,
- ritual.

Hard:
- no reliable system API,
- no birth-date lookup of another history,
- no reproducible prophecy mechanism.

A rare memory-leak carrier may interpret involuntary fragments through folk symbolism, but the ritual does not cause the access.

---

# 12. Everyday rituals

Small recurring habits can imply culture better than exposition.

Candidates:
- families keep a shared annual household archive/photo day,
- workers mark first independent qualification with team meal/gift rather than military pinning,
- moving homes often includes exchanging a small locally made object with neighbors,
- disaster anniversaries encourage voluntary service/memorial attendance rather than compulsory ceremony.

Use only 2~3 repeatedly.

No uniform jumpsuit society.

Normal:

- youth trends,

Status can appear through:
- material quality,

not just obvious luxury logos.

# 14. Media / social culture

Meridian has:
- public news,
- private media,
- neighborhood feeds,
- professional channels,
- fandom/creator ecosystems.

300k population effect:
- reputations travel faster than in a nation-state,
- still large enough for strangers/anonymity,
- viral rumor can become shipwide quickly,
- old posts/screenshots persist socially.

Act4 fake-prophet economy uses this heavily.

# 15. Historical memory culture

People know broad Earth-origin history but most emotional identity is Meridian-born history.

Different families value Earth heritage differently:
- some carefully preserve language/ritual,
- some mix traditions,
- some care little.

Hard:
Do not make every citizen obsessed with Earth nostalgia.

---

# 16. Cross-lineage cultural clues

Culture gives subtle contradiction types beyond death records:
- a food commemorates an event that did not occur locally,
- a sports hero differs,
- a neighborhood song has an extra verse,
- someone expects a holiday that is not on current calendar,
- family recipe references a person absent here.

Use sparingly.
Most cultural differences are not ontology clues.
