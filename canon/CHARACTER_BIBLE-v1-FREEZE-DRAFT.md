# 1. Maren Vale / 마렌 베일 — LOCK

- female
- age 41 at story start; ~55 near ending
- ordinary Seeded-Origin citizen
- older sister of Ella Vale
- aunt / temporary long-term guardian of Noah Vale
- job: **연속성 심사관** at **연속성심사국**

## Core Goal
Keep Ella's return, Noah's life, and her own professional standing from collapsing into one another.

## False Belief
Correct classification + correct decision can contain emotional ambiguity.

## Strengths
- identifies factual contradictions
- makes decisions under pressure
- reliable logistics/follow-through
- practical care

## Flaws
- protects by deciding for others
- applies principles selectively inside family
- handles practical damage before apology
- dislikes admitting uncertainty

## Voice
`clarify → narrow → decide`
Precise, short-to-medium, more formal when emotional.
Dry low-frequency humor.

## Secret — PR-H1 LOCK
About 1~2 years before Ella's accident, Ella drafted but did not formally register a conditional refusal of aggressive Reconstruction under severe expected autobiographical/relational loss. A private message/context makes the fear serious.

After the accident, Maren lawfully accesses Ella's emergency personal health/planning archive, sees the draft and remembers/locates the private context, but does not surface it to clinicians/ethics review.

The draft was not binding; disclosure could have triggered stricter substituted-judgment review/delay but would not automatically prohibit Reconstruction.

Maren's reasons include:
- it was unregistered
- Ella was undecided
- clinicians thought meaningful recovery possible
- Noah was nine with no other legal parent
- Maren wanted Ella back

Payoff priority:
Ella independently discovers her own old draft and Maren's emergency-access metadata in Act 4 (~112~122).

## Arc
control/protect
→ professional decisions create costs
→ family hypocrisy exposed
→ accepts that family cannot be solved by ruling
→ contributes one narrow final continuity decision without becoming civilization's sovereign
→ learns to remain family without owning others' choices

FORBIDDEN:
hidden Natural-Origin twist, Derived special origin, genius detective, final constitutional savior.

## Arc
