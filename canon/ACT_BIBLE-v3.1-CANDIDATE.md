Elastic:
**380~430**

Not frozen.

Reason for length:

- recurring persons must accumulate meaning,

Reader receives rotating rewards:
1. survival,
2. character reevaluation,
3. mystery answer,
4. world/culture attachment,

Each major arc resolves most of its local problem while deepening the global mystery.
