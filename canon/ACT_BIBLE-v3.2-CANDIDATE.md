# 0. Length / story engine

Working center:
**~410 episodes**

Elastic:
**~380–430**

Not frozen.

The story is long because the reader must accumulate:
- attachment to ordinary Meridian life,
- trust/distrust toward recurring people,
- grief for the first lost history,
- reevaluation of familiar people under different conditions,
- survival competence,
- mystery answers,
- institutional consequences.

**same face != restored shared history.**

### 3C EP124~136 — 나 말고도 있다

Baseline shift:
`내가 과거로 돌아왔다`
→
`내가 기억하는 과거가 유일하지 않다.`
