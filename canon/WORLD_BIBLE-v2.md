# 4. Seed Verification / Adult Detectability

Confirmed Seeded-Origin requires **verified successful prenatal completion**, not a treatment order.

Possible prenatal outcomes:
1. successful completion,
2. incomplete attempt followed by adjustment/repeat within the window,
3. incomplete/unverified course,
4. deliberate full-window refusal.

Treatment-response signatures are transient.
Decades of development/plasticity/aging/injury erase any simple unique adult marker.

Therefore:
- prenatal provenance can establish origin,
- ordinary adult body/brain scanning cannot reliably classify origin.

FORBIDDEN:
- adult origin scanner,
- casual medical test proving Amara's category.
