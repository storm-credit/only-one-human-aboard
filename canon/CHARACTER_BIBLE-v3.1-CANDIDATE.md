# 1. Protagonist — 루카스 켈러 / Lukas Keller

Final name **APPROVED 2026-08-21** (CHG-055).
Reader-facing call-name: **`루카스`** only. Do not alternate `루카스 / 켈러` for style.
Household surname line: 마르틴 켈러 (부) · 클라라 켈러 (누나). 나디아 소토 (모) · 사미르 코스타 · 에바 코스타 unchanged.

## Surface profile
- male,
- ~36–38,
- ordinary Meridian citizen,
- **선체 손상분석관** working title/function,
- not military,
- not detective,
- not system architect,
- not HUMAN:1,
- not a known memory-anomaly celebrity.

## Profession — current meaning
He does not merely repair broken machinery.
His job is to determine:
- what failed,
- in what order,
- whether the damage matches the official cause,
- whether a repaired structure is safe to return to service,
- whether old material/rework history is consistent with records.

Typical domains:
- pressure boundary,
- bulkhead/door structure,
- thermal/cooling damage,
- electrical/fire secondary damage,
- structural fatigue,
- old repairs,
- access/service spaces,
- damage chronology.

Hard limits:
- he cannot command evacuation,
- he cannot investigate citizens as police,
- he cannot access every archive,
- he is not a universal engineer,
- he often needs specialists.

## Career path
Early 20s:
ordinary inspection/maintenance route.

Late 20s:
Arun incident.
A correct/defensible emergency decision still leaves one physical/record inconsistency he cannot explain.

30s:
additional qualification and field specialization into damage/failure analysis.

Series opening:
competent, experienced, not famous.

## Core disposition
**Responsibility toward fixable danger.**

He dislikes leaving a problem whose physical cause is still unresolved.

## Core belief
`망가진 건 원인을 찾으면 고칠 수 있다.`

## False extension
`고치면 원래대로 돌아간다.`

The first is often useful.
The second is what the series breaks.

## Strengths
- physical causal reconstruction,
- spatial memory,
- patience with ugly practical detail,
- works under pressure instead of performing heroism,
- recognizes when a failure sequence is physically impossible,
- can say `모른다` about specialties he does not own.

## Flaws
- treats emotional conflict as a solvable defect,
- assumes responsibility before asking whether it is his to take,
- becomes more controlling when afraid,
- has difficulty respecting another person's chosen risk,
- can become obsessed with restoring an earlier state rather than accepting a changed one.

## Ordinary life
Leading:
- lives alone at opening,
- keeps irregular but not isolated contact with parents/sibling,
- eats outside often because shift work disrupts routine,
- owns a small number of old repaired objects he refuses to replace while they still function,
- does not collect artifacts obsessively,
- has an ordinary hobby to choose at G10/G06 so it connects to district life rather than engineering.

## Family
Leading candidate:
- both parents alive,
- neither is mission authority,
- one adult sibling with own household,
- one niece/nephew candidate,
- family scenes mostly mundane.

Hard:
no secret architect parent / inherited system access.

## Romance
H-A:
Mira was a serious former partner.
The relationship ended because affection did not erase control/autonomy conflict.

H-B:
the same serious romance did not occur in the same form.
Protagonist initially behaves with emotional familiarity H-B Mira never granted.

## Arun wound
~8–10 years before opening candidate.

Facts in H-A memory:
- compartment crisis,
- larger cascade risk,
- protagonist participates in a decision/assessment leading to closure,
- Arun remains inside,
- official death,
- decision prevents wider loss.

One pre-series anomaly only:
a small autobiographical/record mismatch tied to the incident.
No prior full regression.

## H-A → H-B arc
H-A:
ordinary professional with unresolved guilt.

Act2 end:
one exceptional continuity failover misbinding before confirmed death.

H-B:
he believes he returned to an earlier/similar Meridian.
Knowledge works temporarily.
Pre-existing relationship/history deltas prove otherwise.

Long arc:
`repair` → `prevent recurrence` → `restore people/relationships` → `memory is not unique past` → `counterpart is not replacement` → `no privileged original state` → `choose one present and accept irreversible consequence`.

## Voice direction
- concise,
- practical,
- asks sequence/cause questions,
- dry irritation rather than speeches,
- under stress sentences become shorter, not more philosophical.

Hard:
No thematic monologues explaining civilization.

# 2. Mira Solano — Logistics / Allocation

Name status: `C1 candidate`.
Age: ~35–37.

## Core disposition
**Autonomy + practical independence.**

## Work
Transport/resource scheduling and cross-district allocation.
Owns constraints the protagonist cannot solve with a wrench:
- vehicle/route capacity,
- staff shifts,
- delivery priority,
- emergency rerouting,
- stock movement.

## Ordinary goal unrelated to protagonist
Build enough credibility to redesign one chronically inefficient district distribution system without turning into a permanent manager/politician.

## Private-life direction
- has friends/colleagues outside protagonist circle,
- family contact exists but does not need tragic backstory,
- values scheduled private time and hates `emergency` becoming an excuse for permanent availability.

## Pressure logic
Stable:
assertive, reliable allocator.

Crisis:
refuses people deciding her risk for her.

Collapse:
can make brutal separation decisions to keep network functioning.

Counterfactual:
may become family partner, cold district separator, ordinary scheduler, or civic reformer without changing her autonomy core.

## Independent relationships
- Juno: formal flow vs informal community trust.
- Selene: logistics limits vs emergency priority.
- Niko: technical urgency vs resource entitlement.

## H-B protection
H-B Mira must receive more long-run narrative weight than H-A relationship nostalgia.
She is not a replacement version.

## Voice
- fast, concrete decisions,
- dislikes vague moral demands without quantities,
- not robotic; irritation often comes through logistics metaphors only if natural, not as a gimmick.

hero, autocrat, failed lenient commander, ordinary middle-level officer.

## Independent relationships
- Rafi: when rescue ends.
- Sora: operational speed vs evidentiary restraint.
- Mira: priority demand vs actual transport capacity.
- Gideon: long professional trust/caution friction.

## Voice
- short directives when commanding,
- more patient and understated off duty,
- does not speak like a soldier in every scene.

# 15. Family/private-life protection rule

Every core character does NOT need tragic family baggage.

Across core cast, distribute private situations:
- stable family,
- distant family,
- chosen family,
- single household,
- former partner,
- no children,
- caring responsibility,
- close friends,
- ordinary loneliness,
- busy social network.

Do not use the same `dead spouse/child` motivation repeatedly.

At least half of family/private scenes should remain non-crisis.

# 16. Character independence checklist

Each Core must be able to answer (standing execution check, applied per episode):
1. What do they want this year if protagonist never existed?
2. Who do they call first when protagonist is unavailable?
3. What institution or community do they belong to?
4. What relationship can improve/worsen without protagonist?
5. What mistake can they make independently?
6. What can they solve without protagonist?
7. What ordinary activity proves they are more than plot function?
8. What does H-B version believe about current life before protagonist arrives?

Current v3.1 gives a first answer for most but not all.
