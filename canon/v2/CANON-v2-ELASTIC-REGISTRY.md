# 5. Forbidden Hardening

Do NOT turn an elastic or bounded item into a new premise silently.

Examples:
- exact local district becomes a social caste boundary
- high-income housing becomes separate citizenship tier

- one companion animal becomes an ecological loophole that breaks closed-loop assumptions
- one equipment model becomes an adult origin scanner
- one staging orbit implies instant space-city independence
- one slang term becomes an ancestry detector
- one rare historical record becomes a complete truth document

# 7. Elastic Decision Test

Before inventing a detail during scene design, ask:
1. Is the governing mechanism already Canon?
2. Does this choice stay inside quantitative/social bounds?
3. Does it create a new institution, privilege or technology class?
4. Does it change a locked character/reveal/future constraint?
5. Would the same choice force dozens of later scenes to change?

If 1=yes and 2=yes and 3–5=no:
`ELASTIC — choose locally`.

Otherwise:
run a narrow Canon change/micropass.
