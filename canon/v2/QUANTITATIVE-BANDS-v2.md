# Q1 — Ship / Habitat / Transit — BOUNDED

## Rotating Habitats
- effective inhabited radius: **~0.9–1.1 km**
- preferred center: ~1.0 km
- axial inhabited length: **~5–7 km**
- preferred center: ~6 km
- rotation: **~0.90–1.00 rpm class**
- preferred center: ~0.95 rpm
- main urban apparent gravity: **~0.95–1.0g class**

At ~1 km radius / ~1g:
- rotation period ~63 s class.

## Radial gravity
At fixed spin, apparent gravity decreases inward.
Major ordinary residential/commercial urban volume should therefore remain relatively close to the main floor radius rather than relying on hundreds-of-meters radial skyscrapers.

## Gross urban surface
Three major cylindrical inner surfaces combined:
**~94–132 km² gross inner-surface class**, center ~113 km².

At ~300k citizens:
gross density order ~2,300–3,200 persons/km² before multi-level floor area.

## Households
- ~120k–130k households class
- average household occupancy order ~2.3–2.5.

## Transfer nodes
Per Habitat:
- major radial/Spine passenger hubs: **~4–8 order**, center ~6
- major heavy-freight/hazard interfaces: **~2–4 order**
- many more local trunk/local stops.

## Travel-time guidance
- neighborhood/local: ~5–20 min
- same-Habitat normal trip: ~15–45 min
- far same-Habitat: ~30–60 min class
- ordinary cross-Habitat door-to-door: **~60–120 min**.

## Engineering isolation
Per Habitat major atmospheric/pressure isolation sectors:
**~30–60 order**, highly nonuniform.

Distributed short-duration refuge/relocation headroom:
**~5–10% of Habitat population class**.

# Q2 — Water / Air / Power / Thermal / Reserve — BOUNDED

## Ordinary delivered civil water
**~80–120 L/person/day equivalent**.

Shipwide ~300k:
**~24,000–36,000 m³/day delivered civil-flow order**.

This is circulated delivery, not net loss.

## Civil-water recovery
**~98.5–99.5% class** for ordinary recoverable streams, with lower/variable recovery for contaminated/process-specific streams.

## Distributed processed-water service buffer
**~24–72 hours ordinary-service-equivalent** distributed across local/Habitat systems.

Emergency restricted water support:
**several days to ~1 week class** for human-critical needs while nonessential uses are curtailed.

## Human atmosphere metabolism
Shipwide human metabolism produces/uses few-hundred-tonnes/day class of O2/CO2 flow.
Atmospheric inventory is large enough that one processor failure is not instant oxygen disappearance.

## Civil electrical power
Ordinary shipwide civil average:
**~6–10 GW class**, within the v1 multi-GW to ~10GW order.

Largest single generation unit should not normally exceed roughly **~20–25% of ordinary demand**.

## Backup layers
- immediate critical ride-through: **~15–60 min class**
- district essential storage/microgrid bridge: **~2–6 h class**
- longer outages rely on alternate generation/distribution, shedding and repair, not giant batteries.

## Thermal rejection
Effective civil radiator/rejection infrastructure:
**~15–40 km² class**.

Ordinary operational/contingency thermal headroom:
**~20–40% class**.

Heat rejection remains a real arrival/maintenance bottleneck.

## Direct critical physical-infrastructure workforce
**~15k–25k people class** across utilities, maintenance, Outer Works, critical fabrication and related roles.

Strategic spares use criticality classes rather than one universal percentage.

# Q3 — Settlement Planet — BOUNDED

Real-system star anchor:
**Epsilon Indi A**.

Settlement world is a fictional future mission-era discovery.

## Orbit / year
- semimajor axis: **~0.50–0.54 AU**
- center ~0.52 AU
- year: **~150–165 Earth days**
- center ~158 d
- incident flux working center ~0.89 Earth flux class.

## Planet body
- mass: **~0.95–1.08 M⊕**
- radius: **~0.98–1.05 R⊕**
- surface gravity: **~0.93–1.02g**
- preferred center ~0.97g.

## Rotation / seasons
- day: **~28–31 h**, center ~29.5h
- obliquity: **~10–18°**, center ~14°.

## Atmosphere
At first settlement region:
- total pressure: **~0.88–0.98 bar**, center ~0.93 bar
- O2 volume fraction: **~8–10% class**
- ambient O2 partial pressure: **~7–9.5 kPa class**.

Operational consequence:
- routine outdoor breathing support mandatory,
- pressure suit normally unnecessary,
- brief accidental unassisted exposure can be survivable but rapidly impairs useful performance, especially under exertion/vulnerability.

Bulk atmosphere direction:
- nitrogen-dominant,
- inert gases possible,
- sub-percent routine CO2 below acute toxicity concern,
- exact trace chemistry ELASTIC.

## Climate
- cool-temperate
- water-rich
- broad global-mean class ~5–12°C working order
- real weather/seasons
- no Earth-clone paradise.

## Radiation / UV
Not a full radiation-suit world.
Outdoor UV/radiation practice is somewhat more cautious than typical Earth surface life; exact magnetic/ozone details remain bounded/elastic.

## Biology scope
Uncertain organics/microbial-scale biosignature possible.
No confirmed complex/intelligent alien civilization as series conflict.

# Numerical Hard Stops

Do not use a number that implies:
- Habitat radius tens of meters with ~1 rpm Earth gravity,
- 300k people in a tiny station-scale volume,
- one utility plant failure kills entire civilization instantly,
- a few-hour battery powers all Meridian for days,
- 10 GW heat disappears without massive rejection infrastructure,
- destination atmosphere is both 8–10% O2 and casually breathable all day,
- thousands/tens-of-thousands already live on surface by EP230,
- new orbital habitat is a mature city by first year,
- D0 ordinary telemetry survives centuries by default,
- Earth gives real-time orders near destination.
