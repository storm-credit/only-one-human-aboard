# 1. Core Premise — LOCKED

- Interstellar generation ship: **Meridian / 메리디언**.
- Current population: **roughly 300,000**.
- Citizens are genuinely born, grow, learn, form families, work, become ill, age and can die.
- Most citizens possess living human-form biological bodies and living biological brains.
- Consciousness is not centrally rendered by a ship computer.
- The old decisive distinction is **cognitive developmental origin**, not body material.
- At the major Reveal, current active Natural/Bio-Origin Cognition citizens = **exactly 1**.
- That citizen is **Amara Okoro / 아마라 오코로**.
- The overwhelming remainder are Seeded/Synthetic-Origin Cognition citizens.
- Modern civic society recognizes both as persons/citizens/humans in ordinary social/legal usage.

Interpretive lock:
**The title's `one human` is a shocking legacy-classification result, not the narrator's final moral ontology.**

FORBIDDEN:
- 299,999 people as fake NPCs
- objective narrator claim that only Amara is a real person
- human-good / AI-evil binary
- chosen authority based on Natural-Origin status.

# 2. Biological Body / Cognitive Origin — LOCKED

Most citizens have:
- human DNA/cells/organs,
- real metabolism,
- sleep/hunger,
- ordinary injury/disease,
- biological aging,
- living neural tissue.

### Natural-Origin Cognition
High-order cognitive-network formation proceeds without verified successful Seed completion through the relevant prenatal critical window.

### Seeded/Synthetic-Origin Cognition
A verified artificial developmental prior influences early high-order cognitive-network formation during the prenatal critical window.

The category is developmental provenance.
It is not:
- robot versus human,
- organic versus machine,
- soul versus copy.

# 3. Developmental Connectomic Seed — LOCKED

Common term:
**시드**.

Technical term:
**발달 커넥톰 시드**.

The Seed is a computationally generated developmental prior/attractor applied during an early prenatal critical window.

It may influence the initial trajectory of high-order network organization.

It does NOT:
- upload memories,
- install finished personality,
- choose profession,
- assign loyalty,
- insert commands,
- create hidden administrator access,
- remote-control the citizen,
- create prophecy or special cognition.

After prenatal completion, the child develops through ordinary pregnancy, birth, childhood, education, relationships and lived experience.

---

# 4. Seed Success Verification — LOCKED

Classification requires **verified successful prenatal completion**, not a prescription/order/attempt.

Prenatal closed-loop monitoring verifies treatment response during the critical window.

Possible outcomes:
1. verified successful completion,
2. incomplete attempt followed by repeat/adjustment in-window,
3. incomplete/unverified course,
4. deliberate full-window refusal/noncompletion.

The treatment-response signature is transient.
Decades of plasticity, learning, injury and aging erase any simple adult marker.

Therefore:
- prenatal provenance can establish origin,
- ordinary adult imaging/medicine cannot reliably determine origin.

FORBIDDEN:
**adult direct origin scanner**.

# 6. Historical Population Transition — LOCKED

Departure passengers were Natural-Origin humans.

They were not:
- massacred,
- secretly replaced,
- mass-uploaded,
- converted into a machine population.

Historical causal chain:
1. long-voyage medicine/Continuity research develops Seed technology,
2. families adopt it for probabilistic medical/developmental benefits and care-path predictability,
3. Seeded children are biologically born and live normal developmental lives,
4. legal conflicts establish equal civic personhood,
5. provider expertise/network effects make Seed standard prenatal care,
6. original Natural-Origin generations age and die naturally,
7. rare unseeded births continue legally,
8. current active count happens to equal one.

FORBIDDEN:
- exact-one Witness/Origin preservation protocol,
- hereditary last-human lineage,
- one catastrophe replacing the population,
- `one forever` demographic law.

Future births may change the count.
