No major character exists only to explain the premise.

- household/economic life,

# 2. Maren Vale / 마렌 베일 — LOCKED

- female
- 41 at start → ~55 near ending
- ordinary Seeded-Origin citizen
- Ella's older sister
- Noah's aunt and long-term temporary guardian during Ella recovery
- **연속성 심사관** at **연속성심사국**

## Authority boundary
May make temporary/reviewable Continuity-right decisions in ordinary jurisdiction.
May not:
- issue final criminal judgment,
- revoke citizenship permanently,
- force Reconstruction,
- rewrite records freely,
- decide settlement policy,
- define civilization-wide human status.

## Mandatory family recusal
Automatically recused from formal decisions directly involving:
- Ella Continuity status,
- Ella parental reinstatement,
- Noah guardianship/residence,
- Maren's own guardianship conduct,
- formal effect of PR-H1.

In family conflict she is a relative/guardian/witness/record holder/possible ethics subject, not judge.

## Core flaw / voice
Protects by deciding for others.
Voice:
`clarify → narrow → decide`.
Anger becomes quieter/more exact.

## PR-H1 role
Maren lawfully accessed Ella's emergency planning folder after the accident and failed to surface a serious unfiled conditional Reconstruction-preference draft/context.
The draft was nonbinding; disclosure could have caused stricter review/delay, not automatic denial.

## Stable ordinary-life anchors
- 1환 mixed residential life with Noah at series start
- comfortable senior-professional middle/upper-middle security, not elite wealth
- lap swimming/structured pool exercise when schedule allows
- audio/music/long-form history/documentary interests
- conservative personal-agent permissions
- practical, repeat-wear understated clothing
- no special scar/cybernetic/origin marker.

## Arc
control/protection
→ professional/family costs
→ hypocrisy exposed
→ accepts recusal/others' agency
→ remains useful through narrow judgment without becoming sovereign.

FORBIDDEN:
- hidden Natural-Origin twist
- master detective who owns every discovery
- final constitutional savior
- formal adjudication of own family.
