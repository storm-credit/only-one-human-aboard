# 1. Infrastructure Philosophy — LOCKED

Meridian is simultaneously:
- a spacecraft,
- a city,
- a closed-loop industrial civilization,
- a ~450-year historical built environment.

Citizens should experience a mature civilian city.
Operators must understand a pressure/heat/maintenance-constrained machine.

The setting is neither:
- sterile station corridors,
- nor a magical Earth city with no engineering cost.

---

# 2. Urban Architecture — URB-H1 LOCKED

Selected model:
**Compartmented Mixed-Use City + Layered Retrofit + Local Public Realms**.

## Hard layer
Changes slowly:
- pressure boundaries,
- structural frames,
- utility trunks,
- fire/smoke zones,
- transit/service corridors,
- emergency/refuge infrastructure.

## Soft layer
Changes repeatedly across centuries:
- homes,
- schools,
- shops,
- clinics,
- restaurants/cafés,
- offices,
- workshops,
- public squares/courtyards,
- parks/green space,
- cultural/religious/community space.

Old does not mean abandoned.
New does not mean structurally independent of the old engineering frame.

# 3. Housing / Resilience — LOCKED MODEL

Housing stock includes:
- normal private/tenure units,
- rentals/cooperative housing,
- temporary/recovery/protected housing,
- some turnover/vacancy buffer.

100% physical occupancy would be operationally brittle and is not the model.

Housing varies by:
- size,
- room count,
- privacy/acoustics,
- light/view,
- location/commute,
- heritage value,
- interior customization.

Dwelling rights never privatize:
- pressure hull,
- life-support trunk,
- emergency corridor,
- critical structural commons.

Exact property law is owned by `SOCIETY-v2.md`.

# 5. Transit — TRANS-H1 LOCKED

Movement hierarchy:
1. walking / mobility aids / small personal mobility,
2. local district transit,
3. Habitat trunk transit,
4. controlled radial gravity transition,
5. Spine inter-Habitat express,
6. destination Habitat local leg.

Cross-Habitat travel is multi-leg.
The ~60–120 minute ordinary range is door-to-door, not pure Spine vehicle time.

Most citizens do not cross Habitats every day.
Large private-car-dominated urbanism is not required.

Exact node counts/travel bands:
`QUANTITATIVE-BANDS-v2.md`.

# 6. Freight / Logistics — LOCKED

Freight is partially separated from passenger flow.

Major streams:
- commercial delivery,
- food,
- medical material,
- waste/recycling,
- industrial components,
- hazardous material,
- strategic arrival cargo.

Bulk/hazardous flows use controlled corridors/times/interfaces where practical.

Automated routing/handling is common; human logistics work remains important for:
- exceptions,
- hazards,
- cross-domain coordination,
- degraded operation,
- arrival novelty.

Kai's career fits this ecology without making him a chosen controller.

Ordinary citizens do not step directly from ~1g street to free zero-g.

Cross-Habitat travel uses controlled radial transition toward lower gravity and engineered interfaces to the non-rotating Spine.

Low-g travel may involve:
- orientation changes,
- restraints/handholds,
- accessibility support,
- occasional motion discomfort.

# 8. Air — UTIL-H1 LOCKED

Atmospheric management is hierarchical:
- strategic gas inventory / bulk processing,
- Habitat/district circulation,
- building local conditioning,
- room comfort controls.

Sensors monitor:
- pressure,
- O2/CO2,
- humidity,
- contaminants.

Citizens control comfort parameters, not shipwide atmospheric chemistry.

No single air processor is allowed to be the civilization's one failure point.

# 9. Water / Wastewater — LOCKED + BOUNDED

Water uses differentiated streams with very high recovery.

Normal citizens can:
- shower,
- cook,
- clean,
- do laundry.

Efficiency exists mainly in infrastructure rather than permanent household ration misery.

Wastewater becomes:
- recovered water,
- nutrients/feedstocks,
- controlled residues.

Closed-loop recovery is excellent but never perfect.

# 10. Power — LOCKED + BOUNDED

Civil fusion power supports the mature civilization at multi-GW scale.

Grid hierarchy:
- strategic generation/distribution,
- Habitat/district network,
- building/local feeds,
- emergency critical-load backup.

Critical life/safety loads receive priority over discretionary loads.

Local storage bridges interruption/shutdown.
It does not power whole Habitats indefinitely.

Exact average/backup bands:
`QUANTITATIVE-BANDS-v2.md`.

# 11. Heat — LOCKED + BOUNDED

All civil power ultimately becomes heat.

Thermal chain:
- room/device/building,
- local/district loops,
- useful heat recovery where practical,
- Habitat trunks,
- large exterior rejection/radiator systems.

Thermal margin constrains:
- industry,
- maintenance,
- high-load medical/technical services,
- arrival deployment.

Arrival pressure is not a simplistic household-electricity versus propulsion competition.

Radiator/headroom scale:
`QUANTITATIVE-BANDS-v2.md`.

# 12. Solid Waste / Material Cycling — LOCKED

Preferred hierarchy:
**repair → reuse → component recovery → material recycling → feedstock conversion → secure residue**.

Hazard/material streams are separated.

The civilization still needs:
- strategic stock,
- contamination management,
- make-up material,
- eventually local-system ISRU.

FORBIDDEN:
- 100% lossless recycling,
- universal matter replicator.

# 13. Normal Utility Failure — LOCKED

Most failures are local and legible:
- hot-water outage,
- one building/district on backup,
- reduced ventilation,
- branch water restriction,
- waste delay,
- transit closure,
- network degradation.

District isolation means every fault is **not** a shipwide survival crisis.

Repeated local failures can still create:
- cost,
- political anger,
- labor pressure,
- maintenance backlog.

---

# 14. Emergency Architecture — EMG-H1 LOCKED

Emergency scope ladder:
- routine/site fault,
- local emergency,
- district/Habitat significant emergency,
- shipwide strategic emergency.

Authority grows only when real physical scope requires it.

Response integrates:
- automatic physical safeties,
- local trained response,
- medical/public safety,
- technical incident command,
- civic/legal review for prolonged rights restrictions.

Automatic safety can act quickly.
It cannot declare legal guilt or permanent civic consequence.

---

# 15. Fire — LOCKED

Fire response addresses smoke/toxic gases as much as flame.

Typical actions:
- detection,
- local suppression,
- power/process isolation,
- ventilation/smoke control,
- fire barriers,
- evacuation/refuge,
- cleanup/air verification.

Indoor oxygen-rich environments remain normal fire hazards even though the destination surface later has low ambient O2.

---

# 16. Decompression — LOCKED

Localized pressure loss is dangerous but normally bounded.

Response:
- rapid detection,
- automatic closure/isolation,
- movement behind safe boundaries,
- breathing support where necessary,
- person accounting,
- leak localization/repair,
- controlled repressurization/reopening.

No routine whole-Habitat evacuation assumption.

Pressure-sector/refuge scale:
`QUANTITATIVE-BANDS-v2.md`.

# 17. Contamination / Health Isolation — LOCKED

Chemical, smoke, biological, water, radiological and industrial contamination use different protocols.

There is no generic `quarantine everyone` switch.

Health isolation must be:
- medically justified,
- scoped,
- reviewable,
- separated from criminal guilt where appropriate.

# 18. Emergency Culture — LOCKED

Citizens know simple age-appropriate emergency actions and drills.

Specialist response remains professional.

Ordinary home emergency kits are short-duration civic kits, not personal EVA systems.

Meridian uses federated classes:
- control automation,
- predictive/diagnostic models,
- administrative/search agents,
- personal assistants,
- robots/drones.

# 20. AI Authority Boundary — LOCKED

AI may:
- optimize,
- recommend,
- summarize,
- search within permission,
- diagnose/forecast with uncertainty,
- operate tools/robots,
- execute predefined physical safeties.

AI may not be final autonomous authority for:
- citizenship/personhood,
- criminal guilt/sentencing,
- indefinite detention,
- parental-right termination,
- political value allocation,
- Amara/settler legitimacy.

No universal citizen score.

# 22. Network — NET-H1 LOCKED

Meridian uses:
- redundant civic backbone,
- segmented protected/critical domains,
- permissioned gateways,
- local fallback/partition modes.

A network outage does not automatically stop all life support.
One credential does not open all systems.

Private messaging/social/media are ordinary.

Civic identity is durable; authentication is contextual and layered.

Work/medical/legal/security access is role-scoped.

# 28. Spares / Fabrication — LOCKED

Meridian fabricates many parts locally but cannot instantly reproduce everything.

Constraints include:
- precision,
- specialized material,
- yield,
- calibration/metrology,
- rare equipment,
- specialist labor,
- time.

Strategic spare planning uses criticality/lead-time classes, not one universal spare percentage.

Act5 can therefore treat a physically small precision machine as strategically more important than large scrap mass.

# 31. Companion Animals — BIO-PET-H1 LOCKED DIRECTION

Selective companion-animal populations exist alongside genetic conservation.

Plausible managed groups include selected:
- cats,
- small/medium dogs,
- small mammals,
- aquatic/terrarium species,
- carefully controlled birds/invertebrates.

Large live livestock is not ordinary food infrastructure.

Animal systems require:
- veterinary care,
- managed breeding/genetic diversity,
- welfare law,
- waste/feed integration,
- allergy/zoonosis management.

Arrival biosecurity prevents casual release of Earth-origin species into uncertain local ecology.

Exact species population/benefit formula remains ELASTIC unless plot-relevant.
