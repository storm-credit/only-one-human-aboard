# 1. Historical Ground Truth — LOCKED

1. Departure passengers were Natural-Origin biological humans.
2. They were not massacred, secretly replaced or mass-uploaded.
3. Seed arose from long-voyage neurodevelopment/Continuity medicine.
4. Seeded children were biologically conceived/born and developed through lived childhood.
5. Seed never installed memory/personality/loyalty/commands.
6. Seed adoption spread through medical benefit, evidence, provider expertise and path dependence.
7. Seeded-origin citizens secured equal civic recognition through rights reform.
8. Natural-Origin births remained legal.
9. No exact-one preservation program ever existed.
10. Original Natural-Origin generations aged, parented and died normally over many generations.
11. Current Count=1 is a demographic state at story time.
12. Reconstruction always depended on surviving biological continuity; no anchorless adult-copy society emerged.
13. Meridian's political autonomy arose through lived civilian legitimacy and communication latency, not a mutiny or confirmed Earth apocalypse.

# 3. Era 0 — Departure / Founding — BOUNDED ~VY0–20

Population:
- all departure passengers Natural-Origin.

Government:
- mission technical command has broad practical weight,
- civilian representation already exists,
- no hereditary captain/state dynasty.

Economy:
- provisioning/allocation-heavy,
- critical food/housing/medical/labor strongly scheduled,
- personal property/exchange secondary but present.

Urban form:
- three rotating Habitats + Spine are relatively new mission infrastructure,
- earliest 1환 civic layers later become the roots of 구시가지.

Medicine:
- advanced trauma/prenatal care,
- early longitudinal neural mapping,
- no mature Reconstruction doctrine.

Culture:
- Earth-born adults dominate memory/cultural production,
- multiple Earth languages/religions remain firsthand.

Earth contact:
comparatively immediate and institutionally significant.

# 4. Era 1 — Early Voyage Consolidation — ~VY20–80

Core shift:
Meridian becomes a permanent lived city rather than merely transport.

Changes:
- first no-Earth-memory generations become adults,
- local councils/civil administration expand,
- mission-duty assumptions weaken for descendants,
- market/co-op life above basic provisioning expands,
- schools/shops/cultural/public spaces multiply,
- interiors are subdivided/combined/repurposed,
- early Continuity mapping/repair research accelerates,
- ship-born slang/food/ritual appears.

Earth still matters, but local decisions increasingly cannot wait for external consultation.

---

# 5. Era 2 — Continuity Medicine / First Seed Cohorts — ~VY60–140

Reconstruction development:
- longitudinal mapping improves,
- biological/neural repair advances,
- stored information is understood as repair reference rather than substitute person,
- surviving neural substrate becomes explicit in continuity doctrine.

Seed research:
- developmental-prior medicine explores safer/more predictable prenatal network formation without prescribing personality.

First Seed cohorts:
roughly VY80–120 working range.

They are:
- biologically conceived/born,
- raised through ordinary childhood,
- memory/personality formed through life.

Adoption drivers:
- probabilistic risk reduction,
- clinical predictability,
- compatibility with then-maturing recovery medicine.

Seed begins as medicine, not a population-replacement program.

---

# 6. Era 3 — Seed Normalization / Rights Reform — ~VY120–230

Seed evidence/provider expertise expands.
Full-window refusal remains legal but becomes rarer.

Reconstruction doctrine matures toward:
- Recovery Map as repair/reference constraint,
- surviving Neural Anchor requirement,
- substantial rehabilitation.

Rights conflicts arise because old law was not designed for Seeded developmental origin.

Recurring disputes include:
- care/insurance,
- family status,
- inheritance,
- continuity recognition,
- professional eligibility,
- citizenship wording.

Legal direction stabilizes:
**developmental origin alone cannot diminish civic personhood/citizenship.**

Natural/Synthetic-origin technical terms become increasingly unsuitable as ordinary public identity labels.

# 7. Era 4 — Civic Maturation / Urban Layering — ~VY220–320

Modern governance becomes recognizable:
- elected shipwide civic authority,
- local councils,
- professional civil service,
- independent adjudication/appeal,
- narrowed technical safety command,
- commons certification/oversight.

Economy matures into:
**Layered Commons + Market City**.

Labor:
- inherited mission duty is obsolete,
- skill needs handled through pay/training/credential/incentive except narrow emergencies.

Property:
- durable residential/business interests coexist with common survival structure.

Urban life:
- 구시가지 becomes heritage/social category,
- repeated retrofit accumulates local history.

Seeded births become overwhelming majority.
Meridian-created culture becomes old enough to have its own heritage canon.

# 9. Era 6 — Mature Meridian / Earth As Ancestral Relation — ~VY370–420

For essentially everyone alive:
Meridian is the only lived homeland.

Earth becomes:
- ancestry,
- archives,
- religion/cultural memory,
- delayed correspondence,
not a remembered childhood place.

Communication can persist but is:
- high-latency,
- asynchronous,
- irregular,
- non-governing.

Near destination, one-way communication is decade-scale order.
A normal policy question/response cycle therefore spans decades.

Long gaps do NOT prove Earth destruction.

Culture:
- Ship Common remains widely intelligible,
- heritage languages vary from living to ceremonial/scholarly,
- inherited religions + secular life + ship-born movements coexist,
- Earth cultural arrivals enter an already mature Meridian canon.

Natural-Origin births are extremely rare but legal.
There is no expectation one must always exist.

- reserve audits,
- fabrication expansion,
- orbital/surface preparation,

Culture:

- current civic registry does not use developmental origin as normal public identity,

- deep archives retain old schema mappings,
- a lawful cross-system audit can eventually compute Count=1.

# 15. Representative Historical Anchors — BOUNDED / LOCKED FUNCTION

These are author-facing anchors; exact public names may remain Elastic.

## A1 — Second-Ring Water Isolation — ~VY40–55
Multi-day 2환 water-branch contamination/restriction event.

Legacy:
- distributed water buffers,
- branch isolation,
- incident transparency,
- clearer line between immediate technical action and prolonged civic restriction.

No near-extinction event.

## A2 — Anchor Doctrine Cases — ~VY95–115
Series of severe neural-injury/continuity cases.

Legacy:
- Recovery Map ≠ person,
- surviving Neural Anchor required,
- staged rehab and multi-domain review.

No scientific soul detector.

## A3 — Origin Equality Decisions — ~VY165–185
Rights/legislative cases rejecting developmental origin as civic-rank basis.

Legacy:
- equal civic personhood,
- stronger separation between protected provenance and public civic profile,
- origin terminology deprecation.

## A4 — Commons–Tenure Settlement — ~VY245–270
Long property/housing/maintenance conflict separates:
- structural commons,
- transferable residential interests,
- private interiors,
- public acquisition/compensation duties.

## A5 — Three-Habitat Common-Mode Mesh Incident — ~VY325–345
Software/model-stack common-mode disruption affects several civic/operational services while critical lower-level safety remains functional.

Legacy:
- segmentation,
- rollback,
- local fallback,
- version/model certification,
- permissioned data gateways.

No AI rebellion.

## A6 — Long-Reply Settlement — ~VY385–405
A significant Earth exchange demonstrates that external guidance can arrive after Meridian circumstances/law have changed.

Legacy:
Earth remains culturally/historically important but not real-time executive government.

No Earth-apocalypse requirement.

## A7 — Early Arrival Reserve Hearings — ~VY410–425
Debates over apparently underused assets reveal hidden value in redundancy/repair/thermal/precision chains.

Legacy:
- asset criticality,
- redundancy impact,
- transfer/replacement review,
- social displacement review,
- ancestry of Final Approach certification logic.
