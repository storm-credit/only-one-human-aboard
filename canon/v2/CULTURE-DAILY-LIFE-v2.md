# 1. Cultural Thesis — LOCKED

Meridian is a roughly 450-year-old civilian city descended from Earth.

It is not:
- a permanent mission barracks,
- a museum frozen at launch culture,
- an alien posthuman civilization detached from human history.

Its people inherited Earth institutions/cultures, changed them, and created centuries of ship-born traditions.

Meridian is home for essentially everyone alive.
Arrival is a future choice, not the discovery of the only authentic home.

---

# 2. Language — LNG-H1 LOCKED

One evolved **Ship Common** is near-universal for daily/civic communication.

It descends strongly from launch-era international English but is not identical to modern English after ~450 years.

Language drift is moderated by:
- universal literacy,
- schools,
- archived media,
- legal/technical standards,
- a connected ~300k population,
- cross-Habitat mobility.

Heritage languages survive unevenly through:
- family,
- religion,
- art,
- scholarship,
- community identity.

Translation assistance is ordinary but does not erase linguistic identity.

Reader convention:
**the Korean novel renders ordinary dialogue in natural Korean, not pseudo-translated English syntax.**

Names do not prove ancestry, language, religion, Habitat or class.

---

# 3. Civil Time — CAL-H1 LOCKED

Meridian retains an Earth-descended synchronized civic time system:
- 24-hour civil day,
- 7-day week,
- ~365-day civil year,
- inherited month/date convention.

A departure-relative **Voyage Year / VY** exists for:
- archives,
- mission/engineering history,
- scholarship,
- commemoration.

No internal Habitat time zones.

Artificial lighting creates ordinary:
- day,
- evening,
- night,
- sleep/wake rhythms.

The ship operates continuously; weekends/rest cycles remain socially real.
Shift workers produce offset-night/early-morning subcultures.

---

# 4. Holidays / Observances — LOCKED MODEL / ELASTIC NAMES

Meridian has civic observances serving functions such as:
- departure/founding remembrance,
- major disaster/institutional remembrance,
- civic/rights milestones,
- district festivals,
- later arrival anniversaries.

Religious communities also maintain inherited/adapted calendars.

Exact names/dates remain ELASTIC unless elevated through History/story use.

Shared civic ritual does not require shared metaphysics.

# 5. Religion / Nonbelief — REL-H1 LOCKED

Meridian contains:
- evolved descendants of Earth religions,
- secular/nonreligious citizens,
- philosophical/ethical communities,
- small ship-born spiritual movements,
- mixed/private practices.

No state religion.
No mandatory secular ideology.

Different communities may interpret Seed/Reconstruction differently, but civil law controls rights/personhood.

FORBIDDEN:
- one major religion automatically becoming a Natural-Origin movement,
- retroactively making Amara's parents cult members,
- religion receiving civic power to declare Seeded citizens nonpersons,
- Reconstruction serving as universal scientific proof of afterlife/soul.

# 6. Food Production / Cuisine — FOOD-H1 LOCKED

Food derives from mature closed-loop agriculture/biotech including:
- conventional crops/greens/legumes,
- fungi,
- microbial/algal ingredients,
- precision fermentation,
- cultured meat,
- limited richer ecology.

After centuries, these are normal ingredients—not novelty substitutes.

Cuisine combines:
- Earth recipe lineages,
- substitutions/adaptations,
- ship-born dishes,
- family traditions,
- restaurants/cafés/co-ops,
- district specialties.

Authenticity is culturally debated, not biologically fixed.

Basic nutrition belongs to the Civic Floor.
Status/market difference appears through:
- premium ingredients,
- artisanal preparation,
- heritage crops/products,
- dining space/service,
- frequency/convenience.

Ella's food/hospitality cooperative fits ordinary society rather than being plot-specific infrastructure.

# 7. Clothing / Appearance — CLO-H1 LOCKED

Ordinary civilians wear recognizable varied clothing categories.
They are not permanently dressed in standardized ship jumpsuits.

Textiles are largely locally regenerated/recycled/bioproduced.
Fashion changes through:
- youth culture,
- media,
- material innovation,
- historical revivals,
- arrival-era influence.

Repair/resale/alteration are normal but not morally compulsory.

Special workwear exists for:
- medicine,
- utilities,
- public safety,
- manufacturing,
- food service,
- transport,
- Outer Works.

EVA suits are specialist gear, not streetwear.

Hair/cosmetics/tattoos/piercings/moderate body modification vary culturally within safety constraints.
Prosthetics/assistive devices may be visibly customized.

No Habitat dress caste.
No universal aesthetic of a perfect repaired body.

# 8. Domestic Life / Hygiene — DOM-H1 LOCKED

Mature closed-loop infrastructure supports ordinary urban comfort:
- showers,
- toilets,
- laundry,
- dishwashing,
- cooking,
- cleaning,
- climate/light control.

Sustainability is embedded mainly in:
- recovery systems,
- efficient fixtures,
- heat reuse,
- material cycles,
not constant deprivation rituals.

Homes are genuinely private.
Safety sensors monitor system conditions rather than continuous intimate behavior.

Higher status may buy:
- more space,
- better views/location,
- stronger acoustic/privacy quality,
- storage,
- custom interiors,
- better appliances/services,
- schedule convenience.

It cannot buy a different class of breathable air/personhood.

# 9. Media / Journalism / Social Networks — MEDIA-H1 LOCKED

Media ecology is plural:
- public-service information,
- private/co-op/nonprofit journalism,
- local media,
- creators,
- social feeds/groups,
- private messaging,
- work/school channels.

No single feed defines reality.
No perfect fact-check AI exists.

Civic identity and social identity are not identical.
People may use:
- verified public identity,
- ordinary named profile,
- lawful pseudonymous identity
depending on context.

Protected civic/medical/Seed data does not automatically populate social profiles.

# 12. Sport — LOCKED MODEL / ELASTIC SPECIFICS

Earthlike-gravity sports remain ordinary in rotating Habitats.

Low/zero-g facilities support ship-born sports.

A recurring zero-g sport may use the existing design direction:
- enclosed padded 3D court,
- wall/rail push-off movement,
- no personal thrusters in standard play,
- soft rebound ball,
- defended scoring zones,
- trajectory/timing/team-positioning emphasis.

Exact name, court dimensions and velocity rules remain ELASTIC/conditional micropass if repeated detailed action requires them.

Noah's zero-g sport interest is ordinary youth life, not thematic symbolism.

# 13. Intimacy / Relationships — INT-H1 LOCKED

Adult society supports:
- dating,
- partnership/marriage,
- cohabitation,
- single life,
- separation/divorce,
- varied consensual relationship forms.

Civil equality does not depend on:
- gender,
- orientation,
- developmental origin.

Consent/privacy are strong norms without implying conflict-free society.

Reliable contraception and assisted reproduction decouple intimacy from actual birth throughput.

Population capacity is managed through reproductive/birth systems, not by licensing romance/sex.

Genetic screening cannot become state mate assignment/eugenic caste ranking.

# 14. Meridian Identity — ID-HOME-H1 LOCKED

For most citizens Meridian is the only lived homeland:
- birthplace,
- neighborhood,
- school/work world,
- political community,
- memorial landscape,
- culture.

The ship is not merely a vehicle awaiting `real life` on a planet.

Earth is:
- ancestry,
- archive,
- family/religious/cultural memory,
- high-latency relationship,
not firsthand childhood memory for anyone alive under current chronology.

Citizens may feel:
- nostalgia,
- curiosity,
- indifference,
- critique,
- reverence,
without one mandatory cultural attitude.

Founders are historically important but not genetic aristocracy.
Mission history is constitutional inheritance, not compulsory religion.

# 15. Habitat / District Identity — LOCKED

Local stereotypes, pride and jokes exist.

However:
- Habitats remain class-mixed,
- families/work cross boundaries,
- residents move,
- no Habitat becomes an ethnic/political caste.

District identity may be stronger than macro-Habitat identity.

# 16. Arrival Culture — LOCKED

Citizens may prefer:
- staying aboard Meridian,
- orbital/resource work,
- surface settlement,
- future new habitat
for mixed personal reasons.

No path is inherently more human/authentic.

A real planetary horizon/sky can be emotionally overwhelming without proving ship life was fake.

Arrival introduces new:
- fashion,
- food possibilities,
- work identities,
- local rituals,
- family geography,
but not an instant unified frontier culture.
