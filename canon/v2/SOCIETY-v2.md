# 1. Social Constitution — LOCKED

Meridian is a democratic civic city-civilization constrained by physical commons rather than ruled by them.

Citizens:
- have equal civic personhood regardless of developmental origin,
- receive a guaranteed survival/basic-service floor,
- use money/markets for ordinary differentiated life,
- may own personal/business/residential interests but not sovereign life-support structure,
- choose careers rather than receive lifetime assignment,
- can form diverse families/households,
- receive public education and healthcare,
- are subject to real criminal/public-safety powers with due process,
- retain privacy against automatic engineering/medical omniscience,
- age, become disabled, retire and die.

Meridian is neither frictionless utopia nor totalitarian scarcity state.
Institutions may be delayed, biased, captured, expensive or wrong; they remain reviewable.

# 2. Governance Architecture — GOV-H1 + GOV-Q8-H1 LOCKED

Selected model:
**Layered Civic Republic + Commons Constraints + Narrow Safety Command**,
implemented through a
**Mixed-Member Civic Assembly + Assembly-Selected Collective Executive + Independent Multi-Level Courts**.

## Civic value authority
Elected civic institutions decide:
- law,
- taxation/budgets,
- distributive priorities,
- rights,
- ordinary public policy,
- long-term strategic choices.

## Representation
Architecture includes:
- elected shipwide legislature / **선내의회**,
- elected local Habitat/district civic councils,
- professional civil service,
- collective civic executive,
- independent adjudication/oversight.

## Technical constraint bodies
Professional technical institutions may certify:
- minimum safe maintenance,
- environmental/engineering limits,
- immediate hazard state,
- required redundancy margins.

They do not become general sovereigns.

## Ship Operations Command
Navigation/propulsion/critical technical operations use professional command hierarchy.
It is subordinate to civic law outside narrow objective emergency powers.

No captain is head of state.

# 3. Franchise / Elections — GOV-Q8-H1 LOCKED + BOUNDED

## Franchise
- ordinary voting age: **18**,
- one person, equal civic vote,
- developmental origin, Reconstruction history, profession, Habitat and property do not change vote weight,
- early off-Meridian citizens retain shared civic franchise under transition law.

No:
- Natural-Origin reserved seat,
- technical-profession weighted vote,
- Habitat sovereign bloc vote.

## General election cycle
Shipwide general election:
**every 4 civil years**.

Local councils may also use roughly four-year terms with staggered schedules; exact local staggering remains ELASTIC.

## 선내의회 size
BOUNDED:
**~120–180 seats**, preferred center ~150.

Preferred working center at 150:
- ~60 residential district seats,
- ~90 compensatory shipwide proportional seats.

Exact seat count may vary inside the band unless an episode requires a specific tally.

## Two-vote mixed-member system
Each voter normally casts:
1. one local residential-district candidate vote,
2. one shipwide party/civic-list vote.

The shipwide list vote determines broad overall proportionality; compensatory list seats correct district-seat distortion.

Districts are population-based residential civic districts with independent boundary review.

Hard:
- no Spine occupational constituency,
- no engineer caste seat,
- no equal 1환/2환/3환 block regardless of population.

A small anti-fragmentation electoral threshold may exist; exact percentage is ELASTIC unless plot-relevant.

# 4. Parties / Executive — LOCKED

Meridian may have:
- long-lived parties,
- temporary civic coalitions,
- independent district candidates,
- eligible independent/coalition shipwide lists.

Do not transplant present-day Earth left/right alignments mechanically.
Issue coalitions may cut differently across:
- labor/business,
- privacy/rights,
- public services/tax,
- maintenance/heritage,
- arrival strategy.

The four settlement paths are not required to become four parties.

## Civic Executive Council
The Assembly selects a **small collective executive council**, preferred **5–7 members**.

Functions:
- coordinate civil administration,
- propose/prepare budget,
- implement Assembly law,
- coordinate cross-agency policy,
- nominate/appoint senior administrators under statutory oversight,
- coordinate the civic side of major emergencies.

One member may act as convenor/public coordinator but is not a president, captain or sovereign executive.

## Formation / removal
The executive is formed by Assembly majority/coalition after elections.
Removal uses **constructive no-confidence**: the Assembly replaces the executive/coalition rather than simply creating a vacuum.

Professional civil servants and technical commanders do not receive legislative seats merely by office.

Technical/commons bodies certify the physical feasible set; elected institutions choose political/distributive priorities within lawful safety limits.

# 6. Emergency Authority / Elections — LOCKED

Immediate physical emergency powers require:
- objective trigger/hazard,
- narrow scope,
- proportional action,
- time limit/expiry,
- logging,
- post-event review.

Automatic physical safeties may close doors/valves, isolate power/process sections or stop dangerous equipment.

Persistent civic restrictions require legal/civic basis.
Technical emergency action does not determine guilt, citizenship, family status or permanent property rights.

A genuine hazard may temporarily delay voting only when safe voting/communications are physically impossible.
Delay must be:
- publicly justified,
- narrowly scoped,
- reviewable,
- rescheduled as soon as practicable.

Author-facing hard guardrail:
**ordinary emergency power cannot postpone a general election indefinitely; delay beyond roughly 90 days requires extraordinary legislative + high judicial/constitutional reauthorization and recurring review.**

# 7. Court Architecture / 시민항소원 — LOCKED

Meridian has a multi-level judiciary.

## First-instance adjudication
Ordinary first-instance divisions/tribunals handle:
- civil/contract/property,
- family/guardianship,
- criminal,
- administrative disputes.

## General appellate review
Reviews significant legal/procedural errors and domain-appropriate fact/application disputes.

## High civic/rights/constitutional review
Handles:
- major civic-rights questions,
- agency overreach,
- constitutional/charter conflicts,
- high-level administrative-rights appeals.

**시민항소원** is a specialized high-level civic/administrative rights appeal institution within this upper review layer.
It is **not the only court in Meridian**.

Constitutional/charter review may be performed by a high-court chamber/panel whose reader-facing proper name need not be prominent.

No single official is final truth keeper.
Maren remains mandatorily recused from formal adjudication of her own family case.

# 8. Judges / Prosecutorial Independence / Election Integrity — LOCKED DIRECTION

Judges are not directly elected partisan politicians.

Selection direction:
- professional qualification,
- independent nomination/qualification process,
- democratic confirmation/appointment,
- long staggered terms,
- removal only for cause/serious misconduct through independent process.

Highest rights/constitutional judges use roughly **10–14 year class** staggered, nonrenewable or strongly renewal-limited terms.
Exact judge count/confirmation threshold remains ELASTIC.

Criminal investigation/prosecution operates under statute and judicial review.
The civic executive may set lawful policy/budget but cannot order conviction or dictate a judge's verdict.

Election integrity requires:
- independent election administration,
- public auditability,
- campaign funding/disclosure rules,
- conflict-of-interest disclosure,
- neutral public election information,
- plural media.

Private wealth may affect influence/reach but cannot buy extra votes.

# 9. Vacancies / Reconstruction / Arrival Franchise — LOCKED DIRECTION

Government continues through death/incapacity/Reconstruction of an official.

- district vacancy: acting procedure plus by-election/statutory replacement,
- list vacancy: normally filled through the same list/succession mechanism,
- executive incapacity: collective council continues and Assembly replaces the member.

Reconstruction does not automatically restore an office whose lawful term/vacancy process has already moved on.

Early off-Meridian residents retain equal shipwide civic franchise while local resident/worker councils and later municipal institutions grow with actual population.

No founder or Natural-Origin extra vote/veto.

# 10. Criminal Justice / Public Safety — CJ-H1 LOCKED

Model:
**Layered Public Safety + Restorative Default + Narrow Coercive Escalation**.

Recognizable crime includes:
- theft/fraud,
- assault,
- domestic/sexual violence,
- stalking/harassment,
- corruption,
- privacy/credential/cybercrime,
- dangerous negligence,
- homicide/serious violence at lower frequency.

Local teams handle routine incidents.
Specialist/cross-Habitat investigation handles serious, technical, critical-infrastructure or complex cyber cases.

For suitable lower-harm cases, where victim-safe:
- restitution,
- repair,
- treatment,
- supervised community sanctions
may be preferred.

Serious danger can justify secure custody under due process.

FORBIDDEN:
- death penalty as normal system,
- unsafe-space exile,
- forced restorative mediation for victims,
- AI declaring guilt/dangerousness as binding decision.

# 16. Found Property / Salvage — PROP-SALV-H1 LOCKED

**Finding an object does not by itself create ownership.**

Legal treatment distinguishes:
1. lost personal property,
2. apparently abandoned personal property,
3. private/co-op/business property,
4. public/common infrastructure,
5. controlled hazardous/professional equipment,
6. decommissioned public/enterprise equipment,
7. heritage/provenance-significant material.

Lost property carries reasonable reporting/return duties when ownership can be identified.
Apparently abandoned personal property may become claimable only after reasonable notice/holding procedures and confirmation it is not controlled/public/heritage property.
Exact waiting period remains ELASTIC.

Public/common/strategic infrastructure does not become private merely because it is old, disconnected or decommissioned.
Controlled tools retain custody/use restrictions.
Heritage discoveries may trigger provenance/reporting/public-interest review.

First discovery of an arrival resource does not create celestial sovereignty.

# 18. Labor — LAB-H1 LOCKED

Normal occupational choice is protected.

Needed skills are encouraged through scholarships, apprenticeship, compensation, training capacity, recruitment and relocation support—not permanent assignment.

Workers may organize and strike.
Critical life-safety sectors can require narrow minimum-service/emergency staffing.

Hazardous work receives stronger credentialing, safety oversight, compensation/leave and disability/pension protection.

Automation changes employment but does not assign citizens' lives.

# 24. Human-Subject Research Ethics — RES-H1 LOCKED

Research involving identifiable living citizens requires lawful purpose and appropriate consent/ethics safeguards.

More-than-minimal-risk human research requires independent ethics/scientific review addressing:
- physical risk,
- privacy/data risk,
- coercion/dependency,
- vulnerable populations,
- capacity/consent.

Interventional/recruitment consent must disclose material purpose, procedures, risks, data use, foreseeable future use, compensation where relevant and withdrawal limits after irreversible aggregation/deidentification.

Participants can generally stop future participation.
Already lawfully collected data may be retained only under the consent/statute/ethics plan.

Research receives minimum necessary data access.
Research status does not grant unrestricted D4 provenance access.

Special safeguards apply to medical/employment/school/detention/guardianship/capacity dependencies.
Civic Floor services cannot be conditioned on research participation.

**Amara has no research duty because she is unique.**
She may accept, refuse, limit or withdraw where possible without losing citizenship, healthcare or migration rights.

Low-risk deidentified/aggregate existing-record research may sometimes use a lawful ethics waiver; this never authorizes an identity hunt.

- concrete acute danger or severe decision incapacity,

# 26. Death / Finality — DEATH-H1 + MAID-H1 LOCKED

Distinguish:
1. clinical death/injury state,
2. viable Reconstruction review when genuinely relevant,
3. legal finality,
4. mourning/body/estate consequences.

Most deaths do not remain indefinitely in Reconstruction limbo.
Old stored data alone cannot preserve legal life forever.
Palliative/end-of-life care is ordinary.

## Medical aid-in-dying — MAID-H1
A narrow lawful pathway exists for a **contemporaneously decision-capable adult 18+** with:
- a serious irreversible/incurable medical condition,
- enduring suffering the person finds intolerable,
- voluntary request free of coercion,
- informed discussion of reasonable treatment/palliative/support alternatives.

Required process includes:
1. initial request,
2. treating clinician assessment,
3. independent second clinician assessment,
4. decision-specific capacity assessment when doubt exists,
5. palliative/support alternatives offered,
6. waiting/reconfirmation appropriate to urgency/condition,
7. final contemporaneous confirmation where feasible,
8. documentation and retrospective oversight review.

Exact day-count remains ELASTIC.

Disability, poverty, unemployment, inadequate housing or social isolation **alone** are never qualifying medical grounds.
The Civic Floor cannot make death the cheaper substitute for support.

No spouse, guardian, proxy, family member, AI, old Recovery Map or advance directive may actively authorize aid-in-dying for a currently incapable person.
Advance directives may still guide withholding/withdrawal of unwanted burdensome treatment under separate end-of-life law.

Acute/treatable psychiatric crisis pauses the pathway where it compromises capacity. Sole-psychiatric-condition eligibility is outside current Canon scope and requires a narrow future micropass if ever used.

Reconstruction scarcity or resource saving cannot be used to pressure MAID-H1.

# 29. Weapons / Dangerous Tools — LOCKED DIRECTION
