# 1. Social Constitution — LOCKED

Meridian is a democratic civic city-civilization constrained by physical commons rather than ruled by them.

Citizens:
- have equal civic personhood regardless of developmental origin,
- receive a guaranteed survival/basic-service floor,
- use money/markets for ordinary differentiated life,
- may own personal/business/residential interests but not sovereign life-support structure,
- choose careers rather than receive lifetime assignment,
- can form diverse families/households,
- receive public education and healthcare,
- are subject to real criminal/public-safety powers with due process,
- retain privacy against automatic engineering/medical omniscience,
- age, become disabled, retire and die.

Meridian is neither frictionless utopia nor totalitarian scarcity state.
Institutions may be delayed, biased, captured, expensive or wrong; they remain reviewable.

# 2. Governance Architecture — GOV-H1 LOCKED

Selected model:
**Layered Civic Republic + Commons Constraints + Narrow Safety Command**.

## Civic value authority
Elected civic institutions decide:
- law,
- taxation/budgets,
- distributive priorities,
- rights,
- ordinary public policy,
- long-term strategic choices.

## Representation
Architecture includes:
- elected shipwide legislature / **선내의회**,
- local Habitat/district civic councils,
- professional civil service,
- independent adjudication/oversight.

Exact electoral formula/seat count remains ELASTIC unless story-relevant.

## Technical constraint bodies
Professional technical institutions may certify:
- minimum safe maintenance,
- environmental/engineering limits,
- immediate hazard state,
- required redundancy margins.

They do not become general sovereigns.

## Ship Operations Command
Navigation/propulsion/critical technical operations may use professional command hierarchy.
It is subordinate to civic law outside narrow objective emergency powers.

No captain is head of state by default.

# 3. Emergency Authority — LOCKED

Immediate physical emergency powers require:
- objective trigger/hazard,
- narrow scope,
- proportional action,
- time limit/expiry,
- logging,
- post-event review.

Automatic physical safeties may close doors/valves, isolate power/process sections or stop dangerous equipment.

Persistent civic restrictions require legal/civic basis.

Technical emergency action does not determine:
- guilt,
- citizenship,
- family status,
- permanent property rights.

---

# 4. Courts / Appeals / Oversight — LOCKED

Separate adjudication/review protects against:
- unlawful detention/search,
- administrative abuse,
- family conflicts of interest,
- capacity-allocation abuse,
- property acquisition abuse,
- official misconduct.

**시민항소원** functions as a recurring civic/administrative rights/appeal institution within the broader legal architecture; it is not necessarily the entire judicial system.

No single official is final truth keeper.

Maren is mandatorily recused from formal adjudication of her own family case.

# 5. Criminal Justice / Public Safety — CJ-H1 LOCKED

Model:
**Layered Public Safety + Restorative Default + Narrow Coercive Escalation**.

Recognizable crime includes:
- theft/fraud,
- assault,
- domestic/sexual violence,
- stalking/harassment,
- corruption,
- privacy/credential/cybercrime,
- dangerous negligence,
- homicide/serious violence at lower frequency.

## Ordinary public safety
Local teams handle routine incidents.
Specialist/cross-Habitat investigation handles serious, technical, critical-infrastructure or complex cyber cases.

## Lower-harm cases
Where suitable and victim-safe:
- restitution,
- repair,
- treatment,
- supervised community-based sanctions
may be preferred.

## Serious danger
Secure custody/coercive restriction remains available under due process.

FORBIDDEN:
- death penalty as normal system,
- unsafe-space exile,
- forced restorative mediation for victims,
- AI declaring guilt/dangerousness as binding decision.

# 6. Search / Surveillance / Data Rights — LOCKED

Engineering telemetry is not general personal surveillance.

Protected domains include:
- medical,
- mental health,
- reproductive/Seed provenance,
- Continuity/Reconstruction,
- private communications,
- home/family life.

Sensitive search requires legal/clinical purpose and appropriate authority.
Emergency access is narrow/logged/reviewed.

Citizens have meaningful rights to:
- correct false data,
- challenge use,
- obtain review of improper access,
- delete/minimize data where no overriding legal/medical/historical need exists.

Retention classes are owned by `QUANTITATIVE-BANDS-v2.md`.

- schedule control,

# 10. Property / Housing — PROP-H1 LOCKED

Three layers:

## Structural Commons
- pressure hull,
- major structure,
- life-support backbone,
- public emergency routes,
- major rotation/transport/utility systems.

## Residential Tenure Interest
Transferable/use-value forms may include:
- ownership-like occupancy interests,
- rental,
- cooperative share,
- institutional/temporary/protected housing.

## Private Interior Property
- personal objects,
- furnishings,
- safe customization,
- heirlooms.

Citizens can build meaningful housing wealth without owning the ship's survival structure.

Civic minimum housing makes absolute unsheltered homelessness rare; loss of preferred housing/location remains serious.

# 11. Reconstruction / Property Finality — LOCKED

A prolonged severe recovery case may move through:
1. acute/recovery pending,
2. long functional absence with temporary asset management,
3. legal finality when continuation will not proceed.

Person continuity does not automatically unwind every lawful third-party transaction that occurred during a legally valid absence/finality process.

A returning/reconstructed citizen may recover surviving legal rights while current lawful occupancy/business reliance is separately protected and adjudicated.

Reconstruction does not automatically restore:
- old home,
- old job,
- old guardianship,
- every heirloom,
- old relationship configuration.

# 12. Labor — LAB-H1 LOCKED

Normal occupational choice is protected.

Civilization steers needed skills through:
- scholarships,
- apprenticeship,
- compensation,
- training capacity,
- recruitment,
- relocation support,
not permanent assignment.

Workers may organize and strike.
Critical life-safety sectors can require narrow minimum-service/emergency staffing.

Hazardous work receives stronger:
- credentialing,
- safety oversight,
- compensation/leave,
- disability/pension protection.

Automation changes employment but does not assign citizens' lives.

# 13. Civil Majority / Youth — LOCKED

Civil majority:
**18**.

At 18, ordinary baseline includes:
- full civil capacity,
- voting,
- adult contracts/residence,
- adult medical consent,
- partnership/marriage,
- independent destination/residence filing.

Structured paid apprenticeship / limited normal paid work:
**generally ~16+ with youth safeguards**.

Before 18:
youth autonomy is graduated and decision-specific.

Relevant growing voice includes:
- daily routine,
- activities,
- education/training,
- privacy,
- medical assent/participation,
- residence preference,
- future-location preference.

No AI maturity score grants legal adulthood.
No Seed/Reconstruction-specific adulthood rule exists.

# 14. Partnership / Parenthood / Guardianship — FAM-H1 LOCKED

Marriage/partnership, parenthood, guardianship, residence and property are legally distinct dimensions.

- gender-neutral civil partnership/marriage,
- sole parenthood lawful,
- donor conception normal,
- genetic donor ≠ automatic parent,
- adoption exists,
- guardianship may be temporary/limited,
- divorce/separation available without fault proof,
- child support/welfare follows means/child need.

A partner is not automatically a parent.
A guardian is not automatically an adoptive parent.

# 15. Reconstruction + Family — LOCKED

A reconstructed parent's identity/personhood does not automatically restore every current caregiving function after long absence.

Formal return/reinstatement questions may consider:
- current function,

- youth voice,
- safety,

Family parties with conflicts of interest cannot adjudicate their own case.

# 16. Education — EDU-H1 LOCKED

Model:
**Common Civic Foundation + Choice-Rich Adolescence + Apprenticeship/Higher Training + Lifelong Modular Retraining**.

Common foundation includes:
- literacy/language,
- math/science,
- history/civics,
- arts,
- physical education,
- media/digital literacy,
- practical closed-system safety/ecology.

Later adolescence expands:
- academic,
- technical,
- creative,
- apprenticeship,
- project/service pathways.

Higher learning exists through a network of institutes/professional schools/research/training centers rather than one mandatory university track.

Adult retraining is normal over long healthspan.

FORBIDDEN:
AI aptitude score binding a child's life path.

# 17. Healthcare — MED-H1 LOCKED

Universal clinical floor includes:
- primary care,
- emergency care,
- essential medicine,
- reproductive/prenatal care,
- basic/crisis mental-health care,
- essential rehabilitation.

Advanced medicine improves tissue/organ/cancer/trauma outcomes but does not eliminate chronic disease, disability, psychiatric illness, aging or death.

Reconstruction is a separate extreme/high-load intervention, not ordinary healthcare for every injury.

# 18. Mental Health / Capacity / Disability — LOCKED

Mental illness is healthcare, not automatic criminality.

Short involuntary crisis intervention requires:
- concrete acute danger or severe decision incapacity,
- least-restrictive feasible option,
- rapid review.

Capacity is decision-specific.
Supported decision-making is preferred where feasible.

Disability rights include:
- accessibility,
- assistive technology,
- adapted education/work/housing,
- income/support services.

Support cannot be conditioned on accepting invasive elective repair.

# 19. Death / Finality — DEATH-H1 LOCKED

Distinguish:
1. clinical death/injury state,
2. viable Reconstruction review when genuinely relevant,
3. legal finality,
4. mourning/body/estate consequences.

Most deaths do not remain indefinitely in Reconstruction limbo.
Old stored data alone cannot preserve legal life forever.

Palliative/end-of-life care is ordinary.
A tightly regulated medical aid-in-dying pathway may exist at low salience; exact procedure remains ELASTIC unless plot-relevant.

# 20. Funeral / Mourning / Material Return — LOCKED MODEL

Meridian supports plural religious/secular mourning.

Possible practices include:
- viewing/memorial gathering,
- cremation/thermal reduction,
- controlled biological/chemical reduction,
- retention of small memorial material,
- family/community ceremonies.

Large permanent cemetery burial aboard ship is generally impractical.

Bodies are not casually treated as anonymous feedstock.
Legal/ritual separation precedes material return.

Memorial recordings/avatars remain records/models, not automatic restored persons.

# 22. Weapons / Dangerous Tools — LOCKED DIRECTION

High-penetration/high-energy interpersonal weapons are strongly restricted because a closed inhabited environment amplifies external harm.

Routine public safety emphasizes:
- de-escalation,
- evacuation/separation,
- barriers/access denial,
- controlled restraint/lower-penetration incapacitation.

Exceptional higher-force capability exists under strict storage/authorization/review.

Industrial tools remain realistically dangerous and professionally accessible.

FORBIDDEN:
- universal civilian arms-race culture as default,
- magical perfectly safe stun weapon,
- autonomous AI lethal-guilt pipeline.

Exact equipment catalogue remains ELASTIC/conditional P1.
