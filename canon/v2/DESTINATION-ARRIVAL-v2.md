# 2. Settlement World — PLAN-H1Q BOUNDED

Selected physical model:
**temperate water-rich rocky world + near-Earth total pressure + strongly oxygen-poor atmosphere + near-Earth gravity + moderate seasons**.

Quantitative center/bands:
- orbit ~0.50–0.54 AU, center ~0.52 AU
- year ~150–165 Earth days, center ~158 d
- mass ~0.95–1.08 M⊕
- radius ~0.98–1.05 R⊕
- surface gravity ~0.93–1.02g, center ~0.97g
- day ~28–31 h, center ~29.5h
- obliquity ~10–18°, center ~14°
- surface pressure ~0.88–0.98 bar, center ~0.93 bar
- O2 ~8–10% class / pO2 ~7–9.5 kPa class.

Operational consequence:
- routine outdoor breathing support is mandatory,
- ordinary pressure suit is normally unnecessary,
- exposed skin can experience real wind/rain/temperature,
- loss of breathing support is serious but not explosive decompression.

Exact trace atmospheric chemistry remains ELASTIC.
