This file is the canonical macro narrative architecture after Canon Freeze Red Team v1.

---

# 1. Series Architecture — CANON

Target main-series length:
**~200–230 episodes**, current center ~230.

Hard structural principle:
> **The factual one-human Reveal occurs in the first third. The aftermath is longer than the buildup.**

FORBIDDEN:
- hiding the answer for 150–200 episodes
- case-of-the-week reset after each arc
- replacing consequences with a later `bigger ontology truth`

---

# 2. Reader Promise — CANON

Across early/mid/late series the story must continue delivering:
1. ordinary life in a closed, unusual civilization
2. personal Continuity/family cases exposing world rules
3. relationships surviving but changing under memory/body/origin disruption
4. decisions creating later consequences
5. private choices scaling into civic/resource questions
6. mystery rewards opening new action rather than merely another secret

Design regression at roughly 20 / 100 / 200 episodes passed.

# 7. Count Discovery / Publication — CP-H1 CANON

The Count cluster is not an automatic public dashboard.

Sequence:
1. restricted internal crosswalk identifies the anomaly
2. archive/medical/legal specialists independently verify category meaning, successful-Seed provenance and current count
3. privacy review flags that `1` is re-identifying
4. the category is judged legally material because it is incorporated into future arrival-transition schemas under public review
5. a public schema-integrity report releases the exact aggregate while keeping individual provenance sealed
6. the public Count creates downstream re-identification pressure

The publication is lawful but controversial.

This pre-transition audit occurs years before Final Approach certification becomes binding, so Count/identity consequences have time to reshape society before the late legal crisis.

No single hero or villain `causes` the Reveal.

# 8. Reveal Sequence — CANON

## Count ~53–58
RW-H1 layered data becomes public through CP-H1.

## Meaning ~59–66
Within roughly 5–8 episodes the reader understands:
- citizens' bodies/brains are living biological human-form tissue
- `Seeded/Synthetic-Origin` describes verified developmental cognitive provenance
- Seed does not contain memories/orders/admin access
- modern people are full citizens/persons
- old `Human` category was narrower
- adult medicine alone cannot prove provenance

## Amara Identity ~67–76
- Amara can already strongly suspect herself after Meaning because she knows her own full Seed-refusal family history
- protected official provenance confirms her
- public re-identification pressure narrows candidate families
- Amara/family decide how to respond
- limited public confirmation is her choice

This phase is not a contrived `who is it?` mystery for Amara herself.

## Consequence 77–88+
Family/work/media/legal consequences open the longer story.
