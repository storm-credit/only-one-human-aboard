# Derived Person Boundary — 4 Designs v0.1

Status: `CANON FREEZE BOUNDARY COMPARISON / NOT CANON`

Purpose:
RB-B의 `Recovery Map is not an executable soul-file` 원칙을 지키면서, 과거 brainstorming에 남아 있는 anchorless duplicate/Derived Person 가능성을 정리한다.

Hard rules already frozen:
- Seed cannot upload memories/personality.
- Recovery Map is not a standalone mind.
- normal Reconstruction requires surviving neural anchor.
- death must remain real.

---

# DP-A — Mature Anchorless Copy Technology Exists

## Core
Recovery Map + life-log로 새 biological brain/body에 성인 인격을 복제 가능.

## Fatal Conflicts
- Recovery Map becomes de facto soul file.
- old scans enable age rollback/copies.
- death stakes collapse.
- why not mass-produce people?

## Verdict
`REJECT`

---

# DP-B — Rare Experimental Approximation

## Core
Researchers can build a new neural system strongly modeled on a deceased person's records, but:
- memories are reconstructed/inferred, not transferred perfectly
- development takes substantial time
- result is legally/ethically a new person
- fidelity is low enough that society does not call it normal restoration

## Strength
preserves a few speculative edge cases.

## Risk
still creates a second technology stack and may reopen `copy` plot.

## Verdict
`POSSIBLE THEORY / DO NOT USE AS ROUTINE`

# DP-C — Black-Market Adult Copies

## Core
officially impossible but criminals secretly do it.

## Fatal Risk
villain tech can solve every world rule.
Undermines all medical limits.

## Verdict
`REJECT`

# DP-D — No Current Anchorless Person Reconstruction

## Core
Current technology cannot produce a continuity-plausible adult person from a Recovery Map alone.

Possible outputs without anchor:
- diagnostic simulation
- behavioral model
- voice/life-log avatar
- statistical neural reconstruction proposal

But none is a living adult continuation of the deceased.

A completely new biological person can of course be born/developed, but cannot be loaded with an old person's lived self under current Seed/Recovery rules.

## Strength
- cleanest RB-B boundary.
- death remains real.
- no duplicate loophole.
- lowers jargon/worldbuilding burden.

## Weakness
loses a handful of speculative identity-case ideas.

## Verdict
`CURRENT BEST`

# Recommended Boundary — DP-H1

`DP-D No Current Anchorless Person Reconstruction`

Keep DP-B only as **research speculation / philosophical legal hypothetical**, not a living recurring citizen category.

Status:
`PROVISIONAL CANON BOUNDARY PRIORITY`.

---

# 1. Canon Functional Rule

If the original neural anchor is destroyed beyond recovery:
**normal personal Reconstruction is impossible.**

Records can preserve:
- biography
- voice
- preferences
- life-log
- Recovery Map

but cannot restart that person's lived neural continuity in a new adult brain.

---

# 2. What About AI Avatars?

Society may create non-personal tools using records:
- memorial chatbot/avatar
- historical simulation
- diagnostic reconstruction model

These are not automatically civic persons and are not treated as restored deceased citizens.

Do not make them central unless a specific later case justifies it.

---

# 3. Story-Engine Repair

Older brainstorm events involving:
`original survivor + illegal Derived Person duplicate`
are **superseded as current story-engine options**.

Replace with conflicts that preserve RB-B, e.g.:
- two incompatible medical reconstructions of the same surviving anchor at different stages are impossible simultaneously; instead dispute over which recovery plan to follow
- memorial/life-log avatar misused as legal evidence
- family wants to treat a high-fidelity behavioral simulation as the deceased person, but law refuses
- identity dispute over severe memory loss after anchor-preserving reconstruction

No live duplicate required.

---

# 4. Natural/Synthetic Origin Connection

Seeded-Origin citizens are NOT `Derived Persons`.

They are:
- biologically born
- independently developing persons
- new consciousnesses originating under generated developmental prior

This distinction must remain explicit.

---

# 5. Red Team

## RT-DP-01 — Does banning anchorless copies make the SF less ambitious?
It makes the central question sharper: synthetic cognitive origin can create new people, but records cannot cheaply resurrect old people.

Verdict: `PASS`

## RT-DP-02 — Could future destination-era technology eventually solve it?
Possibly outside current series, but Canon for this novel says **not an available practical technology during the story**.

Verdict: `PASS`

## RT-DP-03 — What if a memorial simulation claims consciousness?
That is a separate AI-personhood problem and risks widening the novel. Keep ordinary memorial tools non-personal/limited unless deliberately introduced in a later design reopening.

Verdict: `PASS / DO NOT EXPAND`
