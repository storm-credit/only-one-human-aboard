legacy arrival clauses는 비구속/준비용 framework로 취급.

### Weakness
C8 Reveal가 사실상 trigger처럼 보일 수 있음.

`ESSENTIAL INTERPRETIVE LAYER, NOT SOLE TRIGGER`

## Era 2 — Arrival Preparation Era
T-30~T-10 candidate.

Already happening before story:
- technical site planning
- forward scout analysis
- industry design
- training
- budget proposals

But:
**strategic habitat assets are not yet irreversibly reassigned under destination government law.**

Planning remains under ordinary ship civic law.

Old mission provisions are studied, but not yet the operative source for:
- new settlement membership
- mission-reserved assets
- transition jurisdiction.

---

## Era 3 — Final Approach Transition
around `T-10 ± 1y` candidate.

A scheduled certification determines that:
1. destination data is sufficient for irreversible planning
2. deceleration/navigation commitment has passed a practical return/retarget threshold
3. strategic assets can begin legally transferring to arrival entities

At this point a **Transition Reconciliation Process** becomes mandatory.

It must reconcile:
- current Civic Law
- Founding Mission/Arrival clauses
- settlement charters
- asset ownership/responsibility

This is Act 5 legal engine.

# 2. Why The Old Human Categories Matter Only Now

Legal scholars already knew old wording.

Before Bio-Origin reveal, common modern interpretation could simply be:
> old `human` language applies to current human citizens generally.

Because modern society no longer used origin classification as civic identity.

After Count/C8 reveal:
- old technical provenance meaning becomes newly concrete.

But still no immediate legal effect until Final Approach Transition asks:
> which old categories are operative in settlement/mission clauses?

Thus:
- **C8 does not activate the law.**
- **Arrival certification activates binding reconciliation.**
- C8/Count changes how one disputed clause is interpreted.

This is the required separation.

# 7. Why It Was Not Fully Rewritten Earlier

Not:
`people forgot.`

Better:
current civic law evolved for voyage society.
Arrival law remained a **contingency/transition layer** whose exact application depended on destination conditions not known until late approach.

Over centuries, many amendments can exist.
The problem is not an untouched 450-year-old single document.

Instead:
- Founding Charter
- later voyage amendments
- current civic law
- arrival implementation codes
form a layered legal stack.

Old origin terms survive in a few foundational cross-references.

This is more realistic and prevents `one ancient scroll solves all`.
