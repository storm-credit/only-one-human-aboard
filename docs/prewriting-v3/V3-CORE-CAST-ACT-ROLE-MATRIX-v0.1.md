# 0. Execution rule

For every recurring character in every active Act, the writer should know six things:
1. `Independent want` — what the character wants even if protagonist vanishes.
2. `Act pressure` — how the Act's problem attacks that want/value.
3. `Independent action` — one thing they can initiate/solve without protagonist.
4. `Relationship movement` — one relationship that changes, not necessarily with protagonist.
5. `Material/visual state` — what readers can recognize physically.
6. `Carry-forward` — what state enters the next Act.

A character may be intentionally LOW/ABSENT in an Act. Absence is preferable to token cameos.

Legend:
- `P` = primary supporting pressure/decision role
- `S` = secondary recurring role
- `L` = low-density/continuity only
- `—` = intentionally absent or offstage

# 2. C01 — 루카스 켈러 / Lukas Keller

Invariant:
- hull/structural damage analyst,
- belief: `망가진 건 원인을 찾으면 고칠 수 있다`,
- false extension: `고치면 원래대로 돌아간다`,
- lean practical visual identity + field case + physical marking language.

## Act1 — Home / competence baseline
Independent want:
close routine failures correctly, keep work/family obligations under control, avoid old Arun guilt becoming public/emotional work.

Pressure:
minor discrepancies refuse clean causal closure.

Action:
solves ordinary incidents through sequence reconstruction; deliberately says `모른다` when outside specialty.

Relationship movement:
Mira shows autonomy friction; Gideon/Niko establish professional generation triangle; family proves protagonist has ordinary life.

Material state:
clean-but-used field case; early marking language; standard crawler/drone work.

Carry-forward:
confidence in physical evidence rises at the same time records become less trustworthy.

## Act2 — Catastrophe / irreversible decision
Want:
find the real failure chain fast enough to keep unsafe structures closed and viable routes open.

Pressure:
people demand action before certainty; old Arun closure wound returns.

Action:
owns technical recommendation, never evacuation command; accepts one irreversible physical choice.

Relationship movement:
Selene/Mira/Rafi challenge his tendency to convert responsibility into control.

Material state:
field case contaminated/damaged; markings multiply; work jacket/gear shifts from routine to emergency wear.

Carry-forward:
continuity misbinding occurs before confirmed death.

## Act3 — False regression pleasure → wrong familiarity
Want:
prevent what he thinks is the known disaster and restore lost relationships.

Pressure:
foreknowledge works, then pre-existing H-B differences make restoration impossible.

Action:
uses knowledge for genuine wins, then begins falsifying his own memory instead of only the world.

Relationship movement:
misreads H-B Mira; encounters different Niko/Gideon histories; current Arun absence is still unresolved.

Material state:
field-case repair/certification detail differs at EP095; familiar environment contains subtle material deltas.

Carry-forward:
`memory = future` belief weakened.

## Act4 — Memory as social power
Want:
determine which predictive claims are physically/socially actionable without becoming an oracle himself.

Pressure:
fraud, rumor and true leakage all look similar before consequence.

Action:
tests claims against current causal conditions; his interventions themselves cause divergence.

Relationship movement:
Sora/Hana/Ivo/Cassian become independent truth routes; Mira resists being treated as a variable in his old memory.

Material state:
less focus on new gear; familiar routes/places change because people believe predictions.

Carry-forward:
accepts that remembered path is one path, not privileged future.

## Act5 — Physical evidence / current Arun
Want:
find evidence that cannot be explained as memory error.

Pressure:
legacy structures and current people contradict H-A grief.

Action:
puts records behind physical chronology; investigates without claiming counterpart ownership.

Relationship movement:
H-B Arun refuses projection; Hana/Sora control evidence provenance; Gideon holds context protagonist lacks.

Material state:
field tools return in Legacy Layer; impossible repair chronology overlays his own marks.

Carry-forward:
`same face != same owed relationship` becomes lived rule.

## Act6 — Current home collapses
Want:
keep H-B physically survivable without rebuilding the conditions that caused cascade.

Pressure:
scarcity makes every repair political; world moves without him.

Action:
permanently disables a dangerous high-capacity path instead of restoring it.

Relationship movement:
Niko earns independent local judgment; Juno/Mira/Selene/Rafi/Leila build solutions outside his ownership.

Material state:
kit becomes mismatched; isolation/lockout material overtakes repair tools; clothing and posture show accumulated wear.

Carry-forward:
learns that good repair can mean non-restoration.

## Act7 — Scale reveal without world-hop
Want:
understand what kind of physical system can contain all contradictory evidence without invalidating current lives.

Pressure:
answers threaten to make him treat lived Meridian as `fake`.

Action:
uses damage/maintenance/namespace evidence as one route among many; accepts he is not HUMAN:1.

Relationship movement:
Arun/Ivo/Hana cases force personhood separation from source history.

Material state:
Outer Ark utility ecology appears; protagonist's familiar interface logic becomes an interpretive bridge, not secret access power.

Carry-forward:
no privileged original history needed for present life to be real.

## Act8 — Personhood / title meaning
Want:
help create survivable rules without turning technical resource facts into moral rankings.

Pressure:
D3 rights and HUMAN provenance tempt category errors.

Action:
provides failure/resource evidence but does not become moral sovereign.

Relationship movement:
Leila/D3 affected persons/legal actors own consent/personhood questions; Mira relationship begins to be based on current choices.

Material state:
less `hero gear`; care/governance spaces dominate.

Carry-forward:
accepts future cannot be made safe by sacrificing full lived persons as disposable rehearsal.

## Act9 — First unrehearsed future
Want:
keep first settlement alive through a failure no previous history exactly solves.

Pressure:
common-mode cascade plus irreversible losses.

Action:
infers propagation/coupling from physical damage; works with others to segment, shed load, preserve local control and repair.

Relationship movement:
current Mira relationship chosen anew; Niko owns field judgment; institutions consciously record causal credit.

Material state:
battered incomplete kit persists; settlement workwear lighter but still functional; no hero armor.

Carry-forward:
`understand failure` remains, `restore original state` is gone.

# 15. Cross-character Act requirements

## Act1
Must prove at least 6 recurring people have ordinary wants unrelated to mystery.
No more than 2 scenes may use `character appears only to foreshadow future role` logic.

## Act2
Every emergency specialist must have a different failure mode:
- protagonist = over-responsibility/control,
- Selene = boundary/coercion,
- Rafi = rescue fixation,
- Leila = capacity triage,
- Mira = allocation exclusion,
- Gideon = caution/hesitation,
- Sora = suspicion.

## Act3
H-B deltas must be social/material and pre-existing.
No one receives a full alternate-costume redesign.

## Act4
Fraud/true leakage/institutional reaction must be distributed across Sora, Ivo, Cassian, Mira, Juno, Selene rather than protagonist investigating every case personally.

## Act5
Records become action:
- Hana = provenance,
- Sora = chain of custody,
- protagonist/Gideon = physical chronology,
- Arun = current-life contradiction,
- Leila = present-person care.

## Act6
At least three decisive causal steps occur outside protagonist ownership.
Neutral-corridor chain remains independent.

## Act7
No character becomes exposition mouthpiece.
Each truth route has an owner:
- physical = protagonist/Gideon/Niko,
- provenance = Hana/Sora,
- lived-memory = Ivo/Arun/affected persons,
- logistics/social consequence = Mira/Juno/Selene/Leila.

## Act8
Affected D3 persons own moral claims.
Core cast supplies capacities/limits, not permission to exist.

## Act9
Final crisis resolution requires at least five independent functions and no single hero machine/character.
Ending does not roll-call all 13.
