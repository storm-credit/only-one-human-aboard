# Delta 1 — protagonist field-case repair history

## EP095 — micro plant
Protagonist wakes/returns with the same functional field-case family he expects.

Difference:
one banal repair patch/edge reinforcement or certification-service sticker is in a place/configuration he does not remember.

His reaction:
- assumes the case was serviced/repaired during a period he forgot,
- or assumes his own post-crisis memory is fuzzy.

Important:
Do NOT linger. One sentence/gesture maximum at first.

## EP103~104 — recontextualization
When cooling-component physical history proves pre-existing H-B divergence, the earlier case detail becomes retrospectively meaningful:
`my own work life also had a history here before I arrived.`

No exact explanation required.

# Delta 2 — Mira personal carry / route-tag habit

## EP101 — first relational-material contradiction
Mira uses a familiar class of route slate/tags, but:
- one personal pouch/container is different,
- or a small habitual placement/labeling convention protagonist associates with their long H-A relationship never existed.

Protagonist makes a casual comment or reaches/assumes a routine that H-B Mira does not understand.

This must read emotionally first:
**he knows a version of her private routine she never lived.**

## EP109~112 — payoff
During explicit relationship comparison, object/routine becomes one of several low-stakes pieces showing their relationship history diverged long before H-B arrival.

Hard:
object is not proof by itself and never becomes a clue key.

# Delta 3 — cooling component / machine generation

## EP103 — hard professional trigger
The component/repair state is from a generation or retrofit path inconsistent with H-A memory.

Visual/material manifestation:
- different certified adapter plate,
- different joint housing geometry,
- different repair layer/fastener family,
- visibly older/newer interface pattern.

This is not `the machine is a different color`.

## EP104
Independent records/material state confirm the replacement predates protagonist's arrival.

Function:
first unmistakable **physical-world pre-existing delta**.

# Delta 5 — family household / school-life object

## EP108 — private-life proof
Use one mundane household/school/community object rather than tragic family change.

Candidate selected direction:
**niece/nephew school/community item + household placement/history**.

Examples:
- a school project/display item from a program protagonist remembers the child never attending,
- a household furniture/storage arrangement caused by an earlier move that did not happen in H-A,
- a local school route badge/card/photo that places the family in a different neighborhood history.

Required:
family members can explain the H-B history naturally.

Hard:
- no parent death/marriage shock,
- no secret family clue,
- no DNA/identity reveal,
- emotional effect = ordinary life existed without him.

**tiny self-object mismatch** — dismissible.

**private relationship routine/object mismatch** — uncomfortable.

**hard machine/material history mismatch** — cannot be caused by his intervention.

**two other people share an independent career history** — social proof.

**family ordinary-life history differs** — personal proof.
