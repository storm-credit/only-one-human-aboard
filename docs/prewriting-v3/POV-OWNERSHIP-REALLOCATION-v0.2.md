# 0. Interpretation rules

For current prewriting:

1. Episodes NOT listed as secondary-owned below are protagonist-owned.
2. `MIXED SECONDARY-OWNED` means the named secondary character retains the episode's decisive local choice, but the protagonist appears in a meaningful separate scene.
3. `FULL ABSENT` means the protagonist does not appear in the episode by default.
4. A v0.1 card converted from secondary-owned → protagonist-owned keeps its original secondary scene/function as a separated B-plot, opening, consequence scene or handoff unless a later QA proves it redundant.
5. Do not insert the protagonist into authority domains he does not own merely to satisfy presence.
