# 1. ACT1 — FUN ENGINE MICRO-PATCH

## Problem
Act1 must make Meridian worth losing. Forty-two episodes cannot feel like `waiting for Act2`.

## Selected solution
Act1 uses a repeating **3-flavor local reward rotation**:

### F1 — Work Puzzle Reward
A concrete damage-analysis problem has a local answer/payoff even when it is not a central mystery clue.
Examples already compatible with cards:
- vibration/crack source is one layer upstream,
- ordinary industrial accident reconstruction,
- repair signoff dispute,
- safe-access geometry,
- material/wear interpretation.

Hard:
At least half of F1 cases are genuinely ordinary. Do not turn every successful diagnosis into ontology evidence.

### F2 — Relationship / Social Friction Reward
A scene pays through a choice or boundary, not exposition.
Use:
- protagonist tries to solve somebody else's problem and is refused,
- Mira chooses network fairness over his convenience,
- Niko pursues qualification outside protagonist control,
- Selene limits his emergency authority,
- family makes an ordinary choice without consulting him.

Hard:
By EP027, protagonist's overcontrol must cause one real interpersonal cost that is not instantly repaired by a perfect apology.

### F3 — City-Life Reward
Use recurring pleasures/inconveniences that remain **non-clues**:
- one food/venue habit,
- Garden Commons leisure/sport,
- school/family scheduling hassle,
- shift-change commute congestion,
- local community event/service,
- Niko peer/social life.

Hard:
At least four recurring ordinary details remain permanently ordinary and are never converted into foreshadowing.

## Band-level payoff rule
Every Act1 density band B01~B06 must contain:
1. one local practical payoff,
2. one relationship/social payoff,
3. one reusable ordinary-life anchor.

The central mystery may advance softly, but no band exists only to plant clues.

## EP029~042 tonal rule
Physical contradictions must be written as **annoying professional anomalies**, not horror punctuation.
Avoid repeated endings equivalent to `something was wrong`.

## Act1 end
EP042 remains a confident ordinary ending with a maintenance window/next job, not a siren cliffhanger.
The reader continues because they care about Meridian and its people; Act2 then destroys that security.

Verdict:
**PATCH ACCEPTED.**

# 2. ACT4 — FOREKNOWLEDGE CASE DIVERSITY MICRO-PATCH

## Problem
Repeated structure `someone predicts X → investigate claim → maybe true` would become monotonous.

## Selected social-effect families
Act4 rotates the anomaly through **five different consequences**. Only one is the direct attack plot.

### A. Money / Allocation
EP137~145 emphasis:
- early asset/resource movement,
- freight reservations,
- paid warning services,
- panic cancellation,
- unequal ability to act on rumors.

Question:
`Who can afford to behave as if a rumor might be true?`

### B. Household / Daily-Life Cost
EP146~150 emphasis:
- missed work,
- childcare/school-route changes,
- unnecessary evacuation/purchases,
- family arguments about whether to believe warnings.

Question:
`What does uncertainty cost ordinary people even when nothing supernatural happens?`

### C. Security / Self-Fulfilling Threat
EP151~165 emphasis:
- real sabotage/hostage action,
- altered policing routes,
- rumor exploited by offenders,
- intervention changes casualties and attack geometry.

Question:
`Can information about an event become one of its causes?`

### D. Reputation / Trust
EP166~174 emphasis:
- Ivo's credibility,
- Cassian suspicion,
- people gaining/losing status because of apparent foresight,
- intimate and professional trust damaged by claims that cannot be proven.

Question:
`What happens when a person's social value depends on being believed?`

### E. Institution / Evidence
EP175~181 emphasis:
- privacy of anomaly claimants,
- evidence thresholds,
- publication/verification policy,
- no official prophet bureau,
- no blanket criminalization.

Question:
`How does a normal institution handle a phenomenon it cannot classify?`

## Repetition firewall
Across Act4:
- no more than two episodes in a row may be primarily `claim verification`,
- every 5~7 episodes must shift dominant consequence family,
- at least 3 apparent-foreknowledge cases close as ordinary fraud/luck/leakage,
- genuine cases remain fewer and more costly to interpret than false cases.

Verdict:
**PATCH ACCEPTED.**

# 3. ACT5 — RECORD → ACTION MICRO-PATCH

## Problem
Act5 has strong evidence but risks becoming archive/registry tourism.

## Hard conversion rule
**Every major record/evidence discovery must cause a present-tense action, access change, relationship conflict, legal problem, or safety decision within <=2 episodes.**

### Chain 1 — Impossible repair history
Physical wear/repair evidence
→ safety access cannot be treated as routine
→ current inspection route/closure changes
→ people/work schedules are affected.

### Chain 2 — Memorial without disaster
Memorial/kinship contradiction
→ current family/descendant has privacy/grief/inheritance stakes
→ evidence cannot be handled as a collectible clue.

### Chain 3 — Living/dead registry collision
Old death marker
→ current living person's legal/privacy status becomes relevant
→ protagonist cannot simply confront them
→ lawful contact and current-person primacy matter.

### Chain 4 — Arun
Archive contradiction
→ protagonist wants emotional closure
→ current Arun has independent work/home obligations
→ confrontation changes present relationship, not just theory.

### Chain 5 — HUMAN:1
Legacy field/count
→ access/security/resource-routing question
→ physical preservation route has present operational protection
→ meaning remains unknown until Act8.

## Rhythm firewall
- no two consecutive episodes may be `people sitting and reading old records` without a field/current-life scene between them,
- one archive episode should create a question; the next useful beat should make somebody **do** something because of it,
- Legacy Layer remains mostly ordinary service infrastructure; investigation has dust, access, safety, scheduling, people and material limits, not secret-dungeon spectacle.

Verdict:
**PATCH ACCEPTED.**

# 4. ACT7~8 — REVEAL SCENEIZATION MICRO-PATCH

## Problem
These Acts contain the densest ontology and ethics. Their highest risk is becoming a setting lecture or philosophy panel.

## Universal Reveal Scene grammar
For major reveal beats use:

**CURRENT PROBLEM → WRONG/INCOMPLETE MODEL → CONCRETE EVIDENCE → DECISION COST → ONLY THEN NEW INTERPRETATION/LABEL**

Never reverse this into:
`new term → definition → history → example`.

## Reader-facing vocabulary limits
- D0/D1/D2/D3/D4 remain primarily author-side design language.
- Do not dump a five-tier taxonomy in dialogue.
- `atomic/nanoscale anchors + distributed ultradense substrate` is enough physical language.
- no electron-city lecture.
- maximum one genuinely new high-level ontology concept per major scene; a second term is allowed only if it immediately changes action.

## Act7 scene anchors

### Knowledge Without History
Current repair/policy problem first.
An inherited rule helps but is partly wrong for current conditions.
Only after use do provenance gaps matter.

### Counterpart Rights
Current patient/family/legal conflict first.
The rule `same lineage != same person` is experienced as consent/liability before terminology.

### C4 mechanism
Physical routing/index evidence first.
The protagonist's Act2 experience is explained only after other characters falsify simpler theories.
No system confession.

### Uncountable Lineages
Protected load/namespace/maintenance evidence first.
Scale is inferred because the known city cannot account for the physical/administrative footprint.
No giant-number spectacle.

## Act8 scene anchors

### HUMAN definition
Medical/privacy/preservation routing first.
`Natural-Origin departure-continuity` is reconstructed as a legacy field because present decisions require its meaning.

### HUMAN >1
Retired/damaged preservation pathways + old inventory/provenance first.
The emotional point is not a number; it is that `1` emerged from attrition.

### D3 rights
Affected people receive an operational threat first.
Their own proposal, disagreement and consent precede legal philosophy.
The child is an ordinary person with an ordinary want, never the sole argument for adult rights.

### Reform
The final payoff is a changed machine/operations rule:
- destructive closeout blocked,
- safe quiescence distinguished,
- new involuntary full-lived creation restricted,
- resource/representation problem remains unfinished.

## Meeting-room firewall
Across Act7~8:
- no three consecutive episodes dominated by archive/meeting/technical explanation,
- after any explanation-heavy episode, next episode must contain at least one of: physical task, private relationship decision, medical action, access conflict, resource decision, public consequence.

Verdict:
**PATCH ACCEPTED.**

# 5. ACT9 — ENDING COMPRESSION MICRO-PATCH

## Problem
The final ten episodes could become a queue of character epilogues.

## Selected solution
Do not create one farewell episode per subplot.
Use **four closure beats**, each closing multiple long arcs.

### C1 — Cost of No Retry / EP401~403
- permanent settlement damage remains,
- people/resources are redistributed,
- no alternate version erases the loss,
- ordinary repair begins immediately.

Closes:
death stakes + Act9 survival + no-retry ethic.

### C2 — Current Relationships / EP404~405
Mira/protagonist and selected core relationships resolve through **current commitments/tasks**, not a montage of speeches.
Arun receives at most a brief current-life acknowledgement; his primary guilt arc is already paid in Act7.

Closes:
restoration-vs-current-choice + protagonist control flaw + Mira endpoint.

### C3 — First Life / EP406~408
HUMAN:1 medical event is ordinary clinical/caregiver work.
No crowd, anthem, throne, prophecy or instant protagonist bond.
The birth/first neonatal record may coincide with wider ordinary settlement work, but does not stop society.

Closes:
title MacGuffin + HUMAN attrition + `no prior lived history` contrast.

### C4 — Ordinary Unrehearsed Future / EP409~410
Return scope to work/home/city routine.
A small current problem exists whose exact answer is not in inherited history.
Protagonist does not panic or seek restoration; people choose, repair and proceed.

Closes:
protagonist false belief `fixing means returning` → `repair can mean building forward`.

## Compression gate
Every EP401~410 must close/change at least **2** of:
- practical settlement state,
- relationship state,
- protagonist arc,
- HUMAN/title line,
- institutional norm,
- ordinary-life baseline.

If any episode exists only to say goodbye to one character, merge/compress it during prose execution.

No new ontology after EP370.
No sequel-bait alien revelation.
No final thesis lecture.

Verdict:
**PATCH ACCEPTED.**
