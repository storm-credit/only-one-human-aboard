1. more than one new recurring non-character label is taught in an ordinary episode,
2. model codes replace functional description,
3. surnames are alternated merely for prose variety,
4. H-B sounds like a separate fantasy culture,
5. Act7 creates a ship-class glossary,
6. Act8 turns moral conflict into acronym dialogue,
7. Act9 gives every new landmark a mythic proper name,
8. credits are used as a universal solution to strategic scarcity.
