## 1. Card fields

Each episode card records:

- **POV Owner** — primary close-third owner. `[주인공/TBD]` until final name freeze.
- **Time / Duration** — relative chronology, especially strict in Acts2/6.
- **Primary Zone** — experienced-Meridian location.
- **Immediate Want** — what the POV person wants now; must work without lore.
- **A-Plot Action** — concrete event/problem/decision.
- **B-Plot / Relationship** — durable relationship movement or offscreen-state consequence.
- **World/Institution Movement** — what changes outside private emotion.
- **Clue / Device State** — NONE or explicit `PLANT / REINFORCE / TRIGGER / PAYOFF / AFTERSHOCK`.
- **Knowledge Fence** — what this POV may not know/reveal.
- **End Turn** — action/result/question that earns the next episode.
- **Continuity Output** — facts that must persist into later cards.

## 2. Episode usefulness gate

PASS only if an episode does at least **2** of:
- survival/practical action,
- relationship change,
- mystery movement,
- world/institution consequence,
- character judgment change,
- ordinary-life attachment,
- causal setup/payoff.

For very quiet Act1 episodes, ordinary-life attachment + relationship movement is sufficient if the scene also establishes a reusable place/person/routine.

FAIL if:
- pure lore explanation,
- pure travel with no relationship/world result,
- pure repeat of previous event from another angle,
- secondary POV exists only to tell reader information,
- protagonist is dragged into a problem solely so he can solve it.

## 3. POV rules

Series center diagnostic:
- protagonist-centered 272/410 = 66.3%,
- other POVs distributed across recurring people/institutions/ordinary actors.

Episode ownership is a diagnostic, not a ban on one short secondary scene.

Hard:
- one clear POV per continuous scene,
- no head hopping,
- a POV whose honest interiority would spoil a required mystery stays ineligible until Reveal Ledger permits,
- secondary POV must have an immediate want independent of exposition.

## 4. Off-protagonist causality

Every major Act must include:
- >=2 causal developments outside protagonist presence,
- >=1 problem solved or worsened independently,
- >=1 relationship/institution change he does not control.

But every Act also requires protagonist-owned:
- irreversible decision,
- mystery/interpretive advance,
- relationship movement,
- competence payoff/failure.

## 5. Clue density

Explicit cross-history autobiographical memory is extremely rare.

Do not use a memory anomaly whenever a normal clue works.
Prefer:
- wear,
- procedure,
- records,
- social reaction,
- institutional inheritance,
- material contradiction,
- later correlated rare memory fault.

## 6. Ordinary-world density

Across each ~10~15 episodes, include scene-use of at least one:
- work routine,
- food,
- commute,
- family,
- school/childcare,
- leisure/sport,
- housing,
- ordinary bureaucracy,
- health/clinic,
- local business/community.

These scenes must still work if ontology clues are removed.

## 7. Act2 / Act6 chronology rule

Act2:
~48~52 hours total. Follow `V3-ACT2-CONCURRENCY-GEOGRAPHY-CAUSAL-LEDGER-v0.1`.

Act6:
~8~10 weeks total. Follow `V3-ACT6-CONCURRENCY-GEOGRAPHY-CAUSAL-LEDGER-v0.1`.

No protagonist teleportation.
Travel/access must respect current transit/closure state.

## 8. End-hook rotation

Rotate among:
- practical complication,
- decision consequence,
- relationship boundary,
- physical clue,
- wrong recognition/testimony,
- institutional action,
- temporary success,
- local answer creating deeper question.

Avoid consecutive cliffhanger-by-danger endings.

## 9. C2 handling

Still-open surface labels:
- protagonist name,
- family names,
- final district names,
- currency term,
- minor shops/room codes/prices.

Use stable descriptive placeholders rather than freezing weak names.
