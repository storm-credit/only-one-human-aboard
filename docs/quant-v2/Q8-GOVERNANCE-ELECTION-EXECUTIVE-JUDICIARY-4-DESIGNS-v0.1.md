# Q8 — Governance Election / Executive / Judiciary — 4 Designs v0.1

Status: `ENCYCLOPEDIC v2 FINAL P1 MICROPASS / DESIGN COMPARISON / NOT CANON`
Date: 2026-08-18
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Parent architecture:
`GOV-H1 — Layered Civic Republic + Commons Constraints + Narrow Safety Command`

Goal:
Close the last governance-scale pre-Freeze gap:
- how citizens elect shipwide government,
- how the executive is selected/removed,
- what the ordinary court hierarchy is,
- where 시민항소원 fits,
without creating captain-presidentialism, Habitat federal vetoes, technocracy or a modern-Earth copy with unnecessary complexity.

Existing locks:
- civil majority = 18
- elected 선내의회
- elected local councils
- small accountable civic executive
- professional civil service
- independent review/courts
- narrow technical Ship Operations Command
- no captain/AI sovereignty
- no Habitat caste/sovereignty
- 도착전환위원회 is temporary statutory body.

---

# DESIGN A — DIRECT PRESIDENTIAL CIVIC REPUBLIC

## Model
Citizens directly elect:
- 선내의회,
- one shipwide president/civic chief executive.

Executive has a separately elected mandate and appoints cabinet/agency heads.

## Strengths
- very legible,
- clear individual accountability,
- strong election drama if ever needed.

## Weaknesses
- creates one symbolic `leader of the ship`,
- invites president/captain conceptual merger,
- dual mandates can deadlock in a closed technical civilization,
- over-personalizes arrival choices the story intentionally distributes.

## Story trap
One charismatic executive can begin to look like the person who decides whether civilization stays aboard or settles.

Verdict:
`REJECT`.

---

# DESIGN B — PURE SHIPWIDE PROPORTIONAL PARLIAMENTARY REPUBLIC

## Model
Citizens cast one shipwide proportional vote.
All Assembly seats are allocated from shipwide party/civic lists.
The Assembly selects/removes a small executive council.

## Strengths
- highly proportional,
- no singular president,
- compact,
- easy coalition accountability.

## Weaknesses
- weak local geographic link,
- residents may feel no specific elected representative owns neighborhood/relocation issues,
- Habitat/district councils then carry too much geographic representation burden.

Verdict:
`STRONG BUT NOT SELECTED`.

---

# DESIGN C — MIXED-MEMBER CIVIC PARLIAMENTARY REPUBLIC

## Model
One shipwide legislature combines:
- local residential-district representatives,
- compensatory shipwide proportional seats.

Citizens normally cast two votes:
1. local representative,
2. shipwide party/civic list.

The list vote determines overall proportional balance; list seats compensate for district-seat distortion.

The Assembly selects a small collective Civic Executive Council and can replace it through constructive no-confidence.

## Strengths
- proportional without losing local accountability,
- compatible with mixed Habitat society,
- local districts matter without becoming sovereign mini-states,
- no direct-elected president/captain figure,
- coalition government fits long-lived civic negotiation,
- supports local relocation/Old Quarter/arrival politics without Habitat veto blocs.

## Weaknesses
- slightly more complex than pure PR,
- requires clear district-boundary and compensation rules,
- can still develop parties/political careers like any real democracy.

Verdict:
**`SELECTED — GOV-Q8-H1`**.

---

# DESIGN D — ELECTION + SORTITION EXECUTIVE HYBRID

## Model
Assembly elected normally, but part of executive/constitutional review is filled by randomly selected citizens for fixed terms.

## Strengths
- reduces permanent political class,
- reflects a 300k participatory society,
- can improve legitimacy of hard allocation decisions.

## Weaknesses
- procedure-heavy,
- difficult continuity during Final Approach,
- risks becoming an author's idealized democracy mechanism,
- randomly selected citizens in executive responsibility raise competence/accountability problems.

Limited citizen panels can still exist for consultation/review, but not as the normal executive architecture.

Verdict:
`REJECT AS CORE MODEL`.

# SELECTED MODEL — GOV-Q8-H1

# `Mixed-Member Civic Assembly + Assembly-Selected Collective Executive + Independent Multi-Level Courts`

---

# 1. Franchise — LOCK CANDIDATE

- ordinary voting age: **18**
- one person, equal civic vote
- developmental origin, Reconstruction history, profession, Habitat and property do not alter vote weight
- early off-Meridian citizens retain franchise under transition law.

No occupational/technical weighted votes.
No Natural-Origin reserved seat.

---

# 2. Shipwide Election Cycle — LOCK CANDIDATE

General election:
**every 4 civil years**.

Reason:
- familiar/legible,
- enough democratic turnover across a 14-year series,
- not so frequent that government is permanently campaigning.

Ordinary local council elections may also use 4-year terms but can be staggered so every level does not change on one day.
Exact local staggering = Elastic.

---

# 3. 선내의회 Size — BOUNDED CANDIDATE

Recommended total:
**~120–180 seats**, preferred center **~150**.

Why:
- ~300k population means roughly 1 representative per 1,700–2,500 residents at total-seat scale,
- large enough for real committees/coalitions/minor groups,
- small enough to remain institutionally legible.

Preferred working structure at 150 seats:
- ~60 local district seats,
- ~90 compensatory shipwide list seats.

Exact seat number can remain BOUNDED unless an episode needs a vote count.

---

# 4. Two-Vote Mixed-Member System — LOCK CANDIDATE

Each voter normally has:

### Local vote
Elects one representative from a residential civic district.

### Shipwide vote
Chooses a registered party/civic list or eligible independent list.

Overall Assembly composition is made broadly proportional to the shipwide vote through compensatory list seats.

Local seats provide recognizable geographic accountability; they do not grant Habitat sovereignty.

Districting is based primarily on resident population, with independent boundary review.

Hard:
- no `Spine profession constituency`,
- no engineer caste seat,
- no equal 1환/2환/3환 bloc regardless of population.

A small electoral threshold may exist to prevent pathological fragmentation; exact percentage remains Elastic unless a political arc needs it.

---

# 5. Parties / Lists / Independents — LOCK DIRECTION

Meridian can have:
- long-lived parties,
- temporary civic coalitions,
- independent district candidates,
- independent/coalition shipwide lists where legal thresholds are met.

Do not transplant current Earth left/right labels automatically.
Coalitions can form around:
- public services/tax,
- privacy/rights,
- labor/business,
- maintenance/heritage,
- arrival strategy,
without every question aligning on one ideological axis.

No four settlement-path parties are required.

---

# 6. Civic Executive Council — LOCK CANDIDATE

The Assembly selects a **small collective executive council**, preferred **5–7 members**.

Reader-facing prose does not need a flashy formal name beyond `행정부 / 행정협의체 / 의회가 선출한 행정부` unless a recurring official becomes important.

Functions:
- coordinate civil administration,
- prepare/propose budget,
- implement Assembly laws,
- coordinate cross-agency policy,
- appoint/nominate senior administrators subject to statutory review,
- coordinate civic side of major emergencies.

One member may serve as convenor/first coordinator for meetings/public communication, but is **not a president/captain/sole executive sovereign**.

---

# 7. Executive Formation / Removal — LOCK CANDIDATE

Executive is chosen by Assembly majority/coalition after general election.

Removal uses **constructive no-confidence**:
Assembly cannot simply remove the executive and leave a vacuum; it must simultaneously approve a replacement executive/coalition.

Benefits:
- preserves accountability,
- reduces chronic executive collapse,
- fits a closed civilization that values continuity without making government unremovable.

Individual members can resign/be replaced through normal statutory process.

---

# 8. Civil Service / Technical Command Boundary — LOCK

Professional civil servants and technical commanders are not Assembly members by virtue of office.

Executive cannot simply order:
- unsafe pressure operation,
- impossible thermal load,
- unreviewable citizenship deletion.

Technical bodies may certify safety floors/constraints.
Elected bodies make distributive/value decisions among feasible options.

Ship Operations Command remains a professional technical chain for navigation/propulsion/structural/exterior operations, **not the executive government**.

---

# 9. Local Councils — LOCK DIRECTION

Residential local/Habitat/district councils are directly elected locally.

Typical scope:
- local public realm,
- neighborhood services,
- local school/service implementation,
- minor housing/land-use administration,
- cultural/community grants,
- complaints/municipal coordination.

They do not control:
- shipwide citizenship,
- propulsion,
- strategic life-support law,
- independent armed forces,
- Habitat sovereign veto.

Local terms can be ~4 years with staggered schedules; exact election calendar remains Elastic.

---

# 10. Court Architecture — LOCK CANDIDATE

Meridian has at least three functional levels/classes.

## Level 1 — Ordinary first-instance adjudication
Handles ordinary:
- civil/contract/property,
- family/guardianship,
- criminal,
- administrative disputes.

These can be organized in specialized divisions/tribunals without requiring many reader-facing proper nouns.

## Level 2 — General appellate review
Reviews legal/procedural errors and significant fact/application disputes according to domain.

## Level 3 — High civic/rights/constitutional review functions
Handles:
- major civic-rights issues,
- agency overreach,
- constitutional/charter conflicts,
- high-level administrative rights appeals.

**시민항소원** is a specialized high-level civic/administrative rights appeal institution within this top layer, **not the only court in Meridian**.

Constitutional/charter review may be performed by a high-court chamber/panel whose author-facing function is fixed but reader-facing proper name is optional.

---

# 11. Judges — LOCK DIRECTION

Judges are **not directly elected partisan politicians**.

Selected appointment direction:
- professional judicial qualification,
- independent nomination/qualification commission,
- civic confirmation/appointment through defined democratic process,
- long staggered terms,
- removal only for cause/serious misconduct through independent process.

For highest rights/constitutional judges:
preferred term class **~10–14 years, nonrenewable or strongly renewal-limited**.

Exact number of judges and confirmation threshold remain Elastic unless used on-page.

Goal:
independence from one election cycle without creating life-tenure aristocracy.

---

# 12. Prosecutorial / Public-Safety Independence — LOCK DIRECTION

Criminal investigation/public safety operates under statute and judicial review.

The civic executive sets lawful policy/budget but cannot order conviction or dictate an individual judge's verdict.

Sensitive searches/detention require appropriate legal authority.

AI may assist evidence analysis but cannot become prosecutor+judge.

---

# 13. Election Integrity / Campaigning — LOCK DIRECTION

Required functions:
- independent voter/election administration,
- public auditability,
- campaign funding/disclosure rules,
- conflict-of-interest disclosure,
- equal access to public election information,
- plural media environment.

Private wealth may influence campaigning/social reach but cannot buy extra votes.

Exact campaign-spending caps and public-finance formulas remain Elastic.

---

# 14. Emergency + Election Rule — LOCK CANDIDATE

A genuine hazard may justify a **narrow temporary delay** of voting in affected districts or shipwide only if safe voting/communications are physically impossible.

Any delay must be:
- publicly justified,
- narrowly scoped,
- time-limited,
- reviewable,
- rescheduled as soon as practicable.

Author-facing hard ceiling:
**ordinary emergency powers cannot postpone a general election indefinitely; any delay beyond roughly 90 days requires extraordinary constitutional/court + legislative reauthorization and remains subject to recurring review.**

This prevents `emergency = permanent government` drift.

Exact disaster-specific mechanics can vary.

---

# 15. Vacancy / Continuity Rule — LOCK DIRECTION

Death/incapacity/Resconstruction of an elected official does not freeze government.

Local district vacancy:
- temporary acting/seat procedure + by-election or statutory replacement within bounded period.

List-seat vacancy:
- normally filled from the same registered list/succession rule.

Executive incapacity:
- collective council continues; Assembly replaces the member.

Reconstruction does not automatically restore an office whose lawful term/vacancy process has already moved on.

This matches general property/social finality principles.

---

# 16. Arrival-Era Representation — LOCK DIRECTION

Early off-Meridian citizens vote in the shared shipwide civic system while temporary local resident/worker councils grow around real settlement populations.

As permanent municipal institutions mature, local electoral structures can be created under transition law.

First settlers do not gain:
- extra shipwide vote weight,
- founder veto,
- hereditary office.

Amara has no Natural-Origin seat or veto.

# 17. Ordinary Election-Day Test

A normal adult can:
1. verify civic registration,
2. receive neutral election information,
3. vote for a local district candidate,
4. vote for a shipwide list,
5. follow several media/creator analyses,
6. see audited results,
7. experience coalition formation afterward,
without knowing every technical committee or encountering Ship Operations Command.

Result:
`PASS`.

# 19. Criminal Appeal Test

A citizen is convicted of serious data sabotage and argues illegal protected-record search.

Path:
- first-instance criminal adjudication,
- ordinary appellate review,
- high rights/administrative review if constitutional privacy issue arises,
- 시민항소원 may become relevant where state/agency civic-right action is challenged, depending procedural route.

No new court principle required.

Result:
`PASS`.

# 20. Closed-Habitat Hostile Questions

### Why not equal Habitat representation?
Habitats are municipal/geographic communities, not sovereign states. Equal bloc representation would harden the exact Habitat tribalism the project forbids.

### Why not direct president?
The story/world already prefers distributed accountability and no symbolic captain-ruler. Collective parliamentary executive fits better.

### Why not pure technocracy?
Technical certification limits the feasible set; elected institutions choose values/priorities inside it.

### Why not pure direct democracy?
300k citizens can use referenda/panels occasionally, but routine complex governance still needs accountable representative institutions/professional administration.

### Why not sortition everywhere?
Useful as consultative supplement, not a stable executive/judicial core.
