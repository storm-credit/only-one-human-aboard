Goal:
Use one clear civil-majority threshold while preserving graduated youth autonomy instead of inventing a separate age for every decision.

---

# 1. Selected Default

**Civil majority = 18 years.**

At 18, an ordinary citizen normally gains full independent civil capacity for:
- voting/civic participation where otherwise eligible,
- ordinary contracts,
- independent residence/tenure decisions,
- ordinary employment agreements,
- adult medical consent,
- partnership/marriage,
- independent privacy/account administration,
- destination-path/residence filings.

Professional licenses, hazardous-work credentials and public offices can still require separate competence/training/age conditions.

---

# 2. Why 18

Advantages:
- familiar to readers,
- avoids needless SF-age jargon,
- fits Noah/Kai/Mina M1 age progression,
- supports Act6's transition from dependent preference to adult filing,
- keeps the setting focused on human/social consequences rather than exotic age law.

There is no story benefit in moving majority to 16, 21 or a complex biological maturity score.

---

# 3. Graduated Autonomy Before 18

Under 18 does NOT mean no rights/voice.

Youth autonomy grows through:
- maturity,
- decision complexity,
- risk,
- privacy interest,
- existing caregiving context.

Relevant domains:
- daily routine,
- school/activities,
- communications/privacy,
- ordinary medical assent/participation,
- residence preference,
- education/training path,
- future-location preference.

A youth's preference may be highly influential without being legally dispositive in every domain.

---

# 4. Paid Work / Apprenticeship

Selected simple threshold:
**structured paid apprenticeship / limited ordinary paid work can begin around 16**, with safeguards.

Before 16:
- school projects,
- supervised service,
- creative/community work,
- limited age-appropriate paid activity may exist under narrower rules,
but should not be treated as independent adult labor.

16–17 safeguards include:
- education continuity,
- hour limits,
- hazardous-work restrictions,
- guardian/youth-service oversight where appropriate,
- direct pay/property rights appropriate to youth status.

Hazardous professional credentials may require 18+ and additional training.

---

# 5. Medical Decision Rule

Do NOT create a single blanket `medical consent age` below 18.

Before majority:
- ordinary care increasingly includes the young person's assent and privacy,
- sufficiently mature minors can have substantial decision authority in low/moderate-risk personal care under law,
- emergency life-saving care and high-risk irreversible decisions use stronger guardian/clinical/review safeguards,
- capacity is decision-specific.

At 18:
full adult medical consent becomes the normal baseline.

This avoids turning every youth medicine scene into an age-threshold puzzle.

---

# 6. Privacy Rule

Youth records are not automatically transparent to guardians in every detail.

As maturity increases:
- personal communications/privacy expand,
- sensitive medical/counseling information gains stronger confidentiality,
- safety/dependency exceptions remain bounded.

No universal AI parental dashboard exposes a teenager's complete life.

---

# 7. Residence / Guardianship

Before 18:
- legal guardianship/dependency remains real,
- youth residence preference receives increasing weight,
- a 12-year-old cannot simply declare an adult residence contract,
- a 16–17-year-old's sustained preference can be very important in family adjudication.

At 18:
ordinary independent residence decisions no longer require guardian authorization.

---

# 8. Destination / Arrival Path

Before majority:
family/dependent planning may record:
- current preference,
- `undecided`,
- review date,
without permanently binding a child to one future settlement path.

At 18:
the citizen files their own residence/deployment preference, subject to ordinary capacity/credential rules.

This supports Mina's Act6 arc and Noah's/Kai's adult choices.

---

# 10. Marriage / Partnership

Normal independent civil partnership/marriage:
**18+**.

No special earlier marriage exception is needed for this series.
Keeping one clear threshold avoids unnecessary legal clutter.

---

# 11. Voting / Office

Ordinary voting eligibility begins at civil majority:
**18**.

Specific public offices may require:
- professional experience,
- conflict-of-interest rules,
- potentially a higher minimum age only if later governance design truly needs it.

Default:
do not invent higher office ages unless plot-relevant.

---

# 12. Criminal Responsibility

Do NOT map `civil majority = all criminal rules begin at 18` mechanically.

Youth misconduct uses age/maturity-sensitive juvenile/public-safety/family/education processes.
Serious harm can receive serious intervention without treating a 14-year-old identically to a 40-year-old.

Exact juvenile sentencing bands remain procedural C2 unless plot-required.

---

# 13. Hard Stops

- AI maturity score automatically grants adulthood,
- Seed status changes age of majority,
- Reconstruction history resets legal age,
- Natural-Origin citizens mature legally differently,
- child preference = absolute at all ages,
- guardians own all youth private data,
- 12-year-olds routinely sign adult housing contracts,
- every domain has a different arbitrary age threshold.

---

# 14. Verdict

Q5 blocking P0: **0**.

Provisional v2 rule:
- **civil majority = 18**
- structured paid apprenticeship/limited employment generally **16+** with youth safeguards
- below 18 autonomy is graduated/decision-specific
- adult medical consent / independent residence / voting / partnership baseline at 18
- no separate Origin/Reconstruction adulthood rules.
