# 1. Selected Physical Model — PLAN-H1Q

**Temperate water-rich rocky world + near-Earth total pressure + strongly oxygen-poor air + mild Earthlike gravity + moderate seasons.**

Narrative experience:
- people can stand outdoors without a pressure suit,
- wind/rain/temperature can be felt directly by exposed skin,
- routine outdoor activity requires a breathing mask/hood or oxygen-enriched local air,
- losing breathing support is a serious rescue problem but not an explosive decompression event,
- the world is clearly not Earth 2.0.

# 6. Oxygen Class — Selected Compromise

Selected ambient O2 volume fraction:
**~8–10% class**
under ~0.88–0.98 bar total pressure.

Ambient O2 partial pressure order:
**~7–9.5 kPa**.

This is far below Earth's ~21 kPa sea-level oxygen partial pressure.

Human consequence:
- normal unassisted outdoor work is unsafe,
- cognition/endurance degrade rapidly without supplemental oxygen,
- healthy adults may survive a short accidental exposure rather than instantly die,
- exertion greatly shortens useful time,
- children, elders, cardiac/pulmonary patients and injured people have smaller margins,
- routine field clothing therefore includes reliable breathing support.

Reader-facing rule:
**`마스크를 잃으면 죽는 진공`은 아니지만 `마스크 없이 그냥 살아도 되는 공기`도 아니다.**

Exact emergency impairment minutes are NOT fixed; physiology varies and rescue scenes should use medical QA if needed.

# 7. Breathing Equipment Architecture

Routine surface outdoor wear:
- lightweight sealed or semi-sealed breathing mask/hood,
- compact oxygen-enrichment/rebreather unit,
- CO2/moisture management,
- environmental/pressure/oxygen sensor,
- emergency reserve.

Normal conditions do NOT require:
- rigid pressure suit,
- full EVA suit,
- vacuum gloves,
- pressure airlock for every ordinary building door.

Buildings/vehicles generally maintain Earth-compatible breathable indoor gas mixtures.

Transitional entry:
`dust/biosecurity/weather cleanup + mask interface + ordinary pressure-compatible door/vestibule`
rather than spacecraft-style decompression cycling every time.

# 8. Bulk Atmospheric Composition Direction

Do not over-freeze trace chemistry.

Author-facing bulk direction:
- nitrogen dominant,
- substantial inert gas fraction possible (argon-class),
- O2 ~8–10%,
- CO2 **sub-percent and below acute human-toxicity concern under routine outdoor exposure**, while still potentially climatically important,
- water vapor varies strongly with climate/weather,
- trace gases remain mission-survey detail.

Exact percentages other than O2 class remain C2 until science/story need.

Hard:
Do not use toxic CO2 to create a second mandatory full-body environment suit after already choosing oxygen-poor air.

# 9. Why Oxygen Exists — Scope Guardrail

A significant O2 fraction can become a biosignature question.

Series-safe direction:
Mission-era remote/probe data may support several non-exclusive contributors:
- photochemical/geochemical oxygen production,
- long-lived simple microbial-scale biosphere possibility,
- mixed abiotic/biotic interpretation.

Hard:
- no confirmed intelligent/complex indigenous civilization,
- no ruins,
- no alien ecology takeover,
- no late `the oxygen proves aliens` master twist.

The series does NOT need to resolve the planet's deep oxygen history completely.
Biosecurity treats uncertainty seriously.

# 13. Fire / Combustion Consequence

Low ambient oxygen makes open combustion substantially harder/slower than Earth normal air.

Consequences:
- surface outdoor wildfire risk from Earthlike vegetation is not assumed,
- combustion engines are not naturally advantaged,
- industrial processes use controlled oxidizer/oxygen environments,
- indoor human habitats use oxygen-rich breathable mixtures and therefore retain ordinary fire-safety concerns.

This creates a useful contrast:
outdoor low-O2 environment is not breathable but can be less fire-supporting.

---

# 14. Agriculture

Initial agriculture does NOT simply plant crops in open soil.

Reasons:
- low O2 atmosphere,
- unknown soil chemistry/ecology,
- climate uncertainty,
- biosecurity,
- crop atmospheric requirements.

Early food production uses:
- controlled greenhouses,
- sealed/semi-sealed agricultural halls,
- treated local water/bulk mineral input,
- Meridian seed/genetic/agricultural knowledge.

Open-field agriculture is a later research outcome, not first-year default.

---

# 15. Buildings

Because exterior pressure is near Earthlike:
settlement buildings are not vacuum vessels by necessity.

But they still need:
- breathable-air containment,
- oxygen-loss monitoring,
- weather/temperature control,
- contamination/biosecurity interfaces,
- smoke/fire compartmentation,
- medical/rescue air reserves.

Pressure differential between inside and outside is modest compared with spacecraft habitats.
This significantly reduces long-term surface construction burden versus Mars-like vacuum/low-pressure settlement.

# 17. Hostile Checks

## Is ~8–10% O2 too breathable?
It is not safe normal-work air at ~0.93 bar. Supplemental oxygen remains mandatory by operational policy.

## Is it instantly fatal if mask fails?
No by design; the exact impairment curve varies. This gives rescue-story margin without making respirators cosmetic.

## Does near-Earth pressure make the planet too easy?
No. Low oxygen, climate, soil/biology uncertainty, industrial immaturity and rescue/logistics remain real constraints while eliminating arbitrary pressure-suit misery.

## Does significant O2 force intelligent aliens?
No. It raises a biosignature/geochemistry question, not a requirement for complex life. The series keeps this secondary and unresolved unless explicitly reopened.

## Is ~0.52 AU plausible around the real star anchor?
Yes at the selected ~0.75 M☉ / ~0.24 L☉ class: year ~158 d and flux ~0.89 Earth.
