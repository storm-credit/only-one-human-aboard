# V3 VP-M1 READY-TO-RUN PROMPT CARDS v0.1

Status: `TOOL-AGNOSTIC READY PROMPTS / NOT CANON / NO PROSE / ART NOT FROZEN`
Date: 2026-08-20
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

## 사용법

- 아래 프롬프트는 어떤 이미지 도구에도 기본 입력으로 사용할 수 있는 **tool-agnostic master cards**다.
- 특정 도구의 파라미터/문법은 나중에 adapter로 붙인다.
- 한 번에 13개를 생성하지 않는다.
- 현재 질문을 해결하는 최소 묶음만 실행한다.

공통 스타일:

```text
2D semi-real animation / game-concept illustration,
clean controlled line art,
moderate realistic anatomy and material detail,
not photorealistic,
production concept-art presentation,
clear silhouette readability,
functional industrial/civic design,
subtle future technology integrated into lived everyday use,
light neutral background for object/character QA,
no franchise logos,
no real-company branding,
no dramatic cinematic effects.
```

공통 금지:

```text
photoreal portrait,
hyper-detailed skin pores,
over-aged face,
all characters sharing the same handsome face,
combat armor,
weapon-centric design,
special-forces styling,
humanoid battle mecha,
fighter-jet styling for utility machines,
neon cyberpunk default,
sterile white sci-fi everywhere,
dark metal corridor everywhere,
RPG rarity glow,
faction-color coding,
text-heavy fake infographic,
unreadable AI gibberish labels.
```

# C01 — PROTAGONIST / NEUTRAL IDENTITY QA

```text
Create a neutral 2D semi-real character identity sheet for the male protagonist of a long-form generation-ship mystery/survival SF story.

He is a 37–39-year-old civilian hull/ship damage analyst, an active field professional, not military.

IDENTITY LOCK:
- lean practical adult build, slightly long torso and arms, moderate shoulders, no gym bulk
- long-medium face, moderate width, clearly not broad
- firm tapered jaw, not square
- low-to-moderate cheek projection
- medium-long nose with a very subtle off-center bridge
- slightly deep-set attentive eyes, medium spacing
- straight brows with mild downward outer angle
- medium-width mouth with slight natural asymmetry
- mild under-eye fatigue and fine adult lines only; do not age him beyond late 30s
- clean-shaven or nearly clean-shaven
- short-to-medium practical dark hair, not idol styling
- calm, observant, skeptical, methodical expression
- standing posture relaxed; surface-inspection posture can lean forward, but neutral sheet remains upright

STYLE CALIBRATION:
clean long-form protagonist readability + adult field-professional solidity.
Attractive but grounded, not idol-glamorous, not photoreal.
Do not copy a real actor or a previous sample face.
Ethnicity/nationality is intentionally not fixed; do not default him to a specific nationality.

PRESENTATION:
- plain light background
- flat honest lighting
- front neutral head
- 3/4 head
- side profile
- simple full-body in neutral fitted gray work layer
- no signature props or color during this confusion test

HARD AVOID:
apparent age 45+,
photoreal actor portrait,
heavy beard,
square-jawed action hero,
special-forces harness,
hero armor,
exact celebrity likeness.
```
