# DESIGN A — UNITARY PARLIAMENTARY CIVIC REPUBLIC

## Core
A shipwide elected legislature is the primary democratic authority.
The legislature selects a small executive cabinet/council.
Habitats have elected local councils for municipal services but no separate sovereignty.
Professional agencies execute policy.
Courts review law and administration.

## Structure
- **선내의회**: elected shipwide legislature
- **Executive Council**: small cabinet chosen by/answerable to the Assembly
- **1/2/3환 Local Councils**: housing, schools, local public space, neighborhood services
- **Professional Agencies**: utilities, health, transport, archives, Continuity, etc.
- **Courts**: trial/administrative levels + 시민항소원 high appeal function
- **Independent Audit/Privacy/Ethics functions**

## Safety Operations
Navigation, propulsion and life-support have professional operational command chains.
Technical commanders can issue narrow immediate safety orders, but are not heads of state.

## Advantages
- immediately understandable
- clear electoral legitimacy
- easy accountability
- fits existing 선내의회
- strong separation between politics and professional operations

## Weaknesses
- risks feeling like 21st-century parliamentary state copied into space
- shipwide legislature may centralize too much
- local Habitat identity can feel administratively thin

## Biggest Trap
`Earth government with SF wallpaper`.

## Story Compatibility
High.
Maren/Tomas remain civil service; Raul works through courts/advocacy; arrival transition can be handled by statutory committee.

# DESIGN B — POLYCENTRIC COMMONS GOVERNANCE

## Core
No single executive cabinet dominates.
Authority is distributed among:
- elected civic assembly,
- Habitat/local councils,
- infrastructure commons boards,
- independent rights/court bodies,
- temporary cross-domain coordination councils.

The system evolved because air/water/heat/housing/industrial capacity cannot be treated as ordinary ministries alone.

## Structure
- **선내의회**: rights, taxation, broad policy, budget ceilings
- **Habitat Councils**: local civic life
- **Commons Boards**: water/air/heat/food/housing-capacity/major industrial assets
- **Judicial/Appeal bodies**
- **Coordination Secretariat** rather than strong executive

## Advantages
- strongly fits closed-loop civilization
- reflects Layered Commons + Market City
- supports ending's polycentric politics
- avoids one leader choosing civilization future

## Weaknesses
- accountability can become opaque
- too many boards can create committee-fiction
- ordinary citizens may not know who is responsible
- entrenched technical professions can become de facto oligarchy

## Biggest Trap
`Bureaucratic polycracy where everyone has partial authority and nobody is accountable`.

## Story Compatibility
Very high thematically, but dangerous for readability.

# DESIGN C — DUAL CIVIC / MISSION MANDATE

## Core
Two constitutional authority tracks coexist:
1. Civic government for citizens/rights/economy/family/local life
2. Mission Operations Directorate for navigation, propulsion, structural safety, life-support emergency authority

Mission authority is inherited from voyage survival law but progressively narrowed by civic law.

## Structure
- elected **선내의회** + civic executive
- professional **Mission Operations Directorate**
- Charter limits mission authority to defined operational domains
- courts arbitrate boundary conflicts
- Habitat councils remain local

## Advantages
- strong spaceship plausibility
- explains why technical command remains robust after centuries
- creates natural historical layers between 개척헌장 and 시민기본법
- useful for Final Approach transition

## Weaknesses
- easily becomes `civilian vs captain/engineers` cliché
- mission bureaucracy may accidentally become hidden sovereign
- can overpower the personhood/family story with constitutional conflict

## Biggest Trap
`The captain was secretly president all along`.

## Story Compatibility
Medium-high if mission authority is strictly narrow.

---

# DESIGN D — DELIBERATIVE / SORTITION CIVIC FEDERATION

## Core
Elected institutions are supplemented by randomly selected citizen panels for high-impact commons allocation, constitutional review and long-horizon planning.
Habitats have meaningful local governments.

## Structure
- elected 선내의회
- Habitat councils
- citizen juries/panels selected by lot for certain major reviews
- executive administrators
- courts
- technical agencies

## Advantages
- closed 300k society can plausibly support high civic participation
- weakens permanent political class
- helps legitimacy for birth capacity / ship dismantling / arrival choices
- reduces simple left/right party transplant

## Weaknesses
- procedure-heavy
- citizen panels can look like author's ideal-democracy device
- random selection can produce competence/legitimacy questions
- frequent panels could make plot feel like civic workshop fiction

## Biggest Trap
`utopian governance gimmick that removes ordinary political self-interest`.

## Story Compatibility
Medium.
Useful as a limited mechanism, not whole system.

# RECOMMENDED HYBRID — GOV-H1

# `Layered Civic Republic + Commons Constraints + Narrow Safety Command`

Status:
`PROVISIONAL PRIORITY / NOT CANON`

## Principle
Political value choices are democratically accountable.
Physical safety constraints are professionally managed.
Commons limits are real but do not become sovereign technocracy.
No captain or AI is head of the civilization.

# GOV-H1 — TOP-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE

## 1. 선내의회 — Shipwide Legislature
Primary democratic authority for:
- laws
- taxation/public revenue framework
- broad budget
- rights legislation
- major strategic asset rules
- creation/sunset of temporary transition bodies
- oversight hearings

Election model candidate:
- shipwide proportional representation with geographic balancing/constituency component
- exact seat count remains ELASTIC for later Society Bible consolidation

Hard rule:
No Habitat has sovereign veto over shipwide life-support or rights law.

## 2. Civic Executive Council — Small Accountable Executive
A small multi-member executive chosen through the Assembly and removable by it.

Functions:
- coordinate ministries/agencies
- propose budget
- implement Assembly law
- appoint senior administrators subject to review
- coordinate emergencies at civic level

No singular President/Captain figure is required.
Exact title/name may remain low-salience.

## 3. Habitat / District Councils
1환 / 2환 / 3환 local councils handle:
- local streets/public space
- neighborhood services
- local school/service implementation
- minor housing/land-use decisions
- local cultural/community grants
- local complaints

They cannot:
- define citizenship
- command propulsion/life support
- block shipwide emergency safety rules indefinitely

Goal:
Habitats feel politically lived-in without becoming ethnic/faction states.

## 4. Professional Civil Service
Agencies operate under statute/budget/oversight.

Examples:
- Continuity Service / 연속성심사국
- health systems
- transport/logistics
- archives/records
- housing administration
- utilities/food/ecology
- public safety

Civil servants are not direct political representatives.

Maren/Tomas fit here.

## 5. Infrastructure Commons Authorities
Critical physical commons have dedicated professional boards/authorities for:
- air/water
- heat rejection
- food/ecology
- housing-capacity certification
- major industrial reserve
- strategic energy/thermal safety

They may certify:
- physical safe minimums
- maintenance floors
- engineering constraints

They may NOT decide alone:
- which social group deserves resources
- citizenship/personhood
- distributive priorities above their statutory safety floor

Political bodies choose among feasible options after physical constraints are published.

This preserves `Layered Commons` without technocratic sovereignty.

## 6. Ship Operations Command — Narrow Technical Command
Navigation/propulsion/structural/exterior operations use a professional command chain.

The senior operations officer is **not** head of government.

During defined technical incidents they may order:
- compartment isolation
- evacuation
- access closure
- propulsion/thermal operational changes
- temporary movement restrictions in hazard zones

They may NOT:
- suspend citizenship
- detain political opponents
- rewrite civil law
- seize permanent governing power

## 7. Courts / Review
At minimum, the final Society Bible should distinguish:
- ordinary civil/family/contract courts or tribunals
- criminal justice courts
- administrative review
- **시민항소원** as a high-level civic/administrative rights appeal body
- constitutional/charter review function, whether a separate chamber or high-court panel

Do not make 시민항소원 the only court in civilization.

Raul operates as advocate/lawyer through these systems.

## 8. Independent Oversight Functions
Functions required; flashy names optional:
- public audit
- privacy/data protection
- ethics/conflict-of-interest review
- ombuds/administrative complaint
- election integrity

These should often be offices/functions rather than five new memorable institutions.

## 9. Budget / Capacity Boundary
Political budget authority belongs to elected government.

But money cannot override certified hard capacity floors.

Example:
- Assembly can fund more Reconstruction capacity.
- It cannot declare heat rejection or hospital throughput infinite.
- Commons/technical authorities publish feasible ranges.
- elected bodies choose priorities inside those ranges.

This is the governmental expression of `Layered Commons + Market City`.

## 10. Emergency Power — EP-GOV Candidate
Emergency authority should be:
- domain-specific
- threshold-triggered
- time-limited
- logged
- reviewable afterward
- automatically narrowed/expired when the incident ends

No general indefinite `state of emergency` should be easy.

Immediate technical safety orders can precede judicial review, but long rights restrictions require civic/legal authorization.

## 11. AI / Automation Governance Boundary
Until Package C fully defines AI ontology:
- scheduling/prediction/diagnostic systems may recommend and automatically execute narrow pre-authorized safety actions
- no administrative AI is a sovereign officeholder
- coercive civic decisions require an accountable human office/process
- algorithmic decisions affecting rights require audit/appeal pathways

This prevents `the ship AI secretly governs everyone` drift.

## 12. 도착전환위원회
Treat as a statutory temporary cross-domain body, not a new government.

Candidate composition:
- appointed/confirmed senior civil administrators
- relevant technical/arrival experts
- rights/legal representatives
- Habitat/local implementation delegates
- affected-service representation where appropriate

Authority:
- coordinate transition implementation
- publish readiness/translation plans
- propose regulations within delegated scope

Cannot:
- replace 선내의회
- erase 시민기본법
- appoint Amara as legitimacy key

Sunset:
- mandatory review after system insertion
- functions transferred to permanent ship/orbit/surface institutions or ended

# ORDINARY CITIZEN EXPERIENCE

A normal citizen should experience government mostly through:
- local service office / digital civic portal
- school/housing/transport/health systems
- taxes/fees/payroll rules
- local council decisions
- occasional elections
- appeals/complaints when harmed

They should NOT routinely interact with:
- shipwide executive
- Assembly leadership
- propulsion command
- high court

This keeps the government large enough to be real but small enough in reader-facing jargon.

# 14-YEAR STORY EVOLUTION

Early Acts:
- ordinary civic administration dominates
- arrival bodies still advisory/planning-heavy

Middle:
- Count audit tests privacy/oversight architecture
- capacity/resource conflicts test Commons transparency
- Maren/Raul cases reveal appeal/review boundaries

Late:
- Final Approach activates delegated transition powers
- Habitat/local government deals with relocation/service consequences
- shipwide government handles asset strategy
- operations command handles physical insertion
- courts/appeal bodies protect rights continuity

Post-insertion:
- temporary arrival bodies begin sunset/transfer rather than becoming permanent super-government

# RED TEAM

## RT-1 — Why no Captain dictatorship?
Because voyage technical command and civic sovereignty diverged over centuries. A propulsion/navigation commander retains operational authority only within defined technical domains.

Status: PASS candidate.

## RT-2 — Why can politicians not simply order unsafe resource use?
Critical commons authorities publish certified safety floors; elected bodies choose priorities inside feasible ranges. Technical boards cannot choose distributive morality above those floors.

Status: PASS candidate.

## RT-3 — Why don't engineers become unelected rulers?
Their binding authority is limited to domain safety constraints and immediate incident command. Budgets, rights and distributive policy remain politically/legal accountable.

Status: CONDITIONAL PASS — oversight/capture rules need Society Bible consolidation.

## RT-4 — Does this create too many institutions for readers?
Potentially. Reader-facing recurring names should remain limited to already important bodies plus maybe 1–2 new names. Most functions stay descriptive.

Status: PASS with naming guardrail.

## RT-5 — Why haven't Habitat councils become separate states after centuries?
Life-support, transport, civil rights and major industry remain tightly ship-integrated; local councils have meaningful municipal power but no independent survival infrastructure or citizenship.

Status: PASS candidate.

## RT-6 — Can emergency power become authoritarian loophole?
Only if rules are vague. v2 must require domain trigger, time limit, public log, review and automatic expiry.

Status: CONDITIONAL PASS — exact emergency framework must be specified in Infrastructure/Security packages.

## RT-7 — Does Raul automatically dominate politics because rights law matters?
No. Raul is an advocate/lawyer, not elected government leader. Broader implementation belongs to institutions and ordinary claimants.

Status: PASS.

## RT-8 — Does Maren become high-level state official by proximity to plot?
No. Continuity Service remains one civil administrative domain. Maren's jurisdiction is deliberately narrow.

Status: PASS.

## RT-9 — Does the transition committee become a shadow government?
Guardrails: statutory delegation, published scope, legislative budget, judicial review, sunset/transfer after insertion.

Status: PASS candidate.

## RT-10 — Does algorithmic optimization erase politics?
No. Automation may model feasible sets or execute narrow safety automations, but contested rights/resource value choices remain accountable human/institutional decisions.

Status: CONDITIONAL PASS pending Package C AI/automation ontology.
