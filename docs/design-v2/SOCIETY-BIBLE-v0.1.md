# 1. Social Constitution In One Page

Meridian is a democratic civic city-civilization constrained by physical commons rather than ruled by them.

Citizens:
- possess equal civic personhood regardless of Seeded/Natural origin,
- have a guaranteed survival/basic-service floor,
- use money and markets for ordinary differentiated life,
- may own personal/business/residential interests but not sovereign life-support structure,
- choose careers rather than receive lifetime assignments,
- form diverse ordinary families under protected parent/child/dependent rights,
- receive common public education and healthcare,
- are subject to real criminal law/public-safety powers with due process,
- age, become disabled, retire and die despite advanced medicine,
- retain privacy against automatic conversion of engineering/medical data into state omniscience.

The civilization is neither utopia nor dystopia:
its institutions can be delayed, biased, captured, expensive or wrong, but their formal architecture protects rights and review.

# 2. Authority Architecture

## Civic value decisions
Elected institutions decide:
- law
- budgets
- distributive priorities
- rights
- ordinary public policy

## Technical safety constraints
Professional/technical bodies can certify:
- minimum safe maintenance
- environmental limits
- immediate hazard conditions

They cannot convert expertise into general sovereignty.

## Emergency command
Narrow technical incident authority may temporarily control hazard zones/actions.
Persistent rights restrictions require civic/legal basis and later review.

## Courts/appeals/oversight
Separate adjudication/appeal/oversight protects against:
- unlawful detention/search
- family conflicts of interest
- allocation abuse
- public-official misconduct
- property acquisition abuse

No single official is final truth keeper.

# 3. Civic Floor vs Market Layer

## Civic floor
Guaranteed baseline:
- breathable environment
- water
- basic food
- minimum safe housing
- basic education
- primary/basic/emergency healthcare
- basic mental-health crisis access
- minimum civic/digital access
- basic old-age/disability/unemployment support

## Market layer
Money meaningfully differentiates:
- housing size/location/privacy
- dining/hospitality
- leisure/culture
- custom goods
- premium services
- business ownership
- collections/heritage goods
- schedule flexibility
- investment/savings

## Hard-capacity commons
Not legal auction goods:
- birth capacity
- high-load Reconstruction
- rare emergency clinical capacity
- strategic infrastructure reserves
- habitat expansion/survival system control

Wealth can influence society; it cannot legally purchase personhood or override physical safety allocation.

# 4. Money / Tax / Business

One primary shipwide monetary system supports:
- wages
- prices
- rent
- savings
- lending
- business
- taxation

Enterprise forms are plural:
- private small business
- investor-owned firms
- cooperatives
- public/common enterprises
- nonprofit/association entities

Business failure and bankruptcy are real.
Civic survival floor remains protected from debt seizure.

Public revenue supports the floor and infrastructure through progressive/ordinary taxation and common-asset returns.

# 5. Class / Inequality

Meridian has real inequality but no formal class caste.

Class appears through:
- income
- wealth
- housing
- commute
- schedule control
- savings/debt
- professional prestige
- business ownership
- social networks
- leisure
- private enrichment
- heritage/cultural capital

No Habitat maps to one class.

Intergenerational advantage exists through wealth/network/culture, but professional licenses, public office and civic hard-capacity priority are not inherited.

# 6. Housing / Property

Three layers:

## Structural Commons
Hull, pressure structure, life-support backbone, major emergency/rotation/public systems.

## Residential Tenure Interest
Transferable occupancy/use interests, rentals, cooperative shares, institutional/temporary housing.

## Private Interior Property
Personal goods, furnishings, safe customization, heirlooms.

Citizens can have meaningful housing wealth without owning the ship's survival structure.

Civic minimum housing means homelessness from absolute lack of shelter is rare; losing preferred housing remains a severe economic/social consequence.

---

# 7. Reconstruction + Property Finality

A prolonged severe recovery case can move through:
1. acute/recovery pending,
2. long functional absence with temporary asset management,
3. legal finality if continuation will not proceed.

Personhood continuity does not automatically reverse every lawful third-party property change.

Returning citizens can regain what remains legally theirs while current lawful occupancy/business reliance is separately protected/adjudicated.

EP001 is the model principle.

---

# 8. Work / Labor

Normal occupational choice is protected.

Civilization steers needed skills through:
- scholarships
- apprenticeship
- compensation
- training capacity
- recruitment
- relocation support

not permanent forced job assignment.

Workers may organize and strike.
Critical life-safety sectors can have narrow minimum staffing/emergency rules.

Hazardous work receives stronger pay/leave/disability/pension protection.

Automation changes employment but does not assign citizens' lives.

---

# 9. Family / Partnership / Parenthood

Marriage/partnership, parenthood, guardianship, residence and property are separate legal dimensions.

- gender-neutral civil partnership/marriage
- sole parenthood lawful
- donor conception normal
- genetic donor ≠ automatic parent
- adoption exists
- guardianship can be temporary/limited
- divorce/separation is available without fault proof
- parent support follows child welfare and means

Reconstructed parent continuity does not automatically restore every current caregiving function.
External review can stage return based on function, child welfare, existing caregiver history and youth voice.

Maren remains recused from own family adjudication.

---

# 10. Childhood / Youth Rights

Children are full persons with developing autonomy.

They gain increasing voice in:
- daily routine
- activities
- education
- residence preference
- work/training
- privacy

One general civil-majority threshold will later be locked, with only a small number of earlier domain thresholds.

Youth preference matters without becoming absolute at 12.

# 11. Education

Common public foundation teaches:
- literacy/language
- math/science
- history/civics
- arts
- physical education
- media/digital literacy
- practical closed-system safety/ecology

Later adolescence expands choice:
- academic
- technical
- creative
- apprenticeship
- project/service pathways

Higher education is a network of institutes/professional schools/research/training centers rather than one required university track.

Adult retraining is normal over a long healthspan.

No AI aptitude score may bind a child's future.

# 12. Healthcare

Universal clinical floor provides:
- primary care
- emergency care
- essential medicine
- reproductive/prenatal care
- mental-health basic/crisis care
- essential rehabilitation

Advanced biological repair improves:
- organ/tissue treatment
- cancer control
- trauma recovery
- healthspan

but does not eliminate:
- chronic disease
- disability
- mental illness
- severe trauma
- aging
- death.

Reconstruction remains separate high-load extreme intervention.

---

# 13. Mental Health / Capacity / Disability

Mental illness is healthcare, not automatic criminality.

Short involuntary crisis care requires:
- concrete acute danger/severe incapacity
- least-restrictive option
- rapid review

Capacity is decision-specific.
Supported decision-making is preferred where feasible.

Disability support includes:
- accessibility
- assistive technology
- adapted education/work/housing
- income support

Support cannot be conditioned on accepting invasive elective repair.

---

# 14. Crime / Public Safety

Most crime is recognizably human:
- fraud/theft
- assault
- domestic/sexual violence
- stalking/harassment
- corruption
- data/privacy/credential crime
- dangerous negligence

Serious violence and homicide exist but are not dominant setting texture.

Local public-safety teams handle ordinary incidents.
Shipwide specialist investigation handles serious/cross-Habitat/cyber/critical cases.

Restitution/repair/treatment are preferred for suitable lower-harm cases.
Serious danger can justify secure custody.

No death penalty.
No unsafe-space exile.
No victim forced into restorative mediation.

---

# 15. Surveillance / Data

Engineering telemetry is not general personal surveillance.

Protected domains:
- medical
- mental health
- reproductive/Seed provenance
- Continuity/Reconstruction
- private communications
- home life

Sensitive searches require legal basis.
Emergency access is narrow/logged/reviewed.

AI can flag technical anomalies but cannot declare guilt, origin or binding dangerousness.

---

# 16. Weapons / Dangerous Tools

Purpose-built high-penetration/high-energy interpersonal weapons are strongly restricted because of closed-habitat externality.

Routine public safety emphasizes:
- de-escalation
- evacuation
- barriers
- access control
- restraint
- controlled low-penetration incapacitation

Exceptional higher-force response exists under strict storage/authorization/review.

Industrial tools remain realistically dangerous and professionally accessible rather than magically impossible to misuse.

---

# 17. Death / Legal Finality

Distinguish:
1. clinical death,
2. viable Reconstruction review where truly relevant,
3. legal finality,
4. mourning/body/estate consequences.

Most deaths do not enter prolonged Reconstruction limbo.
Old scans alone cannot preserve legal life indefinitely.

Palliative care and end-of-life planning are normal.
A tightly regulated medical aid-in-dying pathway remains provisional/low-salience pending final review.

---

# 18. Funeral / Memorial / Material Return

Large permanent cemetery burial aboard ship is impractical.

Safe plural practices may include:
- ceremonial viewing
- cremation/thermal reduction
- controlled biological/chemical reduction
- retained small memorial material
- family/religious/secular ceremonies

Bodies are not casually treated as anonymous feedstock.
Ritual/legal separation precedes material return.

Recordings and memorial media remain records, not executable continuation of the dead person.

---

# 19. Inheritance / Heirlooms

Inheritance may transfer:
- money
- business interests
- eligible housing interests
- private objects
- heirlooms
- IP

It may not transfer:
- public office
- professional credentials
- security clearance
- civic hard-capacity priority
- ship survival infrastructure

A reconstructed/returning citizen does not automatically repossess every heirloom already lawfully transferred.

# 20. Whole-Life Social Flow

## Birth
Parents choose family form; birth capacity is civic allocation rather than market purchase; prenatal care and Seed rules apply.

## Early childhood
Supported childcare/health/development.

## School age
Common public education + activities/friends/family life.

## Adolescence
Increasing autonomy + elective study + youth programs + optional apprenticeship.

## Early adulthood
Work/higher training/housing/partnership decisions; civic majority.

## Working adulthood
Career changes, business, family, housing, taxes, unions/co-ops, illness and ordinary risk.

## Mid/late adulthood
Retraining, accumulated property, care responsibilities, retirement planning.

## Aging
Partial/full retirement, healthcare, disability/access, possible elder support.

## Death/finality
Clinical/end-of-life process → continuity review only if genuinely applicable → legal finality → funeral/mourning/estate.

At no stage does origin category determine personhood or baseline civic rights.
