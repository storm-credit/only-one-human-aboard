# 0. Historical Ground Truth

Hard historical rules:

1. Departure passengers were Natural-Origin biological humans.
2. They were not massacred, mass-uploaded or secretly replaced.
3. Seed arose from neurodevelopment/Continuity medicine.
4. Seeded children were conceived/born biologically and developed through lived childhood.
5. Seed did not install personality, memory, loyalty or commands.
6. Seed adoption spread through medical benefit, evidence, provider expertise and path dependence.
7. Seeded-origin citizens gained/secured full equal civic recognition through legal normalization.
8. Natural-Origin births remained legal and never became an exact-one preservation program.
9. Original Natural-Origin generations simply aged, had descendants and died over many generations.
10. Current Natural-Origin count = 1 is a demographic state at story time.
11. Reconstruction always remained dependent on surviving biological continuity; no anchorless adult copy society emerged.
12. Meridian became politically autonomous through lived civilian legitimacy and communication latency, not through a single mutiny or Earth apocalypse.

# 1. Time Framework

Primary author-facing historical clock:
**Voyage Year (VY)** from departure.

Modern ordinary civil life still uses Earth-descended civil years/months/days; Voyage Year is archival/historical/mission-relative metadata.

Current voyage duration remains elastic at roughly mid-440s years order.

Era boundaries below are intentionally overlapping/approximate.

# ERA 0 — DEPARTURE / FOUNDING

Approx: VY 0–20.

## Population
- all passengers Natural-Origin.
- first ship-born births occur under advanced Earth-derived reproductive medicine.

## Government
- mission technical command has unusually broad practical influence.
- civilian representative mechanisms exist but many decisions remain mission/provisioning-heavy.
- no hereditary captain/state dynasty.

## Economy
- strategic provisioning dominates.
- food, housing, medical throughput and critical labor are strongly scheduled.
- personal property/exchange exists but is secondary to mission logistics.

## Urban form
- three rotating Habitat structures and Spine are mission hardware.
- interiors are relatively new/planned.
- earliest long-lived 1환 civic areas later become the historical core of 구시가지.

## Medicine
- advanced trauma/neurorehabilitation and prenatal medicine.
- longitudinal brain mapping exists experimentally/high-cost.
- no mature Reconstruction doctrine yet.

## Culture
- Earth-born adults dominate cultural production/memory.
- multiple Earth languages/religions/identities remain firsthand.
- mission Common is heavily international-English-derived.

## Earth contact
- psychologically direct and institutionally important.
- transmission delay still comparatively short.

## Material provenance
High-value future heritage classes begin:
- Earth-Origin objects,
- launch hardware,
- founding records,
- original household objects.

# ERA 1 — EARLY VOYAGE CONSOLIDATION

Approx: VY 20–80.

## Core shift
Meridian stops being experienced only as temporary transportation.
The first generations with no Earth memory become adults.

## Government
- local councils/civil administration expand.
- technical command increasingly has to justify authority by safety domain rather than mission tradition.

## Labor
- launch duty commitments weaken as a model for descendants.
- professional careers, apprenticeships and local labor institutions expand.

## Economy
- emergency rationing evolves toward stable baseline provisioning.
- market/co-op exchange above essentials expands.

## Urban form
- schools, shops, cultural spaces and local public realms multiply.
- housing is combined/subdivided/converted.
- initial planned Habitat layouts begin accumulating local history.

## Medicine
- severe neural injury and long-voyage medicine drive Continuity mapping research.
- clinicians learn that stored structural/functional information can aid repair but cannot replace destroyed biological continuity.

## Culture
- first durable ship-born slang, food adaptations, school rituals and civic observances emerge.
- heritage traditions remain strong.

## Earth relation
- Earth still matters, but local decisions increasingly cannot wait for off-ship consultation.

# ERA 2 — CONTINUITY MEDICINE / FIRST SEED COHORTS

Approx: VY 60–140.

## Reconstruction development
- longitudinal mapping improves.
- tissue/organ repair and neural rehabilitation advance.
- the conceptual distinction between `repair reference` and `person` strengthens.
- surviving neural substrate becomes increasingly explicit in medical continuity doctrine.

## Seed research
Developmental-prior research asks whether prenatal neurodevelopment can be made safer/more predictable without determining personality.

First Seed cohorts appear roughly within VY 80–120 working range.

They:
- are born biologically,
- grow through infancy/childhood,
- acquire memories/personality through life,
- are not preloaded minds.

## Adoption drivers
- probabilistic risk reduction,
- clinical predictability,
- improved compatibility with then-immature mapping/recovery medicine.

## Social response
- cautious families/providers coexist with enthusiastic adopters.
- Seed is initially a medical option, not universal identity.

## Culture
- ship-born identity deepens.
- Common continues to stabilize across Habitats while heritage languages diverge in vitality.

# ERA 3 — SEED NORMALIZATION / RIGHTS REFORM

Approx: VY 120–230.

## Medical expansion
Seed evidence base grows.
Provider expertise and care pathways increasingly assume it as standard prenatal practice.

Natural-Origin birth remains legal but becomes less common.

## Reconstruction maturation
Working medical doctrine converges toward modern principles:
- Recovery Map as repair/reference constraint,
- surviving Neural Anchor as continuity prerequisite,
- rehabilitation as a major post-Reconstruction specialty.

Natural-Origin adults remain medically reconstructable when suitable anchor survives.

## Rights conflicts
Legacy law was not written for Seeded developmental origin.
Disputes arise around:
- insurance/care,
- inheritance,
- parental/family status,
- continuity recognition,
- citizenship wording,
- discrimination.

## Legal direction
Repeated reforms establish:
**developmental origin alone cannot diminish civic personhood/citizenship.**

This becomes part of the legal ancestry of modern 시민기본법.

## Terminology
`Natural/Synthetic-Origin Cognition` remains technically used but becomes socially contentious/limited as a public status label.

## Economy/government
Civilian legal institutions deepen at the same time, distinguishing administrative, civil/family, criminal and rights review.

# ERA 4 — CIVIC MATURATION / URBAN LAYERING

Approx: VY 220–320.

## Government
Modern architecture becomes recognizable:
- elected shipwide civic authority,
- local Habitat/district councils,
- professional civil service,
- courts/review,
- narrowed technical safety command,
- commons authorities/certification.

Not every current institution has its final name yet.

## Economy
`Layered Commons + Market City` matures.

Civic floor includes reliable essentials while above-floor life includes:
- wages,
- firms/co-ops,
- shops/restaurants,
- housing-market differences,
- culture/leisure markets,
- private assets.

Critical life-support/capacity remains commons-governed.

## Labor
Inherited mission duty is obsolete.
Critical staffing uses wages/training/credentials/incentives and only narrow emergency obligations.

## Housing/property
Durable occupancy/property rights become normal while pressure/utility structure remains common infrastructure.

## City
- old districts gain historical status.
- 구시가지 becomes an explicit heritage/social category.
- buildings repeatedly retrofit around hard structural/pressure frames.

## Seed demography
Seeded births become overwhelming majority across this broad era.
Rare unseeded births continue.

## Culture
Meridian-created cuisine, literature, music, games and rituals become historical traditions themselves.

# ERA 5 — NETWORKED CIVILIZATION / AUTOMATION GOVERNANCE

Approx: VY 300–380.

## AI/automation
High-capability systems spread through:
- utilities,
- transit,
- medicine,
- logistics,
- administration,
- education,
- personal assistance.

## Governance lesson
Capability does not justify universal authority.
Over decades, experience with:
- overcollection,
- common-mode software failure,
- opaque recommendations,
- legacy integration risk
pushes the civilization toward modern AI-H1 / NET-H1 principles.

## Modernizing norms
- critical-domain segmentation,
- model/version certification,
- rollback/fallback,
- permissioned data gateways,
- human final responsibility for rights decisions,
- privacy/purpose limits.

No AI coup or sovereign machine era is needed.

## Archives
Repeated migrations produce specialized archive/records engineering:
- source schemas,
- mapping logs,
- snapshots,
- provenance,
- cold/offline redundancy.

This is the ancestry of ARC-H1.

## Maintenance
As systems age, independent inspection, predictive maintenance, calibration and strategic spare management strengthen.

# ERA 6 — MATURE MERIDIAN / EARTH AS ANCESTRAL RELATION

Approx: VY 370–420.

## Identity
For nearly everyone alive, Meridian is the only lived homeland.

Earth is:
- ancestry,
- historical archive,
- religion/cultural memory,
- delayed correspondence,
not a remembered childhood place.

## Earth contact
Preferred model: `EC-H1`.

Communication can persist but becomes:
- high-latency,
- asynchronous,
- irregular,
- non-governing.

Late-voyage one-way communication is roughly decade-scale order near destination.
A normal policy question/response cycle is therefore measured in decades, making Earth unsuitable as executive government.

Long gaps do not imply confirmed Earth destruction.

## Culture
- Common is evolved but still shipwide intelligible through schooling/media/mobility.
- heritage languages range from living community language to ceremonial/scholarly forms.
- inherited religions coexist with secular life and ship-born rituals/new movements.
- Earth cultural packages can still trigger revivals, but arrive into a mature local canon.

## Infrastructure
Aging/heritage tension becomes prominent.
Old Quarter and selected machines carry both history and maintenance cost.

## Natural-Origin rarity
Fully unseeded births are extremely rare but legal.
No public expectation exists that one Natural-Origin person must always be alive.

# ERA 7 — FINAL APPROACH / ARRIVAL PREPARATION

Approx: VY 420–insertion, story beginning ~T-14 within this era.

## Government
Arrival planning becomes operational.
Temporary statutory coordination structures such as 도착전환위원회 gain practical importance but do not replace the civic government.

## Industry
- strategic reserve audits,
- new fabrication lines,
- orbital/surface equipment preparation,
- specialist training,
- relocation/staging,
- increased maintenance overlap.

## Core political-economic conflict
Some assets can accelerate settlement only by reducing Meridian redundancy.
There is no free transfer of every useful system.

## Culture
Arrival stops being abstract mission destiny and becomes personal:
- where will I live?
- will my family split?
- what counts as home?
- what happens to old districts?

No single answer is socially predetermined.

## Current Seed context
Seed is ordinary prenatal care.
Historical origin categories are not everyday identity.
Rare refusal remains legal.
Amara's parents' choice is unusual but not secret-state opposition.

## Story-start information architecture
- current civic registry does not publicly label developmental origin,
- medical records retain Seed procedure history,
- deep archives retain old provenance/origin schemas,
- lawful cross-system analysis can eventually compute Count=1,
- identity remains separately protected.
