# PHASE 0 — PRE-DEPARTURE / EARLY DEPARTURE

Approx: prelaunch to VY 0–30.

## Medicine
- advanced prenatal screening/gene medicine already exists.
- neural imaging/mapping is sophisticated but not capable of full adult restoration from data alone.
- trauma/neurorehabilitation is advanced.

## Continuity concept
Researchers already distinguish:
- structural/functional mapping,
- surviving biological substrate,
- autobiographical/behavioral continuity.

But later `Recovery Map + Neural Anchor` doctrine is not yet mature.

## Birth
All actual departing humans are Natural-Origin.

---

# PHASE 1 — EARLY CONTINUITY MAPPING

Approx: VY 20–70.

Drivers:
- long-voyage trauma care,
- radiation/neurovascular injury,
- industrial accidents,
- desire for better rehabilitation after severe neural damage.

Developments:
- longitudinal neural mapping becomes more common for high-risk workers/medical research cohorts,
- clinicians learn that recovery is more reliable when there is extensive pre-injury structural/functional data,
- surviving neural tissue remains essential.

Key historical lesson:
**records can guide repair, but data alone do not constitute a restored adult person.**

This becomes the distant ancestor of modern Neural Anchor doctrine.

---

# PHASE 2 — DEVELOPMENTAL PRIOR RESEARCH

Approx: VY 50–100.

Research question:
Can developmental medicine reduce severe neurodevelopmental variance and also improve future mapping/recovery predictability without fixing personality or memory?

Early tools:
- prenatal developmental modeling,
- probabilistic neural-growth priors,
- closed-loop monitoring,
- limited intervention windows.

The eventual Seed concept emerges from **developmental medicine**, not an AI-person project.

---

# PHASE 3 — FIRST SEED COHORTS

Approx: VY 80–120.

First successful cohorts receive prenatal developmental seeding under research/medical protocols.

Important:
- children are conceived/born biologically,
- no mind is uploaded into fetus,
- no predefined identity/personality,
- memories arise through lived development.

Initial parental motives:
- lower risk in selected developmental conditions,
- more predictable neurological development,
- improved compatibility with then-current mapping/recovery medicine.

Early cohorts are small enough that long-term outcomes remain uncertain.

---

# PHASE 4 — MEDICAL EXPANSION / PROFESSIONAL STANDARDIZATION

Approx: VY 110–180.

Seed adoption grows because:
- outcome evidence accumulates,
- provider expertise grows,
- monitoring protocols become standardized,
- insurers/public medical systems increasingly treat Seed as normal best-studied prenatal care,
- more facilities optimize around the Seed pathway.

Natural-Origin birth remains legal and medically supported, but increasingly becomes an under-studied pathway.

Important asymmetry:
Refusal carries **uncertainty and friction**, not criminal punishment.

---

# PHASE 5 — EARLY RECONSTRUCTION MATURATION

Approx: VY 100–190.

Reconstruction evolves from advanced rehabilitation toward what modern citizens recognize as neural reconstruction.

Conceptual breakthroughs:
- Recovery Map becomes clearly defined as repair constraint/reference, not executable self,
- surviving Neural Anchor becomes explicit continuity prerequisite,
- better tissue/organ repair extends survival after catastrophic injury,
- Rehabilitation after reconstruction becomes a distinct specialty.

Seeded cohorts show somewhat more predictable mapping/reconstruction outcomes in early-era medicine, reinforcing adoption.

But Natural-Origin patients remain reconstructable when sufficient anchor survives.

# PHASE 6 — FIRST RIGHTS CONFLICTS

Approx: VY 120–190.

Trigger categories:
- old insurance/public care wording,
- inheritance after reconstructed status,
- whether `synthetic-origin cognition` fits legacy human/citizen language,
- parental rights,

- continuity-status disputes.

Society does **not** suddenly debate whether these children have bodies or feelings; everyone knows them as ordinary family members.

The legal problem is that inherited institutional language was written before the developmental pathway existed.

# PHASE 7 — CIVIC PERSONHOOD NORMALIZATION

Approx: VY 160–230.

Repeated cases/reforms establish that:
- developmental origin cannot by itself reduce citizenship,
- Reconstruction history cannot by itself reduce civic personhood,
- bodily modification cannot by itself reduce civic personhood.

These reforms are precursors to the modern 시민기본법 principle.

Historical `Natural/Synthetic-Origin Cognition` fields remain useful in medicine/research/provenance but lose legitimacy as public status labels.

# PHASE 8 — MAJORITY SHIFT

Approx: VY 180–280.

Seeded births become the overwhelming majority over several generations.

Why adoption persists even as later medicine improves:
- strong evidence base,
- provider training,
- integrated prenatal workflow,
- family familiarity,
- no major routine downside,
- legal/insurance/medical path dependence.

Natural-Origin birth does **not** go to zero.

Possible low-rate motives for refusal:
- developmental autonomy ethics,
- religious/philosophical reasons,
- family history of perceived adverse effects,
- distrust of standard intervention,
- rare medical circumstances.

# PHASE 9 — TERMINOLOGY DEPRECATION

Approx: VY 220–330.

Current language shifts:
- `Seed completion / Seed application history` becomes ordinary medical terminology,
- `Natural/Synthetic-Origin Cognition` becomes historical/technical language,
- public identity systems stop treating origin as routine visible status.

Why:
- origin no longer predicts civic rights,
- population is overwhelmingly Seeded,
- current medicine increasingly cares about procedural history rather than old metaphysical label.

Old schemas remain in deep archives.

# PHASE 10 — MODERN RECONSTRUCTION EQUALITY

Approx: VY 250 onward.

Mapping/reconstruction improves enough that the original early advantage of Seed is much smaller for adult treatment.

Modern clinicians can treat/reconstruct Natural-Origin adults if a suitable Neural Anchor survives.

Therefore:
- Amara can live an ordinary healthy life,
- her clinical records need not scream `Natural-Origin`,
- provenance remains required to establish origin definitively.

This is critical to no-adult-scanner Canon.

---

# PHASE 11 — RARE UNSEEDED RIGHTS STABILIZATION

Approx: VY 280 onward.

By now, fully unseeded birth is rare enough that law/policy focuses on:
- informed consent,
- provider competence,
- extra monitoring where medically justified,
- protection against discrimination,
- child welfare standards that do not amount to automatic prohibition.

There is no state mission to preserve or eliminate Natural-Origin people.
