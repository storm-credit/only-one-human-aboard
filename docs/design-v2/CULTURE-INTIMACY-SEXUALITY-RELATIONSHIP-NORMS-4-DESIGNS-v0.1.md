Date: 2026-08-17

Parents:
- FAM-H1
- MED-H1 reproductive/sexual health
- MEDIA-H1 privacy
- PROP-H1 household law

Purpose:
Design ordinary intimacy and sexual/social norms without making sexuality either erased or a futuristic spectacle.

---

# DESIGN A — CURRENT LIBERAL URBAN NORMS PRESERVED
Core: orientation/gender-neutral law, dating/marriage broadly familiar.
Strength: low exposition.
Weakness: 450-year social evolution disappears.
Verdict: `GOOD LEGAL BASE / NEED SHIP-BORN MODIFIERS`.

# DESIGN B — FULL POST-FAMILY SEXUAL LIBERATION
Core: partnership is fluid, marriage rare, reproduction fully institutionalized.
Strength: futuristic.
Weakness: conflicts existing family arcs and overstates technology's cultural effect.
Verdict: `REJECT`.

# DESIGN C — REPRODUCTIVE SCARCITY MAKES SEX/PAIRING HIGHLY REGULATED
Core: birth capacity drives partnership licensing and strong social control.
Strength: closed-system logic temptation.
Weakness: authoritarian reproductive society; unnecessary for Canon.
Verdict: `REJECT; BIRTH CAPACITY ≠ SEXUAL CONTROL`.

# DESIGN D — PLURAL ORDINARY INTIMACY + STRONG CONSENT/PRIVACY + DECOUPLED REPRODUCTION
Core: familiar relationship forms coexist with broader acceptance, reliable contraception/assisted reproduction and legal separation of sex, partnership and parenthood.
Verdict: `CURRENT BEST BASE`.

# RECOMMENDED HYBRID — INT-H1

Name: **Plural Adult Intimacy + Strong Consent/Privacy + Reproduction Decoupled From Partnership**
Status: `PROVISIONAL PRIORITY / NOT CANON`

## 1. LEGAL EQUALITY
Civil law does not discriminate by:
- sexual orientation
- gender
- origin class
in adult consensual partnership/marriage rights.

## 2. COMMON RELATIONSHIP FORMS
Ordinary social life includes:
- dating
- long-term partnership
- marriage/civil partnership
- cohabitation
- separation/divorce
- single adulthood
- sole parenthood

Two-adult pair bonds remain common but are not mandatory social adulthood.

## 3. NONMONOGAMY / MULTI-PARTNER RELATIONSHIPS
Consensual nonmonogamy can exist socially.
It is not designed as the default future norm and does not automatically create multi-parent/property rights.

Exact legal recognition beyond standard civil partnership remains low-priority unless later character design needs it.

## 4. SEX ≠ REPRODUCTION
Reliable contraception and assisted reproduction strongly separate sexual activity from planned childbirth.

Birth-capacity regulation applies to actual planned births/reproductive care, not to consensual sex or relationship licensing.

## 5. CONTRACEPTION
Highly reliable, reversible options are widely accessible through ordinary healthcare.

No gender-specific moral burden is assumed by law.

## 6. FERTILITY / ASSISTED REPRODUCTION
Assisted conception/donor material are socially ordinary enough not to define a family as abnormal.

Ella's donor conception is compatible.

## 7. SEXUAL HEALTH
Civic healthcare includes:
- contraception
- STI prevention/testing/treatment
- reproductive counseling
- sexual-health care

Privacy protections apply strongly.

## 8. CONSENT
Consent is a strong social/legal norm.

Core principles:
- voluntary
- ongoing
- capacity-sensitive
- revocable

Intoxication/incapacity/power imbalance can matter legally/socially.

## 9. AGE / YOUTH
Youth sexual-development norms exist but exact legal thresholds are deferred to final Family/Education integration.

Reference rule:
- one clear general adulthood framework
- age-appropriate close-in-age rules/protections may exist
- adults in authority cannot exploit minors/students under formal power

Do not create a complicated age chart in prose.

## 10. PRIVACY
Sexual/relationship life is private by default.

Employers/government do not receive general access to:
- dating history
- sexual-health records
- intimate messages

Exceptions require ordinary legal/medical safety basis.

## 11. PUBLIC AFFECTION
Moderate public affection is culturally ordinary, with local/generational variation.
No need for puritanical spaceship norms.

## 12. DATING / SOCIAL NETWORKS
People meet through:
- friends/work/school/community
- clubs/events
- social/dating services

No state genetic mate-matching system.
No AI assigns optimal partners.

## 13. GENETIC RELATEDNESS
Because population genetics matter over centuries, reproductive medicine may screen close genetic relatedness/health risks when conception is planned.

Hard rule:
This does not become a state dating caste or eugenic marriage license.

The genetic archive broadens future diversity rather than ranking romantic partners.

## 14. ORIGIN CLASS
Natural vs Seeded origin has no lawful sexual/partnership hierarchy.

Post-Count curiosity/fetishization of Amara or Natural-Origin may occur as ugly social behavior, but the story must not validate it as meaningful biological superiority.

## 15. WORKPLACE BOUNDARIES
Relationships between coworkers are not universally banned.
Stronger rules apply where there is:
- direct supervisory power
- evaluation authority
- medical/therapeutic relationship
- teacher/student authority
- legal/custodial power

Disclosure/recusal can be required.

## 16. PROFESSIONAL ETHICS
Clinician-patient, counselor-client, teacher-minor, guardian-dependent and detainee-officer relationships have strong exploitation boundaries.

## 17. MARRIAGE / CEREMONY
Civil status is separate from cultural/religious ceremony per FAM-H1/REL-H1.

## 18. DIVORCE / BREAKUPS
Breakups are socially ordinary but still emotionally/economically consequential.
Long life does not imply partnerships are disposable or permanent.

## 19. LONG HEALTHSPAN
A 100+ year life makes:
- multiple major relationships across a lifetime plausible
- late-life partnership/new family forms possible

But no character is required to follow this pattern.

## 20. HOUSING
Couples may maintain:
- shared home
- separate homes
- partial co-residence
particularly around work/arrival transition.

Marriage does not require instant housing merge.

## 21. COMMERCIAL SEXUAL SERVICES — PROVISIONAL LOW-SALIENCE
Reference direction:
Consensual adult sexual services may exist under ordinary labor, health, privacy and anti-coercion law rather than being automatically criminalized.

Hard rules:
- trafficking/coercion/exploitation illegal
- minors prohibited
- worker health/privacy/exit rights protected

This is not a planned plot domain and need not appear in prose.

## 22. SEXUAL MEDIA
Adult sexual media can exist under consent/age/privacy/provenance law.
Unauthorized intimate synthetic media is a privacy/harassment offense.

No need for explicit content in setting bible beyond legal/social existence.

## 23. RELIGION / SUBCULTURE
Communities can hold different sexual ethics, but civil law protects equal rights and consent.
Private belief does not authorize coercion.

## 24. CLASS
Wealth affects:
- privacy
- leisure
- housing
- social access
but cannot legally purchase consent or parenthood/birth priority.

## 25. ARRIVAL
Geographic family/partner separation becomes a major ordinary issue:
- long-distance relationships
- split households
- different settlement choices

Legal partnership status continues across locations during transition.
