# SOCIETY ECONOMY DEEP LAYER — 4 DESIGNS v0.1

Status: `ENCYCLOPEDIC v2 DESIGN COMPARISON / NOT CANON`
Date: 2026-08-17
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Parent Canon baseline:
`Layered Commons + Market City`

Existing hard economic rule:
- civic survival floor includes air/water/basic food/minimum housing/basic education/basic+emergency care
- ordinary market activity exists: wages, money, businesses, shops, restaurants, culture, hobbies, private/co-op services, housing quality/location
- hard-capacity commons are not simply purchasable: birth capacity, high-load Reconstruction, habitat expansion, strategic industrial assets

Purpose:
Design the deep economy so a random citizen can:
- earn income
- pay taxes
- rent/move/buy occupancy rights
- borrow
- start/close a business
- inherit
- retire
- become unemployed/disabled
- become rich/poor
without inventing major new rules during prose.

Evaluation criteria:
1. compatibility with Layered Commons
2. ordinary-life legibility
3. real inequality without dystopian caste
4. market dynamism
5. closed-habitat resource realism
6. anti-command-economy
7. anti-plutocracy
8. story texture
9. property/inheritance plausibility
10. arrival-transition adaptability

---

# DESIGN A — SOCIAL MARKET CITY

Core:
A shipwide fiat currency supports wages, prices, firms, rent, lending and investment.
A tax-funded civic floor guarantees survival basics.
Critical commons and hard capacities are allocated through public rules rather than price alone.

Strengths:
- intuitive for readers
- supports shops/restaurants/co-ops/careers/class difference
- clear taxation and budgets
- flexible across 450 years

Weaknesses:
- can feel like modern welfare capitalism pasted into space
- money can quietly leak into every scarce domain if guardrails are weak
- wealth concentration needs control mechanisms

Biggest trap:
`21st-century city economy with airlocks`.

Verdict:
`STRONG BASE / NEED CLOSED-SYSTEM MODIFIERS`.

---

# DESIGN B — DUAL COMMONS CREDITS + MARKET MONEY

Core:
Citizens use ordinary money for market goods but separate nontransferable capacity credits for housing, food, healthcare, birth, reconstruction and other scarce commons.

Strengths:
- clearly prevents rich people buying survival capacity
- closed-system scarcity is visible
- allows differentiated queues/entitlements

Weaknesses:
- game-like
- too many currencies/credits
- encourages black-market conversion
- reader must track token systems
- risks turning family decisions into resource-point accounting

Biggest trap:
`RPG ration tokens disguised as social policy`.

Verdict:
`USE ONLY FOR NARROW ENTITLEMENT/QUEUE TOOLS, NOT GENERAL ECONOMY`.

---

# DESIGN C — COOPERATIVE STAKEHOLDER ECONOMY

Core:
Most medium/large enterprises are worker/consumer cooperatives or public-benefit corporations.
Private small business remains common.
Capital accumulation is moderated through cooperative governance and limited absentee ownership.

Strengths:
- fits long-lived closed community
- makes Ella/Jun/co-op life natural
- reduces billionaire/corporate-sovereign problem
- gives labor meaningful agency

Weaknesses:
- can become ideologically neat
- investment/risk allocation may become vague
- innovation/business failure may feel sanitized
- not every worker wants governance meetings

Biggest trap:
`everyone works at a wholesome co-op`.

Verdict:
`STRONG SECONDARY LAYER / NOT UNIVERSAL FORM`.

---

# DESIGN D — ALGORITHMIC RESOURCE ECONOMY

Core:
Most production and distribution are centrally optimized by ship systems; money persists mainly for luxuries.

Strengths:
- technically plausible temptation
- efficient closed-loop accounting
- reduces artificial scarcity

Weaknesses:
- kills market-city premise
- AI becomes de facto planner/sovereign
- weakens class/business/labor story texture
- citizens become users of allocation software rather than economic actors

Biggest trap:
`the AI economy solved politics`.

Verdict:
`REJECT AS PRIMARY MODEL`.

# RECOMMENDED HYBRID — ECO-H1

Name:
**Civic Floor + Monetary Market + Capacity Commons + Mixed Enterprise**

Status:
`PROVISIONAL PRIORITY / NOT CANON`

## Core principle
Money coordinates ordinary choice.
Law and public institutions protect the survival floor.
Physical scarcity is acknowledged openly.
Some hard capacities use eligibility/queue/allocation rules that money cannot simply bypass.

This keeps Meridian a real city rather than either:
- a ration bureaucracy, or
- a plutocratic market in oxygen.

---

# 1. CURRENCY / ACCOUNTING

Meridian uses one primary shipwide monetary unit for:
- wages
- prices
- rent/occupancy payments
- business accounting
- taxes
- loans
- savings
- investment

Design direction:
- mostly digital account money
- resilient offline/emergency settlement paths exist for network disruption
- no crypto-style speculative monetary system as civic foundation
- no social-credit score merged with money

Reader-facing naming:
Use ordinary `크레딧/선내 화폐/계좌` language unless later Culture Bible finds a better natural term.
Do not create lore burden through fancy currency names unless useful.

Monetary authority:
Human-accountable public institution sets issuance/financial rules.
Automation can clear transactions and model supply; no AI monetary sovereign.

---

# 2. WHY MONEY STILL EXISTS IN A CLOSED SHIP

Even with automated production and civic basics, scarcity remains in:
- skilled labor time
- desirable locations
- larger/private space
- restaurant/hospitality
- art/entertainment
- custom goods
- high-end services
- rare materials
- heritage objects
- business capacity
- leisure/travel convenience

Money is useful for ordinary preference tradeoffs.

What money does NOT directly buy:
- more breathable air entitlement
- exemption from emergency rationing
- a birth slot
- automatic Reconstruction priority
- citizenship/personhood
- safety-rule exemption
- sovereign settlement rights

---

# 3. CIVIC SURVIVAL FLOOR

Guaranteed baseline includes:
- breathable environment / water
- basic nutritionally adequate food access
- minimum safe housing
- basic education
- primary/basic healthcare + emergency care
- minimum digital/civic access
- protection from destitution-level loss of all income

This floor is not luxury equality.
People can still be materially better/worse off above it.

Guardrail:
No criminal or debt sanction removes the survival floor.

---

# 4. PUBLIC REVENUE / TAXATION

Provisional revenue mix:
1. progressive personal income/payroll taxation
2. business profit/surplus taxation
3. consumption taxes/fees on selected nonbasic goods/services
4. occupancy/property-value related charges where appropriate
5. returns/rents from publicly/common-held strategic assets
6. user fees for optional premium services where they do not undermine basic access

Avoid:
- charging citizens separately for every breath/liter as ordinary taxation
- dozens of micro-fees that make the city feel like a survival subscription

Basic public utilities are funded collectively, though excessive/premium use may face pricing or allocation rules where necessary.

---

# 5. BUDGET

Elected government controls the public budget within certified physical feasibility.

Major budget domains:
- life-support/ecology
- health
- education
- housing floor
- transport
- public safety
- infrastructure maintenance
- archives/civic systems
- retirement/disability/social support
- arrival preparation in later Acts

Commons authorities may certify minimum safe maintenance/stock levels but do not decide all distributive priorities.

---

# 6. WAGES / WORK INCOME

Most working-age adults receive income through:
- wages/salary
- cooperative distributions
- self-employment/business income
- professional contracts
- creative royalties/licensing
- investment returns within legal limits

Pay differences are real.
Higher compensation may reflect:
- scarce skill
- hazardous work
- responsibility
- difficult shifts
- leadership
- market demand

But critical professions do not become hereditary castes.

Exact wage numbers are not locked yet; relative class bands matter more than fake precision.

---

# 7. ENTERPRISE FORMS

Economy permits multiple forms:

## Small private enterprise
Examples:
- restaurants
- retail
- repairs
- design
- media
- crafts
- professional services

## Worker/consumer cooperatives
Common where:
- service continuity matters
- employees invest long careers
- community/customer stake is high

Ella's hospitality/food cooperative fits here.
Jun's contracting/co-op structure also remains compatible.

## Conventional corporations / investor firms
Allowed, especially in:
- manufacturing
- software
- media
- specialized services

But subject to competition/labor/safety rules.

## Public/common enterprises
Critical utility and strategic-capacity functions can be publicly/common controlled.

Hard rule:
No single private corporation owns life support, citizenship records or propulsion sovereignty.

---

# 8. BUSINESS FORMATION / FAILURE

Starting a normal business requires:
- legal registration
- tax/account setup
- relevant safety/health/professional licenses
- premises/access rights if physical

No general state permission based on ideological worth.

Business can fail.
Consequences may include:
- insolvency
- layoffs/reassignment
- debt restructuring
- sale of assets
- cooperative dissolution/merger

But failure of a private business cannot be allowed to abruptly terminate a civic survival service without continuity arrangements.

---

# 9. CREDIT / LOANS / DEBT

Credit exists for:
- housing occupancy acquisition/upgrade
- business capital
- education/professional retraining costs above public baseline
- durable goods
- personal liquidity

Lenders may include:
- banks/credit institutions
- cooperative finance
- public development/transition finance in selected domains

Debt rules:
- interest exists
- default exists
- collateral exists
- bankruptcy/insolvency relief exists
- survival floor cannot be seized
- debt cannot turn into hereditary personal bondage

No debtor's prison.

---

# 10. BANKRUPTCY / INSOLVENCY

Individuals can discharge/restructure some debts after formal process while preserving:
- minimum housing
- essential household goods
- medical access
- basic income floor

Fraudulent asset hiding remains punishable.

Businesses may:
- reorganize
- liquidate
- sell transferable assets
- preserve critical service contracts through supervised continuity mechanisms

This allows real entrepreneurial risk without permanent economic death.

---

# 11. HOUSING PROPERTY MODEL — PROVISIONAL

Physical hull/structural survival systems are not privately owned parcels of sovereign land.

Citizens/households can hold combinations of:
- long-term occupancy rights
- leaseholds
- cooperative housing shares
- privately transferable residential-use rights under housing law
- ownership of interior improvements/furnishings

Minimum safe housing remains civic floor.
Better outcomes can be purchased/earned through market:
- more space
- better location
- privacy
- view/amenity access
- shorter commute
- desirable historic district

Because total habitat area is finite:
- speculative vacancy/hoarding is constrained
- multiple-home ownership may exist but faces strong cost/tax/use rules
- no private person owns the hull or life-support infrastructure behind the dwelling

Exact tenure mix waits for Package C Housing Bible.

---

# 12. RENT / MOVING

Rental market exists above/beside the civic housing floor.

A person may move because of:
- job
- family
- school
- preference
- affordability
- disability/accessibility
- district change

Moving is not merely government assignment.

However emergency relocation/repair can temporarily override occupancy, with compensation/review rules.

Old Quarter conflicts therefore have real property/tenure consequences without treating the hull as ordinary freehold land.

---

# 13. PROPERTY OWNERSHIP

Private ownership can include:
- money/savings
- consumer goods
- business shares
- intellectual property/creative rights
- tools/equipment
- vehicles/mobility devices where applicable
- housing occupancy/lease interests
- collectibles/heirlooms

Restricted/common assets cannot be privately converted merely by possession.

Examples:
- reserve pump ≠ private collectible
- decommissioned tool may become transferable only after lawful release from strategic/common status

This integrates Package C-M.

---

# 14. INHERITANCE

Generally inheritable:
- savings
- eligible business ownership/shares
- household goods
- heirlooms
- intellectual-property interests
- transferable housing/lease/co-op interests subject to housing rules

Not inheritable as private entitlement:
- public office
- safety/security clearance
- professional license
- birth/Reconstruction priority
- citizenship category privilege
- common infrastructure
- Amara's Natural-Origin status as legal authority

If a housing interest is nontransferable by design, heirs receive settlement/compensation rights rather than automatic occupancy.

Reconstruction complication:
A legally returning citizen does not automatically unwind all inheritance settled during their absence/death status.
Exact continuity/property doctrine must later integrate family/property law.

---

# 15. WEALTH / CLASS

Meridian has inequality, but not a hidden hereditary caste system.

Wealth can buy more of:
- space
- desirable location
- customization
- privacy
- hospitality
- high-end leisure
- bespoke goods
- collectibles
- investment ownership
- professional services
- schedule flexibility

Wealth cannot legally buy:
- extra civic personhood
- unsafe resource priority
- permanent monopoly over critical commons
- privileged origin category
- arbitrary queue-jumping for hard medical/birth capacity

Likely social status also comes from:
- profession
- expertise
- institutional reputation
- family networks
- cultural achievement
- heritage ownership

not money alone.

---

# 16. WEALTH CONCENTRATION GUARDRAILS

Potential mechanisms:
- progressive taxation
- inheritance taxation above protected family assets
- anti-monopoly law
- housing vacancy/secondary-use charges
- competition law
- transparency for major strategic contractors
- restrictions on private acquisition of commons assets

Do not make a hard wealth cap unless later design proves necessary.

Goal:
real rich people can exist without billionaire-scale private sovereignty over a 300k civilization.

---

# 17. LABOR ORGANIZATION

Workers may organize through:
- unions
- professional associations
- cooperatives
- workplace councils
- safety committees

Different sectors use different forms.

Important:
Labor conflict is real.
Workers can:
- bargain
- strike within legal/safety constraints
- contest automation
- challenge reassignment

Critical-service strikes may require minimum emergency staffing/mediation procedures rather than total prohibition.

Exact labor law is a next integrated block.

---

# 18. UNEMPLOYMENT

Unemployment can occur through:
- business failure
- automation
- skills mismatch
- relocation
- health
- arrival-transition restructuring

Support includes:
- baseline income support
- retraining
- placement services
- housing floor continuity
- healthcare continuity

No assumption that the ship always has perfect job matching.

Arrival Acts can therefore create real career anxiety without threatening starvation.

---

# 19. DISABILITY / INABILITY TO WORK

A citizen unable to work fully retains civic survival floor.
Additional support may include:
- disability income
- adapted housing
- mobility support
- care services
- supported/reduced-hours work

Benefits are not conditioned on moral worth or origin class.

Exact medical/disability assessment waits for Package A medicine/disability design.

---

# 20. RETIREMENT / PENSIONS

Retirement exists.

Provisional mixed model:
- universal/basic old-age floor
- contributory occupational/public pension component
- private/cooperative savings above it

This allows Tomas to retire without becoming economically irrelevant or destitute.

Retirement age is not one rigid number because:
- healthspan is long
- occupations differ
- dangerous work may retire earlier
- partial retirement is common

Exact age bands remain for later labor/demographic pass.

---

# 21. PRICES / SCARCITY

Normal market goods use prices.

If a scarce good is merely expensive but nonessential:
price can rise.

If scarcity threatens civic survival/basic fairness:
public rules may use:
- temporary purchase limits
- emergency allocation
- priority classes based on need/function
- anti-hoarding rules

Hard-capacity examples:
- birth capacity
- high-load Reconstruction
- emergency ICU throughput
- strategic industrial reserves
use administrative/clinical/public allocation rather than auction.

No `highest bidder gets the last ICU slot`.

---

# 22. BLACK / GRAY MARKETS

Plausible markets:
- scarce luxuries
- unauthorized repairs/mods
- stolen/restricted professional tools
- credentials/access
- privacy/data services
- unlicensed specialty work
- forged heritage provenance
- diverted materials
- queue influence/bribery attempts

Hard rule:
A black market may try to corrupt allocation but does not officially convert hard civic capacity into legal ownership.

No casual `buy a birth slot` norm.

---

# 23. FOOD / UTILITIES

Basic food/water/air are civic floor.

Market layer exists for:
- restaurants
- higher-cost ingredients
- special textures/cuisines
- private dining
- alcohol/stimulants if Culture Bible permits
- home customization/appliances
- premium convenience

Excessive utility demand may face pricing/limits, but ordinary citizens are not billed in a way that makes breathing feel like rent.

---

# 24. EDUCATION / HUMAN CAPITAL

Basic education is public/civic floor.

Higher professional training may combine:
- public funding
- employer sponsorship
- apprenticeships
- competitive limited-seat programs
- personal contribution for optional/premium paths

Money alone cannot purchase a safety-critical credential without competence.

Arrival transition may subsidize scarce future skills.

---

# 25. AUTOMATION

Automation changes labor demand but does not eliminate economy.

Possible effects:
- routine production cheaper
- fewer low-skill repetitive roles
- more maintenance/design/care/service work
- retraining pressure
- ownership of productive capital still matters

AI cannot secretly assign everyone's job for optimal civilization output.

Exact boundary waits for Package C AI Bible.

---

# 26. HAZARDOUS WORK / COMPENSATION

Hazardous work can receive:
- wage premium
- shorter rotations
- extra leave
- enhanced disability/insurance coverage
- earlier pension eligibility

Examples:
- Outer Works
- some industrial maintenance
- high-risk emergency response

No hereditary hazard caste.

---

# 27. INSURANCE

A mixed insurance layer may exist above public guarantees for:
- property loss
- business interruption
- premium health amenities/nonbasic services
- professional liability
- specialized travel/equipment

Basic emergency care and civic survival are not dependent on private insurance approval.

Reconstruction eligibility/capacity cannot become `only insured people live`.

---

# 28. MATERIAL CULTURE INTEGRATION

Money/property rules support:
- antique collecting
- heirloom transfer
- restoration services
- provenance markets
- tool ownership
- high-end craftsmanship

But strategic/common equipment remains outside casual market ownership until decommission/release.

Heritage fraud can be a real economic/criminal offense.

---

# 29. ARRIVAL TRANSITION ECONOMY

Acts 5–7 create unusual economic pressures:
- contracts shift to arrival work
- some ship businesses lose customers/space
- new orbital/surface supply chains appear
- worker retraining accelerates
- public investment rises
- strategic assets are requisitioned/transferred under law, with compensation where appropriate
- property values diverge by future location

Hard rule:
Arrival is not a free land-rush where the first person to step outside owns territory.

Package E must later define:
- surface/orbital tenure
- cross-location taxation
- business continuity
- inheritance
- currency/jurisdiction continuity

---

# 30. ORDINARY HOUSEHOLD EXAMPLES

## Lower-income household
Can still access:
- safe minimum home
- basic food
- school
- healthcare
- transit baseline
but may face:
- less desirable location
- smaller space
- less leisure spending
- little savings
- debt stress

## Middle household
Likely has:
- stable wage(s)
- chosen rental/co-op/occupancy arrangement
- hobbies/eating out
- modest savings
- ability to absorb ordinary repairs/moves

## Wealthy household
May have:
- premium space/location
- business/investment ownership
- rare collections
- greater schedule flexibility
- expensive services
but not civic survival priority.

This inequality must be visible enough to feel real without turning each Habitat into a class zone.
