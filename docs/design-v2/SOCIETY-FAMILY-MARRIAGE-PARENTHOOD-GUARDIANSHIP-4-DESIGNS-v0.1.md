# DESIGN A — MODERN CIVIL MARRIAGE + PARENTAL LAW

Core:
Gender-neutral civil marriage/partnership, ordinary divorce, parenthood established by birth/adoption/recognized intent.

Strengths:
- legible
- low jargon
- easy property/inheritance fit

Weaknesses:
- can feel copied from current Earth law
- donor/sole-parent/Reconstruction cases need more sophistication

Verdict:
`STRONG BASE / NEED FUTURE-SOCIETY MODIFIERS`.

---

# DESIGN B — CONTRACT-FIRST HOUSEHOLD LAW

Core:
Citizens create modular household contracts specifying property, care, parenting, residence and succession.

Strengths:
- flexible
- future-feeling
- handles diverse households

Weaknesses:
- children/weak parties may lose protection
- family life becomes legal engineering
- contract exposition burden

Verdict:
`USE FOR OPTIONAL AGREEMENTS, NOT CORE STATUS`.

---

# DESIGN C — PARENTHOOD-CENTERED / MARRIAGE-LIGHT SYSTEM

Core:
Marriage has limited legal importance; parenthood, dependency and household are the main legal statuses.

Strengths:
- fits donor conception/sole parenthood
- separates romantic relationship from child rights
- flexible across family forms

Weaknesses:
- marriage may feel culturally hollow
- inheritance/property default rules become less intuitive

Verdict:
`ESSENTIAL PRINCIPLE, BUT MARRIAGE SHOULD STILL MATTER SOCIALLY/LEGALLY`.

---

# DESIGN D — REGISTERED MULTI-ADULT FAMILY UNITS

Core:
Law commonly recognizes multi-adult domestic unions with shared parent/property status.

Strengths:
- future social variety
- resilient care networks

Weaknesses:
- adds major complexity not demanded by story
- risks `look how futuristic we are` worldbuilding
- inheritance/custody combinatorics explode

Verdict:
`DO NOT MAKE DEFAULT / ALLOW NONSTANDARD HOUSEHOLDS WITHOUT REBUILDING ALL LAW AROUND THEM`.

# RECOMMENDED HYBRID — FAM-H1

Name:
**Civil Partnership + Independent Parenthood + Protected Dependency + Flexible Household**

Status:
`PROVISIONAL PRIORITY / NOT CANON`

## Principle
Romantic partnership, legal parenthood, guardianship, residence and property are related but distinct legal dimensions.

A spouse is not automatically a parent.
A guardian is not an owner of the child.
A donor is not automatically a parent.
A returning/reconstructed parent is not automatically restored to every practical caregiving role without regard to current child welfare.

---

# 1. MARRIAGE / CIVIL PARTNERSHIP

One primary gender-neutral civil partnership/marriage framework is sufficient for reader-facing law.

It can create defaults for:
- next-of-kin
- shared household obligations
- medical/proxy priority where applicable
- property/succession
- tax/benefit treatment

Cultural/religious ceremonies may vary independently in Package B.

No legal distinction by sexual orientation.

---

# 2. MARRIAGE IS NOT PARENTHOOD

Marriage does not automatically create parent status for every child.

Parenthood is separately established through:
- birth/legal intent
- pre-birth parent designation under reproductive law
- adoption
- later judicial/legal recognition where necessary

This supports donor conception and sole parenthood.

---

# 3. SOLE PARENTHOOD

Sole legal parenthood is ordinary and lawful.

A citizen may intentionally become sole parent through assisted reproduction/donor material if birth-capacity and medical rules are satisfied.

Ella's planned sole legal parenthood of Noah using donor material is fully compatible.

Donor genetic contribution does not automatically create parent rights/obligations unless the legal arrangement says so.

---

# 4. TWO-PARENT / CO-PARENT FAMILIES

Two legal parents remain common.

Parents may be:
- married/partnered
- separated
- never partnered
- living in different households

The law focuses on parent status/responsibility rather than forcing one household form.

---

# 5. MULTI-ADULT HOUSEHOLDS

Households with more than two caregiving adults can exist.

But law does not presume every co-resident adult becomes full legal parent.

Possible statuses:
- legal parent
- guardian
- recognized caregiver
- household member

This allows social variety without turning multi-parent law into default complexity.

Exact maximum legal-parent count need not be fixed unless a story requires it.

---

# 6. DONOR CONCEPTION

Assisted reproduction is normalized.

Legal principle:
**reproductive intent + registered parent status > raw genetic contribution** for ordinary parenthood.

Donors may have:
- privacy protections
- medical-history obligations
- agreed information-release terms

but no automatic custody/parent claim.

Genetic Archive use, if any, remains separately governed.

---

# 7. BIRTH CAPACITY

Family form does not create extra priority by wealth/marital status.

Birth-capacity allocation cannot lawfully favor:
- married over unmarried
- wealthy over poor
- Natural over Seeded origin

Clinical/fairness criteria may consider genuine family/medical circumstances under separate rules.

Exact queue remains reproduction/capacity system.

---

# 8. SEED CONSENT / PARENT AUTHORITY

Prenatal Seed decision belongs to the legally authorized prospective parent(s)/gestational decision structure under reproductive law.

Full-window refusal is lawful but rare.

Hard rule:
A child does not become less of a citizen because parents refused Seed.

Amara's parents' choice remains a legal prenatal decision, not criminal evasion.

---

# 9. ADOPTION

Adoption exists for children who need permanent legal parenthood or where an existing family structure changes.

Core principles:
- child welfare
- relationship continuity where healthy
- youth voice according to maturity/age
- no origin discrimination
- no market purchase of children

Because population is controlled, adoption is not primarily a way to bypass birth-capacity allocation.

---

# 10. GUARDIANSHIP

Guardianship is temporary or limited authority when a parent cannot currently exercise needed functions.

Possible reasons:
- incapacity
- prolonged Reconstruction/rehab
- disappearance
- serious illness
- temporary safety issue

Guardian duties can include:
- residence/school decisions
- routine medical consent
- property management if separately authorized

But guardian does not automatically become permanent parent or property owner.

Maren's role with Noah fits here.

---

# 11. RECONSTRUCTED PARENT RETURN

Key distinction:
**legal parenthood continuity ≠ immediate restoration of every caregiving function.**

When a parent returns after prolonged Reconstruction:
- their legal parent status is strongly protected,
- practical parenting authority/residence can be staged/reviewed based on current capacity and child welfare,
- existing guardian's years of care matter,
- child preference matters increasingly with age,
- no official may declare one adult the `real` parent by metaphysical identity theory.

This directly supports Ella/Maren/Noah Act 1.

---

# 12. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

A family member who works in Continuity/government cannot adjudicate their own family case.

COI-H1 remains mandatory.

Maren may:
- participate as guardian/family party
- provide records/testimony where lawful

Maren may not:
- issue formal family/Continuity ruling in own case
- use professional access as substitute for family-process authority

---

# 13. CHILD/YOUTH VOICE

Youth autonomy grows gradually rather than flipping at one magical birthday.

Relevant domains may have different thresholds:
- daily routine
- school activities
- residence preference
- medical participation
- independent contracts/work
- full civil majority

For major family decisions:
child preference is meaningful but not absolute when young.

Noah at 12 can shape arrangement without becoming his own family-law judge.

---

# 14. CIVIL MAJORITY

A general age of civil majority exists, but exact age remains for Education/Culture consolidation.

Before majority, older adolescents gain increasing:
- consent rights
- privacy
- work/education choice
- residence input

M1 age architecture must be respected as Noah/Kai/Mina mature.

---

# 15. DIVORCE / SEPARATION

No-fault civil separation/divorce is available.

Process can resolve separately:
- partnership termination
- property
- housing
- support
- parent responsibilities
- child residence

No requirement to prove moral wrongdoing merely to end partnership.

Abuse cases use protective/safety procedures, not mediation pressure.

---

# 16. CHILD RESIDENCE / PARENTING ARRANGEMENTS

Possible outcomes:
- primary residence with one parent
- shared residence
- flexible schedule
- youth residential program/institutional stay
- staged transition after parent return

The law can allocate practical responsibility without declaring one person's identity more authentic.

EP027 trial arrangement remains compatible.

---

# 17. SUPPORT OBLIGATIONS

Parents have child-support obligations proportionate to capacity/income, regardless of romantic relationship.

Public civic floor protects the child if a parent cannot pay.

Support is for child welfare, not ownership/access purchase.

---

# 18. FAMILY PROPERTY

Partnership may create shared-property/default rights, but exact model can mix:
- separate property
- jointly acquired household property
- explicit agreements

Do not assume all assets automatically merge at marriage.

PROP-H1 controls housing/property categories.

---

# 19. INHERITANCE / NEXT OF KIN

Default succession can prioritize:
- spouse/registered partner
- children/descendants
- other family
under ordinary rules.

Wills may alter many defaults within dependent-protection limits.

Exact shares are unnecessary until later consolidation.

---

# 20. ELDER CARE

Long healthspan does not erase elder dependence.

Care may combine:
- family help
- public/community services
- paid professional care
- adapted housing

Children are not legally treated as automatic unpaid elder-care property.

Tomas/Amara's older parents can have independent elder lives.

---

# 21. DISABILITY / DEPENDENT ADULTS

Adult guardianship/substitute decision-making should be narrow and capacity-specific.

Prefer supported decision-making where possible.

No total civil erasure merely because a citizen needs care.

Exact mental-health/capacity law later.

---

# 22. DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

Family privacy does not block public protection.

Available:
- emergency separation
- no-contact orders
- protected temporary housing
- child-safety intervention
- confidential medical/legal support

Restorative mediation never mandatory.

CJ-H1 controls criminal/public-safety response.

---

# 23. HOUSEHOLD NAMES / SURNAMES

Do not lock naming conventions here.
Package B Culture/Language should decide:

- hyphenation/double names
- child naming
- marriage name changes

Law does not require one spouse to take another's name.

# 24. SOCIAL NORMALITY

Legal diversity should feel ordinary rather than constantly explained.

Common enough to be unremarkable:
- married couple with children
- unmarried co-parents
- sole parent
- donor conception
- separated co-parent households
- guardian-led temporary household
- older adults living independently

Do not make every family structurally exotic merely to prove futurism.
