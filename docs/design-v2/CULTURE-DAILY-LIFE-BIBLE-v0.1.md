# 1. THE CULTURAL THESIS

Meridian is neither:
- a crew trapped in permanent mission mode,
- a museum of 21st-century Earth,
- nor a totally alien posthuman culture.

It is a 450-year-old civilian city descended from Earth.

Its people have:
- inherited institutions and cultural lineages,
- changed language and customs,
- created centuries of new art/food/sport/history,
- made closed-loop living ordinary,
- formed a home nobody alive chose at launch,
- and disagree about what arrival should mean.

---

# 2. LANGUAGE

One evolved Ship Common is near-universal for civic/daily communication.
It descends strongly from launch-era international English but is not identical to modern English after ~450 years.

Drift is moderated by:
- literacy
- school
- archived media
- technical/legal standards
- small connected population
- cross-Habitat movement.

Heritage languages survive unevenly through family, art, religion, scholarship and identity.

The Korean novel uses natural Korean as a reader translation convention.
Do not write pseudo-translated syntax.

---

# 3. NAMES / HERITAGE

Multicultural names can persist long after full everyday ancestral-language fluency weakens.

Existing cast names remain plausible.

Name does not automatically reveal:
- modern nationality
- language fluency
- Habitat
- religion
- class.

Mixed ancestry and mixed family traditions are normal.

---

# 4. CIVIL TIME

Meridian uses:
- synchronized 24-hour civil day
- 7-day week
- Earth-descended ~365-day civil year
- inherited month/date system

Reason:
timekeeping is biological/civic infrastructure, not a novelty contest.

A departure-relative Voyage Year also exists for archives/engineering/history/commemoration.

No internal Habitat time zones.

---

# 5. DAY / NIGHT / WEEKEND

Artificial lighting creates ordinary circadian day/evening/night patterns.

The ship never stops operating, but social rest cycles/weekends are real.

Shift workers live offset schedules and create distinct late-night/early-morning cultures.

---

# 6. HOLIDAYS / OBSERVANCES

Civic calendar has ship-born observance functions such as:
- departure/founding remembrance
- collective disaster/remembrance days
- rights/civic milestones if Package D history supports them
- district festivals
- later arrival-era anniversaries.

Exact names/dates remain Package D dependency.

Religious communities keep separate inherited/adapted calendars.

---

# 7. BELIEF / NONBELIEF

Meridian contains:
- evolved descendants of Earth religions
- secular/nonreligious citizens
- philosophical/ethical communities
- small ship-born spiritual movements
- loosely mixed/private practice.

No state religion.
No mandatory secular ideology.

Shared civic rituals do not require shared metaphysics.

---

# 8. SEED / RECONSTRUCTION IN CULTURE

Different communities may interpret Seed/Reconstruction differently, but civil law remains controlling on rights/personhood.

Hard cultural guardrails:
- no major religion automatically equals Natural-Origin movement
- Amara's parents are not retroactively assigned cult membership
- no faith gets civic authority to declare Seeded citizens soulless
- Reconstruction remains serious medicine, not universal spiritual proof.

# 9. FOOD PRODUCTION AS NORMALITY

Citizens know their food system is closed-loop and biotech-heavy, but they do not narrate meals as engineering reports.

Normal ingredients derive from:
- crops
- legumes/greens
- fungi
- microbial/algal ingredients
- precision fermentation
- cultured meat
- limited richer ecology.

These are `food`, not novelty substitutes after centuries.

# 10. CUISINE

Everyday cuisine combines:
- inherited Earth recipe lineages
- centuries of substitutions
- ship-born dishes
- family traditions
- restaurants/cafés/co-op food businesses
- district specialties.

Authenticity is culturally debated rather than biologically fixed.

Ella's hospitality/food co-op belongs naturally in this ecology.

# 11. FOOD / CLASS / CELEBRATION

Basic nutrition is a civic floor.

Market/status differences appear through:
- premium ingredients
- heritage crops/products
- artisanal preparation
- dining atmosphere/service
- private dining space
- frequency of restaurant use.

Birthdays, religious events, civic festivals and family rites carry special foods; exact menus later.

# 12. CLOTHING

Ordinary civilians wear recognizable clothing categories rather than permanent jumpsuits.

Textiles are largely locally regenerated/recycled/bioproduced.
Fashion changes through:
- youth culture
- media
- historical revival
- materials
- arrival trends.

Repair/resale/alteration are normal.

Specialized clothing exists for:
- medicine

- public safety
- manufacturing
- food service

No Habitat dress caste.

# 14. APPEARANCE

Hair, cosmetics, tattoos, piercings and moderate body modification are culturally diverse within safety rules.

Advanced prosthetics/assistive devices may be customized openly.

No universal aesthetic of `perfect repaired body`.

- toilets
- laundry

- efficient fixtures
