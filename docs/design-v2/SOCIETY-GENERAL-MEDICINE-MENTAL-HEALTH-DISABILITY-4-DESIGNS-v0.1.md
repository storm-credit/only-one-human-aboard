# SOCIETY GENERAL MEDICINE / MENTAL HEALTH / DISABILITY — 4 DESIGNS v0.1

Status: `ENCYCLOPEDIC v2 DESIGN COMPARISON / NOT CANON`
Date: 2026-08-17

Parents:
- Reconstruction Canon v1
- ECO-H1 civic healthcare floor
- CJ-H1 criminal/mental-health boundary
- FAM-H1 supported decision/guardianship direction
- EDU-H1 disability/special education

Purpose:
Complete ordinary healthcare beyond Reconstruction:
- primary care
- emergency medicine
- infectious disease
- chronic disease
- cancer/organ repair
- reproductive care
- mental health
- disability
- rehabilitation
- capacity/consent
- scarce specialty care
without making future medicine magical or making Reconstruction the answer to every disease.

---

# DESIGN A — UNIVERSAL INTEGRATED HEALTH SYSTEM

Core:
Basic and emergency healthcare is publicly guaranteed through neighborhood primary care, hospitals/clinics and referral networks.

Strengths:
- fits civic floor
- simple
- supports prevention/closed-system public health
- avoids insurance dystopia

Weaknesses:
- may feel too neat
- public capacity queues need realism
- private/premium layer may disappear

Verdict:
`STRONG BASE`.

---

# DESIGN B — HIGH-TECH PREVENTIVE / PREDICTIVE MEDICINE

Core:
Continuous biosensing/genomic prediction catches most disease early.

Strengths:
- futuristic
- plausible long healthspan support
- prevention useful in closed habitat

Weaknesses:
- destroys medical uncertainty
- privacy nightmare
- turns bodies into permanent state data feeds
- risks adult origin inference shortcut

Verdict:
`USE SELECTIVELY / REJECT TOTAL CONTINUOUS SURVEILLANCE`.

---

# DESIGN C — REGENERATIVE / REPLACEMENT MEDICINE HEAVY

Core:
Organ regeneration, tissue engineering, targeted repair and advanced cancer treatment make many current diseases manageable.

Strengths:
- explains healthspan
- distinguishes future medicine from today
- preserves biological citizens

Weaknesses:
- can make death meaningless
- may blur into Reconstruction
- every injury becomes reversible

Verdict:
`ESSENTIAL TECHNOLOGY LAYER WITH HARD LIMITS`.

---

# DESIGN D — COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH / FUNCTIONAL SUPPORT MODEL

Core:
Mental healthcare is integrated with primary care, schools/workplaces and community services; disability support focuses on access and supported autonomy.

Strengths:
- avoids asylum/prison conflation
- fits long-lived society
- supports family/care realism

Weaknesses:
- can become unrealistically stigma-free
- coercive crisis care still needs design

Verdict:
`ESSENTIAL SOCIAL LAYER`.

---

# RECOMMENDED HYBRID — MED-H1

Name:
**Universal Clinical Floor + Advanced Biological Repair + Privacy-Bounded Prevention + Supported Autonomy**

Status:
`PROVISIONAL PRIORITY / NOT CANON`

## Principle
Medicine is much better than today but not omnipotent.
It can prevent, repair and manage far more disease while preserving:
- uncertainty
- scarce specialty capacity
- chronic conditions
- disability
- aging
- death.

Reconstruction remains a narrow extreme intervention, not universal cure/reset.

---

# 1. HEALTHCARE ACCESS

Civic floor includes:
- primary care
- emergency care
- essential medicines
- pregnancy/prenatal care
- mental-health basic care/crisis response
- rehabilitation essential to function
- disability support assessment

Premium market may exist for:
- convenience
- private rooms/amenities
- elective services
- nonessential enhancements
- extra coaching/rehab amenities

Hard-capacity clinical priority is not auctioned.

---

# 2. CARE NETWORK

Reference model:
- neighborhood primary-care clinics
- larger general hospitals/medical centers
- specialty centers
- rehabilitation facilities
- mobile/field medicine for industrial/Outer Works settings
- Reconstruction-specialist facilities as distinct high-load systems

Not every Habitat has a completely separate health system.
Cross-Habitat referral is ordinary.

---

# 3. PRIMARY CARE

Handles:
- routine illness
- prevention
- chronic disease
- minor injury
- medication
- reproductive health
- mental-health screening/basic therapy referral

Many encounters are mundane and should remain so in prose.

---

# 4. EMERGENCY MEDICINE

Emergency care integrates:
- rapid stabilization
- trauma care
- toxic/contamination exposure
- decompression/inhalation injury
- industrial injury
- acute psychiatric crisis

Mass-casualty triage exists for habitat incidents.
Exact emergency command later Package C.

---

# 5. ADVANCED BIOLOGICAL REPAIR

Common future capabilities may include:
- advanced tissue regeneration
- engineered grafts/organs
- vascular/nerve repair
- targeted cellular therapies
- highly precise prosthetics/implants
- improved cancer control

Hard limits:
- severe brain damage remains special
- whole-person identity cannot be reconstructed from generic tissue
- Neural Anchor/Recovery Map rules remain untouched
- aging cannot be reset routinely.

---

# 6. CANCER

Cancer remains possible but many forms are:
- detected earlier
- molecularly classified
- targeted
- surgically/regeneratively treated

Some aggressive/resistant cancers remain dangerous/fatal.

No `cancer abolished` claim required.

---

# 7. CARDIOVASCULAR / METABOLIC / CHRONIC DISEASE

Better prevention/repair reduces burden, but chronic conditions remain through:
- genetics
- aging
- immune dysfunction
- treatment side effects
- environmental/occupational exposure

Long life creates more time for chronic complexity, not zero illness.

---

# 8. INFECTIOUS DISEASE

Closed ship has strong infection surveillance/public-health capacity because outbreaks matter.

Tools:
- wastewater/environmental monitoring
- clinic reporting
- rapid diagnostics
- vaccination
- targeted isolation/quarantine
- ventilation/environment control

Guardrail:
No permanent personal biosurveillance of every citizen by default.

Imported new pathogens are limited pre-arrival but endogenous evolution/lab/food/ecology infections remain possible.
Arrival adds new biosafety uncertainty.

---

# 9. QUARANTINE

Quarantine/isolation requires:
- actual public-health basis
- proportionality
- support for food/work/family needs
- review when prolonged

No political quarantine disguised as health law.

Package C emergency powers later integrates.

---

# 10. REPRODUCTIVE / PRENATAL MEDICINE

Includes:
- fertility care
- pregnancy monitoring
- prenatal genetics
- Seed procedure/verification as ordinary specialized care
- childbirth
- postpartum care

Hard rule:
Seed completion is prenatal technical provenance; adult physical exam cannot retroactively scan origin class.

---

# 11. BIRTH SETTING

Birth can occur in hospital/birth-center style settings according to risk and preference, within medical safety standards.

Do not make all birth artificial/industrial merely because technology is advanced.

Exact birth culture/ritual later Package B.

---

# 12. MENTAL HEALTH ACCESS

Mental healthcare includes:
- counseling/psychotherapy
- psychiatric medicine
- crisis services
- trauma care
- family therapy where appropriate
- rehabilitation/support programs

Access through primary care and direct specialist routes.

Mental illness exists; stigma may be lower in some contexts but not absent.

---

# 13. PSYCHIATRIC CRISIS / INVOLUNTARY CARE

Short emergency restraint/treatment may occur only when there is concrete acute danger or severe incapacity under legal standards.

Requirements:
- clinical basis
- least-restrictive option
- rapid independent/legal review
- time limit
- right to advocacy

Criminal punishment and psychiatric care remain separate.

Hard rule:
Diagnosis alone does not justify detention.

---

# 14. DECISIONAL CAPACITY

Capacity is decision-specific and can fluctuate.

A person may need support for one complex medical decision while retaining ordinary civic/legal autonomy elsewhere.

Prefer supported decision-making over total guardianship where feasible.

This supports FAM-H1 adult-dependency rules.

---

# 15. ADVANCE DIRECTIVES / MEDICAL PREFERENCES

Citizens may record:
- treatment preferences
- proxy choices
- resuscitation/critical-care wishes
- Reconstruction-related preferences under separate specific rules

Hard rule:
Different documents have different legal force.
An unsubmitted/nonbinding draft like PR-H1 cannot magically become a binding order.

---

# 16. RECONSTRUCTION REFERRAL BOUNDARY

Reconstruction is considered only in specific severe neurological/body-loss contexts under existing Canon.

It is not used merely because:
- someone is old
- someone dislikes disability
- someone has ordinary depression
- a relationship wants a personality reset
- a cancer patient wants a younger body

This protects death/disability stakes.

---

# 17. REHABILITATION

Rehabilitation is a major normal field because advanced repair does not instantly restore function.

Includes:
- physical
- occupational
- cognitive
- speech/communication
- psychosocial
- return-to-work/school

Ines can develop work beyond Maren's cases here.

---

# 18. DISABILITY MODEL

Disability is not treated solely as a medical defect to erase.

System combines:
- medical treatment if wanted/useful
- assistive technology
- accessible housing/transit/work/school
- income support
- workplace accommodation
- supported autonomy

Some impairments remain even with advanced medicine.

No moral hierarchy of `fully repaired` over disabled citizens.

---

# 19. PROSTHETICS / IMPLANTS

Advanced prosthetics and implants can be common for disability/repair.

They do not alter civic personhood.

No cybernetic upgrade race is needed.
Elective enhancement, if any, remains later Culture/Medicine detail and cannot become a second premise.

---

# 20. NEURODIVERSITY / DEVELOPMENTAL CONDITIONS

These persist.
Support focuses on:
- education/access
- communication
- adaptive services
- mental-health care if needed
rather than compulsory normalization.

Seed does not erase neurodiversity by design.

---

# 21. PAIN / PALLIATIVE CARE

Palliative care exists even with advanced medicine.

Goals:
- pain/symptom relief
- informed choice
- family support
- dignity
- end-of-life planning

This is essential because death remains real.

Exact assisted-dying rules are deferred to Death/Finality block.

---

# 22. MEDICAL PRIVACY

Medical records are protected.

Access is role/purpose-limited and logged.

Special sensitivity:
- reproductive records
- Seed provenance
- Continuity/Reconstruction
- mental health

CJ-H1 investigative access requires heightened legal basis.

No universal employer/school access.

---

# 23. HEALTH DATA / AI

AI/automation may assist:
- diagnostics
- triage
- scheduling
- imaging/monitoring
- drug selection
- population-health modeling

But high-impact decisions remain accountable to clinicians/institutions.

No AI may infer/declare adult origin from ordinary medical data contrary to Canon.

Exact AI role later Package C.

---

# 24. SCARCE SPECIALTY CAPACITY

Scarcity can occur in:
- Reconstruction
- ICU/mass casualty
- rare specialists
- complex surgery
- high-load rehabilitation

Allocation uses clinical/need/public rules, not bidding.

Rights expansion can still create queues and tradeoffs, supporting Act 4.

---

# 25. HEALTHCARE WORKFORCE

Includes:
- physicians/advanced clinicians
- nurses/care staff
- therapists
- technicians
- mental-health professionals
- public-health specialists
- rehabilitation staff

No one-doctor-per-domain genius structure.

Cross-Habitat referral prevents each location from having every rare specialty.

---

# 26. OCCUPATIONAL MEDICINE

Strong due industrial/Outer Works work.

Functions:
- exposure monitoring
- fitness-for-duty where genuinely safety relevant
- rehabilitation/return-to-work
- hazard prevention

Fitness-for-duty cannot become blanket employer access to all medical data.

---

# 27. AGING / GERIATRICS

Healthy aging is extended, but older citizens still experience:
- frailty
- chronic disease
- sensory change
- cognitive impairment
- care needs

Geriatric care and adapted housing remain real.

No routine chronological reset.

---

# 28. DEMENTIA / COGNITIVE DECLINE

Some forms can be delayed/treated better, but not perfectly eliminated.

Reconstruction cannot be casually used as a memory/personality backup solution.

Advance planning, supported decision-making and family care remain relevant.

---

# 29. CHILD HEALTH

Includes:
- routine pediatrics
- vaccination
- developmental care
- injury
- mental health
- disability support

School health/privacy rules later integrate EDU-H1.

---

# 30. ARRIVAL TRANSITION

New health challenges:
- prolonged deployment medicine
- new gravity/environment adaptation if different
- surface biosafety
- remote care
- smaller early-site specialty capacity
- evacuation back to Meridian/orbit

Hard rule:
Surface foothold does not instantly reproduce Meridian's full medical system.

Package E later defines staging.
