## 6. Rebound — REBOUND-H1

Representative ship-born near-zero-g sport.

### Court
Enclosed padded rectangular-prism court, adult/community standard order:
- ~18m long
- ~12m wide
- ~10m high.

All six surfaces playable push-off surfaces.
Fixed rails/grip zones for restart/safety.

### Teams
5 vs 5, rolling substitutions.

### Movement
- no personal thrusters,
- push-off/wall/rail movement,
- deliberate dangerous grabbing/checking = foul.

### Ball
Soft grippy low-mass rebound ball.

### Scoring
One defended circular gate at each end.
- 1 point: clean gate passage from attacking half.
- 2 points: release behind mid-court plane and clean passage.

### Possession
~5-second hold limit.
No Earth-style traveling rule; cannot deliberately anchor indefinitely while holding.

### Match
Community/youth standard:
4 × ~7-minute active-play periods.

### Core skills
trajectory anticipation / orientation / timed push-off / passing lanes / spatial marking / safe collision avoidance / gate defense.

No thruster-flying/combat drift.
Noah may be ordinary capable participant, not prodigy.
