# INFRA — AI / AUTOMATION / ROBOTICS — 4 DESIGNS v0.1

Status: `ENCYCLOPEDIC v2 DESIGN COMPARISON / PACKAGE C / NOT CANON`
Date: 2026-08-17
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Existing hard constraints:
- no central AI sovereignty / hidden ship-AI dictatorship.
- Seed is prenatal developmental technology, not an AI mind upload.
- current citizens are biological embodied people.
- Derived Person is not an active routine civic category.
- no bigger late ontology twist.
- AI may support narrow safety automation but contested civic decisions require accountable human institutions.
- personal/protected data remains permissioned.
- public-safety systems cannot autonomously decide guilt and apply open-ended lethal force.

Goal:
Make a 450-year advanced civilization plausibly automated without making human work, politics, law, care, mystery or agency obsolete.

---

# DESIGN A — CENTRAL SHIP INTELLIGENCE

One extremely capable integrated AI operates infrastructure, information and governance support.

Advantages:
- technically convenient,
- simple explanation,
- high coordination efficiency.

Fatal weaknesses:
- becomes hidden sovereign,
- can solve/know too much,
- turns every mystery into `why didn't the AI know?`,
- crowds out human institutions and protagonists.

Verdict: **REJECT as core architecture.**

---

# DESIGN B — PERSONAL AGENT SOCIETY

Every citizen has a powerful persistent personal AI handling schedules, negotiation, commerce, education, health and communication.

Advantages:
- plausible advanced consumer tech,
- useful daily-life texture.

Weaknesses:
- can become invisible second protagonist in every scene,
- privacy and manipulation problems,
- makes ordinary misunderstandings too easy to solve,
- risks quasi-person AI ontology.

Verdict: useful only under strong scope limits.

---

# DESIGN C — DOMAIN-BOUND FEDERATED INTELLIGENCE

Different certified systems serve different domains:
- utilities,
- transit,
- medicine,
- logistics,
- archives/search,
- education,
- personal assistance,
- industrial control.

No one model has universal access or authority.

Advantages:
- matches legal/institutional pluralism,
- supports privacy boundaries,
- reduces single-point epistemic omniscience,
- preserves human accountability.

Weaknesses:
- coordination cost,
- version/interface problems,
- still needs clear authority rules.

---

# DESIGN D — ROBOT-HEAVY POST-LABOR CITY

Most physical work is performed by autonomous machines; humans mainly create/govern/socialize.

Advantages:
- futuristic,
- reduces dangerous labor.

Weaknesses:
- weak fit with Jun/Amara/Kai and existing work texture,
- risks abundance utopia,
- ignores maintenance/material/thermal costs,
- can make arrival-bootstrap labor trivial.

Verdict: reject as total model; retain high automation in selected sectors.

---

# RECOMMENDED HYBRID — AI-H1

# `Federated Domain Intelligence + Bounded Personal Agents + Heavy Tool Automation + Human Accountability`

Status: `PROVISIONAL PRIORITY / NOT CANON`

Core rule:
**AI can be highly capable without being sovereign, omniscient or a routine civic person.**

---

# 1. System Classes

Author-facing classes, not necessarily reader-facing terms.

## A1 — Control Automation
Examples:
- pump/valve regulation,
- thermal management,
- power switching,
- train safety,
- pressure/fire safeties,
- manufacturing control.

Traits:
- narrow domain,
- deterministic/verified control layers preferred for immediate safety,
- can execute pre-authorized automatic actions,
- extensive logging.

## A2 — Predictive / Diagnostic Systems
Examples:
- maintenance prediction,
- medical image/biomarker analysis,
- contamination detection,
- traffic forecasting,
- capacity simulation.

Traits:
- recommend/rank/simulate,
- uncertainty represented,
- human professionals remain responsible for consequential interpretation.

## A3 — Administrative / Search Agents
Examples:
- records retrieval,
- form completion,
- scheduling,
- translation,
- summarization,
- case-law search,
- document cross-reference.

Traits:
- permission-scoped,
- cannot silently cross protected databases,
- citations/provenance expected in professional use.

## A4 — Personal Assistants
Examples:
- calendar,
- communication drafting,
- household automation,
- shopping/search,
- navigation,
- study help,
- accessibility support.

Traits:
- natural conversational interface possible,
- user-controlled memory scopes,
- no assumption of independent civic will/personhood,
- no universal access to work/medical/legal secrets.

## A5 — Embodied Robots / Drones
Examples:
- cleaning,
- warehouse handling,
- agriculture,
- inspection,
- hazardous maintenance,
- hospital logistics,
- Outer Works robotics,
- construction/fabrication support.

Traits:
- task/zone permissions,
- physical safety limits,
- remote supervision or exception escalation where needed,
- no magical independence from maintenance/parts/energy.

# 2. No Single Ship Mind

Meridian has:
- many control systems,
- many models,
- many databases,
- many organizational owners,
- shared standards/interfaces,
- emergency coordination layers.

It does **not** have one conscious/omniscient `Meridian AI` that sees all citizens and decides civilization policy.

This is both a technical resilience choice and a constitutional choice developed over centuries.

---

# 3. AI Personhood Boundary

Current setting direction:
Routine deployed AI systems are treated as **tools/services**, not ordinary civic persons.

They may:
- speak naturally,
- generate plans/text/media,
- simulate social responses,
- maintain context within permissions.

They do not, by default:
- possess independent citizenship,
- own unrestricted property,
- vote,
- serve as legal parent,
- become criminal defendant in place of responsible humans/organizations,
- claim human continuity through data persistence.

Hard stop:
No late reveal that ordinary service AIs were secretly the `real people` or that Seeded citizens are AI instances.

If philosophical debates about machine personhood exist historically, they remain background and cannot displace the novel's core conflict.

---

# 4. Why Human Work Still Exists

Automation is extensive, but human labor remains because of:
- accountability,
- exception handling,
- cross-domain judgment,
- care/relationship work,
- physical improvisation in irregular old infrastructure,
- legal/political value decisions,
- maintenance of automation itself,
- design/creative work,
- trust/social legitimacy,
- novel conditions during arrival.

Automation changes job composition and work hours; it does not erase work.

This fits LAB-H1:
Meridian plans skills and trains people rather than coercively assigning lives.

---

# 5. Amara / Water-Reclamation Work

Amara does not manually turn every valve.
Her work can include:
- shift supervision,
- anomaly interpretation,
- physical inspection,
- contamination response,
- work permits/coordination,
- deciding whether a predictive alert is real,
- managing maintenance teams and automation handoffs.

No unique biological/origin ability makes her better at the job.

---

# 6. Jun / Maintenance Work

Old Quarter maintenance is automation-resistant at the margins because:
- legacy layouts,
- one-off repairs,
- heritage constraints,
- undocumented prior modifications,
- resident coordination,
- temporary safe-work design.

Robots/tools assist heavily; humans still own tradeoffs and现场 adaptation.

---

# 7. Kai / Logistics Work

Routing models optimize normal flows.
Kai's apprenticeship learns:
- physical cargo rules,
- exception handling,
- dangerous-goods separation,
- incident rerouting,
- human coordination,
- arrival staging.

A model can propose routes; responsible staff approve unusual high-consequence exceptions.

---

# 8. Medicine

AI may assist:
- diagnostics,
- prognosis,
- treatment planning,
- monitoring,
- resource scheduling.

But:
- informed consent remains human-facing,
- clinicians remain accountable,
- Reconstruction identity/legal determinations are not delegated to a model,
- no AI can infer Natural/Seeded origin from an adult body where Canon says no adult scanner exists.

---

# 9. Law / Government

Allowed:
- search precedents,
- summarize records,
- model budget/capacity options,
- flag conflicts,
- draft administrative text,
- detect statistical disparities.

Not allowed as final authority:
- adjudicate citizenship/personhood autonomously,
- sentence criminals,
- terminate parental rights,
- authorize indefinite detention,
- choose political distributive values,
- declare Amara a legitimacy key.

Human officeholders cannot hide behind `the model decided`.

---

# 10. Capacity Allocation

Models can compute feasible ranges for:
- birth throughput,
- hospital/Reconstruction capacity,
- housing,
- thermal margins,
- strategic spares.

They cannot decide who morally deserves scarce capacity without law/process.

This integrates GOV-H1/ECO-H1/H6.

---

# 11. Education

AI tutors can:
- explain,
- practice,
- adapt exercises,
- translate,
- support disability access.

Schools still matter for:
- socialization,
- labs/physical work,
- sport/arts,
- mentorship,
- civic life,
- safeguarding,
- credentialing.

No single childhood aptitude model fixes a person's career.

---

# 12. Media / Generative Content

Synthetic media is normal enough that provenance matters.

Systems may create:
- entertainment,
- edits,
- simulations,
- personalized assistance.

Media ecology therefore values:
- source signatures/provenance,
- editorial reputation,
- original capture chains,
- disclosure norms.

No perfect deepfake detector guarantees truth.

---

# 13. Personal-Agent Memory

Memory scopes may include:
- local/private household history,
- user-approved long-term preferences,
- temporary task memory,
- institution-managed records accessed only through permission.

Hard rule:
A personal assistant does not automatically know every protected fact about its user.

Citizens can delete/limit some personal-agent memory, subject to lawful record-retention rules for separate institutional systems.

---

# 14. Robotics in Public Space

Common but not visually overwhelming.

Likely visible forms:
- cleaning/service machines,
- delivery carts,
- maintenance crawlers,
- warehouse manipulators,
- hospital logistics units,
- inspection drones in controlled spaces.

Human-shaped android servants are not the default.
Reason:
Specialized geometry is cheaper/safer and avoids unnecessary personlike social ambiguity.

---

# 15. Household Robots

Ordinary homes may use:
- cleaning systems,
- kitchen appliances,
- laundry handling,
- accessibility aids.

Not every household owns a humanoid robot.
Shared/building services may be more common for expensive equipment.

---

# 16. Outer Works Robotics

High automation is especially valuable for:
- inspection,
- radiation exposure,
- hot/cold exterior work,
- tethered repair,
- dangerous precursor tasks.

Human EVA remains necessary for irregular, high-value, difficult-to-automate operations.

No robot army.

---

# 17. Autonomous Force Boundary

Public-safety robots may assist with:
- observation under lawful authority,
- shields/barriers,
- rescue,
- hazardous-object handling,
- remote communication.

They may not independently:
- determine guilt,
- select open-ended punishment,
- use lethal force based on general predictive judgment.

Any automated physical safety action must be narrowly defined and auditable.

---

# 18. Model Certification / Updates

High-consequence domain systems require:
- version control,
- test/certification,
- rollback,
- audit logs,
- failure reporting,
- independent review for major changes,
- human-readable operating limits.

No critical system silently self-rewrites into an unreviewable black box.

Adaptive learning may occur within bounded validated envelopes or be promoted through controlled update processes.

---

# 19. Failure Modes

AI/automation can fail through:
- bad sensors,
- distribution shift,
- corrupted data,
- model error,
- interface mismatch,
- software bug,
- cyberattack,
- human misuse,
- overtrust,
- outdated legacy integration.

Professional culture treats automation as fallible infrastructure.

---

# 20. Cybersecurity

Principles:
- segmentation by domain,
- least-privilege access,
- strong identity/authentication,
- offline/fallback modes for safety-critical systems,
- physical interlocks for catastrophic actions,
- audit trails,
- incident response.

No universal backdoor that instantly controls the ship.

---

# 21. Privacy

Models cannot freely pool:
- medical,
- school,
- transit,
- household,
- legal,
- social,
- employment data
into a universal citizen score.

Cross-domain linkage requires a lawful purpose and scoped authority.

This is essential to Count/Amara re-identification fairness.

---

# 22. Economic Role

AI/robotics lowers labor needs and raises productivity, but costs remain:
- fabrication,
- rare components,
- maintenance,
- energy/heat,
- software assurance,
- skilled oversight.

Advanced automation is part of why a 300k closed society can maintain complex infrastructure, not a source of infinite abundance.

---

# 23. Arrival

Arrival increases need for humans and robots together because:
- novel terrain/materials,
- new industrial chains,
- communication delays between sites,
- incomplete infrastructure,
- higher physical risk.

Robots extend reach; they do not instantly produce a mature colony.

**AI-H1 — Federated Domain Intelligence + Bounded Personal Agents + Heavy Tool Automation + Human Accountability**

Hard stops retained:
- no central AI sovereign,
- no hidden sentient-AI twist,
- no AI origin scanner,
- no autonomous civic adjudication,
