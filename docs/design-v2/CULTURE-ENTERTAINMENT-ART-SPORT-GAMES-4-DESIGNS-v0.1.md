Purpose:
Design what citizens do for pleasure, spectatorship, expression and hobbies across 450 years.

---

# DESIGN A — EARTH CULTURE PRESERVATION DOMINANT
Core: archived Earth music, film, literature and sports remain central.
Strength: continuity/readability.
Weakness: ship-born civilization feels culturally sterile.
Verdict: `HERITAGE LAYER ONLY`.

# DESIGN B — FULL IMMERSIVE FUTURE ENTERTAINMENT
Core: VR/immersive simulation dominates leisure.
Strength: futuristic.
Weakness: makes physical places/sports/live arts irrelevant; generic VR trope.
Verdict: `ONE MEDIUM AMONG MANY`.

# DESIGN C — MISSION-CULTURE ENTERTAINMENT
Core: ship history, engineering and destination themes dominate art/sport.
Strength: distinctive.
Weakness: citizens have no life outside premise.
Verdict: `REJECT AS CULTURAL MONOCULTURE`.

# DESIGN D — PLURAL OLD/NEW MEDIA + PHYSICAL/IMMERSIVE LEISURE
Core: Earth heritage, 450 years of ship-born works, live arts, games, physical sport and immersive media coexist.
Verdict: `CURRENT BEST BASE`.

Most casual readers need not calculate this unless sport scene benefits.

## 12. ZERO-G SPORT — FUNCTIONAL CANDIDATE ZG-H1

Author-facing name:
**3D Rebound Ball / Vector Court candidate**
Proper reader-facing sport name remains ELASTIC until Character/Culture naming pass.

Venue:
- padded enclosed 3D court in a nonrotating/low-g recreation section near Spine facilities
- six usable wall surfaces with launch zones/hand rails/pads

Movement:
- players push off court surfaces/rails
- no personal thrusters in standard play
- momentum management and body orientation are core skill
- wall contact permits redirection

Object:
- soft/high-visibility ball with predictable rebound behavior
- no self-propulsion

Basic objective candidate:
Teams move the ball by passing/deflection and score by sending it through a defended goal aperture/zone at opposite ends.

Core tactical difference:
After launch, a player cannot simply stop in empty space; positioning is about planned trajectories, teammate passes, wall return and timing.

Safety:
- limited-contact rules
- padded boundaries
- helmet/head protection candidate for competitive play
- speed limits/rules enforced by court design and officiating

Why it is culturally valuable:
It is genuinely born from ship architecture rather than an Earth sport merely played upside down.

No final proper name or league format yet.

Creative automation/tools can assist composition/editing/visual production.

Meridian performances/games/sports travel into orbit/surface and adapt over time.
