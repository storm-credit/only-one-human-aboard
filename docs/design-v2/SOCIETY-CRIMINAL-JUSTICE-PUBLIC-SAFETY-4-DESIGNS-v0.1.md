- GOV-H1 candidate: Layered Civic Republic + Commons Constraints + Narrow Safety Command

Purpose:
Design ordinary criminal justice, policing/public safety, investigation, detention, sentencing, surveillance limits and weapons policy for a ~300k closed generation-ship civilization.

Must fit existing hard boundaries:
- no captain dictatorship
- no central AI sovereignty
- no Habitat caste/federal tribalism
- privacy rights matter
- current citizens are full persons regardless of cognitive origin
- life-support/transport/industrial systems create genuine public-safety externalities
- emergency technical command is narrow and reviewable
- reader-facing institution-name burden must stay low

Evaluation criteria:
1. rights protection
2. closed-habitat safety realism
3. ordinary-crime plausibility
4. anti-surveillance-state
5. ability to handle severe violence
6. cyber/record crime realism
7. emergency compatibility
8. citizen legibility
9. story usefulness without crime-genre takeover
10. compatibility with Material Culture weapon/equipment rules

# DESIGN A — RESTORATIVE CIVIC JUSTICE FIRST

Core:
Most nonviolent and lower-harm offenses prioritize:
- restitution
- mediated repair
- treatment
- supervised community obligations
- limited liberty restrictions
rather than incarceration.

Serious violence still permits secure detention.

Strengths:
- closed society has incentive to reintegrate rather than permanently discard citizens
- fits scarce human capital and long-lived social networks
- reduces prison-worldbuilding burden
- strong for family/work/property offenses

Weaknesses:
- can become utopian
- survivors may be pressured into reconciliation
- repeat coercive/violent offenders expose weakness
- public safety can look soft because the author wants a humane future

Biggest trap:
`restorative justice magically works for everyone`.

Verdict:
`USE AS LOWER/MID-SEVERITY BASE, NOT WHOLE SYSTEM`.

---

# DESIGN B — RISK-TIERED PUBLIC SAFETY STATE

Core:
System distinguishes harm/risk tiers and escalates coercion accordingly.

Typical ladder:
1. warning/citation/service repair
2. restitution/supervision/treatment
3. movement/access/occupational restrictions
4. pretrial secure detention when necessary
5. long-term secure custody for serious persistent danger

Strong procedural review surrounds each step.

Strengths:
- realistic for closed habitat
- can incapacitate dangerous offenders
- makes dangerous-infrastructure access restrictions meaningful
- clear citizen legibility

Weaknesses:
- `risk scoring` can become algorithmic social control
- preventive restrictions can punish predicted behavior
- technical categories may hide class bias

Biggest trap:
`Minority Report by administrative score`.

Verdict:
`STRONG STRUCTURE IF RISK IS EVIDENCE-BASED AND HUMAN-REVIEWED`.

---

# DESIGN C — LOCAL PEACE SERVICE + SHIPWIDE MAJOR-CRIME CAPABILITY

Core:
Most public safety is neighborhood/local:
- patrol
- disputes
- domestic safety
- missing persons
- youth incidents
- minor theft/damage
- traffic/transit order

Cross-Habitat/serious cases use a small shipwide investigative function:
- homicide/serious violence
- major fraud/corruption
- cyber/identity/record manipulation
- critical-infrastructure interference
- coordinated organized crime

Strengths:
- government feels lived-in locally
- prevents one giant paramilitary police institution
- supports Habitat variety without sovereignty
- serious crimes still get specialist capacity

Weaknesses:
- local capture/favoritism
- uneven practices between districts
- jurisdiction friction
- specialist unit can become elite secret police if badly bounded

Biggest trap:
`central serious-crime unit quietly becomes intelligence service`.

Verdict:
`STRONG ORGANIZATIONAL LAYER`.

---

# DESIGN D — SENSOR-HEAVY PREVENTIVE SECURITY

Core:
Because the ship has pervasive systems telemetry, public safety uses extensive sensor logs, identity tracking and automated anomaly detection to prevent crime.

Strengths:
- technically plausible temptation
- makes sabotage difficult
- efficient in high-risk engineering spaces

Weaknesses:
- destroys privacy premise
- makes ordinary crime/mystery implausible
- centralizes state power
- blurs safety telemetry with civic surveillance
- AI becomes de facto coercive authority

Biggest trap:
`the ship knows where everyone is all the time`.

Verdict:
`REJECT AS GENERAL MODEL / RETAIN ONLY NARROW SAFETY TELEMETRY`.

# RECOMMENDED HYBRID — CJ-H1

Name:
**Layered Public Safety + Restorative Default + Narrow Coercive Escalation**

Status:
`PROVISIONAL PRIORITY / NOT CANON`

## Principle
The state does not treat every offense as a reason to cage a citizen, but it retains real power to investigate, restrain and securely detain people who pose demonstrated serious danger.

The system is designed around four facts:
1. Meridian is a city, not a prison.
2. Citizens cannot be casually exiled from a sealed civilization.
3. Violence and coercion still exist.
4. Some actions can endanger hundreds or thousands through shared infrastructure.

---

# 1. PUBLIC SAFETY ORGANIZATION

## Local civic safety teams
District/Habitat-based teams handle most ordinary incidents:
- assault/threats
- domestic violence
- theft/property damage
- harassment/stalking
- missing persons
- youth incidents
- public disorder
- transit incidents
- emergency first response until medical/technical teams arrive

They are civil public-safety officers, not military crew.

## Shipwide serious-investigation function
A smaller specialist investigative layer handles:
- homicide and severe violence
- cross-Habitat organized fraud
- serious corruption
- cyber intrusion
- identity/credential fraud
- medical/Continuity record tampering
- critical infrastructure interference
- cases involving public-safety officers themselves, with independent oversight involvement

Naming guardrail:
Reader does not need a flashy proper noun unless a recurring character requires one.

## Critical infrastructure security
High-risk facilities maintain access-control/security personnel, but these staff are not a separate sovereign police force.
They can:
- deny hazardous-zone access
- stop unsafe work
- preserve a scene
- summon civic public safety
They cannot run general criminal investigations outside their mandate.

---

# 2. CRIME BASELINE

Meridian is neither crime-free nor dystopian.

Likely ordinary categories:
- theft and resale fraud
- digital/account fraud
- workplace theft/diversion
- domestic abuse/coercive control
- assault
- sexual violence
- harassment/stalking
- black/gray market transactions
- credential/permit fraud
- corruption/favor trading
- privacy/data offenses
- vandalism
- dangerous negligence

Less common but real:
- homicide
- kidnapping/unlawful confinement
- sophisticated cyber attacks
- deliberate infrastructure interference

Hard rule:
Do not make `critical sabotage` the default serious crime. Most crime should still be recognizably human and ordinary.

---

# 3. INVESTIGATION POWERS

Routine investigation may use:
- witness interviews
- physical/forensic evidence
- transaction and access records with legal process
- device/account search under warrant/order
- facility safety logs where relevant
- medical/Continuity records only under heightened protected-data rules

Search/seizure principle:
**availability of data does not equal automatic state entitlement to read it.**

High-sensitivity categories require stronger authorization:
- medical data
- reproductive/Seed provenance
- Continuity/Reconstruction records
- private communications
- household interior recordings if any exist

Emergency exception:
Immediate access can be granted to stop imminent loss of life/critical-system failure, followed by mandatory logging and retrospective review.

---

# 4. SENSOR / SURVEILLANCE BOUNDARY

Meridian has extensive engineering telemetry, but it is not a universal personal-surveillance grid.

Separate:
- `SYSTEM TELEMETRY`: pressure, heat, flow, structural stress, access state, machine operation
- `CIVIC/PERSONAL DATA`: identity, communications, location history, home life, medical data

A pressure sensor can show a hatch cycled.
It does not automatically prove who morally committed a crime.

Public spaces may have safety/security sensing, but:
- retention is limited by category
- access is logged
- investigative access requires authority except immediate hazard
- private homes are not continuously state-recorded by default
- raw telemetry cannot be silently repurposed into permanent social scoring

AI may flag anomalies; it may not determine guilt or authorize coercive search on its own.

---

# 5. ARREST / TEMPORARY RESTRAINT

Officers may use temporary restraint when there is a concrete basis to believe a person:
- committed a serious offense
- presents immediate danger
- is likely to flee into inaccessible/critical areas
- is actively interfering with emergency response

Prompt review is required.

Closed-habitat difference:
`flight risk` often means access to restricted transport/work zones or evidence systems, not fleeing the country.

---

# 6. PRETRIAL DETENTION

Detention is not automatic after accusation.
Alternatives include:
- supervised release
- no-contact orders
- location/access restrictions
- temporary removal from safety-critical work
- monitored schedule requirements
- protected housing for survivors/witnesses

Secure pretrial detention is reserved for substantial demonstrated risk.

Guardrail:
No algorithmic risk score alone can jail a person.

---

# 7. SENTENCING PHILOSOPHY

## Lower-harm/nonviolent
Preferred tools:
- restitution
- fines where meaningful
- community/service obligations
- treatment
- supervised behavioral conditions
- temporary professional/access restrictions
- mediation/restorative process only with victim consent and safety safeguards

## Serious violence / coercive abuse
May include:
- substantial liberty restriction
- secure custody
- long-term no-contact protections
- intensive treatment where relevant
- continuing review without automatic release

## Critical infrastructure crimes
Sentence must track actual culpability and harm/risk, not treat every rule breach as treason.
Intentional mass-endangerment can justify very long secure custody.

Hard rule:
No death penalty.
No routine physical exile into unsafe space.
No `air ration punishment` or deprivation of civic survival floor.

---

# 8. SECURE CUSTODY

Meridian does maintain secure residential custody capacity, but not a giant carceral sector.

Features:
- small scale relative to population
- medical/mental-health access
- education/work where safe
- external oversight
- family/contact rules subject to safety
- periodic legal review for unusually long restrictions

Rationale:
A closed society needs incapacitation for rare dangerous offenders, but wasting lives through brutal warehousing is economically and morally costly.

---

# 9. DOMESTIC / SEXUAL VIOLENCE

Restorative default does **not** mean forced reconciliation.

Required:
- emergency separation/no-contact orders
- survivor-controlled support choices where possible
- protected temporary housing
- confidential medical/advocacy access
- independent investigation
- child-safety intervention where needed

Mediation is not presumed appropriate.

This prevents a future-humanist system from becoming coercively reconciliationist.

---

# 10. JUVENILE JUSTICE

Youth cases emphasize:
- developmental responsibility
- family/school support
- restitution/repair
- treatment
- supervised autonomy

Serious violent youth behavior can still require secure care.

Juvenile records have stronger sealing/limited-use rules.

No `wise child treated as adult because futuristic society` shortcut.

---

# 11. MENTAL HEALTH / CRIMINAL RESPONSIBILITY

Mental illness is neither an excuse-all nor danger stereotype.

System distinguishes:
- criminal culpability
- acute incapacity
- treatment need
- ongoing demonstrable danger

Emergency psychiatric restraint requires separate clinical/legal review from criminal punishment.

Dependency:
Package A general medicine/mental health must later deepen exact standards.

---

# 12. CYBERCRIME / IDENTITY / RECORD TAMPERING

High-importance offenses include:
- credential theft
- financial-account compromise
- access-control manipulation
- false civic records
- medical-data theft
- archive tampering
- unauthorized Continuity/Reconstruction record access
- malicious industrial-control interference

But architecture avoids one magical master database.

Evidence rules must distinguish:
- data alteration
- access metadata
- account compromise
- actual human authorization

Hard rule:
`the log says Maren accessed it` is evidence, not metaphysical proof of motive/context.
This is especially important for PR-H1.

---

# 13. CORRUPTION / PUBLIC-OFFICIAL CRIME

Public officials can be investigated for:
- bribery
- favoritism
- procurement fraud
- misuse of protected data
- coercive abuse
- evidence manipulation
- emergency-power abuse

Investigations involving public-safety leadership require structurally independent review/prosecutorial assignment.

No police self-certification for severe misconduct.

---

# 14. PROSECUTION / DEFENSE / COURTS

System requires separate functions for:
- investigation
- charging/prosecution
- defense advocacy
- adjudication

No public-safety officer simultaneously determines guilt/sentence.

Right to counsel/advocacy applies in serious criminal matters.

Courts/tribunals must provide:
- evidentiary challenge
- appeal
- review of searches/detention
- remedies for unlawful state conduct

Exact court names/tiers remain for Society Bible consolidation.

---

# 15. WEAPONS POLICY — DEPENDENCY WITH PACKAGE C-M

Civilian weapon ownership is not zero, but **high-penetration/high-energy weapons are strongly restricted** because habitat infrastructure creates unusual external risk.

General civilian self-defense options lean toward:
- alarms/communication
- physical restraint/escape tools
- low-damage defensive devices under regulation

Public-safety equipment favors:
- de-escalation
- barriers/restraint
- low-penetration incapacitation
- protective equipment

Higher-force weapons exist for exceptional threat environments but are tightly controlled, logged and stored.

Outer Works/industrial tools may be dangerous despite not being `weapons`; access law therefore focuses on capability/context as well as object labels.

Exact equipment catalog remains Package C-M provisional until this criminal-justice block is consolidated.

---

# 16. USE OF FORCE

Force ladder:
1. presence/verbal direction
2. separation/barrier/control
3. restraint/incapacitation
4. higher force only for serious imminent harm

Lethal force is legally exceptional and reviewable.

Closed-space guardrail:
Methods with high risk of hull/system penetration or uncontrolled environmental contamination are strongly disfavored.

Every serious use of force generates mandatory incident review.

---

# 17. PROTEST / CIVIL DISOBEDIENCE

Political dissent is not a security offense merely because the ship is fragile.

Peaceful protest is protected.

Public-safety intervention focuses on:
- actual violence
- coercion
- blocked emergency access
- intrusion into hazardous critical areas

Technical safety boundaries may restrict location/method without criminalizing viewpoint.

This is crucial to avoid `life support means government can ban dissent` authoritarian drift.

---

# 18. EMERGENCY PUBLIC SAFETY

During fire/decompression/contamination/major systems incidents:
- technical incident command controls hazard zones
- public-safety staff support evacuation/perimeter/order
- civic government handles broader rights restrictions if they persist

Temporary emergency access/search may occur for immediate rescue/safety.
It does not automatically authorize unrelated criminal fishing expeditions.

Evidence discovered during emergency response requires later legal handling rules.

---

# 19. ORGANIZED CRIME / BLACK MARKETS

A closed, highly tracked economy does not eliminate illicit markets.
Likely gray/black markets cluster around:
- restricted luxury goods
- unauthorized repair/modification
- stolen credentials/access
- scarce specialty services
- diverted industrial materials
- privacy/data services

Avoid giant mafia controlling air/water.
Critical commons are too monitored/interdependent for stable private criminal sovereignty without major institutional complicity.

---

# 20. ORIGIN / RECONSTRUCTION EQUALITY

Criminal responsibility cannot be assigned differently merely because a citizen is Seeded- or Natural-Origin.

Reconstruction raises evidentiary/competency questions only where genuinely relevant:
- memory reliability
- medical capacity
- identity/document continuity

No `synthetic-origin crime predictor` or origin-based preventive policing.

Amara receives no special criminal immunity or liability.

---

# 21. ARRIVAL TRANSITION

As population spreads to ship/orbit/surface footholds:
- local public-safety jurisdictions must be created gradually
- shipwide rights minimums continue during transition
- custody/charges/orders need cross-location recognition
- emergency command may differ by habitat/platform

Do not create legal vacuum just because people leave Meridian.

Dependency:
Package E must later design cross-jurisdiction enforcement and custody after arrival.

---

# 22. ORDINARY CITIZEN EXPERIENCE

Most citizens experience criminal justice rarely.
More common contact:
- reporting theft/fraud
- noise/dispute mediation
- lost/missing person help
- protective/no-contact order
- cyber-account recovery referral
- accident scene management
- community event safety

The setting should not feel police-saturated.

---

# 23. READER-FACING NAMING RULE

Keep proper nouns low.

- `공공안전요원 / public-safety officer`
- `수사관`
- ordinary `법원/재판부`
- existing `시민항소원` only where jurisdiction fits

Do not invent ten bureaus unless recurring plot actually requires them.
