# 1. Shipwide / Recurring Civic Observances — CULT-OBS-H1

## 1. 출항일 / Departure Day
Function:
annual remembrance of Meridian's departure/founding.

Tone:
- neither pure patriotic triumph nor funeral,
- family meals, school history, public performances/exhibits, memorial components,
- Earth-origin/founding heritage becomes unusually visible.

Social disagreement:
- some treat it as ancestry/founding day,
- some criticize mission romanticism,
- younger citizens often experience it mainly as a public holiday/festival.

Hard:
no mandatory ideology.

## 2. 동등시민일 / Equal Citizenship Day
Function:
commemorates the legal reform era that removed developmental-origin distinctions from civic personhood/citizenship.

Tone:
- rights/history education,
- civic groups/legal institutions often hold events,
- not everyone celebrates in the same political style.

Narrative value:
makes modern origin-neutral rights culturally legible before Count without revealing Count.

Hard:
does not imply old discrimination was identical to modern Earth race categories.

## 3. 메리디언의 밤 / Meridian Night
Ship-born secular observance/festival.

Core practice:
- designated public atria/squares temporarily lower nonessential decorative lighting,
- current navigation/star imagery + historical Earth sky archives may be projected/displayed,
- live music, food stalls, neighborhood performances and late-night gatherings are common.

Meaning:
celebrates `Meridian is a lived home under a real sky it cannot normally see` without requiring religion.

Hard:
- essential lighting/safety never switched off,
- no mystical star ritual,
- participation optional.

## District festivals
Exist widely but exact names stay C2 unless a location recurs.
The three above are enough for shipwide reference grammar.

# 2. Small Recurring Ship-Born Food Set — CULT-FOOD-H1

These are reader-translation names; regional/family variants exist.

## 겹전
Savory thin layered pan-cake/crepe family using:
- grain/legume batter,
- fermented greens/fungi,
- sauces/fillings.

Function:
street/market/home food; cheap versions and artisanal versions both exist.

## 야간면
Noodle/broth family strongly associated with shift districts and late-night service culture.

Variants may use:
- fungi,
- greens,
- cultured meat,
- fermented savory broth bases.

It is ordinary comfort food, not `space nutrient noodles`.

## 수로빵
Steamed/baked filled bread family associated historically with 2환 watershed/market districts.

Fillings vary by household/era.
Name survives beyond literal water-worker culture.

## 검은장
Dark long-fermented savory paste/condiment family descended from multiple Earth fermentation traditions and Meridian substitutions.

Debates over `authentic` lineage are cultural, not genetic.

Hard:
- no food item reveals developmental origin,
- no one Habitat has exclusive cuisine caste,
- these four do not exhaust Meridian cuisine.

# 3. Small Recurring Idiom / Slang Set — CULT-LANG-H1

The novel remains natural Korean translation convention.
Do not force these into every chapter.

## `한 바퀴 늦다`
Meaning:
behind the news/trend; slow to catch up.

Origin:
rotation/cycle language fossilized into ordinary idiom.

## `축 잡아`
Meaning:
get your bearings / steady yourself / stop flailing.

Origin:
low-g transfer orientation language.

Use:
can be literal or mildly figurative; do not make every character say it.

## `환 건너`
Meaning:
across another Habitat / across-town but not local.

Use:
spatial social shorthand, e.g. `환 건너까지 갈 일은 아니잖아.`

Hard:
no fake-heavy future dialect spelling.

# 4. Representative Ship-Born Creative Forms — CULT-ART-H1

## 항해연속극
Long-form historical/family serial genre set in different Meridian eras.

Can range from:
- prestige historical drama,
- melodrama,
- comedy,
- revisionist history.

It is not necessarily about the current human/AI mystery.

## 저중력 무용
Performance traditions exploiting reduced/near-zero gravity:
- long trajectories,
- wall/rail choreography,
- slow rotational partner work,
- safety rigging.

Exists as both serious art and youth/pop performance.

## Archive remix culture
Creators legally reuse/recontextualize public-domain/licensed Earth and Meridian archives in music/video/immersive work.

Hard:
archive remix does not imply protected records are freely available.

# 5. Representative Historical Anchors — HIST-ANCHOR-H1

These are **reference milestones**, not a catastrophe-of-the-century chain.
Exact year may remain ± several years unless later needed.

## H-A1 — 제1 순환위기 / First Loop Crisis
Approx: **VY 60s–70s**.

A multi-system water/nutrient/process contamination and allocation episode during early voyage strains several districts for weeks/months.

Consequences:
- stronger local isolation,
- independent quality verification,
- more robust strategic reserve doctrine,
- cultural memory that closed loops are maintained systems, not magic perfection.

Hard:
- no mass extinction,
- no secret Seed connection.

## H-A2 — 앵커 원칙 판결 / Anchor Principle Ruling
Approx: **VY 140s–160s**.

A major medico-legal dispute clarifies that:
- stored Recovery Map/reference information is not itself the person,
- surviving biological Neural Anchor matters for adult Continuity/Reconstruction.

Consequences:
modern Continuity doctrine gains a durable legal/clinical principle.

Hard:
not a one-case invention of all Reconstruction law.

## H-A3 — 기원중립 개정 / Origin-Neutral Citizenship Amendments
Approx: **VY 180s–210s**.

Series of civic reforms culminating in explicit rule:
developmental origin alone cannot diminish personhood/citizenship.

Later remembered through 동등시민일.

Hard:
Natural-Origin birth remains lawful afterward.

## H-A4 — 공통경로 장애 / Common-Path Failure
Approx: **VY 330s–350s**.

A cross-domain software/data/control dependency failure causes widespread but bounded service disruption.

Consequences:
- stronger critical-domain segmentation,
- rollback/fallback requirements,
- model/version certification,
- permissioned gateways,
- skepticism toward universal integration.

Hard:
- not AI rebellion,
- not civilization collapse.

## H-A5 — 구시가지 안전협약 / Old Quarter Safety Compact
Approx: **VY 390s–410s**.

After repeated conflict over repair, condemnation, heritage and resident displacement, civic/technical institutions formalize clearer processes for:
- safety certification,
- staged repair,
- resident voice,
- relocation/compensation,
- heritage value.

Hard:
heritage still cannot override immediate physical safety.

## H-A6 — 도착전환규정 제정 / Arrival Transition Regulation
Approx: **VY 420s / early Final Approach era**, before story start.

Purpose:
translate ship citizenship, commons, property, migration and technical readiness into a future destination context.

It exists before Human Settler becomes an active story conflict.

Hard:
it does not secretly solve the legacy mapping issue in advance.

# 6. Representative Low/Zero-g Sport — REBOUND-H1

Reader-facing working name:
**리바운드 / Rebound**.

Role:
Noah's youth sport can use this family without making him elite athlete.

## Court
Dedicated near-zero-g enclosed padded rectangular-prism court.

Author-facing standard adult/youth large-court order:
- length **~18 m**
- width **~12 m**
- height **~10 m**

Youth/community courts can vary.

All six major surfaces are playable push-off surfaces.
Fixed rails/grip zones exist for restart/safety, not constant hanging.

## Teams
**5 vs 5** on court.
Rolling substitutions.

## Movement
- no personal thrusters in standard play,
- movement by push-off, controlled contact with walls/rails, teammate passing and body orientation,
- protective clothing/padding,
- deliberate grabbing/checking dangerous trajectories is a foul.

## Ball
Soft, grippy, low-mass rebound ball sized for one/two-hand control.

## Goal
One defended circular scoring gate near each end zone.

Scoring:
- **1 point** if ball fully passes through the opponent gate from the attacking half,
- **2 points** if released from behind the mid-court plane and passes cleanly through.

The long score rewards trajectory/timing, not raw throwing power.

## Possession
- player may hold the ball up to **~5 seconds** before pass/shot/release,
- no traveling rule in Earth sense because inertial movement continues,
- a player cannot deliberately pin/anchor indefinitely while holding possession.

## Match
Community/youth standard:
**4 × 7-minute active-play periods** with short breaks.
Clock stops for injuries/major fouls, not every minor out-of-play event because enclosed court has little conventional out-of-bounds.

## Core skills
- anticipation of trajectories,
- orientation,
- timed push-off,
- passing lanes,
- spatial marking,
- safe collision avoidance,
- gate defense.

Hard:
- not Quidditch-like flying with thrusters,
- not combat sport,
- Noah is ordinary capable participant, not prodigy.

Exact league variations remain C2.
