# HISTORY — ~450-YEAR ERA ARCHITECTURE — 4 DESIGNS v0.1

Status: `ENCYCLOPEDIC v2 DESIGN COMPARISON / PACKAGE D / NOT CANON`
Date: 2026-08-17
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Core authority:
- Departure passengers are Natural-Origin biological humans.
- No mass replacement / upload / extermination.
- Seed emerges as practical neurodevelopment / continuity medicine.
- Seed adoption grows through medical benefit, provider-network effects and normalization.
- Seeded children are biologically born and live ordinary human lives.
- developmental origin is removed from lesser-personhood/citizenship logic through rights reform.
- rare unseeded births remain legal/possible.
- current Natural-Origin count = 1 is demographic coincidence, not protocol.
- current voyage length ~mid-440s years order, story begins ~T-14 before system insertion.
- current society is a mature ~300k civilian city-civilization.

This file must supersede, not revive, the rejected old `exactly one Natural-Origin person preserved by protocol` concept.

---

# DESIGN A — FIVE MEGA-ERAS

1. Departure
2. Survival
3. Seed Transition
4. Mature Meridian
5. Final Approach

Pros:
- simple,
- memorable.

Cons:
- too coarse for language/economy/AI/city/provenance design,
- forces many unrelated developments into same bucket.

---

# DESIGN B — CRISIS-DRIVEN HISTORY

History organized around major disasters, shortages and political reforms.

Pros:
- dramatic,
- easy story anecdotes,
- produces memorable artifacts and laws.

Cons:
- makes civilization seem only shaped by catastrophe,
- risks one-event explanations,
- weak ordinary generational continuity.

Use only as punctuation, not backbone.

---

# DESIGN C — INSTITUTIONAL THREAD HISTORY

Separate timelines for:
- government,
- medicine/Seed,
- economy,
- city/infrastructure,
- AI/network,
- culture,
- Earth contact.

Pros:
- encyclopedic,
- strong causal clarity.

Cons:
- hard to understand `what era are we in?`,
- cross-thread interactions can disappear.

---

# DESIGN D — EIGHT-ERA SPINE + CROSS-CUTTING THREADS

A chronological era spine gives shared historical context, while separate thread ledgers track government, Seed/medicine, economy, infrastructure, culture, AI/data and Earth contact through each era.

Pros:
- best blend of readability and reference depth,
- supports provenance dating,
- allows slow social change plus specific crises,
- ideal for Obsidian later.

Cons:
- more work,
- requires careful regression so eras do not become overly neat.

# ERA 0 — DEPARTURE / FOUNDING

Candidate range: Voyage Year 0–~20.

## Social condition
- all departing passengers are Natural-Origin humans.
- mission survival culture is unusually strong.
- Earth national/cultural identities remain vivid and firsthand.
- initial institutions inherit launch-era mission command, technical operations and civilian governance arrangements.

## Infrastructure
- hard rotating Habitat/Spine architecture exists as mission hardware.
- civic neighborhoods are newer, more planned and less historically layered.
- earliest continuously inhabited 1환 zones later become the nucleus of 구시가지.

## Economy
- stronger provisioning/ration/mission-allocation layer than modern Meridian.
- private/personal exchange exists but strategic survival goods are tightly controlled.

## Culture
- Earth media/languages/religions dominate.
- ship-born customs are minimal.

## Earth contact
- delayed communication is still comparatively short and psychologically direct.

Hard rule:
No founder bloodline receives permanent constitutional privilege.

---

# ERA 1 — EARLY VOYAGE CONSOLIDATION

Candidate range: ~20–~80.

## Core development
The mission stops feeling temporary. Children and grandchildren know Meridian as home.

## Government
- purely mission-derived authority begins losing legitimacy for ordinary civilian life.
- elected/local civic institutions expand.
- technical command narrows toward operational safety rather than general sovereignty.

## Infrastructure
- original planned interiors undergo first major civilian retrofits.
- local districts, schools, markets and cultural spaces thicken.
- early maintenance institutions professionalize.

## Economy
- Layered Commons foundations emerge as survival provisioning becomes a stable civic floor rather than total economic allocation.
- market/co-op/private activity expands above the floor.

## Culture
- first durable ship-born slang, cuisine adaptations, school rituals and festivals appear.

## Medicine
- Continuity mapping/recovery research grows through ordinary trauma/long-voyage medicine.

---

# ERA 2 — CONTINUITY MEDICINE / FIRST SEED COHORTS

Candidate range: ~70–~140, overlapping boundaries intentionally.

## Medicine
- early Reconstruction/Neural Anchor science improves.
- developmental priors emerge from neurodevelopment/continuity research.
- first Seeded birth cohorts appear through parental medical choice.

## Adoption logic
Benefits are:
- risk reduction,
- clinical predictability,
- improved compatibility with then-less-mature neural recovery/mapping.

Not:
- personality programming,
- memory installation,
- loyalty engineering,
- machine-body creation.

## Social conflict
- early families debate safety, identity and uncertainty.
- Seeded children are still simply children in households/schools.

## Technology path dependence begins
Provider expertise and longitudinal data gradually make Seed the better-studied pathway.

---

# ERA 3 — SEED NORMALIZATION / RIGHTS REFORM

Candidate range: ~130–~230.

## Demography
- Seed adoption grows across multiple generations.
- Natural-Origin births remain legal and present but decline as a share.

## Rights conflict
Historical technical categories such as Natural/Bio-Origin Cognition and Seeded/Synthetic-Origin Cognition become politically/legal salient because old laws were not written for the new developmental pathway.

Disputes may involve:
- insurance/care,
- inheritance,
- continuity status,
- citizenship wording,
- family rights,
- stigma.

## Legal outcome
Repeated reforms establish the principle later embodied in the 시민기본법 tradition:
**developmental origin alone does not reduce civic personhood or citizenship.**

## Cultural outcome
The old origin categories begin shifting from identity labels to specialized medical/historical provenance terms.

Hard rule:
There is no `one Natural person preservation accord`.

---

# ERA 4 — CIVIC MATURATION / URBAN LAYERING

Candidate range: ~220–~320.

## Government
- modern layered civic republic/commons governance ancestors become recognizable.
- local councils, civil service, independent review and narrowed technical command stabilize.

## Economy
- modern Layered Commons + Market City form matures.
- durable property/occupancy systems, co-ops, regulated markets and welfare/civic floor become normalized.

## City
- neighborhoods become historically layered.
- building conversions, mergers, subdivisions and heritage preservation create recognizable old/new urban fabric.
- `구시가지` becomes a cultural-historical category, not merely `the first corridor`.

## Culture
- Meridian-born art, games, cuisine, rituals and dialect become canon in their own right.
- Earth culture remains ancestor/archive rather than exclusive living mainstream.

## Origin demographics
Seeded birth is now overwhelmingly normal.
Rare legal refusals/unseeded births continue.

---

# ERA 5 — NETWORKED CIVILIZATION / AUTOMATION GOVERNANCE

Candidate range: ~300–~380.

## Technology
- high-capability federated AI/automation becomes ubiquitous across utilities, logistics, medicine and administration.
- personal agents become ordinary.
- robotics expands hazardous/routine labor.

## Governance response
Society learns that high capability does not justify universal access/authority.
Reforms strengthen:
- network segmentation,
- model certification,
- algorithmic accountability,
- privacy/data-purpose limits,
- human final authority in rights decisions.

## Archives
- repeated schema migrations make versioned deep archives/provenance crosswalks a major professional discipline.

## Possible crisis punctuation
One or more non-apocalyptic common-mode software/network/automation incidents may accelerate segmentation and fallback standards.

Hard rule:
No rogue AI coup and no central AI sovereign era is required.

---

# ERA 6 — MATURE MERIDIAN / EARTH AS ANCESTRAL MEMORY

Candidate range: ~370–~420.

## Identity
- overwhelming majority are many generations ship-born.
- `Meridian is home` becomes ordinary rather than ideological.
- Earth survives through archives, religion, language heritage, family stories, art and delayed transmissions.

## Earth contact
Recommended direction:
- contact need not literally vanish in one catastrophe.
- increasing light-time and institutional divergence make Earth unable to govern Meridian in real time.
- communication becomes increasingly archival/advisory/asynchronous.
- there may be long gaps, incompatible priorities or irregular transmission eras without requiring `Earth destroyed`.

## Infrastructure
- aging/heritage tension grows as some early urban fabric becomes expensive to maintain.
- long-lived assets acquire both functional and historical value.

## Demography
Natural-Origin births are extremely rare but not legally impossible.
Amara's parents later act within this rare-rights environment; they are not secret lineage custodians.

---

# ERA 7 — FINAL APPROACH / ARRIVAL PREPARATION

Candidate range: ~420 to system insertion, with story beginning ~T-14 inside this era.

## Government
- 도착전환위원회 and related temporary statutory structures become more operationally important.
- existing civic institutions remain sovereign; transition bodies do not replace them.

## Economy / infrastructure
- strategic reserves reclassified by arrival utility,
- new training and fabrication priorities,
- debate over dismantling/transfer versus ship redundancy,
- housing/logistics adjustments,
- orbital/surface planning.

## Culture
- `where is home?` moves from philosophy to family/career/property decisions.
- surface/orbit/ship futures become real but none is morally pre-certified.

## Story-start condition
- Seed is mundane prenatal medicine.
- origin categories are legally obsolete in ordinary identity use.
- old provenance data still exist in specialized historical layers.
- current active Natural-Origin count happens to be 1.

# SEVEN CROSS-CUTTING THREADS

Every era entry in final Historical Bible must track at least these threads:

## T1 — Governance / rights
Mission authority → civic institutions → modern GOV-H1.

## T2 — Medicine / Seed / Reconstruction
Early Continuity research → Seed cohorts → rights normalization → modern medical path dependence.

## T3 — Economy / labor / property
Provisioning-heavy survival economy → commons/market hybrid → mature ECO/LAB/PROP systems.

## T4 — Urban / infrastructure
planned launch interiors → retrofit city → historic districts → aging/arrival asset pressure.

## T5 — Culture / identity
Earth-firsthand → mixed heritage → Meridian-born culture → Meridian-home majority → plural arrival futures.

## T6 — AI / network / archives
mission automation → distributed civil systems → high-capability AI → privacy/segmentation/versioned archives.

## T7 — Earth contact / mission legitimacy
shorter-lag parent civilization → growing autonomous civic legitimacy → high-latency advisory/archival relationship.

# SELECTIVE CRISIS PUNCTUATION RULE

The final timeline should include several major crises/reforms, but no single catastrophe explains civilization.

Useful crisis categories:
- structural/thermal incident,
- epidemic/medical crisis,
- major Continuity failure controversy,
- early Seed discrimination case,
- automation/network common-mode failure,
- major food/water/ecology disruption,
- labor/governance legitimacy crisis,
- heritage district disaster/closure debate.

Rules:
- not all crises must occur,
- crises accelerate trends rather than create everything from zero,
- no mass human replacement event,
- no AI coup,
- no secret exact-one protocol.

# EARTH CONTACT — PROVISIONAL CHOICE

Recommended:
**`EC-H1 — Persistent-but-Non-Governing Delayed Contact with Increasing Social Distance and Possible Long Gaps`**

Why this is stronger than `Earth suddenly went silent`:
- no unnecessary apocalypse,
- light-time itself explains loss of practical control,
- cultural data can keep arriving while political autonomy grows,
- modern citizens can know Earth history without Earth being an active authority in every decision.

Exact continuity/gap pattern waits for a dedicated Earth-contact history block.
