# 2. Settlement World — PLAN-H1

Working center candidates, all provisional:
- orbit: ~0.50–0.54 AU
- working center ~0.52 AU
- orbital year ~150–160 Earth days order
- low eccentricity
- gravity ~0.97g center
- day ~29.5h center
- obliquity ~14° center
- abundant stable surface liquid water
- cool-temperate climate overall
- total atmospheric pressure near Earth order
- oxygen fraction low enough that normal long-duration unassisted breathing is unsafe
- outdoor work possible with breathing support rather than full pressure EVA suit.

Exact atmospheric fractions remain unfrozen pending physiology QA.

# 3. What `Habitable` Means Here

The world is attractive because it has:
- tolerable surface pressure,
- near-Earth gravity,
- abundant water,
- stable temperate regions,
- broad construction/mineral resources,
- real weather and open terrain.

It is not immediately Earth-breathable.

Therefore arrival gains an enormous living-space/resource opportunity without instantly replacing Meridian.

# 9. ISRU Ladder

Local ISRU initially supplies easier bulk products:
- water,
- oxygen/chemical feedstocks,
- shielding mass,
- aggregate/silicates,
- basic metals/structural components.

Precision ladder remains:
1. imported Meridian precision capital,
2. local assembly using imported precision components + local bulk,
3. local higher-precision production,
4. mature independent industrial ecology.

This takes years/decades.

No asteroid-to-CPU shortcut.

---

# 10. Power / Thermal

Early off-ship nodes need imported/transferred power systems.

Surface gains environmental heat-rejection options unavailable to pure orbital habitats.
Orbit still needs substantial radiators.

Asset value therefore depends on destination:
- radiator may be crucial in orbit,
- precision machine line may be crucial everywhere,
- raw structural mass rapidly loses import value once local bulk production works.

# 11. Meridian Arrival / Insertion

- it occupies a planned stable system trajectory/orbit,
- smaller transfer craft connect ship/orbit/surface/resource sites,
- exact orbit remains quantitative work.

Do not assume enormous ship sits casually in low planetary orbit.
