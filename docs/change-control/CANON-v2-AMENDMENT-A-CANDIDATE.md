# A1. Elections / 선내의회

## Franchise
- ordinary voting age: **18**
- one person, equal civic vote
- developmental origin, Reconstruction history, profession, Habitat and property do not alter vote weight
- early off-Meridian citizens retain shared shipwide franchise under transition law.

No:
- Natural-Origin reserved seat
- professional/technical weighted vote
- property-weighted vote
- equal sovereign Habitat block regardless of population.

## General election cycle
Shipwide general election:
**every 4 civil years**.

Local councils may also use roughly four-year terms with staggered schedules; exact local staggering remains C2 unless story-relevant.

## 선내의회 size
Author-facing bounded range:
**~120–180 seats**, preferred planning center ~150.

Exact seat number can remain C2 inside the band unless an episode needs an exact tally.

## Mixed-member two-vote system
Each voter normally casts:
1. one local residential-district candidate vote,
2. one shipwide party/civic-list vote.

Overall Assembly composition is made broadly proportional to the shipwide list vote through compensatory list seats.

Residential district seats provide local accountability without turning Habitats into sovereign states.

Preferred ~150-seat center may use roughly:
- ~60 local district seats
- ~90 compensatory list seats,
but exact division remains C2 if overall mixed-member proportional logic is preserved.

A small anti-fragmentation threshold may exist; exact percentage remains C2 unless plot-relevant.

# A2. Parties / Collective Executive

Meridian may have:
- long-lived parties,
- temporary civic coalitions,
- independent district candidates,
- eligible coalition/independent shipwide lists.

Do not mechanically transplant contemporary Earth left/right alignments.
Political coalitions may cut differently across:
- labor/business,
- privacy/rights,
- tax/public service,
- maintenance/heritage,
- arrival strategy.

The four settlement paths do not become four mandatory parties.

## Civic Executive
The 선내의회 selects a **small collective civic executive**, author-facing **~5–7 members**.

Functions:
- coordinate civil administration,
- prepare/propose budget,
- implement Assembly law,
- coordinate cross-agency policy,
- appoint/nominate senior administrators under statutory oversight,
- coordinate the civic side of major emergencies.

One member may serve as convenor/public coordinator.
That person is not a directly elected president, captain or sovereign ruler.

## Removal
Use **constructive no-confidence**:
the Assembly replaces the executive/coalition rather than creating an executive vacuum.

Professional civil service and Ship Operations Command continue through political turnover.

# A3. Local Councils / Technical Boundary

Local Habitat/district councils are directly elected locally.

Normal scope can include:
- public realm,
- neighborhood services,
- local school/service implementation,
- minor housing/land-use administration,
- community/cultural grants,
- municipal complaints/coordination.

They do not control:
- shipwide citizenship,
- propulsion,
- independent armed forces,
- strategic life-support law,
- Habitat sovereign veto.

Technical bodies certify physical safety/feasibility floors.
Elected institutions decide political/distributive choices within those real limits.

Ship Operations Command remains professional technical command, not civic executive government.

# A4. Judiciary / 시민항소원

Meridian has a multi-level judiciary.

## First instance
Ordinary divisions/tribunals handle:
- civil/contract/property,
- family/guardianship,
- criminal,
- administrative disputes.

## General appeal
Reviews significant legal/procedural error and domain-appropriate fact/application disputes.

## High civic/rights/constitutional review
Handles:
- major civic-rights questions,
- agency overreach,
- constitutional/charter conflicts,
- high-level administrative-rights appeals.

**시민항소원** is a specialized high-level civic/administrative rights appeal institution inside this upper review architecture.
It is **not Meridian's entire judiciary**.

A high-court chamber/panel may perform constitutional/charter review without requiring another reader-facing institution name.

## Judges
Judges are not directly elected partisan politicians.

Selection direction:
- professional qualification,
- independent nomination/qualification process,
- democratic confirmation/appointment,
- long staggered terms,
- removal for serious cause through independent process.

Highest-rights/constitutional judges use roughly **10–14-year class** staggered terms, nonrenewable or strongly renewal-limited.
Exact judge count/confirmation threshold remains C2.

The civic executive may set lawful policy/budget but may not order conviction or dictate individual judgments.

# A5. Election Integrity / Emergency

Required election functions:
- independent election administration,
- auditable results,
- campaign funding/disclosure rules,
- conflict-of-interest disclosure,
- neutral public election information,
- plural media environment.

Private wealth may affect persuasion/reach but cannot buy extra votes.

A genuine hazard may justify a narrow temporary voting delay only where safe voting/communications are physically impossible.

Delay must be:
- publicly justified,
- narrowly scoped,
- reviewable,
- rescheduled as soon as practicable.

Ordinary emergency authority cannot postpone a general election indefinitely.
A delay beyond roughly **90 days class** requires extraordinary legislative + high judicial/constitutional reauthorization and recurring review.

# A6. Vacancy / Reconstruction / Arrival Representation

Death/incapacity/Reconstruction of an officeholder does not freeze government.

- district vacancy: acting process + by-election/statutory replacement
- list vacancy: same-list/succession rule
- executive incapacity: collective council continues; Assembly replaces member.

Successful later Reconstruction does not automatically restore a public office whose lawful term/vacancy process has moved on.

Early off-Meridian citizens retain equal shipwide franchise while local resident/worker councils and later municipal institutions grow with actual residence.

No founder/Natural-Origin extra vote or veto.

# B1. Found Property / Salvage — PROP-SALV-H1

**Finding an object does not itself create ownership.**

Law distinguishes:
1. lost personal property,
2. apparently abandoned personal property,
3. private/co-op/business property,
4. public/common infrastructure,
5. controlled hazardous/professional equipment,
6. decommissioned public/enterprise equipment,
7. heritage/provenance-significant material.

Lost property carries reasonable reporting/return duties where an owner can be identified.

Apparently abandoned personal property may become claimable only after reasonable notice/holding procedures and confirmation that it is not controlled/public/heritage property.
Exact waiting period remains C2.

Public/common/strategic equipment does not become private merely because it is:
- old,
- disconnected,
- stored,
- decommissioned.

Controlled professional/hazardous equipment retains custody/use rules.
Heritage discoveries may trigger provenance/reporting/public-interest review.

First discovery of an arrival resource does not create celestial sovereignty.

# B2. Human-Subject Research Ethics — RES-H1

Research involving identifiable living citizens requires lawful purpose and appropriate consent/ethics safeguards.

More-than-minimal-risk human research requires independent ethics/scientific review addressing:
- physical risk,
- privacy/data risk,
- coercion/dependency,
- vulnerable populations,
- capacity/consent.

Interventional/recruitment consent must disclose material:
- purpose,
- procedures,
- risks,
- data use,
- foreseeable future use,
- compensation where relevant,
- withdrawal limits after irreversible aggregation/deidentification.

Participants may generally stop future participation.
Already lawfully collected data may remain only as permitted by consent/statute/ethics plan.

Research receives minimum necessary data access.
Research status never grants unrestricted D4/Origin provenance access.

Special safeguards apply where recruitment depends on:
- medical care,
- employer/school authority,
- detention,
- guardianship,
- scarce-capacity allocation.

Civic Floor services may not be conditioned on research participation.

### Amara hard lock
Amara has **no research duty** because she is unique.
She may accept, refuse, limit or withdraw where possible without losing:
- citizenship,
- healthcare,
- housing floor,
- migration rights.

Low-risk deidentified/aggregate existing-record research may use a narrow lawful ethics waiver; this does not authorize a protected-identity hunt.

# B3. Medical Aid-in-Dying — MAID-H1

A narrow lawful pathway exists for a **contemporaneously decision-capable adult 18+** with:
- a serious irreversible/incurable medical condition,
- enduring suffering the person finds intolerable,
- a voluntary request free of coercion,
- informed discussion of reasonable treatment/palliative/support alternatives.

Required process:
1. initial voluntary request,
2. treating clinician assessment,
3. independent second clinician assessment,
4. decision-specific capacity assessment where doubt exists,
5. palliative/support alternatives offered,
6. waiting/reconfirmation appropriate to urgency/condition,
7. final contemporaneous confirmation where feasible,
8. documentation + retrospective oversight review.

Exact day count remains C2.

Disability, poverty, unemployment, housing scarcity or social isolation **alone** are never qualifying medical grounds.
The Civic Floor cannot make death a cheaper substitute for support.

### Proxy prohibition
No:
- spouse,
- guardian,
- family member,
- emergency proxy,
- AI,
- old Recovery Map,
- old advance directive
may actively authorize aid-in-dying on behalf of a currently incapable person.

Advance directives may still govern withholding/withdrawal of unwanted burdensome treatment under ordinary end-of-life law.

Acute/treatable psychiatric crisis pauses the pathway where it compromises decision capacity.
Sole-psychiatric-condition eligibility remains outside active Canon and requires a narrow future Change Control/micropass if ever used.

Reconstruction scarcity, birth/resource capacity or cost-saving can never be used to pressure a citizen toward MAID-H1.
