# COI-C — Mandatory Direct-Case Recusal

Core:
Maren remains an active 연속성 심사관 but is automatically recused from any formal matter directly involving:
- Ella
- Noah
- her own guardianship
- her own emergency-family actions.

Another adjudicator/panel handles the family matter.
Maren participates only as:
- guardian/relative
- witness
- holder of relevant records
- later possible subject of ethics review.

Strength:
- realistic.
- preserves profession engine.
- family and work mirror each other without merging jurisdiction.

Weakness:
Maren has less formal power in the most personal dispute—which is actually thematically useful.

Verdict:
`CURRENT BEST BASE`

# 1. Canon Functional Rule

Maren **must recuse** from any official adjudication directly concerning Ella, Noah, or Maren's own guardianship.

She cannot:
- sign Ella's continuity-status determination
- determine Ella's parental reinstatement
- decide whether Noah must live with Ella or Maren
- rule on the legal effect of the hidden preference draft

She can:
- testify to facts
- submit records
- act as Noah's current guardian until another lawful order changes that
- make personal arguments as a family party

---

# 2. Reconstruction Decision vs Maren's Professional Role

Maren's involvement in Ella's Reconstruction occurred as **family/emergency representative**, not as an 연속성심사국 adjudicator.

Precision candidate:
Ella's ordinary emergency-planning bundle had already named Maren as:
- first-choice guardian for Noah if Ella became incapacitated
- primary family emergency contact / limited planning-record access

This gives Maren lawful access to Ella's relevant personal planning folder without giving her unilateral medical authority.

Clinical Reconstruction decisions still belonged to:
- treating clinicians
- hospital ethics/substituted-judgment process
- applicable civic law.

Maren's withholding of the unsubmitted draft is morally/ethically significant because it deprived that process of relevant context; it did not let her personally order the operation.

---

# 3. Early Story Consequence

Ella's return creates an immediate painful fact:
Maren is professionally skilled at deciding other people's continuity disputes but **has less formal power over her own family's dispute than an ordinary party might emotionally want**.

This is good.

Raul or another advocate can attack institutional reasoning without Maren being the judge in her own case.

---

# 4. Ethics Consequence After PR-H1 Reveal

When Ella discovers Maren knew the draft:
possible consequences include:
- professional ethics review
- temporary reassignment from related cases
- credibility damage
- requirement to disclose conflict/history on certain proceedings

Do NOT automatically fire Maren for dramatic punishment.
Exact sanction remains Act/episode precision.

The point is:
Maren's family behavior can have professional cost without making her office the family court.

# 5. Red Team

## RT-COI-01 — If Maren is recused, how does her profession stay relevant to family plot?
Through mirrored cases, institutional ethics, her guardian role, Raul's challenges, and her own professional assumptions being exposed as inconsistent.

Verdict: `PASS`

## RT-COI-02 — Why can Maren remain Noah's guardian while recused?
Recusal affects decision authority, not preexisting lawful temporary guardianship. A different body decides whether/when that guardianship changes.

Verdict: `PASS`

## RT-COI-03 — Does emergency contact status conveniently expose Ella's private draft?
The draft is in a designated health/planning folder for emergency use; access is lawful but limited. Ella chose Maren as family emergency contact long before the accident. This is ordinary family planning, not post-accident special access.

Verdict: `PASS CANDIDATE`
