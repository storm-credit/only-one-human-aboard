## Weakness
`왜 작성하고 제출 안 했나?`가 편의적으로 보일 수 있음.

### Possible reason
Ella herself was uncertain and kept it as draft after a real case frightened her.

## Verdict
`STRONG BASE`

# 1. Exact Candidate Structure

About **1~2 years before Ella's accident**, a well-known reconstruction case in her social/work orbit produced a person with severe memory/relationship discontinuity.

Ella:
1. opened the standard advance-preference form
2. drafted a conditional refusal
3. did **not** complete final registration
4. separately sent Maren a private message or had a recorded conversation explaining why she was afraid of `being returned for other people's sake`.

Important:
The draft is not `never restore me under any circumstance`.

Better condition:
> If clinicians expect severe loss of autobiographical memory and relational continuity beyond an uncertain threshold, Ella would prefer finality over an aggressive attempt whose primary justification is that family wants her back.

This remains medically/legally hard to apply.

---

# 2. Why Ella Did Not Register It

Current best:
Ella was genuinely undecided.

She:
- feared bad reconstruction outcomes
- also feared leaving Noah without a parent
- started the form after an emotionally difficult case
- postponed final confirmation

Thus the document represents **a serious but unfinished preference**, not a secret final will.

This ambiguity is essential.

---

# 3. How Maren Finds It

After Ella's accident, Maren becomes emergency family representative/guardian and is given lawful access to a limited personal emergency archive needed for:
- Noah's care
- Ella's household administration
- treatment context

The unsubmitted draft is in a personal health/planning folder, not the official directive registry.

Maren also remembers/locates Ella's prior private message.

No hacking.
No secret government database.

---

# 4. Why Maren Does Not Surface It

Maren's rationalizations:
1. It was never registered.
2. Ella was undecided.
3. doctors believe a viable anchor remains and meaningful recovery is possible.
4. Noah is nine and has no other legal parent.
5. Maren wants her sister back.

The last two motives matter.

Maren may tell herself:
`I did not override a directive; I refused to turn an unfinished fear into a death sentence.`

This is defensible but self-serving.

---

# 5. Medical / Legal Effect

If disclosed during treatment:
it would NOT automatically block Reconstruction.

It could have triggered:
- ethics consultation
- stricter substituted-judgment review
- more conservative reconstruction scope
- delay/reevaluation

Therefore withholding it matters without making Maren single-handedly resurrect Ella illegally.

This keeps causality distributed.

---

# 6. Ella's Later Interpretation

Ella can reasonably believe:
> Maren knew the one thing I was most afraid of and decided it did not count because it was inconvenient.

Ella does NOT need to claim:
`I definitely wanted to die.`

Her anger can center on:
**Maren took ownership of an uncertainty that belonged to Ella.**

This directly hits Maren's core flaw without making the moral answer trivial.

Important:

This supports Non-P causality.

# 8. How Ella Discovers It — 4 Micro Options

### X1 — Archive Access Log
Her restored account shows Maren accessed the folder during emergency authority.

Strength: clean, ordinary digital accountability.

### X2 — Rehabilitation Legal Review
During restoration-rights review, counsel finds the draft in records Ella exports.

Strength: fits legal process.
Risk: R too convenient if he is counsel.

### X3 — Maren Confesses
Risk: P controls timing and looks nobler.

### X4 — Noah Accidentally Finds It
Risk: child becomes plot delivery device.

Recommendation:
**X1 Archive Access Log + Ella's own restored account access**.

Ella finds her own draft and sees emergency-access metadata showing Maren opened it.
This proves knowledge without requiring a villain or R.

---

# 9. Noah's Role

Noah should not learn the full issue before Ella confronts Maren.

Afterward Noah may feel:
- Maren chose to bring Ella back partly for him
- guilt that his existence influenced the choice

Guardrail:
Adults must explicitly resist turning Noah into responsible cause.
But he may still feel it.

This creates family consequence without making Noah moral judge.

# 10. Red Team

## RT-PR-01 — Isn't an unsubmitted draft too convenient?
### Defense
Its legal incompleteness is the point and must be seeded through normal advance-preference culture before payoff.
It does not unlock plot facts; it changes relationship trust.

Verdict: `PASS`

## RT-PR-02 — Why would emergency archive access include a draft?
Only if Ella stored it in a designated personal health/planning folder accessible to emergency representative. This should be ordinary but not universal behavior.

Alternative if too convenient:
Maren knew the private message even without seeing the draft until later.

Verdict: `PASS CANDIDATE`

## RT-PR-03 — Does this make Reconstruction against consent a central crime?
No. Ella had not completed a binding refusal. The controversy is substituted judgment under uncertainty.

Verdict: `PASS STRONG`

## RT-PR-04 — Does Ella's anger imply she wishes she were dead now?
No. A person can value present life and still be furious that a past decision was taken from them.
This ambiguity is emotionally stronger.

Verdict: `PASS`
