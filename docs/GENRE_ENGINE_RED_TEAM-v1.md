# GENRE ENGINE RED TEAM v1

Status: `DESIGN REVIEW — NON-CANON / PRE-AMENDMENT`
Date: `2026-08-19`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose: Re-test the frozen narrative architecture before further Deep Design. This document does **not** override Canon. If accepted, it authorizes a bounded P1 amendment pass over the Act Bible / Episode Blueprint while preserving unrelated world and character canon.

---

# 1. Trigger

The project drifted toward a `현장 연속성 심사관 중심 사회 SF` reader experience.

The intended foreground genre is instead:

> **미스터리/스릴러/생존 작품이며, 연속성 심사관이라는 직업과 사회 SF 설정은 그 장르 엔진을 작동시키는 장치다.**

Target early-series mix:

- Mystery 35%
- Thriller 30%
- Survival 20%
- Relationship 10%
- Social / philosophical SF 5%

Late-series social/political scale may increase, but escalation should appear primarily as **larger danger, harder choices, collapsing access, resource conflict and physical consequences**, not as longer meeting-room debate.

---

# 2. Red Team Verdict

**VERDICT: P1 NARRATIVE-ENGINE CORRECTION REQUIRED BEFORE PROSE.**

This is not a prose-tone patch. The current macro architecture contains compatible canon, but the foreground causal engine is underpowered for the intended genre.

The project should **not** discard the Continuity profession, Seed system, citizenship structure, Amara identity, or first-third one-human Reveal. Instead it should rebuild the action logic surrounding them.

Core correction:

> Social-SF rules must stop being primarily topics to discuss and become **mechanisms that create danger, deny access, expose targets, force pursuit, split loyalties and determine who can survive a compartment-level crisis.**

---

# 3. New North-Star Narrative Engine

Default mystery-thriller chain:

> **이상한 죽음/실종 → 기록 불일치 → 누군가 조사를 막음 → 폐쇄/제한 구역 진입 → 생존 위기 → 새로운 증거 → 믿었던 사람의 행동/기록이 의심됨 → 더 큰 비밀로 연결**

This is a repeatable pressure pattern, not a mandatory identical formula for every arc.

Each major arc must contain all three layers:

**Mystery:** What does not fit?

**Thriller:** Who moves because the protagonist noticed it, and what can they take away?

**Survival:** What must the protagonist physically do, protect, escape, repair, endure or choose before complete understanding is possible?

Information without changed danger is not enough.

---

# 4. Reframe Maren's Profession

The Continuity examiner must function as a thriller tool, not mainly as an exposition/job-worldbuilding device.

Her profession gives her:

1. legitimate access to death/disappearance, reconstruction, identity and provenance anomalies;
2. reason to compare records that ordinary citizens never cross-reference;
3. controlled access to restricted archives, medical chains, damaged infrastructure and sealed locations;
4. chain-of-custody habits that let her detect falsified or impossible sequences;
5. authority limits that can suddenly strand her when jurisdiction or credentials are revoked;
6. a traceable audit footprint, meaning that asking the right question can reveal to an adversarial actor exactly what she has discovered.

Therefore the profession creates both **capability and vulnerability**.

Desired reader feeling:

> “그 직업이라서 이 비밀을 발견할 수 있었고, 그 직업이라서 더 위험해졌다.”

---

# 5. Social SF Must Become Threat Mechanics

Keep the civilization design, but convert rules into operational stakes.

Examples of correct use:

- identity classification changes door / archive / medical access;
- privacy law prevents a fast rescue or forces a dangerous workaround;
- quarantine or continuity procedure traps people in a failing compartment;
- a lawful aggregate publication unintentionally makes one family locatable;
- resource-priority rules determine who receives transport, oxygen, medical capacity or evacuation first;
- appeal / recusal / jurisdiction rules remove Maren's authority exactly when she most needs it;
- public classification causes pursuit, protective custody attempts, panic, sabotage or opportunistic violence.

The rule is not the scene's destination. **The rule changes what bodies can do next.**

---

# 6. One-Human Reveal — Mandatory Danger Conversion

Current Canon may retain the first-third factual Reveal and the lawful Count pathway, but the narrative consequence must change immediately.

The Reveal must not first produce a philosophical seminar.

When `Natural/Bio-Origin Cognition = 1` becomes materially knowable/public:

- one actor/interest attempts to secure the one person;
- another attempts to remove, neutralize or prevent access to that person;
- another believes disclosure itself can destabilize the ship and attempts suppression/containment;
- Maren does not initially know which action belongs to which motive;
- Amara becomes a person who must make choices under danger, not a passive answer to a taxonomy mystery.

The exact actors must arise from existing institutions, relationships and incentives. **Do not invent an omniscient secret cabal merely to manufacture thriller energy.**

Required causal order:

> `Count anomaly → targetability → actors move → access/communications/safety collapse → protect/escape/survive → partial explanation → deeper implication`

Philosophical and civic consequences remain important, but they arrive **after or during action**, not before it.

---

# 7. Trusted-Person Suspicion Rule

“믿었던 사람이 의심됨” must be operational, not melodramatic.

Useful forms include:

- their credential appears in a place they denied entering;
- they knew a fact before the official discovery;
- they delayed an alert or redirected Maren away from a location;
- their statement is individually plausible but incompatible with machine/medical/log timing;
- they protected someone by lying, but the same lie also enabled a dangerous event.

The suspicion should create an immediate tactical problem:

> Can Maren still share a route, location, evidence, child/family member, access key or escape plan with this person?

Avoid cheap “secret villain all along” reversals unless independently earned.

---

# 8. Act-Level Surgery Direction

## Act 1 — Investigation must bite earlier

Current world/family introduction can remain, but the opening engine must acquire an abnormal death/disappearance or continuity incident with a record contradiction. Institutional friction alone is insufficient. The arc should force Maren beyond safe professional procedure and into a restricted or physically dangerous location before the Act closes.

Act 1 endpoint should not merely be “profession and family can no longer be separated.” It should also establish:

> **someone or something reacts when Maren gets too close to the wrong contradiction.**

## Act 2 — Count investigation becomes a pressure tunnel

The origin crosswalk should grow out of concrete evidence from earlier danger rather than feel like a detached audit thread.

As Maren approaches Count=1, obstruction escalates from procedural friction to loss of access, surveillance/traceability, compromised records, containment measures, missing witnesses or infrastructure danger.

The Count publication at the end cluster can remain, but it must function as a **trigger**, not just a revelation.

## Act 3 — “한 명이라는 숫자” becomes pursuit + identification

Meaning and fair explanation still happen promptly, but while characters are moving under threat.

Amara's protected confirmation should coincide with competing attempts to reach, isolate, protect, exploit, silence or remove her. The reader learns what `one human` means while the protagonists are trying to keep a person alive and determine whom to trust.

## Acts 4–7 — Scale danger, not meetings

Social, legal and political consequences can become larger, but they must increasingly alter:

- movement,
- habitation,
- life-support/resource access,
- family safety,
- settlement eligibility,
- evacuation,
- infrastructure control,
- public order,
- irreversible destination choices.

Late-series politics should be felt as **what becomes physically possible or impossible**, not primarily as committee dialogue.

# 10. Anti-Regression Rules

The corrected version fails if any of the following becomes dominant:

1. mystery clues mostly arrive through meetings, reports or people explaining history;
2. obstruction means only “a superior says no” without material consequence;
3. a sealed zone exists mainly for lore tourism;
4. Count=1 creates several episodes of discussion before anyone acts;
5. Amara becomes an object everyone debates rather than an active person under pressure;
6. trusted-person suspicion has no tactical cost;
7. the thriller requires a retroactive all-powerful conspiracy that invalidates fair clues;
8. late politics increases only the number of officials in rooms;
9. survival set-pieces can be removed without changing the mystery;
10. the story solves tension by inventing a later metaphysical/ontology twist that replaces the established mystery.

---

# 11. New Scene / Arc Test

Before accepting any major sequence, ask:

**Mystery:** What concrete contradiction is the reader trying to resolve?

**Pressure:** Who/what reacts because it is being investigated?

**Vulnerability:** What access, person, route, evidence, resource or safety can be lost?

**Action:** What must the protagonist physically do before more explanation is possible?

**Evidence:** What new fact is earned through that action?

**Trust:** Whose reliability changes because of that evidence?

**Expansion:** How does the answer open a larger but causally connected danger?

A sequence that answers only worldbuilding questions fails the new genre gate.

---

# 12. Red Team Final Decision

The project is **not** being converted away from Social SF.

It is being corrected into:

> **A mystery / thriller / survival SF in which an advanced continuity society supplies the weapons, constraints, blind spots and moral stakes of the thriller.**

Maren's job remains valuable precisely because it can fuse procedural evidence with dangerous physical access.

The one-human Reveal remains valuable precisely because `1` is not merely an idea; it makes a living person uniquely identifiable and therefore uniquely vulnerable.
