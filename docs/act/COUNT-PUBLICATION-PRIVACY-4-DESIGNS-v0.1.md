# CP-C — Mandatory Public Schema-Integrity Audit

Core:
Upcoming arrival-transition work requires a legally mandated public audit of legacy categories that can affect future civic/settlement rights.

The audit must disclose:
- whether each legally consequential legacy category has current matches
- aggregate count / scope of affected population

Personal provenance remains sealed.

Why exact `1` instead of `<small number>`:
The number is itself legally material because a category that maps one person versus zero versus thousands creates different transition/equality problems.
Suppressing the count would conceal the scale of a category with possible rights consequences.

- state can protect identity while still releasing legally necessary aggregate.

Weakness:

Verdict:
`CURRENT BEST BASE`

# 1. Canon Functional Sequence

## Step 1 — Internal Crosswalk
Legacy origin/provenance schema + prenatal archive + current alive registry produce the anomaly.

Initial access is restricted to authorized audit staff / relevant institutions.

## Step 2 — Verification
Archive/medical/legal specialists independently verify:
- the category meaning
- the count
- data quality

Maren is not sole discoverer.

## Step 3 — Privacy Review
Because exact count is one, privacy officers flag re-identification risk.

They do NOT simply publish the identity.

## Step 4 — Public-Interest / Legal Necessity Test
The legacy category is not an academic curiosity. It is referenced by old mission/settlement legal schemas under review for future arrival transition.

A legally required schema-integrity report must state whether such categories:
- are empty
- affect one/small number
- affect large populations
because that determines whether current law has an unresolved live equivalence problem.

## Step 5 — Exact Aggregate Released, Identity Sealed
The public report releases the exact aggregate `1` with:
- identity withheld
- provenance records sealed
- explicit warning that current civic personhood is not being reclassified by the audit itself

This is the major public Count reveal.

## Step 6 — Re-identification Consequence
Despite protected identity, Count=1 makes historical/public traces re-identifying.
This activates HT-H1 / ID-H1R.

# 3. Why Exact `1` Is Necessary

A privacy-preserving band like `1~10` would be safer, but would not answer a legally material question:

> Is the legacy class extinct, broadly populated, or a singular edge case whose literal mapping would create extreme unequal treatment?

Current candidate legal standard:
For categories incorporated by reference into future rights/jurisdiction systems, exact small-cell aggregate may be disclosed after proportionality review when:
- identity is withheld
- category scope itself is legally material
- public legislative/appeal review cannot function without it

This should be shown as **controversial but lawful**, not obvious perfect policy.

# 7. Red Team

## RT-CP-01 — If the category isn't binding until T-10, why is it legally material at T-13?
Because pre-transition audit must identify unresolved category mappings years before certification. Materiality comes from a known future legal dependency, while binding consequences begin later.

Verdict: `PASS`

## RT-CP-02 — Why not privately fix the law before publication?
Because modifying settlement/charter equivalence for hundreds of thousands of citizens is a public-law decision, not a quiet database patch. The scope of the mismatch is part of legitimate civic oversight.

Verdict: `PASS STRONG`

## RT-CP-03 — Doesn't publication knowingly endanger Amara's privacy?
Yes, and that is the cost/controversy. Identity remains sealed, but unique aggregates can re-identify. The society is not perfectly wise; privacy review weighs public legal necessity against that risk.

Verdict: `PASS`

## RT-CP-04 — Could authorities publish `one or more` instead?
That would conceal whether literal legacy mapping creates a singular asymmetric category or a broader class. Current candidate transparency law treats exact scope as materially relevant after review.

Verdict: `PASS CANDIDATE`
