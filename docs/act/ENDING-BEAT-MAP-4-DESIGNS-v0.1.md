Status: `ENDING STRUCTURE COMPARISON / NOT CANON`

Parents:
- E-H1 Costly Polycentric Arrival
- EC-H1 Shared Constraint Portfolio
- T-H1 Longitudinal Wave
- Act 7 `도착은 판정이 아니다` ~195~230 candidate

Purpose:
최종부가:
- 법정회의
- 공학 착륙쇼
- 가족 멜로드라마
- 주인공 단독구원
중 하나로 축소되지 않도록 sequencing을 비교한다.

# EB-A — Legal Resolution First

## Sequence
1. old mission law crisis
2. rights transition settled
3. resource paths approved
4. system insertion
5. short personal epilogue

## Strength
- 논리/권리 문제 깔끔.
- R/P/M payoff 강함.

## Weakness
- 최종 climax가 법률회의.
- S/N/C8/O가 주변화.
- 도착이 행정처리 후 덤처럼 보임.

## Verdict
`TOO INSTITUTIONAL`

# EB-B — Arrival / Physical First

## Sequence
1. final approach crisis
2. system insertion
3. resource deployments

## Sequence
1. P-S-N settle relationship

# EB-D — Braided Three-Scale Ending

## Sequence
세 스케일이 서로 causal하게 얽힘:

### Scale 1 — One Person / Family
P-S-N, C8 family decisions.

### Scale 2 — Civic / Legal
current rights vs legacy transition categories.

### Scale 3 — Physical Civilization
ship/orbit/surface/new-habitat asset commitments and system insertion.

중요:
한 스케일이 다른 두 스케일의 `상징`만 되지 않는다.
각자 실제 결과를 가짐.

## Strength
- 작품 전체 구조와 가장 일치.
- 한 영웅/한 법/한 착륙으로 닫히지 않음.
- 비용을 분산하지만 실제로 보여줄 수 있음.

## Weakness
- 복잡.
- 마지막 30화가 체크리스트처럼 보일 위험.

## Verdict
`CURRENT BEST BASE`

# 1. Ending Phase 7A — No Single Old Answer (195~206)

In-world:
approximately final few years / transition deepening.

## Beat A1 — Legacy Category Becomes Operationally Dangerous
Transition Reconciliation reaches concrete questions:
- settlement membership
- registry portability
- mission-reserved asset access
- continuity status across new jurisdictions

Some old cross-reference can be read narrowly enough that natural-origin / seeded-origin distinction matters.

Important:
No one says all 299,999 instantly lose citizenship.
The danger is **a procedural shortcut / hierarchy entering transition systems**.

---

## Beat A2 — Multiple Actors Move Independently
### R
challenges the legacy interpretation through court/rights process.

### M
must decide whether Continuity Service will mechanically propagate old category fields into new transition records.

### P
handles a **specific temporary continuity/identity-transfer case** and refuses to treat origin provenance as proof that current civic personhood ceased.

P's decision:
- narrow
- appealable
- does not settle constitutional question

### C8
rejects being used as `the authentic settler` who can speak for everyone.

### O / social actors
fight over asset/place consequences, not human ontology only.

This prevents P-savior structure.

---

## Beat A3 — Interim Rights Continuity
A broader temporary stay/transition rule comes from:
- court / assembly / transition commission combination,
not P alone.

Function:
current civic rights continue during destination jurisdiction transition unless individually lawfully changed.

Not final constitution.

### EC7 Cost
Transition law remains incomplete.

---

# 2. Ending Phase 7B — Choices Become Physical (207~218)

## Beat B1 — Asset Commitments Are No Longer Reversible
Actual allocations proceed.

### EC1 Ship Redundancy Loss
A defined set of:
- spare industrial modules
- thermal/utility reserve
- precision manufacturing capacity
is committed to orbital/surface bootstrap.

Ship remains viable but less overbuilt.

---

## Beat B2 — Old Quarter Partial Loss
### EC2
Some Old Quarter structures/services close or are cannibalized/relocated.

O does NOT save everything.
O's achievement:
- resident-defined sequencing
- compensation/relocation choice
- preservation of selected living/history spaces

Visible loss remains.

---

## Beat B3 — Family Paths Stop Aligning
### EC5
At least two core family networks choose different paths.

Candidate, NOT CANON:
- C8 first child commits to orbital logistics/arrival work
- spouse/C8 or second child may remain ship-side initially
- adult N chooses separate career/residence path
- S and P do not automatically co-locate

No one family must split in exactly same way.

---

## Beat B4 — System Insertion
Suggested placement:
near end of 7B, roughly ep 216~218 candidate.

Physical payoff:
- destination star/system ceases to be future image and becomes operational environment.

Avoid:
- battle
- catastrophic engine miracle
- immediate lush-planet landing.

System insertion is mission completion milestone, not all-story answer.

---

# 3. Ending Phase 7C — The First New Days (219~230)

Can cover:
`weeks to months` after insertion, possibly up to ~1 year only if pacing needs.

## Beat C1 — Surface Is Not The New Capital Yet
### EC3 Surface Growth Delay
- small foothold only
- robotic/uncrewed or limited crew deployment begins
- resource verification / power systems start

No 100k city.

Settlement acceleration faction gets only partial victory.

---

## Beat C2 — New Habitat Is A Promise With A Bill
### EC6
new orbital habitat project begins in:
- design
- resource reservation
- construction seed

But residents will not move in for many years/decades.

Current generation pays before full benefit.

---

## Beat C3 — Capacity Scarcity Did Not Disappear
### EC4
Birth / reconstruction / housing waiting still exists.

Arrival creates future relief, not instant abundance.

Use one ordinary institutional/family consequence rather than statistics dump.

---

## Beat C4 — P-S-N Relationship Closure
Not reconciliation-to-old-state.

P:
accepts N/S decisions without issuing a family solution.

S:
has a life no longer organized around being restored.

N:
is adult and chooses own place/career.

P's ending gain is not `everyone stays`.
It is the ability to remain family without owning their choices.

---

## Beat C5 — C8 Becomes One Citizen Again, But Not Anonymous
C8 cannot erase public history.

Possible outcome:
- still known
- still politically quoted/misquoted
- family members set separate boundaries

C8's origin becomes:
one consequential biographical fact, not office/title.

No coronation / founding father-mother status.

---

## Beat C6 — Civic Transition Continues
### EC7
Legal framework remains imperfect.

What is achieved:
- origin does not automatically suspend current personhood/civic rights
- rights portability principle has a working interim basis

What remains contested:
- future settlement citizenship
- cross-jurisdiction capacity
- future unseeded births

R gets meaningful reform, not utopia.

---

## Beat C7 — Final World State
Four paths are all real:
1. living ship-city
2. orbital industrial/settlement foothold
3. small surface settlement path
4. future new habitat path

None is symbolic only.
None is mature enough to absorb all 300k.

The voyage has ended.
The civilization has not.

# 5. P Final Jurisdiction Sanity

P must NOT:
- issue shipwide constitutional ruling
- command settlement
- allocate industrial assets
- define human for all citizens

P MAY:
- refuse a narrow continuity shortcut in a case within office jurisdiction
- maintain temporary current-right status pending appeal/review
- provide factual continuity assessment

Broader outcome comes from:
- R/courts
- M/institutional implementation
- Assembly/councils
- transition bodies
- citizens/families/workers

`P FINAL SAVIOR RISK = PASS CANDIDATE`

# 6. C8 Ending Sanity

C8 must NOT:
- choose colony legitimacy
- certify `true humanity`
- donate DNA to save species as mandatory ending
- become first governor because human
- unlock ship/planet

C8 CAN:
- choose own residence/work
- refuse/accept research participation
- decide family privacy boundaries only within legitimate parental/individual rights
- publicly reject interpretations

C8 family members may disagree.

# 7. Physical Arrival Sanity

Ending physical sequence candidate:
- final deceleration complete / system insertion
- local reconnaissance confidence increases
- cargo/depot deployment
- first power/ISRU packages start operation/deployment
- limited crew follows or prepares

Mature surface community:
post-ending future, not instant.

If actual operational ISRU needs months after T0, 7C can cover a short denouement window.

# 8. Final Reward Mix

Act 7 rewards should include:
- Institutional: old category cannot auto-erase rights
- Physical: actual system arrival
- Relationship: P/S/N autonomy closure
- Character: C8 loses/renegotiates symbolic ownership
- World: four settlement paths become real
- Cost: visible irreversible losses

Do NOT make final reward:
`one last secret file explaining everything.`
