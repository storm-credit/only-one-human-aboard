# P1-A — Domestic / family material culture

## Reference pool

### Singapore HDB town/neighbourhood planning
Studied principles:
- daily life is not only inside the flat,
- shops, childcare, healthcare, food, play, community space and transit form one lived neighbourhood,
- local shared spaces support repeated encounters across age groups,
- neighbourhood centres evolve/upgraded over decades,
- residents can co-create/modify common social spaces.

### HDB community participatory projects
Studied principles:
- ordinary residents alter murals, gardens, social nodes and common-use spaces,
- shared space accumulates local identity without becoming a faction headquarters,
- child/senior/family activities coexist.

## BORROW for Meridian
- Midring home scenes must include thresholds between home and neighbourhood life,
- school route / food / small service / play / sitting / community repair all within ordinary walking network,
- homes are personalized internally while building utility interfaces remain standardized,
- common courtyards may be modified by residents over years,
- family clutter differs by household life stage.

## DO NOT BORROW
- Singapore-specific facade/void-deck geometry,
- tropical greenery/climate,
- current HDB unit layouts,
- national/ethnic policy as Meridian social template.

## Domestic object families to collect/design later
- modular storage accumulated across years,
- child school/work objects,
- repaired small furniture,
- kitchen/food-service containers,
- family display objects/prints,
- hobby/model shelves,
- worn bags/shoes,
- old-but-unremarkable household devices,
- neighborhood notices / shared-space objects.

## Hard anti-minimalism rule
Meridian homes are NOT default minimalist capsules.
A family home should reveal:
- who lives there,
- age of children,
- schedule,
- hobbies,
- repair/replace choices,
- relationship history,
without requiring exposition.

### Verdict
# **DOMESTIC/FAMILY REFERENCE P1 = CLOSED AT SYSTEM LEVEL**
Exact individual household boards remain downstream character art work.

# P1-B — Med-University rehab / teaching / public care

## Reference pool

### Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital
Studied principles:
- rehabilitation extends into gym, halls, outdoor therapy garden and diverse walking surfaces,
- accessibility is embedded in ordinary fixtures,
- patient/family experience and research/teaching can share one institution,
- nature/light are integrated without turning hospital into spa fantasy.

### Neilsen Rehabilitation Hospital
Studied principles:
- inpatient, outpatient, research/engineering labs and rehabilitation gym co-locate,
- circulation itself can contain therapy,
- mobility garage/fab lab allows patients, clinicians and engineers to modify assistive devices,
- family support and public/social functions coexist with clinical work.

### National Rehabilitation Hospital
Studied principles:
- living/social spaces and therapy areas are integrated,
- wards connect directly to outdoor spaces,
- daily routine relearning is spatially supported.

## BORROW for Meridian Med-University
Med is not a white corridor cluster.
It contains:
- ordinary public entry/wayfinding,
- teaching observation and student movement,
- rehab walking/mobility routes,
- assistive-device fitting/repair,
- family waiting/support,
- public food/service edges,
- clinical rooms,
- emergency flow that can temporarily overtake ordinary flow.

## DO NOT BORROW
- literal contemporary hospital interiors,
- hotel/spa aesthetic,
- glass-and-wood prestige healthcare cliché,
- every patient room being luxurious/private because reference examples are premium institutions.

## Landmark correction
Current `rehab/education promenade` is validated.
Its visual identity should come from:
- varied movement speeds,
- mobility aids,
- teaching/demo niches,
- accessible rest edges,
- clinical task light mixed with civic daylight-like light,
- visible transitions between treatment and ordinary life.

### Verdict
# **MED/REHAB REFERENCE P1 = CLOSED**

# P1-C — Destination low-O2 field breathing / PPE

## Primary technical boundary
NIOSH:
air-purifying respirators filter contaminants but do not supply missing oxygen.

NASA human-system atmosphere guidance:
oxygen partial pressure and total pressure must be treated together; breathing support is an atmosphere/physiology system issue, not a costume detail.

## Design consequence
Filter-only mask = rejected.

Current selected outdoor hierarchy is controlled by:
`docs/design-v3/V3-DESTINATION-RESPIRATORY-SUPPORT-REPAIR-v0.1.md`.

Default work form:
- compact supplemental breathing-gas unit,
- demand/mixing control,
- environment sensor,
- optional separate contaminant filter,
- separate weather/eye/work PPE.

Long-range/specialist:
- larger supply or semi-closed rebreather-like system.

Indoor:
- processed oxygen-compatible civic atmosphere; no mask default.

## Visual reference rule
Do not copy modern industrial respirator shells.
Borrow functional decomposition only:
- gas source,
- regulator,
- fit/interface,
- monitoring,
- duration,
- emergency backup,
- job-specific additions.

### Verdict
# **FIELD PPE / LOW-O2 P1 = CLOSED AT FUNCTIONAL LEVEL**
Exact far-future form waits for original concept synthesis.
