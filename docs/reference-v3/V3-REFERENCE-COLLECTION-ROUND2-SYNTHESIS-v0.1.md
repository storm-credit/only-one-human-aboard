# 1. Character visual direction — 4 approaches

## C-A — Prestige-SF Attractive Uniformity
Everyone is photogenic, lean, clean, lightly weathered.

Strength:
- easy cover art.

Failure:
- same-face AI syndrome,
- age/body diversity collapses,
- ordinary city becomes casting catalogue.

Verdict: **REJECT**.

## C-B — Animation-Level Shape Exaggeration
Every character gets very strong shape language and body contrast.

Strength:
- highly recognisable.

Failure:
- may feel stylised/cartoonish against restrained mystery thriller,
- can make jobs read as archetypes.

Verdict: **SUPPORT ONLY**.

## C-C — Documentary Human Realism
Broad ordinary face/body/age pool, subtle asymmetry, work-derived posture.

Strength:
- believable adult cast,
- low same-face risk,
- strong emotional realism.

Failure:
- weak small-size recognition if used alone.

Verdict: **BASE**.

## C-D — Grounded Morphology + Selective Shape Anchors
Documentary-real faces/bodies plus 1~2 strong silhouette/posture/material anchors per character.

Examples:
- protagonist: long face + forward surface-reading crouch + short asymmetric field jacket,
- Mira: compact body + moving scan posture + cross-body planning carry,
- Selene: broad cheek/body balance + planted threshold stance,
- Gideon: older sturdy lower-face mass + economical movement,
- Arun: broader ordinary worker frame + instructor stance, explicitly not protagonist crouch.

Verdict:
# **SELECT C-D**

Hard gate:
face remains identifiable with hair/color removed.

# 2. Machine/craft direction — 4 approaches

## M-A — Clean Industrial Product Family
Smooth modular housings, clear interfaces, controlled panels.

Risk:
too Apple/Tesla-like, little 450-year repair history.

Verdict: SUPPORT.

## M-B — Heavy Exposed Industrial
Walkways, service panels, clamps, hoses, sacrificial pieces, visible access.

Risk:
everything becomes grimy refinery equipment.

Verdict: SUPPORT.

## M-C — Biomimetic/Legged Machine Identity
Crawler/inspection systems gain strong legged/organic movement.

Risk:
ANYmal influence becomes too obvious; every machine becomes spider/dog-like.

Verdict: REJECT AS FAMILY-WIDE RULE.

## M-D — Interface Lineage + Role-Specific Body
Shared collars, fasteners, diagnostics, safety markers and service logic; each machine body is dominated by its actual role.

Examples:
- crawler = low clamp/access body,
- mapping drone = flat sensor body,
- tug = load/coupling body,
- rescue skiff = casualty access body,
- Outer Ark service tug = open capture/service frame,
- deployment barge = cargo dominates silhouette.

Generations change through:
- sensors,
- control autonomy,
- materials,
- service accessibility,
- payload layout,
not arbitrary cosmetic redesign.

Verdict:
# **SELECT M-D**

This is the primary collectibility engine for machines.

# 3. Civic/background direction — 4 approaches

## B-A — Unified Industrial Ship
All zones share pipes, metal, service corridors.

Failure:
Old Works swallows the entire city.

Verdict: REJECT.

## B-B — Strong Zone Biomes
Garden green, Med white, Belt dark, Midring warm, Old Works orange, etc.

Failure:
feels like game levels/factions.

Verdict: REJECT.

## B-C — One Construction Civilization, Different Use Histories
Same signage, safety interface, structural kits and maintenance standards recur; spatial use creates local identity.

Strength:
one city.

Risk:
not memorable enough alone.

Verdict: BASE.

## B-D — Shared Standards + Landmark Cross-Pollination
Use B-C plus major civic spaces where functions overlap:
- Belt landmark includes pause/light/plant life,
- Garden still reveals structural/service infrastructure,
- Med contains public teaching/caregiver/social movement,
- Midring has school/service/work overlaps,
- Old Works has ordinary break/rest/social edges.

Real-reference logic:
transit/garden/public-space combinations prove categories do not need to be isolated.

Verdict:
# **SELECT B-D**

# 4. Destination direction — 4 approaches

## D-A — Iceland Clone
Black basalt, moss, waterfalls, geothermal steam.

Failure:
recognisably Earth tourism destination.

Verdict: REJECT.

## D-B — Faroe Clone
Fjords, cliffs, mist, green slopes.

Failure:
Earth North Atlantic copy.

Verdict: REJECT.

## D-C — Generic Blue-Green Exoplanet
Pretty open sky, water, mountains.

Failure:
forgettable AI concept art.

Verdict: REJECT.

## D-D — Process-Synthesised Cool Temperate Water World
Combine process families rather than landmarks:
- dark mineral/basalt textures in selected zones,
- broad inland-sea/coastal horizon,
- low vegetation/engineered biocontainment early rather than Earth grass carpet,
- frequent low cloud / shifting light,
- visible water cycle,
- mineral-rich highlands,
- weathered open terrain,
- breathing support clearly present without pressure-suit silhouette.

The planet should feel rewarding because of:
- real sky,
- wind,
- distance,
- uncontrolled weather,
- horizon,
not because it is a perfect Earth copy.

Verdict:
# **SELECT D-D**

# 5. Material culture direction — 4 approaches

## L-A — Minimal Future
Few objects, clean multipurpose devices.
Failure: no collectibility, no personal history.

## L-B — Retro Repair Culture
Everything visibly patched/old.
Failure: caricature; no prosperous ordinary life.

## L-C — Consumer Abundance
Many branded variants and fashion lines.
Failure: world becomes product catalogue.

## L-D — Durable Ordinary Diversity
Mix:
- long-lived repairable basics,
- periodically replaced cheap objects,
- personal customization,
- professional certified equipment,
- hobby replicas/miniatures,
- inherited/reissued designs.

Most objects remain ordinary/no-payoff.

Verdict:
# **SELECT L-D**

# 6. Collectibility direction — 4 approaches

## K-A — Rarity
Rare items/models become desirable.
Failure: RPG loot logic.
REJECT.

## K-B — Power/Performance Progression
Newer model = stronger/better.
Failure: tech-tree logic.
REJECT.

## K-C — Nostalgia/Antique Worship
Old = better/more meaningful.
Failure: sentimental fetish.
REJECT.

## K-D — Recognition Through Lineage + Use History
Desire comes from knowing:
- this is the same interface family,
- this machine was repaired three times,
- this model has one superior and one inferior trait,
- this place changed function,
- this object stayed ordinary while its owner changed,
- this Act9 machine descends from familiar earlier standards.

Verdict:
# **SELECT K-D**

# 7. New brainstorm insight — three layers of collectibility

The project should not treat all collectible appeal as one thing.

## Layer 1 — Character attachment
`whose object/place/machine is this?`
Examples: field case, Mira tags, Niko qualification marker.

## Layer 2 — Functional recognition
`what family/job is this?`
Examples: crawler generation, tug coupling family, rescue access system.

## Layer 3 — Historical completion
`how did it change across the series?`
Examples:
normal → damaged → repaired → repurposed → descendant settlement form.

A strong HIGH-memory element usually needs at least 2 layers.
Not every object needs all 3.

# 8. Reference acquisition quotas before final visual Freeze

These are research targets, not lore counts.

## Core character (13)
Per character final synthesis input should have approximately:
- 4~6 real non-celebrity morphology/age/body references,
- 2 work/posture references,
- 1~2 stylised silhouette/shape references,
- 2 clothing/material references.

No one face source >25% of final morphology decision.

## HIGH machine families (~6)
Per family:
- >=3 real machines/industrial mechanisms,
- >=2 entertainment/concept process references,
- >=1 maintenance/serviceability reference,
- >=1 generation/retrofit example.

## Major civic zones
Per zone:
- >=3 real architecture/public-use references,
- >=1 fiction/game readability reference,
- >=1 emergency/transformation reference.

## Destination
Use >=4 distinct Earth process/geology/weather families.
No one real location should define >35% of visual decisions.
