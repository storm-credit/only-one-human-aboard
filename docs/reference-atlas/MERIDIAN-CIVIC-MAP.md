# 1. 1환 — Civic / Old Urban Emphasis

Status: `CANON-V1 macro function / V2-CANDIDATE urban depth`.

Visible civic texture may include:
- civic/legal administration,
- education,
- major clinical presence,
- cultural/community venues,
- mixed commercial streets,
- old and newer housing,
- **구시가지**.

## 구시가지
A living historically layered district, not a sealed ruin dungeon.

Typical reference qualities:
- older retrofit layers,
- heritage value,
- resident networks,
- awkward but functional building/service interfaces,
- real repair/closure/relocation politics.

Jun Imai has strong ties here.

Hard:
- 1환 is not elite-only.
- old buildings are not automatically unsafe.
- heritage is not automatic immunity from repair/closure.

# 4. Spine / 척추부

Status: `CANON-V1`.

Citizen-facing functions:
- controlled inter-Habitat transfer,
- inter-Habitat express movement,
- logistics/traffic,
- technical/service access,
- arrival staging in later Acts.

Kai's early logistics/traffic apprenticeship strongly intersects the Spine.

Travel rule:
ordinary cross-Habitat `60–120m` is **door-to-door multi-leg time**, not the speed of one central train.

A normal trip may be:
`home → local transit → radial/low-g transfer → Spine → target Habitat interface → local leg → destination`.

# 6. Outer Works / 외곽작업부

Status: `CANON-V1`.

Not an ordinary residential district.

Citizen-facing meaning:
- trained exterior/outer technical work,
- high-risk maintenance/inspection,
- specialized access and equipment,
- important Act5~7 arrival/redundancy work.

Hard:
- ordinary citizens are not all EVA workers,
- Outer Works competence is specialized,
- no military-spacefighter culture is implied.

# 7. Neighborhood Unit — V2 Candidate

A normal neighborhood should support ordinary life within reasonable local travel:
- housing,
- basic food/retail,
- school/youth access,
- primary health/service access,
- public/community space,
- local transit,
- emergency refuge/access.

Not every service exists in every block.
Polycentricity means citizens often live locally while specialized work/services create longer trips.

Exact district/neighborhood count is `C2-ELASTIC` pending final quantitative QA.

# 10. Arrival-Era Civic Geography

Before insertion:
- Meridian remains the sole mature city.
- orbital/surface/new-habitat categories are plans/training/assignments, not finished towns.

After insertion through ending ~+1y:
- Meridian remains overwhelming population/civic center,
- small orbital/resource work residences become real,
- a small surface foothold becomes real,
- new habitat remains future construction program.

Do not draw four equal cities at ending.
