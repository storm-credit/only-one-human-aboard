# Citizen Life-Cycle Flow

Status: `PACKAGE H REFERENCE / NAVIGATION ONLY / NOT CANON`
Date: 2026-08-18

Purpose:
Provide a one-page route through the ordinary institutions/rules a citizen may encounter from prenatal care to death.

## 1. Prenatal / Birth

```text
pregnancy / reproductive planning
        ↓
ordinary prenatal care
        ↓
Seed pathway is modern standard but lawful full-window refusal remains possible
        ↓
physical birth / infant care
        ↓
civic identity + parent/dependent registration
```

Hard:
- Seed is not personality programming.
- refusal is not criminal by itself.
- Natural-Origin child is not lower citizen.
- birth capacity is a physical/civic commons, not a wealth auction.

## 2. Early Childhood

Citizen/dependent receives:
- safe housing floor,
- food/water/environment,
- primary/basic healthcare,
- childcare/family/community life,
- protected developing autonomy/privacy.

Family forms can include:
- partnered parents,
- sole parent,
- donor conception,
- adoption,
- guardianship.

Genetic donor ≠ automatic legal/social parent.

## 3. School Age

Common public educational foundation.

Typical flow:
```text
basic common curriculum
   + friends / play / sport / media / culture
   + growing privacy and voice
        ↓
adolescent choice expands
        ↓
academic / technical / creative / vocational exploration
```

No AI score permanently assigns a life path.

## 4. Adolescence / Transition To Adult Autonomy

Growing rights/voice in:
- residence preference,
- education/training,
- activities,
- privacy,
- work/apprenticeship,
- medical decisions appropriate to competence/age.

Exact civil-majority thresholds remain a later legal micropass if story scenes require precision.

## 5. Education / Apprenticeship / Work Entry

Routes may include:
- higher study,
- professional programs,
- paid apprenticeship,
- direct work + modular credentials,
- later retraining.

Labor system uses:
- career choice,
- credentials for real safety/competence,
- wages/incentives/scholarships to fill shortages,
- narrow emergency staffing obligations only when genuine safety requires.

No inherited/lifetime job caste.

## 6. Housing / Money / Adult Household

Citizen may use:
- rental/lease-like tenure,
- cooperative housing interest,
- transferable residential interest,
- temporary/institutional housing,
- private interior goods.

Money matters for:
- location,
- space/privacy,
- quality/customization,
- dining/leisure,
- savings/debt/business.

Civic Floor protects minimum safe shelter/services, not preferred lifestyle.

## 7. Partnership / Parenthood / Separation

Legal dimensions are separate:
- partnership/marriage,
- parenthood,
- guardianship,
- residence,
- property.

Possible events:
```text
partnership or no partnership
parenthood or no parenthood
adoption/donor/sole-parent route
        ↓
shared or separate household arrangements
        ↓
divorce/separation if relationship ends
        ↓
child welfare/support/residence reviewed separately
```

Population policy does not license private adult intimacy.

## 8. Health / Disability / Mental Health

Universal clinical floor covers ordinary/basic/emergency access.

A citizen may encounter:
- preventive/primary care,
- acute illness/injury,
- chronic disease,
- cancer/infection/metabolic disease,
- disability/rehabilitation,
- mental-health care/crisis support.

Advanced future medicine improves outcomes but does not erase illness/disability/aging.

Mental illness ≠ criminality.

## 9. Severe Neural Injury / Reconstruction Branch

```text
catastrophic neural injury
        ↓
surviving Neural Anchor?
     /        \
   yes        no
    ↓          ↓
Recovery Map + clinical review   adult restoration normally impossible
    ↓
staged neural Reconstruction
    ↓
rehabilitation / function / rights review
    ↓
return to life may be partial, changed and legally staged
```

Hard:
- Recovery Map alone is not a person-file.
- old map does not routinely make someone decades younger.
- reconstructed person's continuity does not automatically reverse every lawful intervening property/caregiving change.

## 10. Crime / Victimization / Public Safety Branch

Citizen may be:
- victim,
- witness,
- suspect/accused,
- offender,
- protected dependent.

System direction:
- prevention/de-escalation where possible,
- investigation with due process,
- victim safety,
- restorative/reparative options for suitable cases,
- real coercive detention/custody for serious danger,
- appeal/review.

Restorative process is not forced on victims.
No universal surveillance grid is required.

## 11. Unemployment / Debt / Business Failure

Possible flow:
```text
job loss / business failure / debt
        ↓
income drop + housing/lifestyle pressure
        ↓
unemployment/basic support + retraining + ordinary insolvency/debt processes
```

Civic survival floor is protected, but financial loss remains consequential.

Wealth may influence social opportunity; it cannot legally buy personhood or hard-capacity priority.

## 12. Aging / Retirement

Longer healthspan does not eliminate aging.

Typical possibilities:
- reduced/changed work,
- pension/savings/public support mix,
- retraining/part-time/community life,
- caregiving roles,
- chronic health/disability support,
- relocation.

Tomas provides a locked narrative example that retirement actually occurs rather than being postponed until death.

## 13. Death / Continuity Review / Finality

Distinguish:
1. clinical death/severe loss,
2. whether viable Reconstruction route exists,
3. continuity/recovery period where appropriate,
4. bounded legal finality,
5. estate/property transition,
6. funeral/mourning/memorial practices.

Death remains real.
A society with Reconstruction still needs legal finality and mourning.

## 14. Estate / Heirlooms

At legal finality:
- lawful estate/inheritance processes operate,
- personal property/heirlooms can transfer,
- public heritage claims may occasionally conflict with private ownership through normal law,
- structural commons never become family inheritance.

If a person later returns through a lawful continuity process under circumstances where property changed, personhood continuity does not magically rewind every third-party right.

## 15. Arrival-Era Additional Branch

Late in the story adults/households may choose among:
- remain Meridian-centered,
- orbital work/residence,
- small surface path,
- future new-habitat path,
- rotational/mixed arrangements.

Rights travel with civic personhood; physical capacity still sequences migration.

Family members can choose different future locations without automatically ending family/legal relationship.

## Random Life-Cycle Guardrails

A normal citizen should never require these assumptions:
- every child becomes an arrival specialist,
- every illness becomes Reconstruction,
- every job is assigned by AI/state,
- every home is owned outright,
- every couple must reproduce,
- every death can be reversed,
- every retirement means 2환 garden life,
- every arrival citizen leaves Meridian.
