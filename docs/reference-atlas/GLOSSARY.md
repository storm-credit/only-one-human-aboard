## Hard Semantic Guardrails

- `Human` without historical/legal qualifier must not be used to mean `only morally real person`.
- `Synthetic-Origin` does not mean machine-bodied adult AI.
- `Seed` does not mean memory/personality upload.
- `Recovery Map` does not mean downloadable soul.
- `Reconstruction` does not mean immortality or age reset.
- `Habitat` emphasis does not mean caste.
- `Old relic` does not mean stronger technology.
- `settler` does not mean Amara-controlled legitimacy.
