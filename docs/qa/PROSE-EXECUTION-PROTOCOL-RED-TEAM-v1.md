# RT-01 — Is `Primary Actor = POV` too rigid?

## Attack
Episode Blueprint의 Primary Actor는 해당 화의 기능상 주도자이지 반드시 감정적으로 가장 좋은 POV라는 뜻은 아니다.
예:
- institution-wide decision
- family confrontation
- public Count
- multi-party hearing
에서 causal owner와 best experiential POV가 다를 수 있다.

If forced:
POV becomes mechanical.

## Repair
POV selection is a **separate execution field**.

Priority:
1. irreversible choice owner
2. immediate cost bearer
3. information-fair observer
4. Maren if genuinely tied

`Primary Actor` is strong evidence, not automatic identity.

Before prose, every episode receives a single `Execution POV` label.
This can be assigned incrementally by Act before drafting, because it does not alter Canon/episode causality.

## Verdict
`PASS WITH REPAIR`

# RT-02 — Does 45~55% Maren POV weaken protagonist clarity?

## Attack
A webnovel reader may feel ensemble drift if half the series leaves Maren.

## Test
Maren remains:
- opening anchor
- central family axis
- recurring professional axis
- bridge among Continuity/privacy/arrival questions
- final narrow continuity authority

Other POVs already own causal arcs by Canon.

Protagonist clarity is determined more by **series-level return gravity** than raw percentage.

## Repair
Do not enforce a low quota.
Use guideline:
- Maren should remain **single largest POV owner**.
- opening EP001~020 should be Maren-dominant but not exclusive.
- no core non-Maren arc may require Maren to be present just to justify POV.

Target share stays roughly 45~60%, not a hard number.

## Verdict
`PASS`

---

# RT-03 — Could Amara pre-Reveal POV itself signal she is special?

## Attack
If she suddenly receives dedicated POV before Count while other ordinary workers do not, experienced mystery readers may flag her.

## Repair
Her pre-Reveal appearances do not all need to be her POV.

Rule:
- at least one can be Amara POV for autonomous life
- others may be Leo/Kai/ordinary co-worker/Maren-adjacent observation depending episode function
- no camera grammar or body sensation marks her as uncanny
- Amara POV cannot inspect her own origin records before the planned sequence

POV significance is diluted by established Ella/Noah/Raul/Jun/Ines POV rotation before Amara becomes central.

## Verdict
`PASS WITH ATTENTION`

---

# RT-04 — Does one POV per episode hurt insertion scale?

## Attack
System insertion, first depot deployment, family dispersal and public transition happen at civilization scale.
A single POV could make climax feel small.

## Repair
Do NOT switch POV inside one episode.
Use **serial scale across adjacent episodes**:
- one episode physical operator/worker
- one family/ordinary resident
- one civic/legal actor
- one Maren continuity consequence

Scale accumulates horizontally over EP207~230.

Public facts can be observed through screens, queues, shifts, transit, changed gravity/operations, not omniscient montage.

Rare omniscient interlude remains unnecessary.

## Verdict
`PASS STRONG`

---

# RT-05 — Is episode length too restrictive?

## Attack
5.5k~7.5k characters may compress technical/social SF or encourage padding when an episode naturally lands shorter.

## Repair
Treat length as **soft production band**, not QA pass/fail.

Recommended default:
~5.5k~8k Korean characters including spaces.

Allowed:
- shorter if episode payoff is complete
- longer major payoff if scene function earns it

Hard quality criterion:
`episode feels like a complete paid installment`, not exact character count.

Platform requirement overrides mechanically.

## Verdict
`PASS WITH SOFTENING`

---

# RT-06 — Does `one new concept per scene` become artificial?

## Attack
Meaning cluster and arrival-law cluster naturally combine terms.
Hard numerical jargon cap can create awkward withholding.

## Repair
Turn into warning, not rule.

- Prefer one main unfamiliar concept per scene.
- Two related concepts are fine when one choice requires both.
- If reader must remember 3+ new labels after scene, rewrite/merge/simplify.

The test is cognitive load, not count.

## Verdict
`PASS WITH REPAIR`

---

# RT-07 — Can `imperfection budget` itself sound AI-generated?

## Attack
Forcing every episode to contain a joke, mistake, errand, selfish action etc. creates synthetic `human texture tokens`.

## Repair
Move from per-episode requirement to **batch-level absence test**.

Every 5-episode QA asks:
> Have all characters been unnaturally efficient/articulate/plot-relevant for too long?

If yes, restore ordinary friction where organically available.
Do not inject quirks solely to satisfy checklist.

## Verdict
`PASS WITH REPAIR`

---

# RT-08 — Dialogue ratio as mechanical target

## Attack
35~55% visible-page dialogue could distort medical/physical or family episodes differently.

## Repair
Keep only as broad style diagnostic.

No episode fails because of ratio.
Fail if:
- exposition replaces conflict
- dialogue becomes transcript-only
- internal narration explains what dialogue already showed

## Verdict
`PASS`

# RT-09 — Supporting POV proliferation

## Attack
230 episodes create temptation to give every client, worker and official one POV chapter.
This fragments reader attachment.

## Repair
Three POV tiers:

### Tier 1 — Core recurring
Maren / Ella / Noah / Amara.

### Tier 2 — Recurring supporting
Raul / Ines / Jun / Tomas / Leo / Kai / Mina when age/function supports.

### Tier 3 — One-off/rare
Allowed only if:
1. episode cannot achieve its emotional/casual function through Tier1/2,
2. new POV does not reveal protected info early,
3. it pays off world-scale perspective,
4. Blueprint execution note records why.

Avoid more than one new rare POV in a short cluster.

## Verdict
`PASS WITH TIER RULE`

# RT-10 — Could close third become pseudo-first-person sameness?

## Attack
If all POV narration uses same sentence rhythm and observation density, changing names does not create actual voice.

## Repair
Narrative lens differs by **attention**, not gimmicky vocabulary.

- Maren notices contradiction/process/order.
- Ella people/memory/social texture.
- Noah peers/options/escape routes; later craft/media patterns.
- Amara work/household/logistics/boundaries.
- Raul framing/audience/power language.
- Ines body/function/time.
- Jun place/maintenance/history.
- Tomas institutional timing/tradeoffs.

Do not create catchphrases.

## Verdict
`PASS`

# RT-12 — Semantic-cheat risk in prose

## Attack
Even structurally fair RW-H1 can feel like wordplay if narration emotionally endorses `real human` before explaining legacy semantics.

## Repair
Prose rule:
- factual screen/report uses historical category labels
- character/media may say provocative `human one` phrasing
- narrator does not certify `real/fake`
- EP059~066 explanation is action-linked and prompt

## Verdict
`PASS WITH HARD GUARDRAIL`

# RT-13 — Can legal/technical scenes become clean AI debates?

## Attack
Protocol still contains structured questions; writer may execute them as symmetrical dialogue.

## Repair
Every dense institutional scene requires at least one non-rhetorical constraint:
- deadline
- missing person/data
- queue/capacity
- physical location inconvenience
- someone wants a different practical outcome
- authority mismatch

The scene cannot exist solely for `position A vs position B`.

## Verdict
`PASS`

---

# RT-14 — Episode POV assignment timing

## Attack
If all 230 POVs are frozen now, execution becomes overplanned and later prose discoveries become costly.
If assigned on the fly per chapter, ensemble balance may drift.

## Repair
Use **Act-ahead POV map**.

Before drafting an Act:
- assign Execution POV to every episode in that Act
- run Maren dominance / rare POV / information fairness check

During prose:
POV can change before drafting an episode if Episode Card causality remains unchanged.

Changing POV after prose requires local continuity check, not Canon Freeze unless info architecture changes.

## Verdict
`PASS / RECOMMENDED`

---

# RT-15 — Does protocol over-constrain prose creativity?

## Attack
Too many rules can make scenes sterile.

## Distinction
Hard constraints:
- Canon
- info timing
- one POV per episode
- causal baseline/payoff
- prohibited drift

Soft guides:
- length
- dialogue ratio
- scene count
- jargon warning
- ordinary texture

Writer is free in:
- exact scene blocking
- imagery
- humor
- sentence rhythm
- minor interactions
- object choices
- emotional pacing
provided hard functions survive.

## Verdict
`PASS`
