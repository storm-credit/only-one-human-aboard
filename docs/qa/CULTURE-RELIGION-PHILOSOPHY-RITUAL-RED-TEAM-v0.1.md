# RT-REL-01 — Would Earth religions survive 450 years in a ship?

Yes, but not unchanged.
Durable transmission mechanisms include:
- families
- texts/media
- ritual communities
- clergy/teachers
- identity and art

Required realism:
practices and institutional forms adapt to space, calendar and mixed population.

Verdict: `PASS WITH HISTORICAL EVOLUTION`.

---

# RT-REL-02 — Could religion disappear instead?

Some traditions/communities may disappear and secular life may be widespread.
The model does not require every launch religion to survive.

Verdict: `PASS`.

---

# RT-REL-03 — Does a shared civic ritual become state religion?

No if it commemorates common history without mandatory metaphysical belief.
Citizens can participate, abstain or interpret differently.

Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-REL-04 — Could `mission stewardship` become compulsory ideology?

Risk.

Hard rule:
Stewardship is one civic/philosophical tradition, not a loyalty test.
Citizens may criticize the mission, prefer ship-home, or reject heroic founder narratives.

Verdict: `PASS WITH ANTI-ORTHODOXY`.

# RT-REL-05 — Does Seed refusal become a religious plot shortcut?

Forbidden.
Amara's parents' refusal remains an ordinary lawful ethical choice with personal reasons, not proof of secret sect membership.

Different belief communities can contain both accepting and cautious members.

Verdict: `PASS STRONG`.
