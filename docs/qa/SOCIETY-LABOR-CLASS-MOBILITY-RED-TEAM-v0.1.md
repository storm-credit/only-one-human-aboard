# RT-LAB-01 — Can critical-service rules swallow the right to strike?

Attack:
Government can label every essential worker `critical` and forbid collective action.

Repair:
Critical designation must be statutory and narrow.
Minimum staffing protects immediate safety only.
It cannot require normal full commercial output during a strike.
Designation/compulsory orders are appealable and time-limited.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH NARROW-DEFINITION RULE`.

---

# RT-LAB-02 — Why would a life-support worker ever be allowed to quit?

Answer:
A free society cannot function if essential workers are permanent property of the mission.

Normal resignation is allowed with:
- notice
- handover
- credential/access return

Emergency abandonment of an active hazard-duty post can be separately regulated, like abandoning immediate professional duty, without creating lifetime assignment.

Verdict:
`PASS STRONG`.

# RT-LAB-03 — Scarce skills could produce extortionate wages.

Direction:
Market premiums are allowed, but government/employers can respond with:
- training expansion
- scholarships
- apprenticeships
- retention incentives
- automation
- relocation support

Do not solve every shortage through wage caps or compulsory assignment.

Verdict:
`PASS`.

# RT-LAB-04 — Professional bodies can become cartels.

Attack:
Doctors/engineers can restrict entry to inflate status.

Required controls:
- accreditation oversight
- transparent qualification standards
- appeal
- public training pipelines
- conflict-of-interest rules

Professional bodies can discipline competence but not privately decide citizenship or labor-market closure without oversight.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH ANTI-CAPTURE RULE`.
