# RT-01 — Is 120k–130k households physically plausible?

Yes at current ship scale.
At ~125k occupied households and ~50–60 m² average private dwelling area, private dwelling floor area is only ~6–7.5 million m² order before shared space. Three ~km-radius, multi-km-long rotating habitats have vastly more gross interior surface potential than this.

Risk is not gross geometric area but serviceable volume, shielding, utilities, heat, schools and maintenance.

Verdict: `PASS`

- more rooms,
- short commute,
- view/light,
- quiet,
- heritage location,
- storage/workshop,
- custom interiors.
