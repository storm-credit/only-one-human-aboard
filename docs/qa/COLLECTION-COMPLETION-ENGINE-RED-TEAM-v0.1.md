## RT-CE-01 — Is this just “worldbuilding is interesting” renamed as a system?

Risk: `HIGH`

Attack:
If no tracked return/transformation exists, “collection engine” becomes empty terminology.

Required proof later:
For selected recurring elements, the design must explicitly record:
- first encounter,
- recognition return,
- recontextualization or changed state,
- optional transformation/payoff.

Not every element requires all four stages, but the series must contain enough complete chains to produce a real cumulative effect.

Verdict:
`CONDITIONAL PASS / EXACT LEDGER REQUIRED LATER`

---

## RT-CE-02 — Does this encourage every detail to recur?

Risk: `HIGH`

No.

Hard noise rule:
Most food, clothing, objects, jokes, stores, commuters and incidental details never need payoff.

Tracked-return elements must remain a minority.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH ORDINARY-NOISE MAJORITY`

---

## RT-CE-03 — Does location collection create tourist-episode structure?

Risk: `MED-HIGH`

Mitigation:
- do not introduce a new district merely to showcase it,
- new places enter through character goals/work/family needs,
- familiar places must change over time,
- later Acts should favor return/transformation over novelty.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH CAUSAL-ENTRY RULE`

---

## RT-CE-04 — Does profession/institution recognition become procedural encyclopedia prose?

Risk: `HIGH`

Mitigation:
Readers learn an institution through a decision and its cost, not an organizational lecture.
Returning to the same institution should reveal another boundary/consequence, not repeat its manual.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH ACTION-FIRST RULE`

---

## RT-CE-05 — Does material recognition become gear porn?

Risk: `MED`

Mitigation:
- few stable model/proper names,
- tools are introduced through use,
- most devices are referred to by ordinary functional terms,
- visual recurrence is allowed without specs dump,
- no equipment power progression.

Verdict:
`PASS`

## RT-CE-06 — Does early mystery still dominate collection pleasure?

Risk: `HIGH`

Current architecture already limits this:
- Count by EP058,
- Meaning by EP066,
- Amara public confirmation by EP075.

CE-H1 must ensure Act 1~2 collection rewards include more:
- place,
- family routine,
- work practice,
- recurring people,
- physical systems
than origin clues.

After Act 3, information-fragment collection must sharply decline as a primary reward.

Verdict:
`PASS IF ACT-LEVEL MIX ENFORCED`

---

## RT-CE-07 — Can the reader remember enough over 230 episodes?

Risk: `HIGH`

Rule:
Collection pleasure cannot depend on perfect memory.

Use three memory levels:
- HIGH: a very small set of core places/objects/institutions,
- MED: recurring elements reintroduced with natural context,
- LOW: recognition bonus only; nothing breaks if forgotten.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH MEMORY BUDGET`

---

## RT-CE-08 — Does the ending become a fan-service roll call?

Risk: `HIGH`

Hard stop:
EP219~230 must not revisit every location/object/person just to tick closure boxes.

Completion should emerge because some known elements naturally participate in new life.
Others may remain unresolved, unseen or merely implied.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH ANTI-CHECKLIST ENDING RULE`

---

## RT-CE-09 — Does “future path completion” make four settlement paths feel like game classes?

Risk: `MED-HIGH`

Mitigation:
- paths emerge through actual jobs/homes/families/resources,
- people can have mixed or changing affiliations,
- paths are not faction colors,
- new habitat remains delayed,
- ship/orbit/surface choices overlap institutionally and economically.

Verdict:
`PASS`

---

## RT-CE-10 — Does return/recontextualization remove surprise?

No, if correctly balanced.

A returning object/place can surprise through:
- changed owner,
- changed use,
- changed legal status,
- physical deterioration,
- new historical meaning,
- loss,
- transfer,
- ordinary persistence despite dramatic events.

Novelty does not require new lore.

Verdict:
`PASS STRONG`

---

## RT-CE-11 — Does this duplicate Package G Narrative Device Atlas?

No.

Difference:
- Package G tracks causal setup/payoff/knowledge dependencies.
- CE-H1 tracks reader familiarity/recognition/completion even when no causal setup exists.

Example:
A cafe recurring because characters use it = collection recognition.
A cup hidden there that later proves a secret = narrative device.

Rule:
Do not automatically promote collection elements into Device Atlas.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH LAYER SEPARATION`

---

Verdict:
`PASS / IMPORTANT`
