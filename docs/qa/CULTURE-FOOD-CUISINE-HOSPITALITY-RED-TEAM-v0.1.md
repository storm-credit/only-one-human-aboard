# RT-FOOD-01 — Is restaurant culture plausible on a generation ship?
Yes. A 300k long-lived city with a monetary market, controlled ecology and centuries of stable production can support hospitality. Restaurants consume scarce labor/ingredients but are ordinary market services, not impossible luxuries.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-02 — Would people really preserve Earth cuisines for 450 years?
Yes as evolving lineages, not frozen recipes. Ingredients/substitutions and mixed families alter them.
Verdict: `PASS WITH EVOLUTION`.

# RT-FOOD-03 — Does `heritage cuisine` create ethnic enclaves/castes?
No. Food traditions cross family/restaurant/district lines; names/ancestry do not map to Habitat caste.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-04 — Are microbial/algal proteins too unpleasant for ordinary cuisine?
Not after centuries of normalization and processing. Citizens perceive products by flavor/texture/quality rather than current-Earth novelty framing.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-05 — Does cultured meat make traditional animal food exact replicas too easy?
Avoid universal perfect replication. Structured cuts, fats, textures and heritage recipes can differ in cost/quality.
Verdict: `PASS WITH PRODUCT VARIETY`.

# RT-FOOD-06 — Why grow coffee/tea/cacao if resource intensive?
Only limited heritage production need exist; fermented analogs/blends can dominate. Exact crop list remains ecology dependency.
Verdict: `PASS CANDIDATE`.

# RT-FOOD-07 — Does civic food floor imply boring standardized meals?
No. Floor guarantees nutrition/access, not one menu. Households and subsidized dining can use diverse ordinary ingredients.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-08 — Could wealthy people buy better water/air through dining?
No. Premium beverage/service cannot mean superior survival water entitlement.
Verdict: `PASS STRONG`.

# RT-FOOD-09 — Does strict waste recovery make eating joyless?
No. Waste sorting/recovery is mundane infrastructure. The culture can still celebrate abundance within sustainable production.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-10 — Does food traceability create surveillance?
Lot/source traceability tracks production and safety, not every consumer's diet by default.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-11 — Are all meals clues because food is used in EP008?
Hard rule: overwhelmingly no. Most meals are ordinary texture; a few accrue relationship meaning.
Verdict: `PASS / MC-H1 COMPATIBLE`.

# RT-FOOD-12 — Does Ella's profession become too thematically convenient?
Her food/hospitality work provides independent adulthood and living-world texture; it does not make her the food-system expert or plot solution.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-13 — Can alcohol exist safely?
Yes under adult/public-safety norms. Closed habitat does not require prohibition; intoxication rules can be stricter in hazardous jobs/zones.
Verdict: `PASS / SOCIAL-NORMS DEPENDENCY`.

# RT-FOOD-14 — Do religious diets become impossible?
Safe substitutions/adaptations are plausible. Exact practices depend REL-H1/history.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-15 — Do Habitats become cuisine factions?
Forbidden. Local specialties are district/history/producer effects, not fixed caste identity.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-16 — Does arrival instantly revolutionize diet?
No. Early footholds depend on Meridian production; new agriculture arrives slowly under biosafety/resource limits.
Verdict: `PASS STRONG`.

# RT-FOOD-17 — Are emergency rations missing?
They exist as emergency/Outer Works material culture, but are not daily civilian cuisine.
Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-FOOD-18 — Could cuisine feel too contemporary?
Package D/B must create a few genuinely ship-born staples/dishes and shifted authenticity debates. Do not solve with dozens of invented food names.
Verdict: `OPEN P1`.
