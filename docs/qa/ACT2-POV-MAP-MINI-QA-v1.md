# 6. Tier-3 Reviewer POV Necessity

Tier-3 recurring roles:
- Protected Reviewer EP051 / EP055
- Privacy Reviewer EP052 / EP057

Could these be removed?

## Protected Reviewer
If replaced by Maren/Tomas/Raul, one of them would gain protected prenatal identity access earlier than Canon permits.
If narrated externally, single-close-third rule breaks or sealed verification becomes hearsay.

Therefore justified.

## Privacy Reviewer
If replaced by Raul/Tomas, public Count decision becomes partisan/stakeholder-controlled and weakens CP-H1 independence.
Therefore justified.

Requirements before Act2 prose:
- give each one stable minor name
- keep personal biography minimal
- distinguish attention lens:
  - Protected Reviewer = evidence boundary/source sufficiency
  - Privacy Reviewer = inference/re-identification/proportional disclosure
- do not merge the two roles for convenience

Verdict:
`PASS / NECESSARY TIER-3 USE`

# 7. Count Cluster Six-POV Relay

EP053 Raul
EP054 Tomas
EP055 Protected Reviewer
EP056 Maren
EP057 Privacy Reviewer
EP058 Noah

## Attack A — Fragmentation
Six episodes / six POVs can look assembled.

## Counter
Each episode owns a distinct non-overlapping question:
1. what did `Human` mean?
2. what is the aggregate result?
3. is the rare row valid?
4. what is the certified number?
5. may the number be public?
6. what happens when ordinary people receive it?

Each answer causes the next question.
This is **causal relay**, not reaction montage.

## Attack B — Why Noah for EP058?
Maren would provide stronger protagonist continuity.

## Counter
Maren already owns EP056's title-level private/institutional hit.
EP058's dramatic function is different: public language outruns understanding.
A 12-year-old ordinary citizen is ideal for showing a number spreading faster than its definitions without producing an adult lecture.
Noah also reconnects the title payoff to the family Reader Promise.

## Attack C — Why not Amara EP058?
It would create too direct a camera cue exactly when Count becomes public and could imply she understands the mapping early.
Keeping her out preserves locked Act3 order.

Verdict:
`PASS STRONG — RETAIN SIX-POV RELAY`
