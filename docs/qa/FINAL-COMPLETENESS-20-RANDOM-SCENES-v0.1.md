## RS-01 — Eight-year-old misses the local school shuttle

Scene:
A child stays behind after an activity and the usual local route is suspended.

Needed rules:
- child mobility/guardian permissions,
- local transit alternatives,
- school/guardian communication,
- public safety without omniscient surveillance.

Existing answer:
Age-dependent device/access permissions, school operations, local transit and emergency contact systems already exist. Staff can arrange an authorized route/guardian pickup without a universal child tracker.

New major rule needed: `NO`.
Verdict: `PASS`.
