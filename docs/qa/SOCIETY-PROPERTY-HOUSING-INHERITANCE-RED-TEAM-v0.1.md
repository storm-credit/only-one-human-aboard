# RT-PROP-01 — Isn’t this just condominium ownership with extra words?

Attack:
If households can buy and sell residential interests, why not just say apartments are privately owned?

Answer:
The distinction is materially important because the ship’s:
- pressure hull,
- life-support backbone,
- fire/decompression systems,
- rotation/structural systems
cannot be privately controlled as ordinary real estate.

The resident owns/holds a valuable occupancy/use interest and interior property, not sovereign control of survival infrastructure.

Verdict:
`PASS STRONG`.

# RT-PROP-08 — What if someone is legally finalized dead and then somehow returns?

Hard compatibility:
No routine anchorless resurrection or magical late duplicate.

If an exceptional lawful continuity scenario exists after prior estate finality:
- good-faith third-party transfers are not automatically unwound
- statutory relief/compensation can exist
- fraud/wrongful finalization remains challengeable

Do not turn Reconstruction into a property time machine.

Verdict:
`PASS`.

# RT-PROP-09 — Could heirs murder someone to inherit, then lose everything if Reconstruction succeeds?

Answer:
Ordinary homicide/inheritance-forfeiture law can bar wrongdoers from profiting.
Reconstruction possibility does not remove criminal motive, but estate finality and wrongdoer rules are separate.

No need for exotic special case beyond normal anti-profiteering doctrine.

Verdict:
`PASS`.
