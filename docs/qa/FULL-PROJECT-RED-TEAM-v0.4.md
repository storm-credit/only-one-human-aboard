# 1. Core Premise Attack — Are The Other 299,999 Really `Artificial Consciousness`?

## Attack
They have biological human bodies and grow from embryos.
If Seed is just prenatal medicine, calling them synthetic-origin may be fake semantics.

## Required Minimum Truth
Seed must not merely reduce disease risk.
It must provide a **computationally generated developmental attractor/prior for the initial high-order self-model / metacognitive network organization**.

What is artificial:
- the initial architecture-generating prior

What is NOT artificial/prewritten:
- memories
- personality
- loyalties
- adult beliefs
- life experience

Thus old `synthetic-origin cognition` is technically meaningful while current people remain lived, biological persons.

## Verdict
`PASS`

Canon Freeze guardrail:
World Bible must preserve this distinction exactly enough to avoid `fancy vaccine` interpretation.
