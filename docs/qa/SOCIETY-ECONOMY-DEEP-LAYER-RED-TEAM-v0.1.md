- job quality/control
- savings/debt
- business success/failure
- leisure
- family mobility
- education/training options
- prestige
- collections
- schedule flexibility
- ability to absorb disruptions
- relocation/arrival transition

Rule:
Civic floor prevents destitution, not disappointment, inequality or loss of chosen life.

Verdict:
`PASS STRONG`.

- queue/allocation criteria are auditable
- conflict-of-interest rules
