# 1. Present-day foundation: embryo cryopreservation is real

Current human reproductive medicine routinely cryopreserves embryos/zygotes, and published clinical data include live births after long storage measured in years to nearly two decades.

This supports only the general premise:
**an early human developmental organism can be cryopreserved, later thawed and retain developmental potential.**

It does NOT prove:
- centuries-long storage,
- zero degradation across interstellar timescales,
- full artificial gestation from embryo to term,
- an ethically/politically accepted `continuity` classification.

Classification:
**REAL-WORLD FOUNDATION / FAR-FUTURE EXTENSION REQUIRED.**

Examples:
- live birth after nearly 20 years of pronuclear-stage cryostorage reported in Fertility and Sterility (2010), DOI 10.1016/j.fertnstert.2010.08.056.
- cohort data on embryos stored 12~17 years and later transferred reported in Human Reproduction (2019), DOI 10.1093/humrep/dey350.
- other large retrospective studies report mixed findings on whether longer storage affects implantation/live-birth success; therefore the novel should not claim current science proves indefinite safety.

# 3. Centuries-long cryopreservation

Present evidence is measured in years/decades, not centuries.

Therefore:
Meridian's centuries-scale survival requires explicit far-future advances in:
- cryoprotectant chemistry,
- vitrification/rewarming,
- radiation protection,
- molecular damage monitoring/repair,
- redundant storage environments,
- contamination control,
- viability testing.

Do not provide an exact `no degradation for 400 years` claim.
Instead establish:
- storage is difficult,
- multiple Natural-Origin continuities were distributed for redundancy,
- real outer-ark damage and independent attrition reduced them,
- as count fell, preservation effort increased,
- one remains because of history, not perfect technology.

This makes the speculative technology less magical because it visibly fails.

Classification:
**FAR-FUTURE SPECULATIVE / STORY-COMPATIBLE.**

---

# 4. Artificial womb / ex vivo gestation

Present research does NOT support full human ectogenesis from embryo to birth.

A major animal-model precedent is the extra-uterine lamb support system reported by Partridge et al. (Nature Communications, 2017), which supported extremely premature fetal lambs in a womb-like extra-uterine system for up to about four weeks.

That is much narrower than the novel's requirement.

Therefore full embryo-to-term gestation is:
**explicit far-future speculative reproductive technology.**

Hard:
Do not cite current artificial-womb research in-story as if it already solved full gestation.

Classification:
**SPECULATIVE / ACCEPTABLE.**

# 5. HUMAN classification continuity

The `HUMAN` field may count a cryopreserved early embryo as a Natural-Origin departure-continuity even when it is not metabolically active during storage.

This is a mission/legal/biological provenance definition, not a claim that the embryo is continuously conscious.

This actually strengthens the setting:
- personhood of D3 citizens is based on lived continuity/experience,
- HUMAN label tracks a different legacy provenance property,
- the two classifications need not coincide.

Hard:
Never use `conscious for centuries` or `dreaming in the freezer` unless a completely new premise is intentionally added.

Classification:
**KEEP.**
