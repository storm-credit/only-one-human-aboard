## Attack

## Defense In Current Blueprint
- title itself supplies macro question
- early world is already strange through Reconstruction/Continuity
- Seed/provenance/old-category mismatch begins before EP020
- EP024~028 converts residue into wider audit direction
- first 20 have family, case, Raul reversal, Noah autonomy, Living World rewards

## Residual Risk
If actual prose downplays old/current category mismatch too much, Count timing will feel late.
If prose overplays it, mystery becomes obvious/lecture-heavy.

## Verdict
`P1 HIGH / BLUEPRINT PASS`

Hard execution requirement:
By EP020 reader must know there is a **real historical/category mismatch worth following**, even without knowing Count or answer.
