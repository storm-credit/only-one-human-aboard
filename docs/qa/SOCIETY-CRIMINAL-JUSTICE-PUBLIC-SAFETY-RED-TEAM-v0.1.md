# RT-CJ-01 — Does “restorative default” pressure victims to forgive abusers?

Attack:
A small closed society may prioritize social reintegration so aggressively that survivors become responsible for restoring community harmony.

Required repair:
- restorative process requires informed survivor consent
- domestic/sexual violence never defaults to face-to-face mediation
- protective separation/no-contact orders come first
- prosecution can proceed without survivor personally negotiating with offender where evidence/public safety requires
- refusal to participate in restorative process cannot reduce survivor services

Verdict:
`PASS WITH HARD VICTIM-CONSENT RULE`.

# RT-CJ-02 — Is a humane future unrealistically soft on murder or persistent violence?

Attack:
No death penalty + small prison sector may read as inability to incapacitate a dangerous person.

Finding:
CJ-H1 explicitly allows long secure custody for demonstrated severe/persistent danger.

Required rule:
- humane conditions do not imply automatic release
- periodic review tests continued legal basis, not guaranteed freedom
- serious repeat violence can justify multi-decade secure custody

Verdict:
`PASS`.

# RT-CJ-03 — Why not exile dangerous people?

Attack:
Science fiction often uses exile/airlock punishment.

Finding:
Pre-arrival Meridian has no safe external society to exile people into. Deliberate unsafe exile is effectively execution.

Rule:
- no routine exile into space/unsafe zones
- post-arrival relocation cannot become disguised penal banishment without due process and viable destination rights

Verdict:
`PASS STRONG`.

# RT-CJ-04 — Can “critical infrastructure safety” justify anything the state wants?

Attack:
Air/water/heat dependence is the perfect authoritarian excuse.

Repair:
Critical-zone exceptions require:
1. defined protected system/zone
2. concrete hazard basis
3. narrow action
4. time limit where temporary
5. access/use logging
6. post-incident review
7. criminal investigation separated from immediate technical containment

Political dissent near a facility is not equivalent to sabotage.

Verdict:
`CONDITIONAL PASS / MUST BIND TO EMERGENCY BIBLE`.

# RT-CJ-05 — Does pervasive engineering telemetry make crime trivial to solve?

Attack:
If doors, machines and transit all log events, mystery/privacy disappear.

Repair:
Telemetry records system events, not omniscient human narrative.
Examples:
- hatch cycled ≠ who physically crossed if credential sharing/spoofing/maintenance mode exists
- account access ≠ motive
- location zone ≠ exact private behavior
- sensor retention and identity binding vary by legal category

Do not deliberately weaken safety systems just to create mysteries.
Instead distinguish machine state from proof of personal culpability.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH EVIDENCE-LAYER DISCIPLINE`.

# RT-CJ-06 — Can AI anomaly detection become predictive policing?

Attack:
A system that flags unusual behavior will naturally be used to score people.

Hard rule:
AI may prioritize technical/anomaly review but cannot:
- assign criminal guilt
- generate a binding personal dangerousness score
- authorize detention/search by itself
- use Seed/Natural origin as a risk feature

Human investigators must articulate a legally reviewable basis for coercive action.

Verdict:
`PASS IF PACKAGE C AI BIBLE MATCHES`.

# RT-CJ-07 — Is location/access restriction a softer name for imprisonment without trial?

Attack:
In a ship, blocking transit/work access can destroy a life.

Repair:
- distinguish safety credential suspension from criminal punishment
- urgent temporary restrictions require rapid review
- longer restrictions need adversarial process
- livelihood effects must be considered; alternative work/access may be required where feasible
- restrictions cannot silently become permanent

Verdict:
`PASS WITH DUE-PROCESS TIMER`.

# RT-CJ-08 — What about rich/connected offenders?

Attack:
Restitution/fines can become cheap for wealthy citizens; local teams can be captured by influential networks.

Repair:
- fines cannot be the only sanction for serious coercive harm
- restitution is repair, not purchase of innocence
- public-official cases can be transferred outside local chain
- conflict-of-interest recusal and independent prosecution/investigation required
- economic Package A must later test wealth-adjusted practical burden without creating arbitrary punishment

Verdict:
`OPEN P1 DEPENDENCY — ECONOMY/CLASS TEST REQUIRED`.

Attack:

- restitution may use time/work plans and public compensation mechanisms where appropriate
- professional restrictions cannot remove civic survival floor
- public defense exists

# RT-CJ-11 — Are civilian weapons unrealistically banned while dangerous tools are everywhere?

Attack:
Industrial civilization contains cutters, pressure tools, powered equipment and chemicals that can be weaponized.

Repair:
Weapon law regulates both:
- purpose-designed weapons
- dangerous capability/access context

But ordinary work tools are not criminalized merely because they can hurt someone.
High-risk tools use workplace credential/access rules.

Civilian high-penetration/high-energy weapons remain strongly restricted because externality is qualitatively different inside habitats.

Verdict:
`PASS / PACKAGE C-M MUST ALIGN`.

# RT-CJ-12 — Why would police ever need lethal force if weapons are restricted?

Attack:
Serious violence can still involve tools, improvised devices, stolen security equipment or direct physical threats.

Rule:
Lethal force remains legally available only against imminent grave harm where lower-force methods are not reasonably sufficient.

It is rare, logged and independently reviewed.

Verdict:
`PASS`.

`PASS WITH SCENE-FORM GUARDRAIL`.
