Status: `REVEAL FAIRNESS QA / DESIGN COMPARISON / NOT CANON`

Purpose:
첫 대형 Reveal이:
- 충격적이면서

# RW-A — `HUMANS: 1`

Example information form:
`ACTIVE HUMANS: 1`

## Strength
- 제목 payoff 최고.
- 즉각적 충격.

## Fatal Risk
30만 시민 모두 living biological persons인 H2 아래에서는 시스템이 사실을 숨기거나 `human`을 작가 편의대로 정의한 것처럼 보임.

독자가:
> 아, 단어 뜻을 나중에 바꾸는 낚시였네.

라고 느낄 위험 최고.

## Verdict
`REJECT AS PRIMARY`

# RW-B — Pure Technical Provenance

Information form:
- `ACTIVE CIVIC PERSONS: ≈300,000`
- `NATURAL-ORIGIN COGNITION: 1`
- `SEEDED-ORIGIN COGNITION: remainder`

## Strength
- 과학/논리적으로 가장 공정.
- 시민성과 origin을 명확히 분리.

## Weakness
- 제목급 충격이 약함.
- 독자가 natural-origin의 역사적 의미를 아직 몰라 `그래서?`가 될 수 있음.

## Verdict
`FAIREST BASE DATA / NEEDS LEGACY MAPPING`

  `그 옛 정의가 지금도 옳은가?`

## Weakness
- 화면/문서에 정보가 많음.
- 너무 설명적이면 Reveal 순간이 행정표 읽기가 됨.

## Verdict
`CURRENT BEST BASE`

# Recommended Hybrid — RW-H1 Layered Legacy Audit

Status:
`PROVISIONAL REVEAL WORDING PRIORITY / NOT CANON`

## Information Order
Reveal moment must expose **both facts**, not one without the other:

### Fact 1
There are roughly 300k active civic persons.

### Fact 2
Legacy cognitive-origin crosswalk finds:
`Natural/Bio-Origin = 1`.

The historical `human` label is visible as a **legacy alias/classification**, not the current system's moral declaration.

---

# 1. Required Semantic Structure

Preferred conceptual layout:

`CURRENT CIVIC REGISTRY`
- active persons: ~300k

`LEGACY ORIGIN RECONCILIATION`
- natural/bio-origin cognition: 1
- seeded/synthetic-origin cognition: ~remainder

`FOUNDING-ERA CATEGORY MAP`
- natural/bio-origin cognition → human / baseline-human [obsolete terminology]

Exact English/Korean labels are NOT yet final prose.

---

# 2. Why The Title Still Pays Off

Reader has already seen old wording where:
`human` historically meant the natural/bio-origin baseline category.

When count=1 appears, reader can truthfully conclude:
> 옛 분류대로라면 이 우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다.

But the same reveal also prevents the false conclusion:
> 나머지 30만은 사람이 아니다.

This tension is the point.

---

# 3. Exact Count Policy

Do NOT repeatedly use magical exact `300,000`.

Current population can be a specific changing number later Canon Freeze, for example:
`~297k~305k` within H6 band.

At reveal UI:
- exact current person count may appear if natural for audit
- prose/marketing can still say `약 30만`.

Natural-origin count remains exact `1` because that is the anomaly.

---

# 4. What Must Be Explained Within 5~8 Episodes

Count reveal alone cannot sit unexplained.

By 59~66 candidate, reader must understand:
1. 30만 citizens have living biological human-form bodies/brains.
2. Seeded/synthetic-origin refers to developmental cognitive provenance.
3. Connectomic Seed does not insert memories/orders/admin access.
4. current society legally/culturally treats seeded people as persons/humans.
5. founding-era `human` was a narrower technical/legal category.
6. origin cannot be diagnosed simply from ordinary adult medicine; provenance matters.

Anything beyond this can wait.

---

# 5. What Must NOT Be Said At Reveal

Avoid:
- `진짜 인간은 단 한 명이다.` as omniscient narration.
- `나머지는 전부 AI다.` without immediate qualification.
- `생물학적 인간: 1` if 30만 all biological bodies.
- `NPC / artificial life` framing.
- narrator declaring which category is philosophically correct.

Characters/media may use provocative shorthand later, but the story's factual layer must stay precise.

---

# 6. Reader Misinterpretation Budget

Immediately after count reveal, 1~2 episode window can allow characters/readers to fear:
- `우리는 AI인가?`
- `우리 시민권은 잘못됐나?`

But factual clarification begins immediately.

Do NOT prolong false premise for 20 episodes merely for suspense.

---

# 7. Media / Political Shorthand After Reveal

Society will inevitably generate slogans like:
- `마지막 인간`
- `인간 한 명`
- `합성인간`

These can exist **as contested social language**, not narrator truth.

This is useful:
characters fight over words because words now allocate legitimacy.

Guardrail:
reader must always know the technical facts even when characters weaponize shorthand.

---

# 8. C8 Disclosure Wording Connection

ID-H1 public identification later should NOT repeat full ontology lecture.

C8 only needs to establish:
- they are the person matched by legacy natural-origin category
- family members are not public property
- detailed medical records remain private

C8 does not have to adopt the label `last real human`.

H8R-2 supports this.
