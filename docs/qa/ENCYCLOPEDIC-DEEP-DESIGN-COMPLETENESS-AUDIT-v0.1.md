- story logic is coherent,
- Canon contradictions are closed,
- EP001~230 can be written without major structural invention,
- blocking P0 = 0.

That is **not identical** to an encyclopedically complete setting bible.

Therefore:
- existing Canon v1 remains a stable baseline,
- prose is re-blocked,
- completeness expansion begins,
- a later v2 consolidation + final completeness Red Team is required before manuscript start.

# 5. Working Principle

Do not expand setting for decorative trivia alone.
Every domain must answer:
- how does a normal citizen live it?
- what institution handles it?
- what scarcity/constraint shapes it?
- what changes over 14 years?
- how can it create story texture without becoming a second premise?
- does it contradict existing Canon or Episode Blueprint?

Goal:
**reference-grade completeness, not lore bloat.**
