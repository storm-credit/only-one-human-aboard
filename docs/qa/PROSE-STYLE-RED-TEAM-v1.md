# RT-01 — `Restrained` can become slow/flat

## Attack
Characters behaving quietly and not over-explaining can reduce webserial urgency.

## Repair
Restraint applies to **expression**, not event consequence.
Every episode still preserves Blueprint:
- pressure
- choice
- reward
- irreversible consequence
- hook

A quiet tone cannot excuse a weak turn.

Verdict: `PASS`.

---

# RT-02 — Mobile readability can become one-line paragraph spam

## Attack
Fast Korean webfiction often separates many sentences, but mechanical one-line paragraphs create AI-like cadence and artificial intensity.

## Repair
Default cohesive paragraph 2~4 sentences when one thought/action belongs together.
Single-sentence paragraph only for a true turn/emphasis.

Do not optimize line breaks before sentence-level rhythm works.

Verdict: `PASS STRONG`.

---

# RT-03 — Domestic base can shrink civilization scale

## Attack
If every large issue is reduced to family inconvenience, arrival/resource/law stakes lose magnitude.

## Repair
Use nested scale:
`physical/institutional event → concrete local consequence → personal choice`.

Do not replace system fact with metaphor.
Show actual:
- capacity number/range when needed
- system status
- location change
- material loss
then return to character consequence.

Insertion/Final Approach may widen descriptive scale briefly while staying POV-bounded.

Verdict: `PASS`.

---

# RT-04 — Procedural clarity can make Act5 dry

## Attack
Precise service/engineering/legal nouns plus specialist POVs can become report prose.

## Repair
Technical-detail cut test:
If 50% of explanation can be removed and the episode loses nothing, remove it.
If removing explanation destroys the character's choice, keep only that necessary causal detail.

Every specialist episode must include:
- time pressure / body strain / queue / incompatible work / location cost / person losing something
not just correct information.

Verdict: `PASS WITH HARD ACT5 WATCH`.

# RT-05 — Emotional understatement can weaken PR-H1

## Attack
Too much restraint could make Ella/Maren rupture feel clinically distant.

## Repair
Do not add speeches; increase **specific relational evidence**:
- remembered behavior
- avoided routine
- what each person does not do
- physical distance
- concrete authority change

EP117 may be emotionally intense while still not turning into courtroom rhetoric.

Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-06 — Multi-POV sentence style may become gimmicky

## Attack
Trying too hard to differentiate voice can produce obvious tics/catchphrases.

## Repair
Voice primarily differs by:
- attention
- omission
- what counts as evidence
- speed of decision
not cosmetic slang or syntax gimmick.

Characters can share ordinary language because they live in same society.

Verdict: `PASS STRONG`.

---

# RT-07 — `Anti-AI banned phrases` can become literal blacklist

## Attack
Avoiding phrases mechanically can make prose unnatural.

## Repair
The listed tics are repetition detectors, not single-use prohibitions.

QA asks:
`Has this rhetorical pattern become a default shortcut?`
not
`Did it occur once?`

Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-08 — Count needs shock

## Attack
Factual restraint may make EP058 underwhelming.

## Repair
Shock comes from:
- information simplicity
- placement after fair clues
- immediate social consequence
not inflated narrator language.

The prose can slow momentarily around visual/interaction consequences, but narrator must not say `the truth changed everything`.

Verdict: `PASS STRONG`.

# RT-14 — Korean prose rhythm

Hard warning against translated-English cadence:
- excessive subject repetition
- abstract noun-heavy sentences
- em-dash explanatory chains
- repeated `~였다. 하지만 ~였다.` binary rhythm
- overuse of explicit logical connectors

Prefer natural Korean omission/context when unambiguous.

Do not chase colloquiality so far that institutional/technical scenes lose precision.

Verdict: `PASS`.
