# RT-01 — Infinite-copy devaluation

Attack:
If astronomical related lineages exist, why care when one Rowan dies?
Another Rowan exists somewhere.

Severity:
**P0 closeout required.**

Current defense:
- a fork/counterpart is independent after divergence,
- backup != resurrection,
- subjective continuity is locally unique,
- current relationships belong to this person.

Still weak point:
The manuscript must prove this emotionally before Act7 scale reveal.

Required fix:
1. Act3~5 show a counterpart cannot fulfill a dead person's obligations automatically.
2. At least one survivor explicitly rejects replacement logic through action, not philosophy.
3. Current-life promises/relationships have legal/social priority over alternate-lineage claims.
4. Act7 reveal must immediately follow a local personhood cost, not an abstract scale spectacle.

PASS condition:
Reader reaction to `there are many Rowans` is `that is horrifying/complicated`, not `so deaths do not matter`.

# RT-02 — Ultradense technology omnipotence

Attack:
If Meridian can physically carry astronomical civilization states at microscopic scale, why can it not solve energy, travel, disease, settlement, resurrection, matter production and every crisis?

Severity:
**P0 closeout required.**

Current defense:
- microscopic anchors are distributed over finite substrate lattice,
- active high-depth execution costs energy/heat/integrity,
- information density != free computation,
- physical hull/navigation/radiation/manufacturing remain finite,
- continuity cannot be reconstructed by copying data alone.

Required fix:
Define narrative-level bands, not exact engineering equations:
- dormant anchor cost,
- low-depth model cost,
- deep lived-lineage cost,
- heat/error ceiling,
- physical damage domains,
- settlement material bottleneck.

Do not give precise pseudo-science if unnecessary.

PASS:
At least 3 major events remain impossible to brute-force with more compute.

# RT-03 — Simulation trope collapse

Attack:
Late reveal could read as `everyone was just a simulation`, retroactively cheapening Act1~6.

Severity:
**HIGH P1.**

Defense:
- citizens are physical processes in an ultradense substrate, not fictional NPCs,
- lived histories have personhood and rights,
- death and relationships are locally real,
- final conflict is rights of persons, not escape from fake world.

Required prose strategy:
Never use `가상`, `NPC`, `게임`, `시뮬레이션이라 가짜` as author truth framing.
Characters may use them as insulting/incorrect theories.

Originality advantage:
The mystery is not `is reality fake?` but `how many real lives has this physical ark created, and what does it owe them?`

PASS:
Act8 ethics makes earlier lives more morally significant, not less.

# RT-04 — HUMAN:1 biological supremacy misread

Attack:
Title + one Natural-Origin human could imply all other citizens are fake/subhuman.

Severity:
**P0/P1 boundary; must close before Canon promotion.**

Required defenses:
1. `HUMAN` explicitly legacy technical classification.
2. Civic law already recognizes other citizens as persons long before reveal.
3. HUMAN:1 has no admin rights, ownership, sovereignty or superintelligence.
4. Other citizens protect/criticize the one for ordinary moral reasons, not worship.
5. Settlement future belongs to the civilization, not the one HUMAN.
6. HUMAN:1 can be vulnerable/dependent and politically irrelevant.

Best thematic inversion:
The system counts HUMAN:1; the story has already spent 300+ episodes proving humanity-like moral worth in countless non-HUMAN persons.

PASS:
Removing HUMAN:1's legal privilege does not break plot mechanics.

# RT-05 — Rare-memory rule versus plot dependency

Attack:
The story currently uses Rowan, Ivo, Cassian, contradictory witnesses, spouse claims, children, ghosts etc. Too many can make `rare critical bug` unbelievable.

Severity:
**HIGH P1.**

Required reduction:
- Rowan = one central anomaly path.
- Ivo = strongest robust L4 case.
- Cassian = narrower L3/ambiguous case.
- other direct autobiographical carriers = very few and mostly one-off.
- most Act5~7 proof must come from physical residue, institutional knowledge, archive inconsistency and social testimony whose source is uncertain.

Rule:
Truth getting closer does NOT increase leak frequency.

PASS:
A reader cannot assemble a stable social class called `returners`.

# RT-09 — Randomness as author excuse

Attack:
`random variables` can become justification for arbitrary outcomes.

Severity:
**P1.**

Rule:
Randomness selects conditions; it does not excuse consequences.

Every major divergence must be traceable through:
`changed condition → character perception → decision → social/physical consequence`.

Novel choice means unpredicted, not causeless.

PASS:
After reveal, reader can look back and understand why a different outcome happened.

Attack:
Personhood, identity, continuity, free will, utilitarian ethics, biological classification can swallow mystery/survival fun.

Severity:
**P1.**

Required balance:
Every philosophical question must attach to:

- a person to rescue,

- a legal boundary affecting current family,

# Originality assessment

Strongest distinctive combination:
1. generation-ark mystery,
2. astronomical civilization-search lineages physically inside one vessel,
3. person importance emerges from nonlinear social cascades,
4. rare memory leakage as critical integrity fault,
5. same people morally diverge without personality rewrite,
6. one Natural-Origin HUMAN with no lived past,
7. finale converts prediction/search technology into a consent/personhood question.

Most derivative-looking surfaces:
- regression imitation,
- simulation suspicion,
- alternate selves,
- AI civilization experiments.

Originality therefore depends on execution:
**do not market the middle as multiverse/loop spectacle; center the same people, contradictory testimony, social cascades and physical ark.**
