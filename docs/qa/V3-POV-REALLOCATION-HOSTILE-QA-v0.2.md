# 11. Knowledge-fence regression

Rule:
Protagonist presence in an episode does NOT grant him knowledge from a separate secondary scene.

For mixed episodes:
- scene A and scene B knowledge remain isolated until explicit handoff,
- later protagonist dialogue may use only handed-off information,
- no narrator fusion of Sora/Hana/Ivo/Cassian hidden knowledge,
- no head-hopping.

Direct Ivo/Cassian eligibility fences remain controlled by Writer Grammar/Reveal Ledger.

Verdict:
**PASS WITH EXECUTION DISCIPLINE.**

# 12. Pacing attack — `Will protagonist presence feel forced?`

Risk: MEDIUM.

Repair rule:
Do not add protagonist scenes merely to satisfy 94.1% presence.
A mixed conversion must do at least one useful job:
- receive consequence,
- advance his own A-plot,
- create contrast with simultaneous decision elsewhere,
- preserve emotional spine,
- set up later handoff.

If the added protagonist scene has no job, revert that episode to full absence and compensate ownership elsewhere.

The 24-episode absence list is a default, not a sacred numerical quota.
