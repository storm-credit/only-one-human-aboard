Not categorically. In a 300k mature civilization, controlled companion populations are a modest load relative to human food/utility systems. Cultural/mental-health value can justify them.

# RT-02 — Does this imply huge live biodiversity?
No. Genetic repositories preserve far more species than live populations do.

# RT-09 — Why not reconstruct dead pets like humans?

# RT-10 — Can escaped animals damage agriculture?
Possible, which is why food/ecology zones have access controls and monitoring. One escaped household pet is not automatically catastrophe.
Verdict: `PASS`

# RT-11 — Can large livestock exist?

Verdict: `PASS`

# RT-12 — Can service animals coexist with robots?
Yes. Functional overlap does not remove cultural/therapeutic value.
Verdict: `PASS`

# RT-13 — Does pet ownership become a class marker?
Some premium breeds/services can show status, but biological pet ownership is not elite-only by design.
Verdict: `PASS`

# RT-14 — Can animals go to the destination surface?
Not freely during early settlement. Strong biosecurity and local ecology assessment precede any release.
Verdict: `PASS`

# RT-15 — Does this create an alien-ecology subplot?
No. Biosecurity is operational worldbuilding; it does not require alien-life takeover.
