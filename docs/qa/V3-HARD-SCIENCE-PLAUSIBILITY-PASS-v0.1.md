# 1. Atomic/electron-scale civilization wording

## Reject
`one ordinary atom/electron literally contains all information and physical inhabitants of one 300,000-person civilization.`

Why:
This invites unsupported information-density, energy, addressing and embodiment claims and makes the setting sound like a pocket universe hidden inside a particle.

## Keep
**atomic/nanoscale addressable civilization anchor/seed + distributed ultradense substrate state.**

Author interpretation:
- microscopic anchor identifies/roots a lineage/state,
- actual physical information/processing is distributed across a larger substrate fabric,
- experienced human-scale city is implemented by the far-future substrate/environment system,
- no claim that an electron is a tiny hollow container.

This preserves the user's key image:
**Meridian physically contains astronomically many microscopic civilization seeds/anchors**
without requiring one atom to store an entire civilization.

Classification:
**MODIFY-ACCEPT / current v0.4 already follows this stronger form.**

# 2. Computation is not thermodynamically free

Known-physics boundary:
- logically irreversible information processing has a nonzero thermodynamic cost in the ideal limit (Landauer principle),
- physical state evolution/information processing also has energy-linked rate limits,
- practical computation operates vastly above such fundamental lower bounds.

Narrative implication:
Keep current rules:
- full-lived depth costs more than shallow forecasting,
- heat/power matter,
- active depth cannot increase without bound,
- astronomical search breadth does not imply astronomical simultaneous conscious worlds,
- under scarcity reduce new search/depth first, then slow/quiesce safely where rights allow.

Do NOT state a precise `X civilizations per joule` number.
Future materials/architecture are too speculative for honest precision.

Classification:
**KEEP STRONGLY.**

Primary science basis:
- R. Landauer, IBM Journal of Research and Development 5(3), 1961, DOI 10.1147/RD.53.0183.
- Bérut et al., Nature 483, 2012, DOI 10.1038/nature10872.
- Margolus & Levitin, Physica D 120, 1998, DOI 10.1016/S0167-2789(98)00054-2.

# 3. Heat rejection remains a real macroscopic bottleneck

Known engineering principle:
spacecraft/habitats must move and reject waste heat through thermal-control systems; heat rejection capacity varies with environment and hardware.

Narrative implication:
Outer Ark heat rejection is one of the best finite constraints on the civilization field.

Keep:
- thermal margins,
- cooling loops/heat transport,
- protected loads,
- capacity reduction during damage,
- resource pressure when high-depth activity and ship operations compete.

Important:
Do not say `vacuum is cold so cooling is easy`.

Classification:
**KEEP STRONGLY.**

Reference family:
NASA thermal-control/radiator technical work, including crewed-vehicle radiator and pumped-loop heat-rejection designs.

# 4. Radiation / substrate integrity

Known engineering basis:
energetic particles can produce:
- single-event upsets,
- transient faults,
- latchup/destructive single-event effects,
- cumulative ionizing/non-ionizing damage.

NASA radiation-hardness practice mitigates risk at component, circuit, software and system levels; residual risk remains.

Narrative implication:
The ultradense substrate may plausibly require extreme:
- shielding,
- error correction,
- redundant encoding,
- isolation,
- integrity scrubbing,
- repair/reconfiguration.

C4 memory/identity misbinding may use radiation/thermal/damage stress as **one contributor** to a very rare multi-layer integrity failure.

Hard:
Radiation is NOT a magic explanation for every memory anomaly.
The rare C4 requires multiple safeguards to fail/collide.

Classification:
**KEEP / USE AS CONTRIBUTOR, NOT UNIVERSAL CAUSE.**

Primary/official basis:
NASA radiation-effects references and Single Event Effects guidance.

# 5. Spacecraft charging

Known engineering basis:
spacecraft can accumulate surface/internal charge in charged-particle environments; electrostatic discharge can disturb/damage electronics, so charging is a recognized spacecraft design issue.

Narrative use:
Early exposed settlement/orbital infrastructure may experience charging/transient electrical stress before mature shielding/grounding/isolation architecture is complete.

Classification:
**KEEP AS PART OF ACT9 HAZARD FAMILY.**

Reference:
NASA-HDBK-4002B, Mitigating In-Space Charging Effects.

# 6. Act9 hazard — REQUIRED REPAIR

## Previous working wording
`charged-particle / electromagnetic-induction environment causes synchronized communication/power/control noise; asynchronous node survives.`

Problem:
`asynchronous` by itself does not protect hardware from radiation, and generic `electromagnetic induction` is too vague and may imply a mechanism that depends strongly on conductor scale/environment.

## Revised leading mechanism
**Energetic-particle / charging event + shared-bus common-mode cascade.**

Causal structure:
1. destination-space / high-altitude exposed deployment enters an unexpectedly severe energetic-particle/charging interval,
2. separate nodes experience transient upsets, charging and protective-mode events,
3. newly commissioned nodes are still tightly coupled through shared power/control/synchronization links,
4. resets/load transfers/control responses propagate through the shared architecture and create a common-mode cascade,
5. one commissioning node remains comparatively stable because it was kept **electrically/control isolated with local power and local autonomy**, not because `time was asynchronous`,
6. protagonist identifies coupling/damage propagation,
7. local teams use staged physical/electrical decoupling and independent restart islands,
8. some infrastructure is sacrificed/lost but active continuities survive.

This preserves the story beat while making the resilience mechanism clearer:
**segmentation / independent power-control islands / staggered restart**
not magical asynchrony.

Echo Outside:
one temporal/spectral feature may resemble earlier data, but the event need not be intelligent or intentionally transmitted.

Classification:
**MODIFY-ACCEPT.**

# 7. Act9 solution science language

Prefer terms such as:
- local control island,
- electrical isolation,
- segmented bus,
- staged restart,
- fault containment,
- independent power reserve,

Use sparingly in reader prose.

Avoid:
- `quantum surge`,
- `phase resonance` without defined mechanism,
- `asynchronous frequency cancelled radiation`,
- arbitrary magnetic shields that stop all particles,
- instant self-healing nanotech that removes cost.

# 8. Shielding realism boundary

Radiation protection can reduce risk but is not absolute.
Very energetic particles remain difficult to stop economically with passive shielding alone.

Far-future Meridian may possess much better materials and active mitigation, but the story should keep:
- residual risk,
- redundancy,
- safe modes,
- distributed fault containment.

Do not rely on a perfect force field.

Classification:
**KEEP FINITE RISK.**

# 9. Physical ultradense substrate — what NOT to explain

Do not provide fake textbook-level claims for:
- exact atomic storage density,
- exact consciousness computation rate,
- exact quantum substrate mechanism,
- exact operations/second,
- exact energy per lived person,
- exact number of simultaneous D3 civilizations.

These values would create false precision unsupported by the speculative premise.

Better author wording:
- far-future ultradense physical substrate,
- atomic/nanoscale addressable anchors,
- distributed state,
- finite power/heat/interconnect/repair,
- depth-dependent resource cost.

The engineering constraints are harder than the fictional substrate mechanism itself.
