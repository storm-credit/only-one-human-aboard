# Design A — Force Maren Majority (~45~60%)

## Required Change
Current 45 Maren episodes.
45% of 230 = ~104 episodes.
Would require **~59 additional episodes minimum** reassigned to Maren.

60% would require ~138 episodes, +93.

## Consequences
To reach even 45%, Maren would need to observe or be inserted into many episodes that currently belong to:
- Amara confirmation/family
- Ella PR-H1 discovery
- Noah/Kai/Mina adulthood
- Jun Old Quarter decisions
- specialist engineering/survey/registry work
- Raul legal wins

This would:
- violate non-Maren causality spirit
- create implausible protagonist mobility/access
- weaken Information Ladder
- turn Amara/Ella into `things Maren watches`
- make Living World smaller
- recreate chosen central investigator/administrator risk

## Market Strength
Very clear single protagonist.

## Fatal Cost
Breaks the architecture the project spent most of Design/Writing Ready protecting.

## Verdict
`REJECT`

# Design B — Moderate Rebalance (~30~35% Maren)

## Required Change
30% = 69 episodes, requiring +24 Maren episodes.
35% = 81 episodes, requiring +36.

## Method
Reassign observer-compatible episodes:
- some institutional consequence episodes
- some time/bridge episodes
- some family interactions
- some public-result episodes

Keep high-autonomy episodes with original owners.

## Strengths
- clearer webnovel protagonist presence
- less radical than Design A

## Weaknesses
Even +24 requires roughly one extra Maren POV every 7~8 episodes across the rest of series.
Many changes would be motivated by **percentage**, not episode causal ownership.

Highest-damage zones:
- Act3 Amara ownership
- Act4 Ella PR-H1 ownership
- Act5 specialist truth/authority boundaries
- Act6 young-adult life choices

It also risks a subtle pattern:
`other person makes choice → Maren watches/reflects`.
That weakens the very autonomy the plot claims to value.

## Verdict
`POSSIBLE BUT NOT RECOMMENDED`

# Design C — Unbounded Ensemble / Remove Protagonist Share Guardrail

## Core
Keep all current maps and remove any quantitative/structural protagonist requirement.
Treat all recurring POVs as an ensemble with Maren merely first among equals.

## Strengths
- perfect causal-owner purity
- maximum Living World width
- no forced observer episodes

## Risks
- reader may stop treating Maren as main protagonist
- Act3 + Act4 low Maren ownership spans a long middle section
- 230-episode webserial can feel like social-SF mosaic rather than one continuing protagonist's journey
- marketing/title/cover/reader attachment may become less focused

This contradicts the Character Bible's function of Maren as the primary series anchor even if not a strict Canon fact about POV.

## Verdict
`REJECT AS UNBOUNDED BASE`

# Red Team

## RT-01 — Is 19.6% simply too low to call someone protagonist?
Not automatically. Maren has 45 chapters, 67% more than Ella, opens/closes the entire series, owns 5/7 Act pluralities and major checkpoints. POV share is not scene presence.

Risk remains real, but current structure is closer to `Maren-led ensemble` than `equal ensemble`.

Verdict: `CONDITIONAL PASS`.

## RT-02 — Could Acts 3~4 make readers forget Maren?
Act3 deliberately transfers ownership to Amara; Maren has crucial EP064/081.
Act4 transfers PR-H1 discovery to Ella; Maren appears as causal subject and owns EP100/120/122.

Execution QA must ensure Maren's wants/relationships remain legible even when not POV.

Verdict: `PASS WITH ACT QA`.

## RT-03 — Does this weaken webnovel marketability?
Compared with Maren-majority POV, yes somewhat.
But forcing majority would likely weaken story distinctiveness, authenticity and later causality more severely.
Act1's 46% Maren opening provides launch clarity before widening.

Verdict: `TRADEOFF ACCEPTED`.

## RT-04 — Why not move just 5~10 episodes to Maren?
Local changes are allowed if prose testing reveals orientation problems, but there is no principled set of 5~10 current episodes whose causal/emotional ownership is clearly inferior.
Do not change maps preemptively for cosmetics.

Verdict: `NO CURRENT REPAIR`.

## RT-05 — Could the story become `POV gimmick = autonomy theme`?
Yes if discussed or patterned too obviously.
The POV system is an internal execution method, not text-visible symbolism.
Characters do not know or comment on it.

Verdict: `PASS`.
