# RT-MED-01 — If medicine is this good, why does anyone die before 120?

Answer:
Medicine improves probability, not immortality.
Residual causes include:
- severe trauma
- aggressive/resistant disease
- complex multi-system aging
- infection
- neurological destruction
- treatment failure
- personal treatment limits/preferences

Reconstruction cannot repair anchorless personhood loss.

Verdict: `PASS STRONG`.
