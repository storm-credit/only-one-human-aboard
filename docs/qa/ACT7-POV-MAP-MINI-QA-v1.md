# 1. Rights Through Transition

EP195~206 moves:
rights deadline → functional legacy translation → representation → Maren narrow case → broader rule → household scarcity → ship choice → city service floor → delayed future path → deployment staging → physical reserve → rehearsal.

POV ownership follows actual consequence owners rather than one legal narrator.

Maren EP198~199 pair is particularly strong:
- 198 she decides what she can decide.
- 199 she experiences what she does **not** decide.

Verdict:
`PASS STRONG`

# 2. Delayed Household EP200~201

Two-episode recurring ordinary household is justified because a rights/capacity conflict needs a real life consequence.

Risk:

Execution guardrail:
- one concrete job/school/home situation
- no tragic overloading
- their choice to remain Meridian changes a real queue/service forecast
- do not connect them personally to core cast

`PASS WITH P1 ORIENTATION WATCH`

# 4. EP214 Navigation Lead

`PASS / FLEXIBLE P1`

# 5. EP216 Maren Insertion

Maren is correct POV for the physical milestone because she is the series anchor, but she is not action owner.

She may experience:
- changed work restrictions
- body/space/operational cues available to ordinary citizens
- imperfect family contact
- public status changes

She may not explain:
- navigation solution
- propulsion math
- hidden engineering decisions

Distributed teams succeed as planned.
No last-second disaster is needed.

Verdict:
`PASS STRONG`
