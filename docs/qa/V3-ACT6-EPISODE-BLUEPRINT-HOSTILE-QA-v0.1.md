## Attack 1 — `51화짜리 정치·배급극이라 장르 재미가 사라지는가?`
Architecture survives because every phase has practical infrastructure/medical/transport decisions and late concentrated physical confrontation.
Execution hard:
- scarcity scenes need concrete goals, not committee debate,
- at least every ~6~8 episodes produce a visible practical consequence/change,
- no long policy-only dialogue chain.

## Attack 2 — `반대로 멸망편이면 총격전이 더 있어야 하지 않나?`
No. The intended collapse is civilization/infrastructure breakdown.
Violence is strongest only in late B34~B36 and must remain subordinate to scarcity, legitimacy and access.
This preserves distinction from generic war fiction.

## Attack 3 — `C3 자원압박이 작가가 원하는 만큼만 부족해지는 편의장치인가?`
Risk: HIGH.
Repair:
show stable operational symptoms across Acts5~7:
- shrinking maintenance windows,
- fabrication limits,
- transport/power curtailment,
- thermal margin language,
- some priorities that cannot be solved by money/politics.
Do not vary scarcity arbitrarily episode by episode.

## Attack 4 — `주인공이 EP264에서 결국 또 문을 닫는 사람 아닌가?`
Current choice is sufficiently distinct only if written as **system topology/physical coupling disablement**, not trapping people behind a door.
Consequences are delayed capacity loss, not immediate enclosed victims.
Hard: avoid visual/dialogue mirroring of Act2 bulkhead scene.

## Attack 5 — `Transit Dispatcher가 결국 데우스 엑스 마키나 아닌가?`
Architecture PASS because:
- seeded Act1/2,
- EP237 action solves a local routing problem,
- immediate result is mixed,
- social links grow over weeks,
- corridor requires Mira/Juno/Rafi/Leila/Selene later,
- dispatcher never single-handedly saves Meridian.
Hard: do not call EP237 `운명을 바꾼 선택` in narration.

## Attack 6 — `중립회랑이 너무 쉽게 합의되는가?`
Risk: HIGH.
Execution must show concrete conditions and costs:
- routes/times/services allowed,
- who guarantees noninterference,
- what happens after violation,
- why distrusted groups accept it,
- what receiving zones lose/gain.
No inspirational handshake solution.

## Attack 7 — `Mira/Juno/Selene/Rafi/Leila 모두 좋은 방향으로 협력해 캐릭터 갈등이 풀리는가?`
No automatic harmony.
The corridor is narrow common interest, not reconciliation.
Their earlier conflicts must survive elsewhere after corridor success.

## Attack 8 — `주인공이 Act6에서 너무 적게 나와 다른 작품이 되는가?`
29/51 = 56.9%, the lowest act but still single largest owner.
He owns technical degradation, signoff refusal, disablement, physical corridor support and mystery inference.
PASS.

## Attack 9 — `주인공은 정치적 책임을 피하면서 기술만 말하는 비겁한 구조인가?`
Risk: MEDIUM-HIGH.
He cannot decide allocation policy, but after EP264 he must acknowledge predictable social costs of eliminating rapid-restoration option and engage with affected people/institutions. Technical jurisdiction does not erase moral responsibility.

## Attack 10 — `도시 전체 붕괴가 8~10주 만에 너무 빠르거나 느린가?`
Plausible for a closed highly coupled city if baseline is already pressured and failure means severe disruption, not total Mad-Max collapse.
Hard:
H-B government, hospitals, utilities and transport never all disappear. They degrade, fragment and improvise.

## Attack 11 — `모든 주요 캐릭터가 철학 실험 샘플처럼 한 번씩 반전하는가?`
Risk: HIGH.
Do not force each character to have a neat moral inversion inside Act6.
Selected strong changes are enough:
- Rafi restraint,
- Leila exception,
- Selene limited relinquishment,
- Niko autonomy,
- protagonist disablement cost.
Other characters may remain consistent.

## Attack 12 — `HUMAN:1 미스터리가 Act6 동안 사라지는가?`
Intentional foreground reduction is good.
People under scarcity care about services first.
Maintain only sparse background resource/protected-load questions; do not force HUMAN clue every few episodes.

## Attack 13 — `Echo Outside가 Act7을 잡아먹는 외계 떡밥인가?`
Hard:
EP278~279 only minor technical resemblance. No decoded message, ship, face, enemy or confirmed intelligence.
It exists to preserve external uncertainty, not create a second plot.
