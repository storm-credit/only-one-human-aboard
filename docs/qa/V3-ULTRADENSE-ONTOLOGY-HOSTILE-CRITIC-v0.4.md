# Attack 1 — `결국 다 시뮬레이션이잖아`

Risk:
Late ontology reveal retroactively empties 200+ episodes of survival.

Defense present:
- one real outer ark exists,
- civilization substrate is physical engineered matter,
- D3 people possess ongoing subjective continuity,
- internal causal rules are enforced rather than arbitrary scenery,
- relationships/death have consequences within each lineage,
- outer hardware can permanently destroy lineages.

Remaining execution requirement:
Never let a late character summarize prior deaths as `가짜였어`.
The revelation should make their ontological implementation surprising, not invalidate their lives.

Verdict: **PASS WITH HARD WRITING GUARDRAIL**.
