# 5. Current Numerical Sanity — Preliminary

These are calculations/working checks, **not yet v2 Canon**.

## Habitat rotation
At effective radius ~1 km, ~1g requires roughly:
- angular speed ~0.099 rad/s,
- rotation ~0.95 rpm,
- period ~63 s.

At radius 0.9–1.1 km the ~1g rotation range is roughly 0.90–1.00 rpm.

This is compatible with a large rotating city but requires ordinary adaptation to Coriolis effects, especially for fast radial/vertical motion.

## Habitat gross inner area
For radius ~1 km and length ~5–7 km:
one cylindrical inner surface is roughly `31–44 km²`.
Three Habitats give roughly `94–132 km²` gross inner surface before multi-level local construction.

At ~300k citizens this is gross city-density order ~2,300–3,200 persons/km² across those surfaces, physically compatible with mixed urban/green/industrial land use.

## Destination orbit sanity
With Epsilon Indi A working mass ~0.75 solar and settlement world ~0.52 AU:
orbital period is ~158 Earth days.
Working stellar flux for luminosity ~0.24 solar is ~0.89 Earth flux.

## Destination gravity sanity
Working mass ~1.01 Earth and radius ~1.02 Earth yields ~0.97g.

## Thermal scale sanity
A 10 GW thermal-rejection problem is legitimately enormous:
ideal blackbody flux is ~459 W/m² at 300 K and ~851 W/m² at 350 K.
Even before geometry/emissivity/background details, one-sided equivalent radiating area is order `~12–22 km²` for 10 GW.

This supports Canon's `heat rejection is a real infrastructure limit`.

These preliminary checks justify proceeding to dedicated Quant Micropass files rather than reopening core architecture.
