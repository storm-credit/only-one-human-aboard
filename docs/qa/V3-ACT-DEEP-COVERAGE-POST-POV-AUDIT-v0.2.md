# 0. Series summary under audit

Opening promise:
Meridian appears to be a mature ~300k-person interstellar city. A late-30s ship damage analyst lives an ordinary technical/family/social life while small inconsistencies appear between physical infrastructure and official history.

Escalation:
A real closed-sector catastrophe destroys the opening baseline. At the climax, a rare continuity-routing integrity failure leaves the protagonist in a familiar earlier-seeming Meridian. He initially believes he regressed, gains real benefit from foreknowledge, then discovers the world already differed before he could have changed it.

Middle engine:
Conflicting remembered histories, social use of apparent foreknowledge, impossible repair histories, memorial/death contradictions and the living H-B Arun progressively break regression, conspiracy and small-parallel-world explanations.

Late truth:
One physical Outer Ark carries an astronomical civilization-lineage search field on an ultradense distributed substrate. Most explored possibilities are not conscious worlds; some deep D3 lineages are fully lived civilizations and their inhabitants are full persons. The protagonist's Act2 transition was one rare continuity misbinding, not time travel and not a chosen-one function.

Title truth:
`HUMAN:1` is an old mission provenance category: Natural-Origin departure continuity. The launch count was greater than one; centuries of real outer-ark attrition left one viable natural-origin continuity. The protagonist is not HUMAN:1 and Natural-Origin gives no moral, political, ownership, reproductive or administrative supremacy.

Ending:
The civilization restricts creating new involuntary disposable fully lived D3 histories, begins a temporary cross-lineage rights/resource compact, and establishes a real destination-side settlement. A genuine external physical crisis cannot be solved by replaying a known history. Existing histories provide tools but no exact answer. Current people solve it through distributed technical, logistical, medical, security and ordinary-worker choices, while accepting permanent loss. The last Natural-Origin continuity begins its first ordinary biological/social life. Civilization keeps non-conscious forecasting but gives up the inherited assumption that persons may be created as disposable rehearsals. The final future is deliberately unrehearsed and irreversible.
