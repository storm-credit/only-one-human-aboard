# 9. P Screen-Time vs Causality

P can still be viewpoint/central connective character.

Allowed:
- P witnesses consequences
- P adjudicates a case created by others
- P travels to active conflicts
- P makes decisions with real cost

Not allowed:
- every social group waits for P
- every mystery clue found by P
- every policy begins from P report
- every family seeks P approval
