# RT-CAL-01 — Why preserve 24h/7d/365d after 450 years?

Because these conventions are embedded in:
- circadian medicine
- labor/school coordination
- legal ages/contracts
- heritage religions
- archives/media
and replacing them provides little physical advantage.

Cultural change occurs in usage/holidays rather than mandatory numeric reinvention.

Verdict: `PASS STRONG`.

---

# RT-CAL-02 — Isn’t Gregorian retention lazy worldbuilding?

It would be if nothing else changed.
CAL-H1 adds:
- voyage epoch
- ship-born observances
- artificial seasonality
- altered school-break logic
- 24/7 shift culture
- post-arrival calendar tension

The calendar is intentionally conservative infrastructure.

Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-CAL-03 — Would all Habitats really share one clock?

Yes; they are one connected city and cross-Habitat transit/emergency systems benefit strongly from synchronization.
Different daily routines do not require time zones.

Verdict: `PASS`.

# RT-CAL-04 — Does synchronized lighting ignore shift workers?

No.
Residential/public circadian baseline exists while hospitals/utilities/transit/industry run continuously. Shift workers use individual/workplace sleep-light adaptation.
