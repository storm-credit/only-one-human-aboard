# V3 CORE VOICE / PRIVATE-LIFE SHEETS v0.1

Status: `PRE-PROSE P1 CLOSURE / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Prevent core characters from reading like philosophical/job functions. Give each a repeatable voice, ordinary goal, non-thematic texture, and relationship life outside the protagonist.

Names remain C1 candidates unless separately frozen. Protagonist name remains TBD.

Rule:
These are **behavioral anchors, not gimmicks**. Do not force every detail into every appearance.

# 1. Protagonist — NAME TBD

## Voice
- usually short declarative sentences,
- asks `언제부터 / 어디서부터 / 그 전에 뭐가 바뀌었지` type causal questions,
- under stress becomes more concise, not more eloquent,
- rarely raises voice until someone treats preventable danger as acceptable,
- dry humor comes as understatement rather than jokes.

## Emotional speech blindspot
Instead of saying `걱정된다`, often asks practical questions:
- `누구랑 가는데?`
- `돌아오는 시간은?`

This can feel caring or controlling depending on relationship.

## Mundane recurring details
1. prefers strong late-shift broth/noodle food and eats too quickly after work.
2. casually follows one Rebound club but is not a superfan; scores can function as ordinary background conversation.
3. dislikes long voice messages and leaves concise text replies, sometimes making family think he is annoyed when he is not.
4. keeps one old non-sentimental household item because replacing a working object feels wasteful; do not make it a clue.

## Ordinary annual goal
Complete a professional recertification/lead-analyst qualification without moving into management.

## Non-thematic weakness
Bad at remembering birthdays unless calendar reminds him.

# 14. Voice separation quick-test

Give the same problem:
`A route is unsafe but people want it reopened.`

Protagonist:
asks what physically fails and when.

Mira:
asks what capacity disappears if it stays closed.

Selene:
asks who owns the decision and what the evacuation alternative is.

Rafi:
asks who remains reachable if they wait.

Sora:
asks why it was marked safe earlier and who changed the record.

Leila:
asks what injury/casualty profile each option creates.

Gideon:
asks what comparable failure happened before and what assumption is untested.

Niko:
asks whether a third route can be made.

Juno:
asks which households/groups get isolated by the choice.

Hana:
asks what evidence will survive the decision and whether the prior safety record is trustworthy.

Ivo:
may say he remembers what happened when it opened—creating uncertainty, not authority.

Cassian:
asks how long each option lasts and what can be secured before the bottleneck.

Arun:
asks whether someone is using `safety` to avoid a decision that still has to be made.

If dialogue collapses into the same answer, rewrite.
