Every 8~15 episodes:
- visible turn, local result, or relationship movement.

Every 20~30 episodes:
- at least two distinct reward types among:
  - survival/practical win or loss,
  - relationship shift,
  - mystery answer,
  - character reevaluation,
  - meaningful world expansion,
  - institutional consequence.

Every 25~35 episodes:
- at least one real local mystery/payoff,
- at least one durable relationship change,
- at least one concrete world/institution consequence.

Every 10~15 episodes:
- at least one scene that would still matter if all ontology clues were removed:
  work / food / family / school / sport / commute / housing / ordinary bureaucracy / leisure / friendship.

Series-wide POV diagnostic:
- protagonist-centered ~65~75%,
- recurring core/major ~15~25%,
- ordinary/emergent/institutional ~5~15%.
