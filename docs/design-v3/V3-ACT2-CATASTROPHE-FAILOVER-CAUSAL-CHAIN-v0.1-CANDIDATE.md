# V3 ACT2 CATASTROPHE → APPARENT REGRESSION CAUSAL CHAIN v0.1

Status: `ACT MECHANICS CANDIDATE / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Make the first giant survival arc and the protagonist's first apparent regression feel causally earned rather than a late technical excuse.

The event must satisfy:
- first disaster is worth 40~50 episodes,
- most of it is ordinary civic survival,
- not all caused by the hidden system,
- deaths matter,
- protagonist's profession is useful but not magical,
- final continuity misbinding is extremely rare,
- clues exist before the transition but do not explain it.

---

# 1. Source stack

Use the current four-source ontology:

- `C1` endogenous civic event,
- `C2` deliberate bounded stress,
- `C3` real outer-ark capacity constraint,
- `C4` rare integrity fault.

Act2 leading stack:
**C1 + human amplification + hidden C3 → one C4 at climax.**

Do NOT make the main first disaster a deliberate C2 test.

Reason:
If the very first giant catastrophe is revealed as intentionally staged, readers may later assume every suffering event was fabricated by the system.

---

# 2. Internal initiating failure — 4 options

## A — Major pressure trunk / bulkhead fatigue

An old structural interface fails, causing local decompression and progressive compartment isolation.

Pros:
- perfect for damage analyst,
- strong closed-space survival,
- bulkhead/Arun mirror.

Weakness:
- pressure disaster alone can become repetitive.

## B — Cooling/thermal distribution failure [LEADING BASE]

A high-load civic/industrial cooling path suffers a real mechanical rupture/failure.
Heat and secondary fires spread through connected service zones.

Pros:
- allows slow escalation,
- creates smoke/heat/power/evacuation problems,
- damage analyst has credible role,
- can interact with pressure doors and electrical isolation,
- hidden outer thermal stress creates thematic echo without one-to-one causation.

## C — Power distribution arc/fire

Electrical cascade causes blackout/fire and transport shutdown.

Pros:
- accessible,
- broad city impact.

Weakness:
- common SF disaster; less distinctive alone.

## D — Water/process contamination

Industrial process breach poisons water/air loops.

Pros:
- social/medical complexity.

Weakness:
- slower survival action; weaker first giant spatial arc.

Verdict:
**B as initiating failure + A/C as secondary cascade.**

# 3. Internal causal sequence

Recommended sequence:

1. old industrial/civic cooling segment develops a fatigue defect.
2. a repair history discrepancy causes staff to underestimate the component's true age/stress history.
3. rupture occurs during a routine high-load period.
4. coolant/heat damages adjacent power distribution.
5. automatic isolation closes several access routes.
6. a pressure-control/bulkhead problem emerges as teams reroute service.
7. transport and evacuation become fragmented.
8. an initially ordinary equipment failure becomes a district-scale crisis.

Important:
The hidden mystery is not required to cause step 1.
Step 1 can be a genuine C1 failure under conditions that exist in this lineage.

---

# 4. Human amplification

The crisis becomes large because people make different reasonable/unreasonable choices.

Possible contributors — use only 2~3:
- a supervisor delayed shutdown to protect production/transport throughput,
- someone falsified a maintenance completion record for career/contract reasons,
- emergency responders reopen a route to rescue trapped residents and create a new hazard,
- security locks an area because sabotage is suspected,
- a black-market access bypass prevents one door from responding normally,
- political pressure delays abandonment of an expensive district asset.

Do NOT make one mastermind responsible for every failure.

Preferred mystery:
There may be a real human misconduct thread, but it explains **amplification**, not the entire catastrophe.

---

# 5. Protagonist job function

As a damage analyst he is brought in because:
- the initial fracture pattern does not match the recorded service history,
- safe reopening of sealed passages requires structural assessment,
- responders need to know which damaged routes can survive pressure/heat load,
- a critical bulkhead has contradictory inspection records.

He does NOT command the operation.

His practical contributions:
- identifies a false-safe corridor,
- predicts a secondary structural failure,
- helps choose a temporary stabilization path,
- later recognizes that one repair layer predates the official incident history.

# 6. Early mystery plants inside Act2

Plant without explanation:

### Plant P1 — impossible repair layer
The failed component contains a repair/material layer that should not exist according to current work records.

Initial explanation:
clerical error / reused stock / undocumented repair.

### Plant P2 — duplicate maintenance identifier
Two physically different items resolve to the same old identifier or one work order points to a location that does not exist on current maps.

Initial explanation:
legacy database corruption.

### Plant P3 — protagonist's Arun response
The emergency forces another `close the barrier with someone inside` decision.
His reaction reveals old guilt without full flashback.

### Plant P4 — tiny time/record mismatch
A timestamp or inspection sequence differs by a small but undeniable amount between physical evidence and system record.

Do NOT include `civilization lineage` terminology.

# 7. Hidden C3 outer-ark condition

While the internal C1 disaster develops, the macroscopic outer ark has its own real problem.

Leading candidate:
**external radiator/thermal-control degradation + scheduled power rebalancing.**

Possible cause:
- long-term radiator degradation reaches a maintenance threshold,
- a small debris/radiation event forces temporary isolation of part of a thermal network,
- outer control reroutes substrate loads.

Normal effect on internal civilization field:
- less redundancy,
- tighter thermal budget,
- slower background forecasts,
- reduced failover margin.

Citizens do NOT see `outer radiator damaged` as a civic news event because their world lacks direct access to Scale A truth.

They may only experience perfectly explainable local service delays if anything.

# 8. Why protagonist continuity receives exceptional load

Do not say the system chooses him as hero.

During the final crisis:
- multiple rescue/engineering decisions create high novelty,
- the local region is temporarily allocated D4-level resolution,
- many active people receive higher fidelity, not just protagonist,
- the protagonist's active continuity sits on a legacy identity-routing path with a tiny unresolved alias/integrity scar.

Candidate origin of the scar:
The old Arun incident coincided with a much smaller integrity correction years earlier.
It produced only one forgotten/contradictory memory fragment and was classified as successfully repaired.

This is **not a prior regression**.
It is an old silent defect.

Do not freeze this scar until Red Team checks chosen-one risk.

# 9. Climax causal chain

Recommended author-side sequence:

1. internal district crisis reaches structural/thermal criticality.
2. responders choose a risky isolation plan.
3. protagonist enters/works at the decisive failure zone because structural assessment is required.
4. D4 allocation rises around the crisis because behavior is highly novel and causally sensitive.
5. outer C3 thermal/power rebalance simultaneously reduces substrate failover redundancy.
6. a substrate/interconnect sector enters emergency migration.
7. most active continuities migrate correctly, slow, or terminate according to actual damage.
8. protagonist's legacy routing scar collides with a closely related lineage address.
9. failover begins **before his continuity ends**.
10. the still-active stream is attached to the wrong related lived lineage.
11. he experiences interruption and then an apparently earlier/similar Meridian.

The system did not plan this outcome.

# 11. Act3 immediate consequences

Early foreknowledge works because target lineage is closely related.

But differences already predate his arrival:
- one repair was never made,
- one couple never married,
- one colleague works elsewhere,
- one dead person is alive,
- one event happened earlier or never happened.

His interventions accelerate divergence.

This creates predictive half-life without magical future sight.

# 12. Why the event is not reproducible

Hard:
- requires a live continuity not yet terminated,
- rare legacy routing scar or equivalent fault,
- simultaneous outer failover pressure,
- closely related viable target lineage,
- exact emergency timing,
- error correction must fail in a very specific way.

Protagonist cannot intentionally arrange all conditions.

Attempting to `die again to reset` should be clearly irrational and dangerous.

# 14. Red-team checks

FAIL if:
1. one villain caused every cascade.
2. system staged Act2 specifically for protagonist.
3. protagonist is the only person allocated high fidelity.
4. a simple workplace crack reveals the full ontology.
5. he definitively dies before failover but is called the same continuous person afterward.
6. the old Arun scar becomes a secret superpower.
7. Act2 lacks any clue later proving the transition was physically prepared.
8. C3 outer stress conveniently causes the exact internal disaster one-to-one.

PASS if:
- C1 remains the incident root,
- human decisions make the outcome worse/better,
- hidden C3 removes safety margin,
- C4 only explains the impossible transition,
- all high-concept explanation is delayed.
