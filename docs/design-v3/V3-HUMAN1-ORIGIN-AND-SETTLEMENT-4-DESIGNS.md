# V3 HUMAN:1 ORIGIN + SETTLEMENT TRANSITION — 4 DESIGNS

Status: `P0 OPTIONS / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Resolve why HUMAN count is exactly one without chosen-one drift, and define how the civilization field becomes a real destination civilization.

---

# 0. Evaluation criteria

Each option is judged against:
1. title payoff,
2. no chosen-one drift,
3. physical plausibility inside speculative SF,
4. emotional strength,
5. compatibility with ultradense civilization substrates,
6. reason for centuries-long journey,
7. final irreversible-choice strength.

---

# A — Designed Single Human Anchor

Premise:
Mission designers intentionally preserve exactly one Natural-Origin Human as a biological continuity/calibration anchor.

Pros:
- clean title logic,
- easy invariant count,
- easy system design.

Cons:
- `why only one?` feels artificial,
- risks sacred/chosen child symbolism,
- can make 300k persons seem constructed for one Human.

Verdict:
`WEAK CANDIDATE`.
Use only if later technical reason becomes exceptionally strong.

---

# B — Many Launched, One Survived [LEADING]

Premise:
Meridian originally carried multiple Natural-Origin Human preservation subjects/continuities.
Over centuries:
- preservation failures,
- radiation damage,
- resource triage,
- accidents,
- redundancy consolidation,
- developmental incompatibility
reduce the set until only one remains viable.

The civilization field does not reset this count because these Humans exist in the macroscopic physical ark, outside ordinary civilization-lineage execution.

Pros:
- HUMAN:1 is not destiny; it is history.
- makes `1` physically meaningful.
- introduces ship-level loss beyond internal civilization histories.
- the one is not special by merit or authority.
- explains why the count remains invariant across internal histories.

Cons:
- requires careful preservation timeline.
- readers may ask why no new biological human is synthesized from genetic archives.

Answer candidate:
New biological bodies can be manufactured later, but they are not `Natural-Origin Continuity` from Earth launch. The legacy field counts uninterrupted natural-origin organismal continuity, not genome similarity.

Verdict:
`LEADING CANDIDATE`.

---

# C — One Remaining After Voluntary Embodiment

Premise:
Multiple natural-origin humans existed, but most eventually transferred/converted into civilization substrate existence or died after living ordinary lives. One remains in preservation.

Pros:
- personhood bridge between Natural-Origin and civic minds.
- gives historical social reason for count decline.

Cons:
- requires defining identity transfer very early in mission history.
- could make HUMAN:1 merely the last person who refused upload.
- may weaken `has never lived` ending contrast.

Verdict:
`INTERESTING BUT NOT PREFERRED`.

---

# D — HUMAN:1 Is a Physical Reference Standard, Not a Person Yet

Premise:
The count refers to one ongoing biological developmental continuity used as reference/calibration for future human embodiment.

Could be:
- embryo,
- early developmental organism,
- suspended infant,
- other protected developmental state.

Pros:
- very strong `no past` contrast.
- no current social authority.
- ties to destination embodiment.

Cons:
- must avoid dehumanizing instrument logic.
- fetal/embryonic suspension science can become distracting.
- exact developmental state changes reader interpretation of title.

Verdict:
`COMPATIBLE AS B+D HYBRID`.

---

# 1. Leading Hybrid — B + D

Current strongest model:

> Meridian launched with multiple Natural-Origin Human continuities. Over centuries only one remained viable. That last continuity is still developmentally suspended and has never lived a social biography.

Therefore:
- not selected as king,
- not the mission owner,
- not the only person,
- not genetically unique,
- not the source of civilization intelligence,
- not an admin key.

It is simply the final uninterrupted living biological continuity from the human launch population.

This gives title meaning without moral hierarchy.

---

# 2. Why genetic diversity is not a problem

HUMAN:1 is not the sole genetic source.

Separate archives preserve:
- gametes,
- embryos that do not count under the same active-continuity field,
- genomic libraries,
- stem-cell lines,
- microbiome archives,
- biodiversity samples.

Thus one active Natural-Origin continuity does not imply one-person genetic bottleneck.

# 3. Settlement transition — 4 models

## S-A — Everyone remains substrate persons forever
Settlement occurs through robotic/remote infrastructure while civic persons stay in substrate worlds.

Problem:
weak final physical transition; title-human contrast may dominate too much.

Verdict: low preference.

## S-B — Macro physical embodiment for all citizens
Destination manufacturing grows/builds human-scale bodies for the civic population.

Pros:
strong physical `first real outside day`.

Risk:
body continuity problem and enormous manufacturing burden.

## S-C — Layered embodiment [LEADING]
Citizens retain identity substrate continuity but receive external physical bodies/interfaces for settlement.
Bodies can be biological, synthetic-organic or hybrid.

The key is not `upload into a fake body`; it is continuity-preserving physical expansion into the destination environment.

Pros:
- keeps established personhood,
- makes settlement physical,
- allows staged transition,
- preserves substrate backup controversy.

## S-D — Generation transition
Only descendants/newly created citizens receive macro bodies while existing substrate citizens remain civic ancestors.

Pros:
interesting society.

Cons:
weakens final cast payoff because Rowan may never physically step onto destination.

Verdict: not preferred for main ending.

---

# 4. Leading settlement model — S-C

Current candidate:
- Rowan and the living civic population remain the same persons by continuity.
- settlement hardware provides external embodiments.
- macro-physical environment creates new constraints not perfectly represented in internal civilization worlds.
- full-person involuntary forks become politically/legally contested.

This allows final survival to happen in actual destination physical conditions.

---

# 5. Final ethical pivot

The destination does not automatically shut down civilization search.

The settlement must decide:
- whether full-person copies/forks remain legal,
- whether alternate lineages have rights,
- whether old lineages can be terminated,
- whether backups count as persons,
- whether citizens can consent to experimental branching.

Leading thematic move:
> **Safety stops being a technical setting and becomes a right people must negotiate.**

---

# 6. Critical blindspots

1. Why did only one active Natural-Origin continuity survive while genetic archives remained healthier?
2. What exactly constitutes uninterrupted `Natural-Origin Continuity`?
3. Does an embryo count as HUMAN under the title field?
4. If new bodies can be biological, why don't they count as HUMAN?
5. Is `HUMAN` a legacy technical class readers can understand without implying moral superiority?
6. Can Rowan's external body be destroyed while substrate continuity survives? If yes, death stakes need a rights/backup rule.
7. If backups exist, why is resurrection not routine?

These must be closed before Canon promotion.
