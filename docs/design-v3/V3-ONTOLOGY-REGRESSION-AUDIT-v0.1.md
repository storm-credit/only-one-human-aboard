# 2. Act 3 repair — Regression is reader hypothesis only

Current craft grammar deliberately imitates regression.
This still works.

Hard clarification:
- external Meridian time never rewinds.
- Rowan's `return` is movement/activation into another closely related civilization lineage or state configuration.
- apparent repeated morning is produced by highly similar historical initialization, not literal temporal rollback.

Reader may believe regression for a while.
Author ontology must not.

# 3. Act 4 repair — `parallel histories` is a false-but-fair theory

The phrase `parallel-history hypothesis` can remain as reader/character language.

But final truth:
- not cosmological parallel universes,
- not Many Worlds,
- not alternate dimensions.

They are independently executed civilization lineages inside one physical Meridian.

Therefore Act 4 payoff should be:
`둘 다 진짜였던 삶이다.`
not yet:
`둘 다 다른 우주다.`

# 6. Act 7 — replace simple branch/merge reveal

Earlier model implied a small set of branches repeatedly merged into one state.
This is now too simple.

Replace with:
**Civilization Search / Lineage Field / Promotion-Synthesis model.**

Possible late evidence:
- enormous lineage index spaces,
- related identity seeds repeated across many civilizations,
- policy/technical patterns transferred without autobiographical memory,
- civilizations promoted/deepened based on information value,
- rare cross-lineage pointer faults.

The horror is not `Which of three worlds is original?`
It is:
> **There may never have been one privileged original civilization after launch.**

Rowan is one real person in one real lived lineage among an incomprehensibly large family of related lives.

# 7. Device Atlas repair — prophet density

Current `Prophet Cluster` wording risks making true memory leakage too common.

New rule:
- many apparent prophets = fraud, coincidence, folklore, misremembering, statistical luck.
- a few suspicious cases = L1/L2 affective or sensory leak.
- one or very few robust cases = L3+ autobiographical leak.

Series-level L4/L5 multi-history rememberers should be exceptionally rare.

Therefore the mystery can feature a `cluster` socially while only one or two cases are ontologically genuine.

---

# 8. Divination repair

Remove any impression of a stable system side-channel.

Allowed:
- symbolic trigger of already-leaked memory,
- accidental corrupted archive output,
- one-off identity collision.

Rejected:
- repeatable birthday query,
- ritual API,
- trainable past-life skill,
- institutionalized working oracle.

# 9. Act 9 — MAJOR repair: why the future becomes irreversible

Earlier concept:
`arrival approaches → compute/branch capacity turns off → one world remains.`

Problem:
With ultradense civilization substrates, computation may remain possible even after arrival.
Turning it off merely because the ship arrived feels arbitrary.

Better architecture:

## Promotion to External Settlement
At destination, one civic/person lineage (or a negotiated synthesis lineage) is selected/promoted for macro-physical embodiment in the destination settlement.

The crucial transition is not:
`computer can no longer calculate.`

It is:
> **This civilization chooses that the people now living the settlement will no longer be forked, reset, replaced or treated as experiment subjects.**

The simulation/search infrastructure may continue for:
- weather forecasts,
- engineering,
- policy modeling,
- non-person agent analysis.

But full lived-person civilization forking becomes ethically prohibited or tightly consent-bound.

This makes final irreversibility:
- moral,
- political,
- legal,
- identity-based,
- and physically consequential.

Once citizens inhabit the external settlement world, a dead friend is not restored by pointing to another related lineage elsewhere.

# 10. Final choice candidate

Late conflict can become:

### Position A — Continue Civilization Search
Argument:
- destination remains dangerous,
- unknown alien/ecological conditions remain,
- billions of lives may depend on continued lived testing.

### Position B — End Nonconsensual Lived Forking
Argument:
- no civilization can become free while every citizen remains reproducible experimental material,
- safety has become permanent postponement of real history.

### Position C — Consent-bound limited continuation
Possible compromise:
- no involuntary full-person forks,
- non-conscious agent simulations remain,
- voluntary bounded lived experiments under rights law possible.

Final series choice need not be a simple delete-the-system button.

Strong current candidate:
**the settlement civilization asserts personhood jurisdiction over itself and ends compulsory lived-history branching.**
