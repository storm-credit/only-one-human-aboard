# V3 PERIPHERAL SCOPE — KEEP / LIMIT / CUT DECISION v0.1

Status: `ACTIVE DESIGN DECISION / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Prevent side ideas from competing with the core engine:
**SF mystery + thriller + survival + protagonist-centered multi-causal civilization drama.**

This decision supersedes earlier v3 candidate enthusiasm where it conflicts.

---

# 0. Decision standard

Keep a peripheral element only if it does at least two jobs among:
- ordinary-world attachment,
- character relationship,
- survival pressure,
- mystery clue/recontextualization,
- civilization/theme proof,
- final payoff.

If it mainly adds spectacle, genre novelty, or lore, CUT/LIMIT it.

---

# 1. Ghost-like / supernatural-feeling phenomena

## Decision: `KEEP, VERY LOW DENSITY`

Allowed:
- rare audio/visual/environment echo,
- traumatic memory projection with later partial corroboration,
- archive/interface residue,
- one or two events that remain locally ambiguous.

Author truth:
core ontology remains SF.
No actual ghost cosmology is required.

Series target:
- strong ghost-like events: roughly **2~4** across full 410-ish series,
- weaker eerie texture only if it does not make leakage seem common.

Best uses:
- Arun-related emotional horror,
- Act5 absent-history exploration,
- one late recontextualization.

CUT:
- recurring haunted-sector arc,
- ghost faction,
- spiritual medium with reliable access.

Reason:
more would compete with the rare Critical Memory Leak rule and turn the mystery into paranormal expectation.

---

# 2. Zombie / infection-horde concept

## Decision: `CUT AS MAJOR ARC`

Reject for current 410 structure:
- full zombie outbreak,
- repeated aggressive infected crowds,
- corpse-revival action arc.

Why:
- generic survival trope competes with the distinctive lineage mystery,
- Act2 and Act6 already carry major survival pressure,
- risks stigmatizing neurological/medical failure,
- consumes too many episodes for limited unique payoff.

Reserve only:
- one short medical/toxic-control incident may superficially resemble `zombie` behavior if an existing medium incident needs it,
- but it must resolve as ordinary toxic/pathogen/control failure and not become a mini zombie genre.

Current status:
**archive idea, not planned content.**

---

# 3. Companion ecology

## Decision: `KEEP AS ORDINARY WORLD TEXTURE / LIMITED`

Retain:
- a small number of engineered companion-animal lineages,
- ordinary household/service roles,
- school/family culture,
- limited environmental sensing if biologically plausible.

Do NOT build:
- collectible battle ecosystem,
- dozens of named species,
- creature power system,
- companion-centered major mystery arc.

Recommended actual use:
- **2~4 recurring companion types** in setting bible,
- **1~2 recurring named individual animals** at most,
- mostly family/community texture.

Possible clue use:
only once or twice, and never as a perfect anomaly detector.

Reason:
helps Meridian feel like a home, but is not one of the series engines.

---

# 4. Paleo / dinosaur material

## Decision: `LIMIT HARD / CUT AS ARC`

Keep only if useful:
- one ordinary educational/cultural paleo hall or mixed-reality exhibit,
- one family/school scene,
- later reuse as shelter/meeting place only if geography earns it.

CUT:
- Lost Paleo Habitat 5~12 episode arc,
- real large dinosaur survival sequence,
- branch-only dinosaur mystery as major clue line.

Why:
- too far from title promise,
- adds another visual IP engine without enough payoff,
- companion + paleo + horror + alien together would make the project feel unfocused.

Status:
**paleo culture = background optional; paleo arc = rejected.**

---

# 5. Occult / divination / folk belief

## Decision: `KEEP AS CULTURE, NOT MECHANIC`

Retain:
- tarot/saju/astrology/fortune traditions as inherited culture,
- ordinary counseling/entertainment/ritual functions,
- perhaps one eerie coincidence or one leak carrier interpreting a fragment through such symbols.

CUT:
- deterministic birth-data access,
- repeatable system bypass,
- technical secret hidden in divination tradition,
- reliable prophecy industry based on actual occult power.

Act4 fake-prophet/social response may use this cultural environment without confirming it.

---

# 6. External intelligence / alien signal — four options

## A — No external intelligence thread
Pros:
focus maximum.

Cons:
outer destination may feel too closed/self-referential; system's ignorance of real external universe is less dramatized.

Verdict: `STRONG SAFE ALT`.

## B — All alien/contact material was synthetic scenario
Pros:
clean ontology.

Cons:
late external world brings no genuinely new information; risks `system already modeled everything` feeling.

Verdict: `WEAKENED`.

## C — Ambiguous real external signal / contact condition [LEADING]
A real outer observation near destination resembles details from an earlier modeled/lived contact-risk history.

Could be:
- structured transmission,
- anomalous artificial-looking signal,
- repeating engineering signature,
- evidence of prior nonhuman technology,
without immediate face-to-face alien characters.

Pros:
- proves the universe contains information Meridian did not create,
- validates need for resilience over perfect prediction,
- adds final uncertainty,
- does not require alien-war novel.

Risks:
- can steal attention from HUMAN:1/rights/settlement if revealed too strongly.

Verdict: **KEEP BOUNDED**.

## D — Confirmed alien civilization / active conflict
Pros:
large spectacle.

Cons:
becomes a different series exactly when current mystery should pay off.

Verdict: `REJECT FOR CURRENT SERIES`.

---

# 7. External-signal density rule

If C is retained:
- Act6: only faint/suspect precursor or scenario resemblance,
- Act7: evidence that some prior contact history was not pure invention,
- Act8: secondary to HUMAN:1/D3 rights,
- Act9: real external condition may constrain settlement but no alien invasion.

Hard:
No new alien mythology in final 40 episodes.
No alien emissary explaining humanity.
No alien chosen-one tie to HUMAN:1.

# 9. Blindspot result

This cut reduces major genre engines to:
1. closed-city mystery,
2. survival/infrastructure crisis,
3. apparent regression / incompatible histories,
4. social foreknowledge consequences,
5. civilization/personhood scale,
6. destination settlement uncertainty.

Companion/folk/horror remain texture.
They no longer compete for full arcs.
