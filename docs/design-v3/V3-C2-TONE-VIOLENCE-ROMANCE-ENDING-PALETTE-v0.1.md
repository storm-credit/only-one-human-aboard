# 1. Violence presentation ceiling

Selected system:
**consequence-forward / anatomy-light / sensory-moderate**.

## V0 — ordinary conflict
- argument,
- physical urgency,
- non-injury work hazard.

Common.

## V1 — visible injury
- cuts,
- bruising,
- burns,
- smoke/heat distress,
- fracture suspicion,
- bleeding controlled in-scene.

Allowed frequently in disaster Acts when causally earned.
Do not fetishize wound description.

## V2 — severe survival injury
- major burn/crush injury,
- serious blood loss,
- traumatic fracture,
- respiratory collapse,
- lethal infrastructure accident consequence.

Use selectively.
Focus:
- rescue decision,
- function loss,
- triage,
- family/worker consequence,
not anatomy display.

## V3 — rare concentrated lethal violence
Reserved for:
- Act2 catastrophe peak,
- late Act6 concentrated violence/infrastructure confrontation,
- rare irreversible incident where death needs immediacy.

Presentation ceiling:
- short direct physical detail,
- stronger aftermath than gore choreography,
- no lingering dismemberment inventory,
- no torture spectacle,
- no repeated body-horror escalation.

## V4 — splatter/torture/body-horror spectacle
# **REJECTED AS SERIES DEFAULT**

May not be introduced merely to make later Acts feel darker.

# 3. Romance surface-share ceiling

Selected relationship:
Lucas/Mira current-life relationship may eventually begin anew, but is NOT restoration of H-A destiny.

Direct romance-coded scene target across 410 episodes:
# **~3–5% of episodes / scene weight**

Interpretation:
roughly 12~20 episodes may contain a clearly romance-forward beat, but most Mira scenes remain logistics, autonomy, trust, crisis or ordinary life.

Broader relationship consequences can appear more often without becoming romance scenes.

Hard:
- no dedicated romance mini-arc longer than 2 consecutive episodes by default,
- no catastrophe confession as shortcut,
- no `we loved before, therefore we belong together`,
- no H-A private memory used as current consent,
- no marriage/child epilogue required,
- no explicit sexual-content escalation required for maturity.

Intimacy default:
**emotionally specific / physically restrained / non-explicit unless a later author decision has a real character reason.**

# 5. Ending emotional temperature

Selected:
# **CAUTIOUS / EARNED HOPE — +1 on a -2 to +2 scale**

Scale:
-2 = nihilistic collapse,
-1 = survival with emotional defeat,
0 = cold/ambiguous,
+1 = earned hope with permanent loss,
+2 = triumphant utopian closure.

Current ending = **+1**.

Required emotional ingredients:
- permanent losses remain named/felt,
- institutions are improved but not perfected,
- future prediction/rehearsal is constrained ethically, not abolished magically,
- settlement works imperfectly,
- ordinary work resumes,
- HUMAN:1 is an ordinary developing person, not salvation symbol,
- Lucas accepts a future that cannot be restored to H-A,
- Mira/current relationships remain chosen, not destined,
- final future is genuinely unrehearsed.

Final emotional motion:
`relief → residue of grief → ordinary task/relationship → open curiosity`.

Do NOT end on:
- victory speech,
- perfect constitution,
- giant twist,
- new villain signal,
- `was it all real?` ambiguity,
- cosmic transcendence.

Preferred final-note grammar:
**an ordinary first day in a future nobody remembers.**

# 6. Color / palette production system

Palette is production guidance, not prose vocabulary and not faction coding.

## Base Meridian anchors
- civic warm off-white / mineral shell: `#D8D4C8`
- graphite work structure: `#343A40`
- steel blue-gray: `#687783`
- soft utility gray: `#A8ADB0`
- restrained wayfinding blue: `#557A91`
- safety amber: `#C28B3C`
- emergency red: `#A54E48`
- living plant green: `#6F8066`

These are anchors, not exact mandatory pixels.

## Rules
1. no zone gets exclusive faction color,
2. safety colors carry function, not morality,
3. characters are not distinguished mainly by palette,
4. Old Works is not permanently orange/rust,
5. Med is not sterile white/blue only,
6. Garden shares structural materials with the rest of Meridian,
7. Act6 wear/damage changes texture and repair layering more than global desaturation,
8. H-B does not receive a different color grade to signal alternate history.

# 7. Act palette behavior

A1:
full ordinary tonal range. Warm/cool functional differences, real clothing/food/plant color.

A2:
emergency amber/red appear through systems and local lighting; do not wash whole Act red.

A3:
nearly identical palette to A1. Differences must be history/use, not alternate-universe color grading.

A4:
public media/signage pressure can increase visual clutter; no noir conversion.

A5:
older material layers become visible; no sepia archaeology filter.

A6:
patchwork, dirt, temporary signage, mismatched replacements. Retain surviving ordinary colors so loss is comparative.

A7:
Outer Ark physical spaces use material/thermal function. Avoid black-monolith sublime cliché.

A8:
care/legal/public spaces remain familiar; HUMAN facilities not sacred gold/white.

A9:
real environment adds sky/weather/mineral/water colors while inherited Meridian equipment keeps recognizable material DNA.
Suggested environment anchors can expand beyond base palette, but not become a separate franchise.

# 8. Visual attractiveness / glamour ceiling

Core cast may include attractive people.
But art direction must preserve:
- age,
- ordinary asymmetry,
- occupational wear,
- varied body mass,
- non-model faces.

Protagonist:
refined/appealing enough for strong IP identity, but no idol costume, fashion-runway field gear or permanent beauty lighting.

Crisis state:
characters become more tired/dirty/injured through causality, not `cool battle damage` styling.
