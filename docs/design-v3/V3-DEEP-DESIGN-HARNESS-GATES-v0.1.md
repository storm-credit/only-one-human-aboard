# V3 DEEP DESIGN HARNESS GATES v0.1

Status: `ACTIVE DESIGN QA / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Prevent the ultradense civilization-field premise from becoming inconsistent, repetitive, exposition-heavy or stake-destroying during Act/Blueprint/manuscript work.

---

# H1 — Anomaly Frequency Harness

Goal:
Cross-history leakage feels like a critical rare bug.

Checks per ~40 episode block:
- explicit L3+ autobiographical leak: usually 0~1 strong case,
- L4/L5 carrier: exceptional arc-level use only,
- soft anomalies can recur but must have mundane alternatives,
- no stable community of verified rememberers,
- no reproducible memory-leak procedure.

Fail if:
reader could reasonably ask `why isn't this already an established science?`

---

# H2 — Death / Continuity Harness

For every major death record:
- lineage ID/context,
- local continuity ends? Y/N,
- backup exists? Y/N/unknown,
- counterpart exists elsewhere? possible/known/no,
- why counterpart is not social replacement,
- who remembers/mourns,
- what consequence survives.

Fail if:
`another version exists` makes current death emotionally or operationally irrelevant.

---

# H3 — Recurring Character Variation Harness

For each Core character design at least:
1. Stable expression,
2. Crisis expression,
3. Collapse expression,
4. Counterfactual expression,
5. one Convergent Choice,
6. one Divergent Choice,
7. one Novel Choice candidate,
8. one lineage where importance is reduced/absent/early-dead if structurally useful.

Fail if:
character feels scripted to always perform the same narrative role.

---

# H4 — Emergent Minor Character Harness

Across the series include at least a few major cases where a non-core person becomes causally central.

Requirements:
- person exists naturally before pivot,
- pivot grows from job/relationship/location/choice,
- not prophecy/chosen-one,
- effect spreads through a plausible social cascade,
- outcome could plausibly differ in another lineage.

Fail if:
unknown character appears only at climax to solve plot.

---

# H5 — Search Architecture Harness

Every civilization-test concept must identify its generation source:
- historical replay,
- uncertainty-targeted test,
- adversarial stress,
- stochastic perturbation,
- evolutionary variation,
- actual external incident,
- genuine novel event.

Fail if:
`random generator did it` is the only causal explanation.

Rule:
randomness explores; selection/deepening is information-driven.

---

# H6 — Exposition Density Harness

Per episode candidate:
- direct abstract ontology explanation usually <15~20%,
- no 3 consecutive explanation-heavy episodes,
- every ontology reveal changes current action/risk/relationship,
- Act7 scale reveal uses multiple evidence routes,
- hardware scale never gets more narrative weight than the people affected.

Fail if:
a reader could skip an explanation chapter without losing an immediate decision.

---

# H7 — HUMAN:1 Interpretation Harness

Run three hostile readings:

## Test A — Chosen One
Does HUMAN:1 gain authority/key/destiny because of classification?
Must answer: NO.

## Test B — Bio Supremacy
Does story imply Natural-Origin Human is more of a person than civic minds?
Must answer: NO.

## Test C — One Child Mission
Does entire civilization exist primarily to serve HUMAN:1?
Must answer: NO.

Fail any → redesign reveal/context.

---

# H8 — Resource / Omnipotence Harness

Every major use of ultradense substrate technology must identify at least one limiting cost:
- power,
- heat,
- bandwidth,
- integrity,
- repair capacity,
- substrate aging,
- external time,
- embodiment/manufacturing,
- physical risk.

Fail if:
technology solves one problem with no new constraint and could trivially solve later stakes.

---

# H9 — Physical Ark Coupling Harness

Each major Act should include at least one believable coupling to actual Meridian physical reality, but not every incident must originate there.

Examples:
- real power degradation,
- radiation event,
- navigation maneuver,
- manufacturing limits,
- external signal,
- heat budget.

Fail if:
internal civilization story could occur identically with no interstellar ship outside it.

---

# H10 — Moral-Choice Harness

For major social dilemmas:
- at least 2 genuinely defensible options,
- no author-approved answer obvious before choice,
- consequences arrive on-page,
- same core trait can produce virtue/vice under changed constraints.

Fail if:
philosophy is delivered as dialogue without operational cost.

---

# H11 — Alternate-Morality Reveal Harness

When revealing `this person was different elsewhere` prefer:
1. reaction,
2. testimony,
3. current contradiction,
4. delayed evidence,
5. recontextualization.

Limit direct high-salience morality-reversal testimony so it remains special.

Fail if:
every major character receives the same `you were evil in my world` scene.

---

# H12 — Final Act Irreversibility Harness

Final irreversibility may NOT rely solely on:
`computer shuts off`.

Must include:
- external settlement embodiment,
- current-person rights,
- refusal/limits on involuntary lived branching,
- actual physical hazard,
- novel decision not pre-solved by prior histories.

Fail if:
finale can be solved by loading a better lineage.

---

# H13 — Payoff Harness

Final ~15%:
- payoff/recontextualization > new plant,
- HUMAN:1, Arun, lineage scale, memory leak, system ethics, Rowan worldview all converge,
- no new ontology layer after core substrate truth.

Fail if:
ending requires a new `true true system behind the system`.

---

# H14 — Series Fun Engine Harness

Every 20~30 episodes must deliver at least two of:
- concrete survival win/loss,
- relationship change,
- mystery payoff,
- new meaningful place/world texture,
- character reevaluation,
- institutional consequence.

Fail if:
worldbuilding becomes the only reward.
