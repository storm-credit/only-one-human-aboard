# CAST / INCIDENT DENSITY HARNESS v0.1

Status: `ACTIVE QA / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
약 410화 구조가 `설정은 많지만 사람에게 정들지 못하는 작품`, `기억이상만 반복되는 작품`, `로언만 모든 일을 해결하는 작품`, `대형재난만 이어지는 작품`이 되지 않도록 밀도를 통제한다.

---

# 0. Series target bands

Center length: ~410 episodes, NOT FROZEN.

Core long-term cast: 10~12.
Major recurring: 15~20.
Arc-centered named: 20~30.
Emergent critical actors: 5~10 actually used from larger seed pool.

Active cognitive load per Act:
- target 8~14 important persons,
- peak ensemble arc 16 temporary upper band,
- more than 16 requires explicit split/POV justification.

---

# 1. First-history attachment gate — EP001~094 candidate

Before first major state discontinuity:

Required:
- reader knows at least 6 core characters by behavior, not profile exposition,
- at least 3 recurring ordinary places have emotional meaning,
- at least 2 non-work relationships for Rowan,
- at least 2 supporting characters have goals unrelated to Rowan,
- at least 1 mundane success worth losing,
- at least 1 companion/ecology/culture element used only as life texture,
- at least 1 ordinary citizen appears more than once without being a clue.

FAIL if:
- EP001~040 is mostly mystery setup,
- every recurring civilian later becomes a clue,
- first major catastrophe happens before reader understands what normal life costs.

---

# 2. Anomaly density bands

Strong explicit cross-history memory:
EXTREMELY RARE.

Per ~40 episode block candidate:
- L4/L5 autobiographical leak: usually 0, occasionally 1,
- L3 specific episodic leak: 0~2,
- L1/L2 weak residue/deja-vu/sensory oddity: 2~5 max,
- mundane false positives/ordinary weirdness may outnumber real anomalies.

Hard:
Two adjacent major incidents may not both depend primarily on memory leakage.

If readers begin expecting `이번 이상도 다른 역사겠지`, density is too high.

---

# 3. Cause diversity

Across any 5 consecutive medium/major incidents, include at least 3 causal families:

A. genuine physical accident/external condition
B. ordinary human conflict/crime/error
C. institutional failure
D. social cascade / emergent actor
E. substrate/system stress
F. rare history-memory integrity fault
G. deliberately induced bounded test condition
H. genuinely novel event not anticipated by model

FAIL:
`everything was system test` or `everything was leakage`.

---

# 4. Rowan causal centrality gate

Per Act:
- at least 2 meaningful changes happen without Rowan causing them,
- at least 1 supporting character makes a decision Rowan cannot reverse,
- at least 1 local problem is solved by someone else or remains unresolved despite Rowan,
- Act5~7: at least 1 major outcome substantially depends on emergent/non-core actor.

Series-wide:
Rowan may be POV center, not causal center of civilization.

---

# 5. Emergent-actor density

Do not turn every ordinary person into a surprise hero.

Target:
- minor actor seed: common,
- actual critical pivot: rare,
- civilization-scale pivot: 2~4 across entire series,
- one spectacular unknown-variable cascade is enough to prove the principle; later variants should be smaller/different.

Hard:
Emergent actor must be seeded before pivot and may return to ordinary relevance afterward.

---

# 6. Core-character reuse ratio

Each new Act should reuse existing meaningful characters more than it introduces new ones.

Candidate ratios:
Act1: introduction-heavy acceptable.
Act2: >=70% of important roles already introduced.
Act3~5: >=75%.
Act6: new factional/arc characters allowed, but core emotional conflicts use known people.
Act7~9: >=85%; no late flood of new core cast.

Act8 onward:
new character may carry local function but should not become another series-long mystery key.

---

# 7. Character judgment reversals

Per major arc:
At least 1 known person's moral interpretation changes or deepens.

But:
- not every arc needs `actually good/actually evil` reversal,
- some arcs reinforce a previous judgment,
- some show ambiguity without resolving it.

Series target:
Each Core receives at least:
- one convergent choice,
- one divergent choice,
- one choice whose outcome is morally mixed.

Only a subset needs clear Novel Choice.

---

# 8. Relationship density

Every ~25~35 episodes should contain at least one durable relationship movement:
- trust gain,
- distrust,
- boundary change,
- debt,
- reconciliation without restoration,
- separation,
- alliance,
- new responsibility,
- refusal.

FAIL if a 30-episode mystery block ends with relationships exactly where they started.

Alternate-history relationship evidence must affect current emotional boundaries; otherwise it is lore-only.

---

# 9. Incident scale rhythm

Avoid constant escalation.

Candidate rhythm over ~35 episodes:
1. 1 medium practical/social incident,
2. 1 relationship/world incident,
3. 1 mystery pressure incident,
4. 1 major escalation or reversal,
5. recovery/aftershock scenes.

After a catastrophe-heavy block, next block needs ordinary-life or investigation breathing space.

No three catastrophe peaks back-to-back.

# 11. World-life density

For each 10~15 episode window, at least one scene should prove Meridian is a society rather than a puzzle machine:
- food,
- work,
- school,
- sport,
- hobby,
- transport,
- family routine,
- companion ecology,
- festival,
- ordinary bureaucracy,
- leisure.

These scenes may later echo, but they must work even if no clue payoff occurs.

---

# 12. Physical Meridian coupling

At least once per Act, show that the lived civilization depends on finite physical infrastructure.

Possible signals:
- power depth,
- heat rejection,
- radiation shielding,
- storage corruption,
- manufacturing allocation,
- external navigation,
- settlement material.

But do not expose full substrate truth before Act7.

Early coupling appears as ordinary infrastructure limits.

---

# 13. Payoff density

Every 25~35 episodes:
- >=1 local mystery answer,
- >=1 relationship movement,
- >=1 survival/world/institution consequence.

Every major arc:
- close 70~90% of local event,
- leave at most 1~2 series-level questions deeper.

Final 60 episodes:
Payoff/Recontextualization >> Plant.

---

# 14. Cut test

For any proposed 5~15 episode block ask:
1. What permanent relationship change remains?
2. What world/institution state changes?
3. What clue/payment changes?
4. What character judgment changes?
5. What future action becomes possible/impossible?

If answers are almost none, compress or cut.

For full series:
If 30 episodes can be removed without damaging relationship/causal/payoff chains, 410 is too long.
