# MEDIUM INCIDENT POOL v0.1 — V3 CANDIDATE

Status: `NOT CANON`
Purpose: provide 15~20 reusable but non-repetitive incident engines between major arcs.

Each incident must do at least two jobs:
`survival / relationship / mystery / world / institution / character judgment`.

---

# MI-01 — Wrong Recognition
A companion animal or ordinary citizen reacts to someone as if they know them.
Mundane explanations remain plausible.
Use as soft clue only.

# MI-02 — The Procedure Nobody Remembers Inventing
A safety procedure works perfectly, but archives contain no matching origin incident.
Late value: knowledge migrated from another lineage.

# MI-03 — Transit Dispatcher Pivot
Routine route change during minor outage accidentally prevents/causes a larger cascade.
Candidate emergent-minor-actor seed.

# MI-04 — False Saboteur
Sora's evidence points strongly at an innocent person because current infrastructure residue originated in another lineage configuration.
Local case resolves; system truth does not.

# MI-05 — Community Kitchen Split
Juno's mutual-aid network faces outsider ration demand.
Tests charity / insider loyalty / fairness without apocalypse.

# MI-06 — Child's Impossible Detail
One child gives a specific statement that later matches a nonexistent room/person.
Use once or very sparingly; most child oddities are ordinary imagination.

# MI-07 — The Quiet Divination Case
A tarot/fortune session triggers one sensory memory fragment in someone already carrying leakage.
No repeatable occult mechanism.

# MI-08 — Genuine Industrial Accident
No system test, no sabotage: a mundane mechanical failure injures/kills people.
Essential to preserve cause diversity.

# MI-09 — Radiation Weather
External physical radiation event forces substrate/energy protections and causes strange but mostly technical service degradation.
One rare memory fault may appear as collateral effect.

# MI-10 — Memorial Without a Disaster
Small plaque, song, holiday food or local ritual refers to a tragedy no official record supports.
World/culture clue.

# MI-11 — Reconstruction Successor Dispute
A highly faithful successor from an old snapshot claims a limited legal/personal right.
Keep local; do not turn series into courtroom procedural.

# MI-12 — The Alternate Spouse Claim
Someone insists they remember a marriage that never occurred here.
Could be fraud, L3 leak or trauma.
Focus on boundaries, not romance gimmick.

# MI-13 — Clinic Triage Mirror
Leila and Rafi take opposite positions from what Rowan expects based on earlier lineage behavior.
Shows same-person variation without exposition.

# MI-14 — School Evacuation
Children, teachers and caregivers reveal how ordinary institutions behave under emergency.
Candidate for minor caregiver becoming decisive.

# MI-15 — Companion Ecology Containment
A synthetic companion line reacts badly to environmental contamination or escapes during maintenance fault.
World texture + practical survival; not monster arc.

# MI-16 — Paleo Hall Reuse
Same public museum/education space functions as normal outing, shelter, clinic or sealed evidence site across different contexts.
Multi-state place foreshadow.

# MI-17 — Archive Integrity Race
Hana must choose between preserving evidence and releasing it before authorities secure/censor it.
No truth-file payoff; only fragment survives.

# MI-18 — Power Depth Triage
A real power shortage forces choice among civic services, substrate preservation, manufacturing and other physical ark needs.
First subtle clue that known city is not the only load on Meridian.

# MI-19 — Embodiment Trial Failure
Late-series external body/continuity migration trial encounters a technical fault.
Raises personhood stakes before final mass transition.

# MI-20 — Real External Signal Echo
A tiny signal pattern matches one detail from an earlier alleged prophecy/contact history.
Does not confirm aliens; proves current external reality can rhyme with prior modeled/lived scenarios.

---

# Distribution rules

1. Do not place memory anomalies in adjacent incidents repeatedly.
2. At least 1/3 should have no hidden-history cause at all.
3. At least 1/3 should primarily deepen relationships/institutions/world life.
4. Physical ark coupling should recur but not dominate every incident.
5. Minor actor pivots should be seeded before becoming decisive.
6. Horror/occult/ecology are seasoning, not the primary incident family.
7. No incident may reveal full substrate ontology before Act 7.
8. Reuse locations more often than inventing new districts.
