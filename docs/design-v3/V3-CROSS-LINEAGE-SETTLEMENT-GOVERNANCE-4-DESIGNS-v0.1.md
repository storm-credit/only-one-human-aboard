# V3 CROSS-LINEAGE SETTLEMENT GOVERNANCE — 4 DESIGNS v0.1

Status: `P1 GOVERNANCE CANDIDATE / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Problem:
At arrival, H-B is not the only morally real D3 civilization.
Other active/quiescent D3 persons may have claims on:
- continuation resources,
- planet-side substrate capacity,
- migration order,
- outer-ark infrastructure,
- future D3 creation rules.

The story needs a legitimate transition without turning Act9 into 40 episodes of constitutional procedure.

---

# A — H-B Becomes the Settlement Government

Mechanism:
Because H-B discovers the truth and is reader-facing, its existing government takes control of settlement resources and represents everyone.

Strengths:
- simple,
- easy narrative focus.

Fatal weakness:
- turns narrative centrality into moral sovereignty,
- other D3 societies become subordinate by author fiat,
- creates a hidden `winning world` structure.

Verdict: **REJECT**.

---

# B — Universal Direct Vote of Every D3 Person

Mechanism:
Every active D3 person votes directly on major settlement decisions.

Strengths:
- superficially democratic,
- equal personhood signal.

Weaknesses:
- potentially enormous electorate,
- different subjective time rates,
- quiescent people cannot participate,
- impossible common information context,
- every technical emergency becomes referendum paralysis.

Verdict: **REJECT AS OPERATING MODEL**.

---

# C — One Civilization, One Vote

Mechanism:
Each D3 polity gets an equal council seat/vote.

Strengths:
- protects small lineages,
- easy federation metaphor.

Weaknesses:
- a tiny polity equals a huge civilization,
- who qualifies as one polity is manipulable,
- branching can manufacture voting units,
- persons become secondary to lineage bookkeeping.

Verdict: **USEFUL COMPONENT / NOT ENOUGH**.

---

# D — Transitional Compact → Dual Representation [LEADING]

Use a two-stage architecture.

## Stage 1 — Emergency Transitional Compact

During Act8~9 discovery/arrival crisis, establish only the minimum legitimate machinery needed to stop unilateral mission control.

Participants candidate:
- H-B civic delegates,
- reachable active D3 delegate channels,
- independent continuity-rights trustees/ombuds,
- technical outer-ark operations representatives,
- no HUMAN:1 special seat merely for biological origin.

Immediate powers limited to:
- prohibit new involuntary D3 creation,
- prevent discretionary destructive termination,
- establish emergency resource floors,
- approve settlement migration priorities,
- protect evidence/claims of quiescent societies,
- authorize only urgent shared-ark decisions.

It is intentionally provisional.

## Stage 2 — Post-survival dual representation

Permanent design can combine two legitimacy sources:

### Person representation
Population-sensitive delegated representation.
Purpose:
Large societies cannot be treated as one voice equal to a tiny group.

### Polity/lineage protection
Minimum representation or second chamber/council for distinct D3 civic societies.
Purpose:
Small lineages cannot be erased by raw population majority.

Exact seat formula is NOT needed in the finale.

Principle:
`persons count, and distinct societies also have protected standing.`

Verdict: **SELECT**.

---

# 1. Scope of shared government

Cross-lineage governance should be narrow.

It controls shared external-domain questions:
- outer ark resource allocation,
- planet-side substrate/fabrication allocation,
- migration/continuity safety standards,
- D3 creation/deepening law,
- shared external defense/contact policy,
- claims during quiescence,
- settlement environmental commons.

It does NOT automatically control:
- every marriage law,
- school curriculum,
- internal criminal law,
- cultural/religious practice
inside each D3 polity.

This prevents a giant centralized meta-state from replacing all societies.

---

# 2. Quiescent D3 claims

A paused society cannot vote in real time.

Hard rule:
**absence due to preservation cannot be converted into forfeiture.**

Interim mechanism:
- preserved constitutional instructions if reliable,
- independent rights trustee/ombuds,
- no irreversible disposal of the polity's continuity assets merely because it cannot answer,
- major permanent decisions concerning it deferred until safe resumption where possible.

Trustee has no right to rewrite culture or merge the population away.

---

# 3. Branch duplicates and voting

If two D3 persons share an ancestor but diverged into independent lives, they are independent persons.

They may:
- vote differently,
- belong to different polities,
- reject each other's claims.

No `one original person, one meta-vote` rule.

Reason:
That would erase the independent personhood already established by divergence.

---

# 4. Anti-vote-manufacturing rule

A system/polity must not be able to create new D3 branches merely to gain more representation.

Therefore:
- new D3 creation is under moratorium during transition,
- later creation does not instantly create external political weight without lifecycle/resource review,
- malicious branching for political advantage is prohibited.

This is another reason D3 creation becomes a constitutional question rather than compute scheduling.

---

# 5. H-B narrative role

H-B matters because:
- its people discover the problem,
- they possess a functioning civic government,
- they are close to the arrival crisis,
- the protagonist and reader know them.

H-B can initiate the compact.

But H-B may NOT:
- declare itself the only valid civilization,
- decide other D3 societies are simulations,
- seize all settlement capacity,
- use HUMAN:1 as legitimacy token.

Narrative victory:
H-B helps create a rule under which **it also accepts limits on its own power**.

---

# 6. System role

The mission system is not a voting citizen or sovereign person by default.

It provides:
- evidence,
- resource forecasts,
- technical constraints,
- execution of lawful operations,
- historical records.

It can object when a command is physically impossible or violates hard life-safety constraints.

It does not get to decide moral personhood merely because it owns the scheduler.

---

# 7. HUMAN:1 role

Before birth:
No political vote/authority.
Protected as a living biological continuity under guardianship/medical ethics.

After birth:
Ordinary age-appropriate civic rights.

Hard:
- no founder seat,
- no tie-breaking vote,
- no hereditary charter key,
- no special land claim.

This directly blocks biological-supremacy reading.

---

# 8. Emergency decisions

Act9 cannot wait for a perfect constitution.

Use an emergency legitimacy rule:
- minimum necessary scope,
- multi-party approval,
- documented reasons,
- time limit,
- post-crisis review,
- no permanent extinction/merger of a D3 polity unless unavoidable physical destruction is already occurring.

This preserves thriller pacing.

---

# 9. Story delivery

Do not explain bicameral formulas in dialogue.

Show legitimacy through conflicts:
- H-B cannot simply take a substrate bank,
- a quiescent lineage has a protected claim,
- an emergency engineer says only two options are physically possible,
- delegates argue who bears risk,
- protagonists accept a less convenient option because another society is not expendable.

The reader should understand the ethical rule before the constitutional details.

---

# 10. Harness

FAIL if:
1. H-B becomes sovereign because it is the protagonist world.
2. HUMAN:1 has political privilege.
3. quiescent lineages lose all property/continuity claims automatically.
4. one tiny branch can manufacture infinite voting power.
5. universal direct democracy blocks every emergency scene.
6. permanent meta-government erases internal cultural autonomy.
7. the finale pretends one emergency compact solves centuries of constitutional debt.

PASS if:
- transition is legitimate enough to stop unilateral harm,
- permanent design is deferred,
- persons and polities both receive standing,
- H-B voluntarily accepts constraints,
- survival pacing remains intact.

---

# 11. Verdict

Selected candidate:
**D — Emergency Transitional Compact → later dual person/polity representation.**

Act8~9 needs only Stage 1 in operational detail.
Stage 2 can be established as a direction, not fully legislated before the ending.

Status:
**SETTLEMENT REPRESENTATION P1 = CLOSED ENOUGH FOR ACT9 / NOT CANON.**
