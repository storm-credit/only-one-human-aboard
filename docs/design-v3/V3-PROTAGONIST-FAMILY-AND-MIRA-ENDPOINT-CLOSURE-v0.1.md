Do NOT use death/divorce tragedy as the main delta.

# 4. Protagonist family relationship grammar

Protagonist's recurring flaw:
care becomes logistical control.

Typical examples:
- asks route/time/backup questions instead of saying he is worried,
- tries to solve school/housing/work issues without being asked,
- assumes practical competence grants relational authority.

Family response:
- they accept help when useful,
- tell him no when he is taking over,
- make major choices before telling him sometimes,
- continue life during his crisis absences.

Series movement:
`family problem = something to fix`
→ `family choice = information he should respect`
→ `being responsible does not mean deciding for them`.

No reconciliation speech needed.

# 5. Mira relationship history — exact candidate

## H-A
Protagonist and Mira had a **long serious relationship** lasting roughly 3~4 years class.
They spent substantial daily time together and may have partially cohabited / effectively shared domestic routine near the end.

Breakup:
~1.5~2.5 years before EP001 class.

Primary reason:
not cheating, death or one dramatic betrayal.

Accumulated conflict:
- protagonist's protective/logistical control,
- Mira's strong autonomy and protected personal time,
- protagonist repeatedly treating relational uncertainty as a solvable systems problem,
- incompatible ways of carrying responsibility under work stress.

They remain in overlapping civic/professional circles and can speak normally, but boundaries exist.

This supports Act1 familiarity without romance chase.

## H-B
They **did not live the same long relationship**.

Selected strongest variant:
- they were close and briefly dated for roughly several months class,
- never built the H-A domestic routine,
- separated earlier and with less accumulated intimacy,
- therefore some public/shared-circle facts match while private routines/jokes/home details do not.

Why:
This gives Act3 the strongest mixture:
`some memories plausibly fit → the private history clearly does not`.

Do not make H-B Mira a stranger; that would make the relationship contradiction too binary and reduce subtlety.
