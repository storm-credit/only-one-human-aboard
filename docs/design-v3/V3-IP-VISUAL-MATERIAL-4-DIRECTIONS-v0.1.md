# V3 IP / VISUAL / MATERIAL — 4 DIRECTIONS v0.1

Status: `BRAINSTORM / COMPARISON / NOT CANON`
Date: 2026-08-20
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Goal:
Choose a coherent visual/material identity that supports mystery, survival, character attachment and collectible/IP recognition without converting the work into military-mecha fiction or gadget catalog fiction.

---

# Direction A — Clean Civic Future

Core image:
bright modular city, clean public infrastructure, smooth soft-edge technology, restrained wear.

Character visual:
- contemporary-readable civilian silhouettes,
- subtle smart textile details,
- profession-specific layers mostly clean and standardized.

Machines:
- integrated smooth shells,
- few visible mechanisms,
- family resemblance across public equipment.

Background:
- bright civic interiors,
- calm signage,
- large clean transit halls,
- garden/public-space emphasis.

Collectibility:
- elegant transit cards/pins,
- design objects,
- compact machine miniatures.

Strengths:
- readable,
- attractive illustrations,
- immediately feels like a mature civilian civilization.

Weaknesses:
- weak damage-analysis texture,
- Old Works loses identity,
- Act2/5 physical-history clues become less tactile,
- can resemble generic premium SF UI city.

Verdict:
`USE AS CENTRAL/PUBLIC-SERVICE LAYER ONLY`

---

# Direction B — Industrial Ark / Mechanical Cathedral

Core image:
exposed structure, layered repairs, huge trusses, ducts, thermal hardware, heavy tools and machine silhouettes.

Character visual:
- strong protective workwear,
- harnesses, utility belts, rugged surfaces,
- obvious occupational silhouettes.

Machines:
- visually rich tugs, crawlers, workframes, drones, rescue cutters,
- exposed articulation and replaceable modules,
- generations visibly distinguishable.

Background:
- industrial scale,
- deep shafts,
- service lighting,
- scars and patchwork.

Collectibility:
- strongest model-kit/gear recognition,
- strong old-vs-new machine model appeal.

Strengths:
- excellent for protagonist profession,
- strong survival physicality,
- excellent Act2/5 clues,
- high visual memorability.

Weaknesses:
- can make all Meridian feel like a factory,
- may push genre toward mecha/space-industrial action,
- ordinary family/civic life becomes visually underpowered.

Verdict:
`STRONG OLD WORKS / OUTER WORKS / MACHINE LAYER, NOT WHOLE CITY`

---

# Direction C — Layered 450-Year Civilian City

Core image:
centuries of civic life visible in overlapping generations: old structure under renovated surfaces, repaired objects beside fashionable new goods, multiple architectural eras living together.

Character visual:
- recognizable civilian fashion,
- personal variation,
- repaired/secondhand/new mix,
- occupation-specific work layer rather than one crew uniform.

Machines:
- not all visually spectacular;
- older certified machines survive beside newer compact variants,
- retrofit scars and compatibility adapters matter.

Background:
- each district has its own accumulated era mixture,
- signage, shopfronts, housing, public facilities and industrial interfaces feel inhabited.

Collectibility:
- strongest heritage/provenance/recognition pleasure,
- readers can notice old models, retired signs, district objects and transformed places.

Strengths:
- best fit for 300k city + centuries-long civilization,
- supports family, work, mystery and material history,
- avoids sterile spaceship look.

Weaknesses:
- less instantly flashy than Direction B,
- requires disciplined art bible to avoid visual mush.

Verdict:
`STRONGEST CIVILIAN BASE`

---

# Direction D — Symbolic IP Stylization

Core image:
strong recurring color/material/silhouette codes assigned to major characters, jobs and zones for instant recognition.

Character visual:
- highly distinct silhouettes,
- signature garments/props,
- controlled color families.

Machines:
- clearly named model families,
- distinctive fronts/limbs/tool-heads,
- easy merchandise recognition.

Background:
- strong zone signatures and landmark shapes.

Collectibility:
- strongest card/figure/model/merchandising potential.

Strengths:
- excellent cover/illustration/readability,
- easy fan recognition,
- strong IP identity.

Weaknesses:
- can become game faction coding,
- professions may look like classes,
- characters can feel costume-designed rather than lived-in.

Verdict:
`USE AS RESTRAINT LAYER: 1~2 SIGNATURES PER CHARACTER/MACHINE/ZONE, NEVER FULL COSTUME CODE`

# Selected Hybrid — VM-H1

# **Layered Civilian City + Industrial Machine Depth + Restrained Signature Recognition**

Base:
- Direction C for city/civilian/material culture.

Add:
- Direction B for Old Works, Outer Works, equipment and craft families.
- Direction A for selected Central/medical/newer public infrastructure.
- Direction D only as a recognition discipline.

## Core visual laws

1. **Meridian is a city first, ship second in daily experience.**
2. The ship's age is visible through compatible layers, not universal decay.
3. Civilian clothes are varied; workwear is specialized.
4. Machines earn stronger model identity than household devices.
5. Old equipment can remain in service because of certification/compatibility, not because old tech is magically better.
6. Every core character gets only **2~4 stable visual anchors**, not a costume checklist.
7. Every major zone gets only **3~5 background anchors** that survive repeated scenes.
8. At least five recurring machine/equipment families gain recognizable silhouettes.
9. Later Acts transform known visuals more often than inventing new ones.
10. Collectibility is driven by recognition/history/variant, not power rarity.

# Proposed signature machine families — brainstorm seed

These are functional seeds, not frozen model names.

## M01 — Hull/Structure Inspection Crawler
Role:
Old Works / confined structural access / damage imaging and physical sampling.

Recognition hook:
low, wide body; articulated sensor mast; clamp feet; scarred replaceable shell panels.

Series use:
Act1 ordinary work → Act2 damaged/lost unit → Act5 legacy-compatible interface → Act7 clue recontextualization.

## M02 — Damage Analyst Field Drone
Role:
small mobile mapping/inspection support for protagonist team.

Recognition hook:
flat triangular/three-arm geometry; throws structured light/marker mesh rather than magical hologram.

Hard:
not omniscient; cannot see through everything; needs surface access/calibration.

## M03 — Emergency Rescue Skiff
Role:
rapid movement through service/low-g/isolated corridors and later external staging variants.

Recognition hook:
compact central cabin + modular rescue cradle/tool pallet.

Variants:
medical / access / evacuation.

## M04 — Utility Tug / Freight Tractor
Role:
Transfer Belt and heavy cargo movement.

Recognition hook:
blunt industrial front, standardized attachment collar, many local body variants.

Collectibility strength:
model generations and district liveries/maintenance patches.

## M05 — Outer Works Service Frame
Role:
EVA support; human-operated powered workframe or tool-support rig, not combat mecha.

Recognition hook:
shoulder/torso tool rail, anchor/tether geometry, oversized manipulators, non-humanoid enough to avoid Gundam drift.

## M06 — Public Safety Barrier Drone
Role:
access denial, visual warning, portable barrier/sensor support.

Recognition hook:
shield/panel deployment rather than weapon silhouette.

Hard:
no autonomous lethal police robot.

## M07 — Settlement Commissioning Node / Field Rig
Role:
Act9 construction and local-control island.

Recognition hook:
visually descends from earlier utility/service hardware so ending feels like transformation, not brand-new technology.

# Proposed collectible-recognition layers

## In-world cheap/common
- transit line pins/patches,
- retired tram route plates/reproductions,
- district festival badges,
- miniature utility tugs/crawlers,
- sports/team objects,
- old interface shells,
- local repair-art objects,
- printed micro-edition posters/cards.

## Professional enthusiasts
- retired tool housings,
- historical work patches,
- certified decommissioned components,
- machine-generation manuals/prints,
- miniature engineering models,
- old safety signage.

## High-end heritage
- Earth-made objects,
- founding-era originals,
- historically significant art/instruments/tools,
- intact early-voyage devices.

Hard:
no numbered legendary set whose completion grants plot power.

# Proposed character visual-design rule

Each core figure gets:
- **Silhouette anchor** — what is visible at distance.
- **Face/hair anchor** — one or two memorable traits, no beauty ranking.
- **Material anchor** — one recurring object/tool/garment.
- **Motion anchor** — how they occupy space under ordinary/stress conditions.
- **Act-change anchor** — one visible change after Act2/6/settlement.

Avoid:
- everyone having a color theme,
- everyone carrying a symbolic heirloom,
- professions turning into uniforms/classes,
- facial descriptions so elaborate they contradict ordinary civilian tone.
