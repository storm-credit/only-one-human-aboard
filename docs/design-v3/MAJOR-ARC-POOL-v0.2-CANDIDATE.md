# MA-08 — The Uncountable Lineages — Act 7

Replaces old `No Original World / Merge Crisis` simplification.

Core:
Rowan and allies infer that contradictory histories are only a tiny visible edge of an astronomically large civilization lineage field physically carried by Meridian.

Evidence routes:
1. lineage namespace scale,
2. substrate maintenance/resource map,
3. knowledge without matching history,
4. rare memory fault correlation,
5. identity anchor collisions.

Major emotional conflict:
`If there are so many related Rowans, which one am I?`

Answer direction:
This Rowan is the real person who lived this life. Scale does not erase local identity.

Permanent outputs:
- regression/parallel-universe theory collapses,
- no privileged current `original` civilization,
- Rowan definitively not HUMAN:1,
- branch rights question opens.

# Major Arc Harness v0.2

1. Target 9~10 major arcs.
2. Typical 25~55 episodes, but exact duration follows relationship/event density.
3. Each changes at least 2 permanently: relationship / mystery / world / institution / character judgment / rights.
4. No three consecutive arcs resolve to same cause.
5. At least 2 major arcs primarily ordinary person/group causation.
6. At least 2 strongly couple to macroscopic physical Meridian.
7. At least 1 major arc demonstrates an emergent minor actor becoming critical.
8. Explicit strong cross-history memory remains exceptional.
9. At least 1 major arc removes/reduces Rowan as causal center.
10. Final two arcs convert ontology into immediate rights/survival choices.
11. A new civilization state alone never earns an arc.
12. Catastrophe is not the default; major arcs may be social/investigative/ethical as long as stakes move.
