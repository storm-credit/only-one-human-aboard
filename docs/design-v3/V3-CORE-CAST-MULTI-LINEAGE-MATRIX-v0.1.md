# V3 CORE CAST — MULTI-LINEAGE MATRIX v0.1

Status: `CHARACTER DEEP DESIGN / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Prevent alternate histories from becoming simple good/evil skins. Each recurring identity lineage keeps recognizable traits while conditions, relationships, status and chance change outcomes.

---

# 0. Rules

For each character track:
- Identity Invariant
- Convergent Choice
- Divergent Conditions
- Novel Choice Potential
- Reduced-Importance State
- Social-Testimony Reveal

No row is a fixed episode promise yet.

---

# 1. Rowan Han

**Invariant:** responsibility + repair orientation + discomfort leaving fixable danger unresolved.

**Convergent:** repeatedly enters dangerous infrastructure when he believes his technical presence can save people.

**Lineage A — Stable Engineer**
Competent, reluctant, ordinary. Closes problems one by one.

**Lineage B — Bulkhead Savior / Murderer**
Seals a sector and saves thousands; survivors inside another remembered lineage call him a killer.

**Lineage C — Control Spiral**
After repeated losses, starts overriding others `for their safety`; becomes frightening without becoming sadistic.

**Novel choice:** refuses open/close binary and invents temporary pressure bypass no prior model selected.

**Reduced importance:** dies early in one infrastructure incident; later social cascade proceeds without him.

**Testimony line:**
`저 사람한테 문 열어달라고 하지 마. 마지막까지 안 열어.`

Current Rowan has no memory of that event.

# 2. Mira Solano

**Invariant:** autonomy; distrust of benevolent control.

**Convergent:** resists anyone deciding her risk threshold for her.

**Lineage A — Logistics Stabilizer**
Independent planner who keeps distribution functioning.

**Lineage B — Separation Advocate**
During collapse, cuts one district from resource network to save the whole. Seen as cold/necessary.

**Lineage C — Rowan Family Life**
Married/partnered with Rowan; autonomy conflict appears inside family rather than romance pursuit.

**Lineage D — Never Meets Rowan**
Becomes important through transport politics; proves relationship is not destiny.

**Novel choice:** gives local groups control over allocation instead of choosing centralization vs complete separation.

**Reduced importance:** ordinary scheduler whose branch never intersects main crisis.

**Testimony:**
`당신은 저 여자 때문에 우리 구획 배급이 끊긴 걸 몰라.`

# 3. Selene Adebayo

**Invariant:** boundaries/order are necessary for survival.

**Convergent:** establishes a line of authority rapidly in chaos.

**Lineage A — Hero Commander**
Clear command prevents panic and mass casualties.

**Lineage B — Emergency Autocrat**
Same instinct evolves into permanent emergency rule.

**Lineage C — Failed Leniency**
Keeps a gate open to respect civilian autonomy; cascading breach kills many.

**Novel choice:** creates rotating civilian command review during active crisis, giving up monopoly without abandoning order.

**Reduced importance:** remains mid-level officer because another commander survives.

**Testimony:**
Current shelter residents applaud her; one leak carrier refuses to cross her security line.
`난 저 사람이 문 닫는 걸 봤어.`

# 4. Rafi Chen

**Invariant:** cannot easily abandon a person who may still be saved.

**Convergent:** returns for one more patient.

**Lineage A — Rescue Hero**
Risk saves trapped civilians.

**Lineage B — Exposure Cascade**
Same rescue spreads contamination and kills more people.

**Lineage C — Burned-Out Triage Officer**
Previous losses teach him brutal triage; Rowan thinks he became cold.

**Novel choice:** instead of one-person rescue vs abandonment, recruits civilians into distributed stabilization chain.

**Reduced importance:** medical role remains local; never meets Rowan.

**Testimony:**
`그 사람은 날 버렸어요.`
Current Rafi has spent the whole arc refusing to abandon anyone.

# 5. Sora Mbeki

**Invariant:** suspicion / need to test stories.

**Convergent:** refuses consensus without evidence.

**Lineage A — Investigator**

**Lineage B — Witch-Hunt Architect**

**Lineage C — Whistleblower**
Suspects the system rather than citizens and exposes hidden classification abuse.

**Testimony:**

# 6. Leila Noor

**Invariant:** compassion expressed through efficient decision-making.

**Convergent:** tries to maximize actual survivability, not symbolic comfort.

**Lineage A — Trusted Physician**
Balances patient choice and triage.

**Lineage B — Utilitarian Administrator**
Sacrifices a small group to preserve medical capacity.

**Lineage C — Personhood Dissident**

**Reduced importance:** ordinary clinic physician away from major disaster.

**Testimony:**

`당신은 나를 치료한 게 아니라 명단에서 지웠어.`

# 7. Gideon Park

# 14. Cross-character convergence tests

## Rowan + Selene
Same event can invert reader judgment depending on who holds door authority.

## Mira + Juno
Autonomy vs community obligation.
One lineage makes Mira's separation save the city; another makes Juno's network save it.

## Rafi + Leila
One-person rescue vs population triage.
Avoid simple heart-vs-logic stereotype; swap who becomes harsher across lineages.

## Sora + Hana
Truth verification vs truth publication.
Both can save or destabilize society.

## Niko + Gideon
Possibility vs caution.
Do not always reward youth/risk.

## Ivo + Cassian
Same rare leak can become warning, power, wealth, paranoia or humility.

# 15. Emergent-important-person rule

Core cast must NOT monopolize causal importance.

At least 2~3 major arcs should contain a non-core person whose mundane role becomes critical through social cascade.

But seed them naturally before pivot.

Candidate role pools:
- cook/ration worker,
- school teacher,
- transit dispatcher,
- sanitation technician,
- child caregiver,
- companion-animal handler,
- junior archive clerk,
- maintenance vendor,
- ordinary neighborhood mediator.

These are role pools, not final named characters.
