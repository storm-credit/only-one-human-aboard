# V3 PROTAGONIST APPARENT RETURN — 4 DESIGNS

Status: `P0 OPTIONS / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Purpose:
Resolve how Rowan can experience an apparent death/return without making ordinary death reversible or cross-history memory a normal feature.

---

# A — Rowan Dies, Another Rowan Receives His Memory

Mechanism:
Rowan-A dies. Rowan-B in another lineage receives A's autobiographical memory.

Pros:
clean regression-like experience.

Fatal problems:
- protagonist actually changes identity without reader consent,
- implies death + memory copy can function as resurrection,
- weakens death-continuity rules,
- makes `which Rowan are we following?` too early and too metaphysical.

Verdict: `REJECT AS PRIMARY`.

---

# B — Emergency Continuity Failover Misbinding [LEADING]

Mechanism:
During the catastrophic end of Act2, Rowan's active continuity anchor is not terminated.
A real substrate/infrastructure failure triggers emergency preservation/failover.

The system successfully preserves **Rowan's ongoing active stream**, but a rare identity-lineage routing fault attaches it to the environment/body-state of a closely related civilization lineage.

From Rowan's subjective point of view:
- catastrophe,
- sensory interruption/critical transition,
- then waking in what looks like an earlier/similar Meridian.

His memory remains because it is the same continuous Rowan stream.

The surrounding people are mostly local persons of the target lineage.

Pros:
- true protagonist continuity,
- ordinary death remains death,
- memory anomaly remains rare,
- explains why Rowan is unusually central to mystery without chosen-one status,
- connects job location/old infrastructure/substrate fault.

Cons:
- technical mechanism must remain hidden until late,
- must explain why emergency failover exists.

Verdict: `LEADING`.

---

# C — Whole Civilization Reconciliation Carries Rowan Forward

Mechanism:
Act2 world is routinely reconciled into a new lineage and Rowan retains memory accidentally.

Problems:
- makes process too normal,
- likely creates many rememberers,
- turns Act2 catastrophe into planned reset,
- weakens rarity.

Verdict: `REJECT FOR FIRST RETURN`.

---

# D — Environment Reconfiguration Around Same Rowan

Mechanism:
Rowan remains on same continuity anchor while the whole surrounding experienced environment is reconfigured to another state.

Pros:
true continuity.

Problems:
- why only Rowan persists becomes hard,
- risks Truman-show special observer setup,
- feels system-selected.

Verdict: `WEAK`.

---

# 1. Why failover exists

Emergency continuity protection is normal infrastructure, not a secret resurrection power.

Purpose:
If a local physical substrate bank fails while an active person process is still running, synchronized fault-tolerant hardware can migrate that **still-active process** before causal continuity breaks.

Analogy:
failover before death, not restore after death.

Normally:
- same person,
- same lineage environment,
- no memory anomaly.

Rowan case:
- infrastructure catastrophe,
- lineage routing/index fault,
- closely related target state selected incorrectly,
- continuity survives but environment lineage mismatches.

Frequency:
extremely rare critical failure.

---

# 2. Why Rowan specifically

Do not use destiny.

Candidate causal chain:
1. Rowan is physically/logically located near old infrastructure boundary during Act2 crisis.
2. emergency repairs create unusual routing path.
3. external physical power/thermal fault overlaps a civilization-state transition window.
4. old legacy index mapping misidentifies a related Rowan lineage endpoint.
5. failover succeeds technically but violates identity/environment integrity.

He matters because he was **in the wrong place at the wrong failure boundary**.

---

# 3. What actually happened to Act2 people

Important:
Rowan's survival does not mean everyone survived.

Possible:
- many active streams terminate,
- some fail over correctly,
- some target local lineages continue independently,
- Rowan alone or among an extremely tiny set misbinds across lineage boundary.

Reader initially interprets all living people after return as resurrection.
Later learns:
those are not necessarily the same individuals who died beside him.

This increases rather than decreases tragedy.

---

# 4. Why target world looks earlier

Target civilization lineage shares a common historical seed with Rowan's source lineage but diverged before the Act2 catastrophe.

Therefore:
- familiar neighborhoods,
- similar jobs,
- same people lineages,
- different relationships,
- some events not yet happened,
- some differences already existed.

No time travel required.

---

# 5. Why Rowan's foreknowledge works temporarily

Closely related lineages share many causal structures.

Early after failover:
- infrastructure schedules similar,
- people habits similar,
- planned events similar.

But:
- pre-existing divergence exists,
- Rowan's intervention adds more divergence,
- random/social variables grow.

Thus predictive half-life naturally appears.

---

# 6. Why other rememberers still exist

Rowan's case is not the only memory anomaly category.

Others may have:
- L1/L2 fragment leakage,
- rare L3/L4 autobiographical leakage,
- archive collision.

But Rowan's **continuity failover misbinding** is rarer and structurally different from ordinary leak cases.

Important:
Characters initially group them together because they do not know the mechanism.

---

# 7. Late reveal ladder

Early interpretation:
`I died and regressed.`

Second:
`I survived somehow, but history changed.`

Third:
`Other people remember different lives.`

Late:
`My continuity survived an infrastructure failover into a related civic lineage.`

Final implication:
He was never chosen to remember. He is evidence of a catastrophic integrity bug.

---

# 8. Emotional payoff

Rowan may eventually understand:
The people he `got back` after Act2 were not restored versions of the exact people he lost.

This prevents him from treating a later alternate Mira/Arun/etc. as replacement.

It directly advances his series flaw:
`restore original state` → `respect the present person who actually exists.`

---

# 9. Guardrails

1. failover cannot activate after confirmed continuity death.
2. it must be normal fault tolerance with abnormal destination binding, not hidden resurrection.
3. no repeated protagonist reset every arc.
4. Rowan cannot intentionally trigger it.
5. death does not increase powers.
6. no save-point mechanics.
7. target lineage already differed before Rowan arrived.
8. the first return remains exceptional across the series.

---

# 10. Current verdict

**B — Emergency Continuity Failover Misbinding = leading candidate.**

This closes the largest contradiction between:
- apparent regression hook,
- rare memory bug,
- ultradense lineage field,
- meaningful death,
- non-chosen protagonist.

Still open:
- exact Act2 physical trigger,
- whether any second person misbinds simultaneously,
- whether Rowan's continuity anchor gains long-term corruption that causes later fragment leaks.
