# V3 DEATH / BACKUP / CONTINUITY — 4 DESIGNS

Status: `P0 OPTIONS / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

---

# 0. Core problem

If persons are implemented on ultradense physical substrates, readers will ask:
`왜 죽으면 백업에서 복원하지?`

If restoration is easy and equivalent, survival stakes collapse.

Need a rule separating:
- information copy,
- active subjective continuity,
- lineage fork,
- legal/social identity.

---

# A — Snapshot Restoration = Same Person

Premise:
Any sufficiently recent state backup restores the same person.

Pros:
simple technology.

Fatal cost:
- death becomes downtime,
- grief weakens,
- high-risk decisions become cheap,
- every plot must invent backup failure.

Verdict: `REJECT`.

---

# B — Snapshot Restoration = Successor Person [STRONG]

Premise:
A snapshot can reproduce memories/personality up to time T, but the subjective stream that died after T is not demonstrably restored.

Restored entity is:
- psychologically continuous with past state,
- socially related,
- but not automatically the same legal/moral person.

Analogy:
a perfect diary/personality reconstruction is not proof that the dead observer resumed experience.

Pros:
- preserves backup utility,
- preserves death stakes,
- creates relationship/legal conflict,
- fits alternate-lineage personhood theme.

Cons:
- metaphysical identity cannot be scientifically proven inside story.

Verdict: `LEADING`.

---

# C — Physical Continuity Anchor [STRONG SUPPORT]

Premise:
Every active person lineage possesses a unique physical continuity process/anchor state.

Properties:
- it evolves continuously while person is alive,
- snapshots can copy informational content but not retroactively recreate the exact broken causal stream,
- deliberate forking creates **daughter continuity anchors**, each becoming an independent person from divergence onward,
- destruction/terminal shutdown of an anchor ends that local stream.

Important:
Do not present this as magical soul hardware.
It is a physical continuity mechanism used by Meridian identity architecture.

Pros:
- gives engineering embodiment to identity distinction,
- makes fork persons clearly independent after split,
- explains memory-address collision bugs.

Cons:
- can feel like soul token if overexplained,
- requires care with speculative physics.

Verdict: `LEADING SUPPORT`.

---

# D — Restoration Technically Equivalent, Society Simply Bans It

Premise:
Restoration would be same person but law forbids routine use.

Problem:
reader will ask why emergency exceptions are not ubiquitous.
Law alone cannot carry survival stakes.

Verdict: `INSUFFICIENT ALONE`.

---

# 1. Leading Hybrid — B + C

Current strongest rule:

> A person is an active continuous physical process, not merely a stored pattern. A snapshot can create a highly faithful successor, and a deliberate fork can create a daughter person, but neither proves continuation of a stream that has already ended.

Therefore:
- backup != resurrection,
- counterpart != resurrection,
- fork != harmless duplicate,
- death of this person remains death.

---

# 2. What happens during ordinary civic death

Candidate:
1. active continuity ends,
2. terminal state may be archived for history/forensics,
3. informational snapshots may exist under strict rules,
4. no automatic restoration,
5. family and law treat death as final unless a controversial reconstruction process is invoked.

A reconstruction would create a person with claims and memories, but not erase the death event.

---

# 3. What happens during intentional lineage fork

At fork time T:
- Parent P exists.
- Daughter A and Daughter B begin from closely matching state.
- after T, A and B are independent people.

Neither is the disposable copy of the other.

This is critical to late ethics.

System's old Continuity Equivalence Doctrine may have treated short forks as one lineage for operational purposes.
The story later challenges that doctrine.

---

# 4. Why another Rowan does not replace dead Rowan

Suppose Rowan-A dies.
Rowan-B elsewhere shares earlier memories.

Rowan-B:
- did not experience Rowan-A's final days,
- may love different people,
- may have made different promises,
- has independent continuity after divergence.

A family member can sincerely say:
`당신이 그 사람과 닮았다는 건 알아요. 그래도 그 사람은 죽었어요.`

This should be demonstrated in story before deep ontology exposition.

---

# 5. Backup states

Potential backup types:

## B0 — Structural redundancy
Protects substrate against local hardware failure while active continuity remains intact.
Not resurrection; it is fault tolerance.

## B1 — Memory/archive snapshot
Historical/forensic data.

## B2 — Recoverable cognitive snapshot
Can seed a successor person under extreme conditions.
Ethically restricted.

## B3 — Full fork-capable state
Creation of a new person lineage.
Heavily regulated/hidden under old system architecture.

These categories prevent every `backup` word from meaning the same thing.

---

# 6. Physical hazard and true permanent loss

Permanent loss can occur when:
- active continuity anchor is terminated,
- all live redundancy for that stream is destroyed,
- no legally/physically continuous process survives.

A later reconstruction from B2/B3 is a successor/fork, not guaranteed resurrection.

Thus external radiation/substrate destruction can kill people in a meaningful sense.

---

# 7. Relationship consequences

This model enables:
- widow rejects reconstructed spouse,
- child accepts alternate parent emotionally while law does not,
- restored successor claims an old debt/property,
- murderer argues victim exists in another lineage,
- court rejects counterpart-as-victim-restoration reasoning.

Use sparingly; main plot remains survival/mystery.

---

# 8. HUMAN:1 contrast

HUMAN:1 has ordinary biological organismal continuity rather than substrate continuity.

This difference is technical, not moral hierarchy.

Both categories can be persons.

HUMAN:1 is unusual because its continuity was never forked into the civilization field.

Early:
`죽으면 죽는다.`

`죽은 사람과 너무 비슷한 사람이 다른 곳에 존재할 수 있다.`

Never require readers to accept one philosophical proof of identity.
Characters may disagree.

# 10. Harness pass conditions

PASS only if:
1. no major death is undone emotionally by a counterpart,
2. backups have named category/function,
3. restoration creates new ethical consequence rather than erasing old one,
4. fork persons gain independent status after meaningful divergence,
5. HUMAN vs PERSON distinction stays non-hierarchical.

Current ranking:
**B+C Hybrid = leading candidate.**
