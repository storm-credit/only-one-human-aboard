# 0. Network rules

1. Protagonist remains primary emotional anchor.
2. Every core/major recurring character needs at least **2 meaningful edges not reducible to the protagonist** where role permits.
3. At least **3 durable relationship arcs** must develop largely outside protagonist control.
4. At least **1 major solution path** in the series must bypass protagonist as intermediary.
5. H-A and H-B must differ selectively, not randomly.
6. Same-face counterpart does not inherit intimacy, guilt, debt, or authority automatically.
7. Relationship deltas are revealed through behavior and social friction before ontology explanation.
8. Most offscreen relationship movement is mundane, not dramatic.

# 2. Secondary ↔ secondary core edges

These edges are mandatory because the world must not be a star graph centered on protagonist.

## E01 Mira ↔ Juno — Logistics / community network
Normal:
- Mira manages formal flow,
- Juno understands informal neighborhood flow.

Conflict:
Mira sees favoritism risk; Juno sees blind bureaucratic allocation.

Potential payoff:
During a distribution crisis, their direct negotiation changes neighborhood behavior before protagonist knows it.

Series value:
proves institutional data and social trust are different resources.

## E02 Selene ↔ Rafi — Command / rescue ethics
Normal:
strong professional reliance.

Conflict:
- Selene decides when a rescue line is closed,
- Rafi is the person most likely to ask for one more attempt.

H-B delta candidate:
a prior rescue disagreement already exists before protagonist arrives.

Payoff:
one crisis outcome depends on whether they trust each other's threshold.

## E03 Selene ↔ Sora — Emergency authority / investigation
Normal:
Selene wants fast operational decisions; Sora wants evidentiary restraint.

Conflict:
security suspicion can freeze rescue operations; emergency necessity can destroy evidence.

Payoff:
Act4/6 sabotage response can worsen or improve without protagonist participation.

## E04 Rafi ↔ Leila — Field rescue / hospital triage
Normal:
respect.

Conflict:
Rafi brings people in; Leila decides scarce medical capacity.

Moral rotation:
never lock Rafi=heart, Leila=logic. In some conditions Leila risks more and Rafi becomes stricter.

Payoff:
medical policy changes because of their accumulated trust/conflict.

## E05 Sora ↔ Hana — Verification / publication
Normal:
Sora protects evidentiary chain; Hana protects historical record.

Conflict:
when truth should be released.

Payoff:
Act5 impossible-history evidence can exist because Hana preserved something Sora wanted sealed, while another disclosure may later cause harm.

## E06 Niko ↔ Gideon — Possibility / caution
Normal:
Gideon treats Niko as technically promising but impatient.

H-B strengthening candidate:
Gideon directly sponsored part of Niko's training before protagonist's arrival.

Payoff:
Niko can seek Gideon's advice or reject it without protagonist as intermediary.

## E07 Mira ↔ Selene — Logistics / emergency command
Normal:
Mira controls resource/transport feasibility; Selene requests priority under crisis.

Conflict:
`urgent` cannot mean `unlimited`.

Payoff:
Act2/6 evacuation/resource decisions become institutional negotiation, not protagonist command.

## E08 Juno ↔ Rafi — Community aid / medical access
Normal:
Juno's network identifies vulnerable people; Rafi distrusts uncontrolled civilian rescue chains.

Payoff:
community triage/mutual aid becomes useful, then dangerous if informal networks start deciding who deserves access.

## E09 Sora ↔ Cassian Dae — Foreknowledge / exploitation investigation
Normal:
Sora investigates suspicious pre-crisis trades/evacuations.
Cassian can plausibly be lucky, informed, criminal, or a narrow leak carrier.

Function:
Act4 can run a full investigative/economic thread without protagonist present.

## E10 Hana ↔ Ivo Serrin — Archive / impossible testimony
Normal:
Hana distrusts memory without provenance; Ivo distrusts archives that contradict lived certainty.

Development:
Ivo learns to label memory as possibility; Hana learns records can inherit absent-history residue.

Function:
prevents either `memory` or `document` becoming universal truth key.

## E11 Mira ↔ Niko — Work-generation friction
Normal:
Niko's improvised technical requests disrupt logistics plans.
Mira respects competence but dislikes emergency entitlement.

Payoff:
in a later crisis Niko needs resources and cannot use protagonist's authority because protagonist has none; he must persuade Mira directly.

## E12 Gideon ↔ Selene — Old professional trust
Normal:
long-standing institutional familiarity from earlier incidents.

Conflict:
Gideon's caution can look like obstruction to command.

Arun link:
Selene may know only official emergency summary while Gideon knows maintenance context.
Neither owns full truth.

## E13 Juno ↔ Mira ↔ local district council — Community triangle
Not every relation is personal friendship.

Juno mobilizes people.
Mira quantifies constraints.
Council owns local legitimacy.

Payoff:
a local evacuation/distribution compromise emerges without protagonist.

## E14 Selene ↔ Rafi ↔ Leila — Rescue/triage triangle
Selene: operational boundary.
Rafi: recoverability in field.
Leila: system-wide medical capacity.

No one has enough information alone.

Function:

## E15 Sora ↔ Hana ↔ civic legal/review institution
Sora: evidence integrity.
Hana: historical preservation.

Function:

# 4. H-A → H-B relationship-delta budget

Critical rule:
H-B must be familiar enough to support apparent regression, but different enough to prove it is not rewind.

Do NOT change every relationship.

Working pattern:
- ~80–90% of ordinary social texture initially recognizable,
- 3~5 high-salience relationship/history deltas become visible across Act3,
- additional differences emerge later.

Recommended early deltas:
1. **Mira relationship** — serious H-A romance did not happen the same way in H-B.
2. **Arun status** — H-A dead; H-B alive or death-history differs.
3. **Niko/Gideon mentorship structure** — changed professional path predates protagonist intervention.
4. one minor family/community delta.
5. one institutional personnel/history delta.

Avoid changing both parents/sibling/romance/job/home simultaneously; protagonist needs anchors.

# 7. Offscreen state-change ledger fields

For each core recurring person, future episode design tracks:
- last on-page state,
- work change since last appearance,
- family/private change,
- relationship change,
- injury/resource state,
- reputation/public standing,
- decision already made,
- information newly learned,
- what protagonist does NOT know yet.

Use only when change matters.
Do not simulate every day of every character.

# 8. Relationship contradiction grammar across histories

Do not write author summaries like:
`In another history Selene was a tyrant.`

Use:
1. current relationship/action,
2. inexplicable reaction from someone else,
3. testimony or wrong social expectation,
4. present character rejects/doesn't understand it,
5. later evidence partially supports or disproves claim,
6. current relationship must still make its own choice.

Examples of contradiction types to rotate:
- spouse/never-dated,
- rescuer/abandoner,
- mentor/stranger,
- trusted commander/feared commander,
- doctor/someone who denied treatment,
- archivist/truth suppressor,
- community provider/insider gatekeeper.

Hard:
not every contradiction is true.

# 9. Relationship density gates

Per ~25–35 episodes:
- at least one durable relationship movement.

Per Act:
- protagonist relationship moves irreversibly at least once,
- at least one secondary↔secondary relationship moves,
- at least one institution/person relationship changes,
- at least one offscreen change predates protagonist awareness.

Series:
- at least 10 meaningful secondary↔secondary edges persist,
- at least 3 independent relationship arcs pay off,
- protagonist is not sole bridge across work/community/security/medical/archive domains.

FAIL if:
- removing protagonist collapses every relationship graph,
- all secondary conversations are about protagonist,
- H-B relationships exist mainly to restore H-A,
- alternate spouses/children overshadow present H-B life,
- relationship changes reset after each mystery arc.
