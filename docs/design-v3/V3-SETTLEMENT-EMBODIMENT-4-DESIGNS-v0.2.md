# V3 SETTLEMENT / EMBODIMENT — 4 DESIGNS v0.2

Status: `P0/P1 ARRIVAL DESIGN / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》

Problem:
The ultradense civilization-field model means citizens are already real persons on physical substrate.
Arrival must therefore NOT imply:
`they become real only when put into human-sized bodies.`

Need to decide how Meridian persons participate in a planetary settlement while preserving:
- personhood,
- physical stakes,
- title/HUMAN:1 contrast,
- survival action,
- finite resources,
- continuity rules.

---

# A — Everyone Gets One Permanent Human-Sized Body

Mechanism:
Every selected settlement citizen is transferred into a human-scale synthetic/biological body.

Strengths:
- intuitive,
- easy action scenes,
- familiar colony imagery.

Weaknesses:
- implies substrate life was incomplete,
- mass manufacture/migration burden huge,
- unclear what happens to other D3 civilizations,
- body supply becomes arbitrary plot gate,
- undermines user's microscopic-physical civilization concept.

Verdict: **REJECT AS UNIVERSAL RULE**.

---

# B — Remote Avatars Only

Mechanism:
People remain in Meridian substrate and control planet-side robots/avatars remotely.

Strengths:
- safe,
- efficient,
- preserves substrate identity,
- scalable.

Weaknesses:
- body destruction is merely equipment loss,
- final survival stakes weaken,
- settlement remains psychologically `ship people operating machines`,
- communication loss can stop society from existing locally.

Verdict: **USE AS TOOL MODE, NOT ENTIRE SETTLEMENT MODEL**.

---

# C — Planetary Substrate Colony

Mechanism:
Meridian fabricates/lands new ultradense substrate banks on the destination.
Person continuities can be migrated through a continuity-preserving live handoff from ark substrate to settlement substrate.

Citizens remain the same kind of persons they always were.

Strengths:
- matches ontology,
- no biological supremacy,
- colony can physically survive separate from ship,
- real substrate destruction creates death stakes,
- can carry very large populations efficiently.

Weaknesses:
- needs visible means to act in macroscopic planetary environment,
- migration continuity mechanics need guardrails,
- purely vault-based civilization could feel visually static.

Verdict: **STRONG BASE**.

---

# D — Distributed Substrate + Voluntary Embodiment [LEADING]

Combine C with multiple physical interface modes.

Core:
- persons' continuity lives in physical ultradense substrate,
- settlement creates independent planet-side substrate banks,
- people migrate/extend settlement through continuity-preserving handoff,
- human-sized bodies are **interfaces/embodiment choices**, not proof of personhood.

Physical modes:

## D1 Remote avatar
- continuity stays in protected local substrate,
- avatar is equipment,
- avatar loss != person death,
- good for construction/hazard scouting.

## D2 Persistent embodied node
- a person's live continuity anchor/process is physically extended or migrated into a local embodied node,
- low latency and high autonomy,
- destruction before failover can kill the person,
- used when independent presence matters.

## D3 Fixed/local civic substrate
- homes, schools, workplaces and communities can remain substrate-resident while using screens, robotics, immersive/physical interfaces.

## D4 Biological life
- HUMAN:1 and later biological people occupy ordinary biological bodies,
- they are one embodiment class among persons, not rulers.

Verdict: **SELECT**.

---

# 1. Continuity migration rule

Do not use:
`copy file → destroy source → declare same person`.

Leading mechanism:
**continuous causal handoff**.

Author model:
- source and destination substrate establish a live synchronized continuity bridge,
- active process extends across both,
- functional center gradually shifts,
- source component is released only after destination continuity is stable,
- no moment of confirmed death followed by restore.

This resembles live migration/fault-tolerant handoff, but story should not explain it with server jargon.

Failure can occur.
Therefore migration itself can have stakes.

---

# 2. Why use persistent embodiment if remote avatars are safer?

Practical reasons:
- communication latency/dropout,
- local autonomy during storms/disasters,
- lower dependency on one protected hub,
- legal/political desire for physically local presence,
- sensory/social preference,
- specialized manual action,
- exploration outside high-bandwidth coverage.

People can reasonably disagree about preferred embodiment.

No cultural consensus is required.

---

# 3. What `settlement` means

Settlement is not just bodies walking outside.

It requires physical independence:
- local power,
- local heat rejection,
- shielding,
- fabrication,
- substrate banks,
- communications,
- agriculture/ecological systems where required,
- shelters,
- transport/robotics,
- biological support for HUMAN:1 and future biological population.

A settlement becomes truly independent when loss of Meridian does not automatically kill its civic population.

That independence is one source of final irreversibility.

---

# 4. Existing D3 civilization claims

Do not solve by saying only H-B is real/worthy.

At arrival there may be:
- current H-B society,
- other active D3 lineages,
- quiescent D3 claims,
- enormous D0~D2 archive/search states.

The mission cannot physically externalize every possible history as a separate macroscopic city.

Leading governance principle:
- settlement infrastructure belongs to the mission/civic process, not one `winning` lineage by natural right,
- existing D3 persons require representation/continuation protection,
- migration can occur in stages,
- no low `civilization score` justifies erasure,
- new D3 creation is restricted until rights/resource governance is established.

Exact representation remains P1.

---

# 5. H-B's role

The story follows H-B because the protagonist lives there, not because H-B is ontologically the only valid civilization.

For narrative practicality H-B can become the **first publicly organized claimant/participant** in settlement reform after discovering the outer architecture.

This does NOT grant H-B ownership of all other D3 persons.

Its final achievement is opening a legitimate path rather than being crowned the winning civilization.

---

# 6. HUMAN:1 interaction

HUMAN:1 is biological.

After birth:
- biological body is vulnerable in ordinary biological ways,
- needs caregivers/medicine/food/environment,
- can interact with substrate persons through physical embodiments/avatars,
- has no special authority over them.

Strong visual possibility:
A biological child grows up surrounded by people who may appear human through bodies/interfaces even though their continuity substrate is different.

Do not use this to create a `real child among fake robots` framing.

---

# 7. Final survival stakes

Act9 should deliberately move some relevant characters from safe remote operation into **persistent local continuity** for credible reasons.

Possible causal needs:
- communication blackout,
- settlement substrate damaged,
- remote control no longer reliable,
- local autonomous repair required,
- evacuation/handoff incomplete.

Then:
- body/node destruction can kill,
- substrate bank loss can kill many,
- outer ark cannot simply restore confirmed-dead persons from snapshots.

This restores survival tension without making all citizens biological.

---

# 8. Why final future is irreversible

Not because:
- computer shuts off,
- all branches vanish,
- everyone becomes biological.

Instead:
1. local settlement becomes physically independent,
2. present persons acquire enforceable rights over continuation,
3. involuntary D3 branching is restricted,
4. current decisions alter real external infrastructure/ecology and cannot simply be rolled back globally,
5. people choose to accept consequences without manufacturing a full conscious rehearsal for every decision.

Forecasting remains possible.
Living every possible future does not remain the default.

---

# 9. Physical expansion after settlement

The microscopic-substrate model enables nontraditional colonization.

Possible layers:
- protected civic substrate vaults,
- distributed micro-substrate nodes across settlements,
- large robotic industry,
- human-scale public embodiments,
- biological habitats,
- remote probes.

This can create a visually rich colony without requiring 300k biological bodies on day one.

---

# 10. Risks

## Risk — Avatar removes danger
Repair:
Distinguish remote equipment from persistent continuity embodiment and create situations where local autonomy requires the latter.

## Risk — Too abstract
Repair:
Reader mostly sees human-scale interfaces, streets, rooms, tools and bodies. Substrate mechanics stay author-side except where plot-relevant.

## Risk — H-B becomes chosen civilization
Repair:
H-B is first narrative claimant, not sole legitimate polity.

## Risk — Infinite population settlement
Repair:
D3 is finite/resource-bounded; staged migration and representation remain real problems.

## Risk — HUMAN child becomes mascot/king
Repair:
ordinary guardianship and civic rights, no sovereignty.

---

# 11. Harness

FAIL if:
1. macro body = real person, substrate person = lesser.
2. every body loss equals death even in remote-avatar mode.
3. every body loss is harmless even in persistent-continuity mode.
4. settlement solves all D3 rights by picking a winner.
5. snapshot restore makes migration death-proof.
6. HUMAN:1 owns the colony.
7. external settlement is merely decorative while everyone remains permanently dependent on ark.

PASS if:
- personhood is substrate-independent from body style,
- local colony gains physical independence,
- multiple embodiment choices exist,
- final danger can still kill continuity,
- settlement reform addresses but does not magically erase ethical debt.

---

# 12. Verdict

Selected candidate:
**C + D hybrid — planet-side ultradense substrate colony + voluntary/functional physical embodiment.**

Universal macro-body migration is rejected.
Remote avatars remain useful tools.
Persistent embodied nodes create local autonomy and real survival stakes.

Status:
**SETTLEMENT EMBODIMENT P0 = CLOSED ENOUGH FOR ACT9 DESIGN / REPRESENTATION P1 REMAINS.**
