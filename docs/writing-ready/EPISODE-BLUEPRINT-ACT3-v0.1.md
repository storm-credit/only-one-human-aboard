**한 명이라는 숫자 — EP059~088**

Sub-Acts:
- 3A EP059~066 — Meaning
- 3B EP067~076 — Amara identity / re-identification / controlled confirmation
- 3C EP077~088 — social/family consequence

Act objective:
Pay the explanation debt immediately, transform `COUNT=1` from an abstract classification into pressure on an ordinary family, and prove that the story becomes **larger after the Reveal rather than emptier**.

Mandatory Act rules:
- Meaning clarification must complete within EP059~066.
- Amara may privately suspect herself only after the Seed ↔ old origin mapping becomes clear.
- protected official confirmation precedes public identity confirmation.
- public identity emerges through re-identification pressure + Amara's controlled choice, not a leak.
- several false candidates exist.
- Amara receives no special power, administrative key, medical superiority, office, colony claim or reproductive duty.
- modern civic personhood remains intact.
- no riot/AI-vs-human-war pivot.
- Noah/Ella/Jun/Raul/Ines/Tomas continue independent stories.

Carry from EP058:
The ship publicly knows that a legacy origin audit returns one active Natural/Bio-Origin match and that old schemas sometimes alias that category as `Human`. The identity is protected. The public does not yet understand exactly what the historical category means relative to ordinary modern Seed care.

# SUB-ACT 3A — EP059~066 — MEANING

Baseline:
The number is public but its meaning is unstable. If the story withholds definitions now, reader trust collapses. These eight episodes must answer the factual questions while opening new social actions.

## EP059 — What Natural Means
- **Time:** immediately after EP058
- **Primary Actor / Function:** historical-medical joint explainer team, Ines as current-medical boundary voice
- **Location:** public audit follow-up + ordinary clinics receiving questions
- **Immediate Desire / Problem:** explain `Natural/Bio-Origin Cognition` without letting the old `Human` alias become `one real person vs 299,999 fakes`
- **External Pressure:** headlines/social posts compress the report to `ONE HUMAN`; clinics receive calls asking for body tests
- **Choice / Action:** institutions publish the first plain-language clarification: origin refers to **how early cognitive self-organization began**, not what adult bodies are made of
- **Reader Reward:** immediate answer to the largest semantic question
- **Irreversible Consequence:** public discourse now has a more accurate vocabulary, but the provocative shorthand cannot be erased
- **Information Release:** current citizens have living biological bodies/brains; Natural vs Seeded is developmental provenance
- **Foreshadow / Payoff:** pays H2R-A fairness debt
- **Relationship Turn:** Ines publicly refuses to let current medicine be used as `real-human testing`
- **Rhythm Tag:** information payoff + current-life pressure
- **End Hook:** next question becomes obvious: if almost everyone is Seeded, **what exactly did Seed do?**
- **Canon Dependencies:** H2R-A / RW-H1
- **Forbidden Drift Check:** no artificial-body reveal; no narrator judgment
- **Next Carry:** explain Seed function and limits
