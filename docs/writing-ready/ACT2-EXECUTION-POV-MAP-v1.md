- methodology summaries relevant to current cases

- protected row identity
- sealed prenatal provenance details

# Tier-3 POV Control

Only two non-core functional POVs are added in Act2:
1. Protected Reviewer
2. Privacy Reviewer

Both recur twice.
Neither should receive a large personal subplot in Act2.
They need enough ordinary specificity to feel human but exist primarily because their information compartments cannot honestly be shown through a core POV.

No separate Audit Lead POV is introduced.
This prevents an accidental `truth detective` secondary protagonist.
