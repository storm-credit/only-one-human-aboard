Act objective:
Prove that the novel remains alive after the main Reveal. The ship must continue to work, parent, move, litigate, repair, learn and age while origin language becomes one new pressure among many.

Mandatory Act rules:
- no second ontology mystery replaces Count.
- origin shorthand enters ordinary life but does not infect every episode.
- Raul's rights wins continue producing real implementation costs without invalidating the rights.
- Ines actively blocks medical misuse of origin.
- Amara household keeps independent work/residence/privacy paths.
- P-S-N family remains formally outside Maren's adjudicative jurisdiction.
- Ella discovers PR-H1 **through her own archive/records**, not confession, villain leak or Maren's convenient breakdown.
- PR-H1 must remain legally imperfect: unsubmitted draft, serious context, possible ethics-review impact, not binding automatic refusal.
- Maren's concealment is wrong/controlling but not retroactively murder or secret adjudication.
- Act ends with relationship/legal agency changed, not reconciliation reset.

Carry from EP088:
Count and Amara identity are public. Meaning is understood. Modern citizenship remains intact. Origin language is now socially actionable. Amara is still a worker/parent rather than officeholder. P-S-N and Old Quarter arcs continue independently.
