# Hard Execution Notes

1. No character-name POV header by default.
2. POV identity must become evident naturally through location, goal and attention.
3. EP005: do not make Maren narrate a medical lecture; Ines demonstrates rule through action.
4. EP013: do not turn Ines into origin-history expert. The record is ordinary prenatal medicine.
5. EP017/024: Maren is not the only person capable of understanding records.
6. EP004 Raul must be a real victory from inside his own goal, not a token rival chapter.
7. Jun POV uses maintenance/place attention, not lyrical nostalgia.
8. Noah at 12 does not sound like adult legal analyst.
