Act 7:
**도착은 판정이 아니다 — EP195~230**

Sub-Acts:
- 7A EP195~206 — Rights Through Transition
- 7B EP207~218 — Choices Become Physical / System Insertion
- 7C EP219~230 — First New Days

## Timeline Lock For This Pass
- EP195 begins roughly **T-3.0y** before system insertion.
- EP206 ends roughly **T-1.3~1.5y**.
- EP207~215 move through final ~year-plus approach.
- **System insertion occurs around EP216** candidate.
- EP217~218 cover immediate post-insertion stabilization/first deployment windows.
- EP219~230 span the first weeks/months, potentially up to roughly **+1 year**.

Exact dates remain ELASTIC pending M1 Time/Age Harness.

Act objective:
Complete the voyage without pretending that arrival resolves the civilization. Rights must survive overlapping jurisdictions, physical choices must consume real assets, families must live through separation, and first settlement steps must remain small enough that Meridian is still the mature city.

Mandatory Act rules:
- old Human Settler mapping can create operational danger but cannot erase current ship citizenship.
- broader rights continuity comes from courts/transition institutions/assembly/service systems, not Maren alone.
- Maren handles one narrow Continuity case inside her jurisdiction; she does not write the constitution.
- Amara is never settler authenticator, governor, genetic/archive key, or compulsory reproductive symbol.
- Tomas is retired; no last-minute elder authority restoration.
- no combat/alien/mutiny climax.
- no last-engine miracle.
- system insertion is a major physical milestone, not the final emotional answer.
- orbital/asteroid industrial deployment starts first; surface foothold remains small; new habitat remains long-term.
- Meridian remains a living city after insertion.
- at least five ending costs are visible in named-character life.
- final law remains incomplete but origin cannot automatically downgrade civic personhood.

Carry from EP194:
At T-3 years, families already live toward different futures. Temporary cross-jurisdiction rights rules exist. Ship redundancy is reduced. Old Quarter has suffered real partial loss. Human Settler literal mapping is stayed but not fully translated. Kai, Noah, Ines and others have deployment paths; Amara, Ella and Maren remain primarily Meridian-centered.
