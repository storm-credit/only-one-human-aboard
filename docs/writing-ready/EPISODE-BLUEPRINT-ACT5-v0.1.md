# Episode Blueprint — ACT 5 v0.1

Status: `WRITING READY PASS-1 / DESIGN ONLY / NO PROSE`

Canon parent:
`canon/ACT_BIBLE-v1.md`

Act 5:
**미래를 위해 현재를 뜯는 법 — EP123~156**

Sub-Acts:
- 5A EP123~131
- 5B EP132~140
- 5C EP141~148 — Final Approach certification cluster
- 5D EP149~156 — first Human Settler translation conflict / irreversible allocation

Act objective:
Make arrival preparation materially irreversible. The future must begin consuming **current** maintenance slack, manufacturing time, expert labor, thermal infrastructure, space and redundancy—without turning the novel into engineering logistics or a binary colony war.

Mandatory Act rules:
- no direct `engine steals city electricity` pressure.
- propulsion and civil grid remain different scales.
- pressure comes from spares / manufacturing / specialists / thermal geometry / reaction-mass logistics / maintenance windows / redundancy.
- Final Approach certification is triggered by multi-institution readiness, not Amara.
- Human Settler legacy category affects destination transition status, not current ship citizenship.
- Amara receives no automatic settlement privilege.
- Old Quarter loss becomes physical and cumulative, not symbolic-only.
- time visibly expands across several years.
- at least two major changes per Sub-Act are initiated without Maren.

Carry from EP122:
Family trust has changed; Ella owns her current directive and Maren has reduced proxy authority. The larger society now enters a phase where arrival preparations can no longer remain background planning.
