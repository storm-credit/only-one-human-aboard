# Why Ella Owns Act 4

The Act's core personal question is not:
`will Maren be forgiven?`

It is:
`what happens when Ella learns that evidence about her own uncertain wishes was withheld from the process that rebuilt her?`

Therefore Ella must own:
- approach to archive
- choice to delay reading
- current-preference recording
- draft discovery
- context discovery
- ethics omission discovery
- access-log discovery
- counterfactual clarification
- confrontation
- proxy change
- current directive

Maren owns:
- professional normalization EP089/100
- professional ethics accountability EP120
- new family baseline EP122

This prevents PR-H1 from becoming `Maren's guilt story`.

# EP117 Confrontation Guardrail

POV = Ella.

Maren may say:
- draft was unregistered
- Ella seemed undecided
- prognosis offered meaningful recovery
- Noah had no other legal parent
- she feared delay/loss
- she wanted Ella back

But narration cannot enter Maren's inner justifications.
Ella hears claims and watches behavior.

The emotional fact is:
Maren knew enough to bring the evidence to the review and chose not to.

Do NOT let the scene become courtroom cross-examination.
End when relationship baseline breaks, not when every argument is exhausted.

---

# EP120 Accountability Guardrail

POV = Maren.

This is the correct place to enter her internal reasoning.
But the goal is not retroactive self-acquittal.

She must distinguish:
- what was legally allowed
- what her family role allowed
- what her professional knowledge made her responsible to notice
- what she wanted personally

Outcome remains proportional:
serious ethics/conflict omission, not criminal secret adjudication.

No career-destruction melodrama.
