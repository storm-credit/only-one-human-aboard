## Timeline Lock For This Pass
Act 5 exits at roughly **T-8 years** before system insertion.
Act 6 spans roughly **five years**, ending near **T-3 years**.
Act 7 then owns the final ~3 years before insertion plus roughly up to ~1 year of first-post-insertion life.

Exact dates inside ranges remain ELASTIC and will be finalized in M1 Time/Age Harness.

Act objective:
Turn settlement pathways into **personal careers, residences and family separations** over real years. The ship must stop feeling like one population awaiting one landing decision.

Mandatory Act rules:
- families can split across ship/orbit/surface/new-habitat preferences.
- no pathway is a fixed ideological faction.
- Noah/Kai/Mina visibly mature.
- Ella is no longer primarily `the reconstructed sister`.
- Tomas reaches actual retirement/authority transfer.
- Amara stops trying to keep family in one future configuration.
- Raul's rights work shifts toward representation/jurisdiction, not ontology.
- Jun may support some settlement transfers while defending ship-home capacity.
- major time skips must have explicit bridge episodes.
- no mature destination colony exists yet; this is pre-insertion preparation.

Carry from EP156:
At roughly **T-8y**, Final Approach rules are active, Human Settler literal mapping is stayed, several provisional destination categories exist, ship redundancy has already been spent, and households now plan against multiple real futures.
