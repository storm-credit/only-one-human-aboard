# Why Maren Drops To Two POVs In Act 3

This is intentional, not protagonist loss.

Act 3 is the point where the series proves:
`the world does not become important only when Maren observes it`.

Maren still owns two critical functions:
- EP064: the Reveal turns inward and confirms she is an ordinary Seeded citizen who chooses a new privacy boundary.
- EP081: origin cannot replace Continuity analysis in her actual profession.

Forcing Maren into Amara confirmation/re-identification would:
- make her chosen investigator
- weaken Amara agency
- break protected identity architecture
- reduce family collateral to secondhand reports

Cumulative through EP088, Maren remains the largest series POV owner at 22 episodes.

Later Acts can naturally return more often to Maren as family/Continuity/arrival threads braid again.
