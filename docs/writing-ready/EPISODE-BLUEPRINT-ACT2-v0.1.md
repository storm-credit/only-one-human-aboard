Act objective:
Turn Act 1's separate family / Continuity / Living World / legacy-schema threads into **consequences that begin to overlap**, while introducing Amara as an ordinary citizen long before her origin matters.

Mandatory Act rules:
- Amara must appear meaningfully at least three times before Count: worker → family → arrival-resource/utility stakeholder.
- None of those appearances may carry ominous origin cues.
- Maren does not lead the legacy-origin audit and does not discover the Count by genius deduction.
- Count comes from an independently governed public-interest schema audit.
- Seed success is proven by prenatal closed-loop verification records (SV-H1), not adult body testing.
- missing/unknown records are never silently classified as Natural-Origin.
- exact personal identity remains protected after Count.
- CP-H1 privacy review must happen before public release of the rare `1` cell.
- the episode-level reader promise remains family/work/living-world + consequences, not 30 episodes of database archaeology.
- no Amara identity confirmation before Act 3.

Act carry from EP028:
A cross-institution legacy-schema audit now exists because current prenatal records, old provenance fields and modern civic registries do not map cleanly. Maren supplies case-context examples only. Family baseline has already changed: Ella has real parenting authority, Noah has an independent youth path, and Maren no longer controls either by default.
