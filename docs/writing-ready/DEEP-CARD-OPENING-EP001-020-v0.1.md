# High-Risk Deep Card — Opening EP001~020 v0.1

Status: `WRITING READY DEEP CARD / DESIGN ONLY / NO PROSE`

Parent:
- `canon/ACT_BIBLE-v1.md`
- `canon/CHARACTER_BIBLE-v1.md`
- `docs/writing-ready/EPISODE-BLUEPRINT-ACT1-v0.1.md`

Purpose:
Protect the first 20 episodes from the two opposite failures:
1. too much SF/world explanation before the reader cares
2. too little title-level mystery pressure, making `유일한 인간` feel unrelated until EP50+

The opening promise is NOT `solve the one-human mystery now`.
It is:
**a difficult family return + a profession that must decide practical continuity rights + a ship society whose ordinary rules are strange enough to generate recurring consequences.**

---

# 1. First-20 Reader Contract

By the end of EP003, reader should know in action:
- Ella has returned/re-entered ordinary life after major Reconstruction.
- Maren has spent years protecting Noah during Ella's incapacity.
- Ella wants her role/life back.
- Noah is not a neutral object waiting to be handed over.

By the end of EP006~009, reader should understand:
- Maren's professional role concerns bounded Continuity/right decisions.
- Reconstruction is not magical resurrection from a soul file.
- decisions can be appealed/reviewed.
- a ruling changes real access/relationship conditions.

By the end of EP010~018, reader should have seen:
- Raul or external challenge proving Maren is not final authority.
- at least one living-world location/function beyond the same office/home.
- ordinary Seed/medical vocabulary or historical residue without explaining the central truth.
- Noah taking at least one action that complicates the adults' binary family framing.

By EP020:
- one early case/family decision has created a later consequence rather than resetting.
- the title's mystery has at least one fair **strangeness signal**—old terminology, provenance field, historical wording, or current-vs-old category mismatch—but no answer dump.
- reader can state the series engine without needing a lore glossary.

---

# 2. Opening Hook Priority

## Hook A — Family
Highest priority.

The emotional hook should be:
`A mother who is alive again wants her child/life back, but the years she missed are real.`

This gives immediate stakes before `what counts as human` becomes explicit.

## Hook B — Professional Choice
Second priority.

Maren's job must show:
- she can make a practical temporary decision
- she can be wrong/reversed
- decisions have downstream cost

Do not open with a lecture defining Continuity philosophy.

## Hook C — Closed Civilization
Third priority.

World details should appear because:
- people commute
- housing/medical/school systems constrain choices
- Reconstruction has queues/rehab/capacity
- Habitats have different rhythms

Do not explain ship architecture in a block before a character needs it.

## Hook D — Title Mystery
Present as **background unease / category mismatch**, not main investigation yet.

Reader should feel:
`something about this society's definitions/history does not line up cleanly.`

# 4. Mystery Fairness Without Over-Flagging

Required early clue families across Act1, with at least one touch by EP020:
1. ordinary Seed-related provenance is a real record field.
2. old documents/categories do not map perfectly to current everyday language.
3. current society treats reconstructed/Seeded people as ordinary civic persons in practice.

Important:
Do NOT film these details with metaphorical neon signs.
Most characters should treat them as boring bureaucracy/history.

The reader should be able to look back after EP056 and say:
`that was there`, not `the story was obviously screaming the answer`.

---

# 5. Family-Hook Attack

Potential failure:
Ella's return becomes abstract `is she the same person?` philosophy instead of a parent/child problem.

Guardrail:
Every early family episode should have at least one concrete stake:
- room/home access
- school pickup/contact
- schedule
- medical/rehab limit
- decision authority
- personal objects/routines
- whose plan Noah follows

Noah must act rather than merely react.

Verdict:
`PASS IF PARENT BLUEPRINT CONCRETE ACTIONS PRESERVED`.

---

# 6. Maren-Sympathy Attack

Potential failure:
Because Maren cared for Noah for years, reader automatically treats Ella as disruptive/less real.

Guardrail:
Opening must give Ella independent competence/agency and at least one legitimate reason she is angry at being treated as a problem to manage.

Also:
Maren must make at least one understandable but over-controlling choice early.

Neither sister is the designated correct one.

Verdict:
`PASS CANDIDATE`.

---

# 7. Noah-Object Attack

Noah must by EP020:
- evade or complicate one adult plan
- protect one current routine/relationship
- show a peer/school/youth life unrelated to adult philosophy

Forbidden:
- child gives wise speech deciding what identity means
- child passively waits for custody-like ruling

Verdict:
`PASS IF ACT1 AUTONOMY BEATS REMAIN`.

---

# 8. Case-of-the-Week Attack

By EP020, at least one early professional case outcome must have a later consequence in:
- Maren/Raul relationship
- institutional workflow
- resource/access queue
- family trust
- later claimant behavior

The reader should already learn:
`a decision is not the end of a case; it changes the next problem.`

Verdict:
`PASS CANDIDATE`.

---

# 9. Title-Promise Delay Attack

Question:
Is Count at EP056 too late for a novel titled 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》?

Defense:
The title itself provides the macro promise. The first 20 episodes must therefore **not pretend there is no mystery**; they show Seed/provenance/old-category mismatch while the story gives strong family/case rewards.

Required cadence:
- EP001~009: strange continuity world
- EP010~020: first historical/category unease
- EP020~028: audit direction becomes explicit
- EP053~058: factual Count payoff

If prose removes early category/provenance unease, the title promise becomes under-served and Gate fails.

Verdict:
`PASS WITH EARLY-CLUE GUARDRAIL`.

---

# 10. First-20 Reward Check

Within first 20 episodes, require at least:
- 1 major family-role turn
- 1 professional case/ruling payoff
- 1 Raul/Maren authority reversal or meaningful challenge
- 1 Noah autonomous choice
- 1 new living-world location with its own ordinary problem
- 1 title-mystery primer

This gives six different reward types before the main mystery investigation begins.

`PASS CANDIDATE`.

---

# 11. Web-Serial Readability Rules

For later beat/prose design:
- first episode cannot require understanding more than 2~3 invented terms.
- first 3 episodes must prioritize person/goal/conflict over architecture.
- early endings should turn a practical decision or relationship, not merely reveal terminology.
- do not stack three consecutive `interview/hearing/document` episodes.
- at least one early episode should show humor/pettiness/ordinary inconvenience so tone is not uniformly solemn.
- physical orientation to Meridian should be learned through movement/use, not map lecture.

---

# 12. Opening Failure Conditions

Writing Ready must reopen if later deep-beat work produces any of:
1. EP001 starts with history lecture.
2. Ella/Noah conflict does not become concrete by EP003.
3. Maren appears to have final unappealable authority.
4. first 10 episodes contain no visible payoff/change.
5. Noah acts only as custody object.
6. Seed is explained as central AI-origin truth before the reader needs it.
7. no title-level category unease appears by EP020.
8. first 20 feel like three unrelated Continuity cases.
