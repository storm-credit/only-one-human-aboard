# Specialist POV Reduction

Act5 had the heaviest specialist execution burden.
Act6 intentionally reduces new specialist POVs.

## New Tier-3
Only:
**Surface Trainee EP170**.

Reason:
The surface cohort must be experienced as ordinary people with different motives, not summarized as a political camp.

Use one trainee with a concrete current job/skill reason.
Do not turn them into a new core protagonist.

## Reused Act5 execution roles
- Asset Coordinator EP190
- Outer Works Lead EP191
- Transition Registrar EP193

No need to invent three new experts.
Their recurrence makes the future transition feel institutionally continuous.

# Maren Return Gravity Without Monopoly

Maren is the largest Act6 owner but only 7/38.

Her episodes are specifically those where her established lens has value:
- 157: future choices become real
- 167: she refuses broader authority
- 168/179: time bridges
- 178: narrow Continuity rule under two-home family reality
- 181: several incomplete paths coexist
- 194: Act closure into final approach

She does NOT own:
- Ella's future
- Noah/Kai/Mina adulthood choices
- Amara household maturation
- Tomas retirement
- Jun preservation decisions
- Ines deployment model
- Raul representation law

This is the intended late-series protagonist model:
**central return gravity, not causal monopoly**.

# EP193 Ensemble Problem

Blueprint lists multiple households' first-year locations.
Single-POV protocol forbids omniscient montage.

Use recurring Transition Registrar because:
- final confirmations are an actual service event
- registrar can encounter several choices legitimately
- emotional meaning is already earned in prior character POV episodes

The registrar should not narrate everyone's private feelings.
Only show:
- the choices people actually file
- procedural consequences
- ordinary interaction around confirmation

This makes geography legible without head hopping.
