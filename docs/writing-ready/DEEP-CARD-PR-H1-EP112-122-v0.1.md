Purpose:
Make the family betrayal land as a consequence of Maren's core flaw—**protect by deciding**—without collapsing into:
- binding-directive murder accusation
- secret self-adjudication

**Ella discovers her own past preference → establishes seriousness → finds omission → finds Maren access → learns counterfactual uncertainty → confronts Maren → changes current authority → keeps Noah out of moral verdict → Maren accepts professional review → Ella defines current preference → family continues under new boundaries**

# 0. Canon Facts That Cannot Drift

- Ella drafted a conditional refusal of aggressive Reconstruction ~1~2 years before accident.
- draft was NOT formally registered.
- private message/context shows the concern was serious.
- severe autobiographical/relational loss was the feared condition.
- Maren lawfully accessed Ella's emergency health/planning archive after accident.
- Maren saw enough of the draft/context to know it could matter.
- Maren did not surface it to clinicians/ethics review.
- Maren was NOT Ella's official adjudicator.
- draft could have triggered stricter substituted-judgment review/delay/narrower approach.
- draft did NOT automatically prohibit Reconstruction.
- surviving neural anchor + meaningful recovery prognosis + Noah dependency remained legitimate decision factors.
