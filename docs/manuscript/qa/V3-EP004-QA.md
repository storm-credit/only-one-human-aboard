# 6. THE REVIEWER CAUGHT ITS OWN ARITHMETIC ERROR

The r1 ending fix — the reviewer's own text — closed on:
> `북측이 시간당 오백이십이면 … 사천 명 가까이가 십칠 분씩 낸다.`

**520 × 8 = 4,160.** `사천 명 가까이` means *approaching four thousand from below*. The sentence states its own multiplicand, so no reader can rescue it.

The reviewer flagged it at r3, having carried it unchallenged through two rounds, on the grounds that it had flagged the structurally identical `14분` defect in r1 and consistency required flagging its own. And it is not cosmetic:

> *"an episode whose thesis is 'do the arithmetic and don't hide the result' cannot have its protagonist do the arithmetic wrong in the closing beat. The beat inverts."*

Corrected to `사천이백`. The exact figure is also better characterization — a round number is what this character would distrust.

# 9. PROCESS NOTE — A FABRICATED CONSTRAINT, CORRECTED

Through r1–r2 this episode was audited against a *"house length band 5,410–5,539"* that the orchestrator had **back-calculated from three accepted episodes and then applied as a ceiling.** It was never a rule.

The author caught it. The only documented rule — `docs/writing-ready/PROSE-EXECUTION-PROTOCOL-v1.1.md` §6, a **v2-era doc absent from the v3 authority list** — gives a soft band of 5,500–8,000 and says `exact count is not Canon`. Against that floor, **EP001 (5,512) / EP002 (5,410) / EP003 (5,539) all sit at or below 5,500**; EP002 is 90 short. The invented band was pinning episodes to the bottom of the legitimate range and nearly caused the Juno negotiation to be cut as "over-length."

The reviewer had independently reached the same conclusion — *"question the band, not the episode"*, noting that a band derived from monologue-shaped episodes should not bind the first episode containing genuine two-party negotiation.

**Rule now recorded in `MANUSCRIPT-STATUS.md` and `MANUSCRIPT-PIPELINE-v1.md` §2.1: floor 5,500, no ceiling. Over-length is not a defect. Never cut a scene for length — the reason to cut must always be a sentence-level defect.**

All ~212 characters of r3 cuts were applied as craft fixes on their own merits, independent of length.
