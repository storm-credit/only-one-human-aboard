# Atlas Guardrails

1. These are high-salience tracked devices only. Ordinary detail must greatly outnumber them.
2. Do not tag every repeated object as Chekhov.
3. A MacGuffin may move action but may not become a universal truth key.
4. A Red Herring must be a reasonable interpretation of true evidence, not narrator deception.
5. A payoff that resolves information but changes no action/relationship/right/resource is insufficient by itself.
6. No late ontology device may be added after Meaning/Amara phases without Canon reopening.
7. Device timing inherits exact episode authority from active Blueprints and M1 if this atlas only specifies a window.
