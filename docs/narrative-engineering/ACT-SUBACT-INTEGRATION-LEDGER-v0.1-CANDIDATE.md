# Cross-ledger hard gates

1. Every major arc changes >=2 of relationship / institution / world / mystery / moral judgment.
2. Every 25~35 episodes pays >=1 local mystery + >=1 relationship movement + >=1 survival/world consequence.
3. Strong memory leak never becomes routine.
4. Rowan is POV center, not universal causal center.
5. At least one major outcome is emergent-minor-actor driven.
6. At least 1/3 medium incidents have no hidden-history cause.
7. Physical Meridian constraints recur every Act but full substrate truth waits for Act7.
8. HUMAN:1 never explains the whole system.
9. Act8~9 new high-salience clues approach zero.
10. Writer Grammar overrides temptation to explain ontology early.
