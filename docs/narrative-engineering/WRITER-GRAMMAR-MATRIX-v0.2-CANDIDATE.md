# Writer Grammar Matrix v0.2 — V3 ULTRADENSE ONTOLOGY PATCH

Status: `V3 DEEP DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》
Relationship: supplements/supersedes conflicting ontology wording in `WRITER-GRAMMAR-MATRIX-v0.1-CANDIDATE.md`.

---

# 0. New hard writing rules

1. Never explain the world as `parallel universes` in author voice.
2. Characters may use regression/parallel-world language as provisional theories.
3. Explicit cross-history memory is a rare critical fault, not routine magic.
4. Most moral/history contrast is revealed through social reaction, testimony, behavior, records and physical residue.
5. Do not label characters as `good in state A / evil in state B` in exposition.
6. Character importance is emergent; a background figure may become critical in one lineage.
7. Do not turn ultradense substrate scale into constant cosmic exposition. Human-scale scenes remain primary.
8. Late ontology reveal must increase ethical stakes rather than reduce earlier life to simulation.

---

# 1. Social Testimony Grammar

Preferred reveal sequence for alternate moral histories:

`ordinary interaction → disproportionate reaction → contradictory testimony → current behavior contradicts testimony → later evidence partially validates both`

Example surface grammar:
- current Selene saves civilians competently.
- a stranger refuses to enter her shelter.
- the stranger says she once sealed an entire residential sector.
- Selene sincerely denies ever doing it.
- no narrator declares who is correct.

Desired reader reaction:
`둘 중 누가 거짓말하지?`
then later:
`둘 다 자기 삶에서는 진실을 말했구나.`

Forbidden:
- narrator summary tables,
- exposition explaining moral philosophy,
- immediate lineage IDs,
- easy proof after every contradictory memory.

---

# 2. Memory Leak Density Grammar

Most episodes contain **zero** explicit cross-history memory.

Use layers:
- L1 affective residue: can recur softly.
- L2 sensory fragment: occasional.
- L3 concrete episodic memory: rare major clue.
- L4 autobiographical collision: arc-level exceptional case.
- L5 multiple coherent histories: series-level anomaly.

Rule:
A reader should be able to spend dozens of episodes wondering whether memory anomalies are trauma, fraud, coincidence or system error.

Do not normalize the phenomenon socially.

# 3. ACT 1~2 reinforcement

No major structural rewrite.

Additional surface details allowed:
- citizens naturally assume their bodies/environment are ordinary physical reality.
- no microscopic/substrate clue should look like a computer-chip reveal.
- physical work detail remains local: pressure, power, structure, transport, maintenance.

Important:
Rowan's engineering reality must feel physically consequential even if the deeper embodiment layer is later recontextualized.

# 4. ACT 3 — Regression Imitation, Not Regression Truth

## 3A
Write like regression fiction briefly because Rowan believes that model.

Hard author rule:
Do not use narrator wording that objectively confirms time reversal.

Allowed:
`어제와 같은 아침이었다.`

Avoid objective claim:
`시간이 되감겼다.`

## 3B
Pre-existing differences must appear before Rowan's interventions can plausibly cause them.

## 3C
The other rememberer is an exceptional event.
Do not immediately introduce a community of rememberers.

---

# 5. ACT 4 — False Theory Social Thriller

## 4A
Most `foreknowledge` cases must have mundane ambiguity.
A profitable investor may have:
- genuine rare residue,
- stolen information,
- luck,
- pattern recognition,
- fraud.

Do not resolve all cases.

## 4B
Predictive failure remains strong, but treat `future memory` as character interpretation.

## 4C
Two mutually incompatible lived histories can be proven without proving cosmological parallel worlds.

End-state theory:
`우리가 하나의 과거만 가진 게 아니다.`

Not yet:
`우주는 여러 개다.`

---

# 6. ACT 5 — Physical/Archive Contradiction

Keep:
- impossible maintenance wear,
- dead-person records,
- sealed rooms,
- historical memorials.

Writer rule:
physical clue first; metaphysical theory much later.

A room that `should not exist` may be:
- a configuration residue,
- a support-space mapping error,
- a lineage artifact,
- an old physical module repurposing.

Never let one room prove the whole ontology.

HUMAN:1 reveal remains minimal and bureaucratic.

---

# 7. ACT 6 — One Lived Civilization Matters Completely

Collapse arc prose must never suggest the world is disposable.

If readers later learn this was one among astronomical civilization lineages, emotional value must increase:
`이런 삶이 셀 수 없이 많이 있었다.`
not decrease:
`가짜였으니 상관없다.`

Use ordinary grief, friendships, ration arguments, burial/memorial behavior and broken institutions to establish full moral reality.

---

# 8. ACT 7 — Scale / Lineage Horror Grammar

Replace old simple `branch/merge mechanism` reveal style.

Primary modes:
**Scale disorientation + identity lineage horror + fair inference.**

## 7A — 돌아온 곳이 없다
Rowan realizes no remembered state has privileged status.

Technique:
- familiar details from mutually incompatible histories coexist,
- institutions use procedures whose origins nobody remembers,
- some technology appears learned from catastrophes absent from official history.

## 7B — 겹친 사람들
Use testimony rather than diagrams.

Strong scene types:
- current kind person feared by another leak carrier,
- former enemy remembered as spouse,
- child recognizing a stranger as parent,
- someone thanking Rowan for a rescue he never performed.

Do not make all witnesses genuine.
Some must be wrong/fraudulent/traumatized so evidence remains difficult.

## 7C — 규모의 증거
Ontology emerges through multiple routes:
1. lineage index fragments,
2. physical substrate maintenance evidence,
3. institutional learning with no matching history,
4. rare memory fault correlation.

Reveal:
there are/were vastly more civilization lineages than characters imagined.

Rowan not HUMAN payoff may occur nearby but should not be caused by the same single truth file.

---

# 9. ACT 8 — Rights, Not Lore

Primary mode:
**Ethical adversarial drama under operational pressure.**

Questions must be embodied in current people:
- Is an alternate lineage person property, copy, child, sibling, or independent citizen?
- Can a lived civilization refuse termination?
- Is extracting survival knowledge from suffering exploitation?
- Can system designers consent on behalf of descendants?

Do not solve through one philosophical speech.

Use:
- evacuation decisions,
- access control,
- preservation of threatened substrate banks,
- whether to activate/deepen another lived civilization,
- whether to recover someone from another lineage.

HUMAN:1 reveal remains quiet.

---

# 10. ACT 9 — Irreversible Future Grammar v0.2

Delete the assumption that calculation must physically cease at arrival.

Primary conflict:
**Whether persons continue to be nonconsensually forked as civilization-test material after settlement begins.**

## 9A — 승격
A chosen/negotiated civic lineage begins macro-physical settlement embodiment.

The first irreversible sensation should be practical, not philosophical:
- injury has no automatic lineage substitute,
- a damaged habitat cannot be replaced by loading another social state,
- decisions create public legal precedent.

## 9B — 마지막 생존
Actual external destination hazard.
Old history knowledge helps but does not contain the answer.

Novel choice must matter.
A supposedly minor person may become decisive.

## 9C — 첫날
Civilization establishes a rule such as:
`No involuntary full-person lived forks.`

Non-conscious modeling may continue.
Voluntary future experiments remain an open civic question.

Final emotional grammar:
- no narrator thesis,
- people make mundane first-day choices,
- HUMAN:1 begins first life,
- nobody possesses a memory of what happens next.

# 11. Hook rotation additions

New hook categories:
- contradictory social recognition,
- ordinary person unexpectedly becomes causal pivot,
- physical evidence implies impossible scale,
- a memory clue is proven false but another part survives,
- current villain performs unmistakably compassionate act,
- current hero triggers harm through the same virtue.

Use these more often than direct lore revelations.

# 12. Dialogue guardrails

Good:
`저 사람은 좋은 사람이야.`
`당신한텐 그랬겠지.`

Good:
`난 당신을 처음 봅니다.`
`나는 당신 장례식에 갔어요.`

Bad:
`우리는 서로 다른 문명 인스턴스에서 동일 인격 계보로 분기되었습니다.`

System vocabulary belongs in late technical investigation, never ordinary emotional testimony.

---

# 13. Current writer priority

At manuscript stage, always prioritize:
1. immediate scene goal,
2. person-to-person tension,
3. physical consequence,
4. contradictory evidence,
5. ontology only when earned.

The series must remain readable as survival/mystery/ensemble drama even before the reader understands the substrate model.
