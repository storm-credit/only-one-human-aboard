# 11. POV eligibility flags for episode-card stage

Use labels:
- `P-DEFAULT` — protagonist preferred.
- `S-ELIGIBLE` — secondary direct POV allowed.
- `EXTERNAL-ONLY` — character may appear but interiority would leak/flatten mystery.
- `ORDINARY-LENS` — non-core POV only for concrete local stakes.

High-risk periods:

## Ivo
Act3~Act4 early: `EXTERNAL-ONLY`.
After genuine status intentional reveal: `S-ELIGIBLE`.

## Cassian
Act4 source mystery early/mid: `EXTERNAL-ONLY` or very narrow interior that does not identify information source.
Later: `S-ELIGIBLE`.

## Arun H-B
First reappearance: preferably protagonist/external lens.
After current independent life established: `S-ELIGIBLE`.

## Sora
Case-specific; use external lens if interior would trivially confirm suspect theory.

## HUMAN:1
Act5~8: `EXTERNAL-ONLY / NO DIRECT POV`.
Act9: direct POV still unnecessary.

# 12. Long-run guardrails

FAIL if:
- protagonist has no direct meaningful scene for >2~3 consecutive full episodes outside a deliberately designed Act6 chain,
- one secondary gets >~12% series-wide without explicit co-protagonist decision,
- every institution is experienced only through protagonist,
- every secondary POV exists to discuss protagonist,
- direct POV exposes information the character would naturally think but story is trying to hide artificially.
