# D01 — ARUN INCIDENT

Type:
`Character Foreshadow + Identity Contradiction + Long Payoff`

## PLANT — Act1 EP001~020
Show behavior, not backstory.
- protagonist hesitates/changes tone around a specific closure protocol,
- an old colleague/mentor knows not to joke about one class of incident,
- protagonist reacts strongly to `reopen for one person` argument.

No name required at first.

Action effect:
protagonist chooses caution in a small ordinary case.

## REINFORCE — Act1 late EP029~042
- old work/incident code appears during mundane inspection,
- one tiny record/autobiographical mismatch exists,
- protagonist dismisses it as trauma/old documentation error.

Do not make it paranormal.

## TRIGGER — Act2 EP076~087
Full human-tragedy version becomes clear:
- Arun remained inside,
- protagonist's technical judgment contributed to closure,
- official death,
- larger loss prevented.

Reader conclusion:
`This explains his personality.`

Important:
At this stage it must work as a complete ordinary tragedy.

## PARTIAL PAYOFF — Act3~5
Act3:
small H-B evidence suggests Arun history differs.

Act5 EP197~212:
H-B Arun is alive or the incident/death record is materially incompatible.
Current Arun rejects protagonist treating him as a returned dead friend.

Action/relationship effect:
protagonist loses automatic emotional claim on Arun.

## FINAL PAYOFF — Act7 EP296~311
Multiple Arun-related histories/evidence exist:
- dead,
- survivor,
- protagonist saved by Arun,
- incident never formed same way.

Meaning:
not `which one is original?`
Instead:
protagonist's remembered guilt is real to his life but not universal history.

## AFTERSHOCK — Act8~9
Protagonist stops trying to restore current people into H-A roles.
This directly supports final irreversible choice.
