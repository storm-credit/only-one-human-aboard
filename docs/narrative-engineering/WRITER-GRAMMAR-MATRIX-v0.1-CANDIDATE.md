# Writer Grammar Matrix v0.1 — V3 CANDIDATE

Status: `V3 DEEP DESIGN CANDIDATE / NOT CANON`
Project: 《우주선에는 인간이 한 명뿐이다》
Purpose: 각 Act/Sub-Act가 같은 문체와 같은 미스터리 방식으로 반복되지 않도록, 집필 단계의 작문법·정보통제·장면 리듬·POV·대사·서스펜스·회수방식을 규정한다.

This document is separate from the Narrative Device Atlas.
- Device Atlas = 무엇을 심고 어떻게 회수하는가.
- Writer Grammar Matrix = 그 구간을 어떤 방식으로 쓰는가.

# 0. Series-wide Base Grammar

## Hoffman layer — always on
1. 독자가 허구를 `설명`으로 이해하기 전에 생활세계로 믿게 할 것.
2. 장면 자체는 명료해야 한다. 미스터리는 사실관계가 아니라 의미에서 발생한다.
3. 추상 설정어보다 행동·물건·공간·직업 절차를 먼저 보여준다.
4. 작가가 아는 시스템 용어를 인물이 모르면 본문도 먼저 말하지 않는다.
5. 독자가 장면을 믿을 수 있어야 나중의 재맥락화가 작동한다.

## Series hard rules
- exposition never leads a major arc.
- major information must change action/relationship/risk/resource.
- ordinary detail > clue detail.
- no three consecutive explanation-heavy episodes.
- every 8~15 episodes: visible irreversible turn or reward.
- each major arc resolves 70~90% of its local event.
- final 15%: payoff > plant.
- death cannot be treated as a reliable reset command.

Each Sub-Act receives values for:

# 2. ACT 1 — 우리가 살던 메리디언 / EP001~042

Primary craft mode:
**Illusion + Ordinary-life realism + Low-frequency uncanny**

Goal:
독자가 메리디언을 먼저 `집`으로 믿게 한다.

## 1A EP001~014 — 평범한 고장
POV:
- Rowan close third or equivalent limited perspective.
- narrow world knowledge.

Scene rhythm:
- medium scenes.
- work task → small human friction → concrete completion.

Information control:
- no ontology clues explained.
- reader knows roughly what Rowan knows.

Prose emphasis:
- tactile engineering detail.
- sound, pressure, heat, smell, worn surfaces.
- ordinary food, commute, companion ecology, neighborhood life.

Dialogue:
- natural workplace shorthand.
- no lore Q&A conversations.

Hook type:
- small unresolved practical problem, relationship friction, tiny anomaly.

Forbidden:
- ominous narrator voice telling reader the world is false.
- `little did he know` foreshadow narration.

## 1B EP015~028 — 맞지 않는 작은 것들
Primary mode:
**Uncanny realism / cognitive dissonance**.

Rule:
한 장면에서 이상현상은 보통 1개만 강하게 둔다.

Method:
- normal scene 90%, anomaly 10%.
- explanation immediately withheld but not artificially blocked.

Examples:
- person remembers wrong detail.
- pet recognizes wrong person.
- divination sentence too precise.
- route feels familiar.

Hook:
`something is wrong`, not `big reveal`.

## 1C EP029~042 — 첫 균열
Primary mode:
**Procedural mystery**.

Method:
Rowan uses profession to test explanations.

Information:
- give measurable anomalies.
- allow reasonable mundane hypotheses.

Scene compression:
slightly faster than 1A/1B.

End-state:
reader wants physical cause of upcoming disaster, not cosmic truth yet.

# 3. ACT 2 — 닫힌 구획 / EP043~094

Primary craft mode:
**Closed-space survival procedural + suspense + ensemble pressure**.

## 2A EP043~058 — 사고
POV:
Rowan dominant, occasional bounded emergency POV.

Sentence/scene rhythm:
- shorter beats as hazard escalates.
- geography must remain clear.

Procedural density:
high, but every technical detail must affect choice.

Suspense mode:
**visible hazard / incomplete cause**.

Reader should know:
where people are, what can fail, what time/risk is left.

Reader should not know:
why all failures are happening.

Forbidden:
tech manual exposition detached from danger.

## 2B EP059~075 — 봉쇄
Primary mode:
**Pressure-cooker ensemble drama**.

Scene focus:
people trapped together long enough for alliances and resentment.

Dialogue share increases.

Conflict types:
- resource priority
- command legitimacy
- rescue risk
- family outside sector
- suspicion of sabotage

Hook:
character choice > new explosion.

## 2C EP076~087 — 선택의 비용
Primary mode:
**Moral suspense / irreversible decision**.

Technique:
- present imperfect options.
- no obviously correct author answer.
- show consequence quickly enough to hurt.

Character pressure:
same core trait becomes virtue or vice.

Forbidden:
philosophical speeches before choice.

## 2D EP088~094 — 끝났다고 생각한 순간
Primary mode:
**False resolution → catastrophic reversal**.

Rule:
local cause should be substantially understood before state catastrophe.

Purpose:
reader feels a whole lived world is lost, not merely a plot level cleared.

# 4. ACT 3 — 같은 아침이 아니다 / EP095~136

Primary mode:
**Regression imitation → rule violation → epistemic thriller**.

## 3A EP095~108 — 다시 시작?
Write briefly like a regression story on purpose.

Technique:
- repeat selected earlier scenes with compressed prose.
- reward reader memory.
- Rowan uses foreknowledge successfully.

But:
minor pre-existing differences are planted before Rowan changes anything.

Forbidden:
full scene duplication.

## 3B EP109~123 — 틀린 기억
Primary mode:
**Pattern-break suspense**.

Method:
- predicted event begins correctly.
- key variable differs too early to be butterfly effect.

Prose:
more comparison language through Rowan perception, but no ledger-like narration.

## 3C EP124~136 — 나 말고도 있다
Primary mode:
**Duel of sincere memories**.

Dialogue:
conflicting testimony scenes can be longer.

Fair-play rule:
both speakers must possess verifiable details.

Ending reward:
not answer to mechanism; answer that Rowan is not alone.

# 5. ACT 4 — 미래를 기억하는 사람들 / EP137~181

Primary modes:
**Social thriller + predictive half-life + folk ambiguity**.

## 4A EP137~150 — 먼저 아는 사람
Craft:
show foreknowledge as power in ordinary society.

Use:
markets, evacuation, relationships, crime prevention.

Avoid:
montage of magical predictions.

## 4B EP151~165 — 틀린 예언
Primary mode:
**Dramatic irony reversal**.

Reader may believe a prediction more than characters, then watch it fail.

Rule:
failure must be causally traceable to divergence, not arbitrary writer trick.

## 4C EP166~181 — 두 개의 미래
Primary mode:
**Fair-play contradiction mystery**.

Technique:
place two mutually exclusive histories side by side and make both evidentially credible.

Dialogue:
precise and sparse around proof.

End:
parallel-history hypothesis becomes emotionally necessary.

# 6. ACT 5 — 없었던 역사 / EP182~228

Primary modes:
**Exploration mystery + archaeological thriller + personal-history rupture**.

## 5A EP182~196 — 금지된 공간
Craft:
exploration geography must be tactile and legible.

Use:
- maintenance wear
- old layout
- impossible rooms
- abandoned folk traces

Mystery method:
physical evidence first, archive explanation second.

## 5B EP197~212 — 죽었던 사람이 산다
Primary mode:
**Identity horror / relationship drama**.

Rule:
returned person is not played as jump-scare monster.

Question:
who owns shared memories, promises, guilt?

Dialogue can carry high emotional load.

## 5C EP213~228 — 숫자 하나
Primary mode:
**Minimalist hard-clue reveal**.

HUMAN:1 should appear plainly.
No thunderous system speech.

Technique:
let characters argue what it means after the fact.

Forbidden:
identity reveal here.

# 7. ACT 6 — 멸망했던 메리디언 / EP229~279

Primary modes:
**War/collapse ensemble + tragedy + civic survival**.

## 6A EP229~244 — 무너지는 질서
Craft:
wide ensemble but stable focal anchors.

Show institution failure through daily consequences before speeches about collapse.

## 6B EP245~261 — 살아남기 위해 어디까지
Primary mode:
**Faction pressure + moral compromise**.

Scene rule:
no faction is represented only by its leader.
show ordinary members and internal disagreement.

## 6C EP262~279 — 초토화
Primary mode:
**Tragic accumulation**.

Sentence rhythm:
not permanently frantic; alternate silence/recovery attempts with violence.

Purpose:
loss must have texture.

Forbidden:
catastrophe escalation merely for spectacle.

# 8. ACT 7 — 돌아온 적 없는 세계 / EP280~327

Primary modes:
**Recontextualization thriller + identity dislocation + system inference**.

## 7A EP280~295 — 돌아온 곳이 없다
Craft:
use familiar places with 2~3 changed details each.

Avoid:
paragraphs listing all changes.

Reader experience:
recognition followed by uncertainty.

## 7B EP296~311 — 겹친 사람들
Primary mode:
**Relationship paradox drama**.

Question:
what does someone owe a life they did not live?

POV:
limited support POV becomes useful.

## 7C EP312~327 — 나는 한 명이 아니다
Primary mode:
**False-protagonist-theory collapse**.

Reveal Rowan is not HUMAN through converging evidence, not a single scanner.

Simultaneously show branch/merge mechanism by consequences.

Forbidden:
admin-console exposition chapter.

# 9. ACT 8 — 한 명 / EP328~370

Primary modes:
**Convergence mystery + ethical conflict + restrained revelation**.

## 8A EP328~342 — 1의 의미
Craft:
converging clues from medicine/history/infrastructure.

Rule:
three independent evidence routes before conclusion.

## 8B EP343~356 — 문명의 비용
Primary mode:
**Ethical adversarial drama**.

Do not give one spokesman the author's answer.

Represent at least:
- necessity defense
- rights critique
- institutional drift explanation
- survivor pragmatism

Show stakes in current policy decisions.

## 8C EP357~370 — 아직 아무도 아닌 사람
Primary mode:
**Quiet reveal / emotional inversion**.

The HUMAN:1 discovery scene should be visually simple.

No crown, no throne, no command key.

Emotional effect comes from contrast:
everyone else has too many pasts; this person has none.

# 10. ACT 9 — 처음 가는 곳 / EP371~410

Primary modes:
**Payoff convergence + real survival + irreversible choice**.

## 9A EP371~384 — 되돌아갈 곳이 없다
Craft:
remove the safety assumption before final crisis.

Explanation must be physical/operational, not only symbolic.

## 9B EP385~400 — 마지막 생존
Primary mode:
**Procedural survival callback**.

Use techniques learned in Act2, but circumstances force different decisions.

Payoff rule:
old skills, relationships, objects, locations and moral lessons re-enter action.

No new major mythology.

## 9C EP401~410 — 첫날
Primary mode:
**Aftermath + restrained closure**.

Sentence rhythm slows.

Resolve:
- survival outcome
- key relationship trajectories
- major clue ledger
- HUMAN:1 meaning
- Rowan's `restore original state` arc

Leave open:
ordinary future uncertainty.

No thesis monologue.

Last emotional grammar:
people act before narrator explains meaning.

# 11. Sub-Act Hook Rotation

To prevent web-novel monotony, rotate chapter-end hooks:
1. physical danger
2. unexpected choice
3. new evidence
4. relationship rupture
5. mistaken assumption collapse
6. quiet uncanny image
7. resource/time loss
8. limited answer

Do not use cliffhanger danger more than 3 consecutive episodes unless in peak survival sequence.

---

# 12. Exposition Budget

Per episode candidate:
- direct abstract explanation usually <15~20%.
- high-concept reveal episodes may exceed temporarily, but must include present-tense conflict/action.

Every explanation scene should be attached to at least one:
- decision
- accusation
- repair
- escape
- diagnosis
- legal/resource consequence
- relationship choice

Forbidden:
`characters sit in room and explain universe for entire episode`.

---

# 13. POV Distance Rules

Close POV default during:
- survival
- horror
- first anomaly
- personal history rupture

Moderately wider POV allowed during:
- civic collapse
- coordinated emergency response
- final convergence

Never omnisciently reveal system motive before character evidence earns it.

---

# 14. Dialogue Grammar by Function

Procedural dialogue:
short, specific, action-linked.

Relationship dialogue:
allows ellipsis, history, misinterpretation.

Mystery dialogue:
avoid characters perfectly summarizing theory for reader.
Use disagreement and incomplete models.

Ethical dialogue:
attach ideology to personal cost.

Prophecy/divination dialogue:
ambiguous enough for two interpretations, concrete enough for later payoff.

---

# 15. Horror Grammar

Ghost-like scenes:
- low explanation.
- sensory contradiction.
- delayed verification.

Zombie-like outbreak:
- prioritize containment, triage, identity fear.
- avoid gore becoming genre center.

Child uncanny scenes:
child must still behave like a child outside anomaly.

---

# 16. Character Pressure Grammar

For each major character, a scene should ask:
`What does this person's stable trait do under this exact pressure?`

Never ask:
`How can I make this version opposite for surprise?`

A different-state choice requires changed conditions such as:
- family alive/dead
- rank/authority
- prior trauma
- resource level
- trusted partner
- foreknowledge
- institutional context

---

# 17. Payoff-heavy Ending Rule

From approximately EP350 onward:
- new Core cast = 0.
- new major ontology = 0.
- new long-range MacGuffin <=2 and preferably 0.
- old devices re-enter as active causes.

By EP400:
reader should be able to explain the main mechanism without an encyclopedia chapter.

EP401~410 exist for consequence, not another twist.

---

# 18. QA Harness

Before accepting any Sub-Act outline, check:
1. Does its craft mode differ meaningfully from adjacent Sub-Act?
2. Is POV scope appropriate?
3. Is exposition subordinate to lived action?
4. Does survival geography remain clear?
5. Does at least one relationship change?
6. Is there a local payoff?
7. Does the ending hook rotate from recent episodes?
8. Are clues fair but not over-highlighted?
9. Is the character acting from stable traits under changed conditions?
10. Does this Sub-Act avoid prematurely explaining the series truth?

Result labels:
`PASS / REVIEW / FAIL`.
